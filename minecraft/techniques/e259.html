Cursed Transmutation

Cursed Transmutation, Elemental Manipulation, Alchemy of the Soul and Cursed Energy Transmutation: reshape matter, elements and flesh itself.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Transmute2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Reshape a 12-block cube of matter where you look (36 blocks) into the chosen form (Technique Key 1): a weapon, your weapon blows add 9 damage for a minute · fire, 20 damage to everything in it and the ground alight · water, everything in it swept back and slowed, and fire put out · stone, a wall that stands a minute; with Variant a bridge two wide from your feet the way you look, and looking straight down a pillar that lifts you.

6th

Alchemy of the Soul1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour CE into flesh, the ally's you look at within 4 blocks or your own: 7% of max health mended per 2 CE.

3rd

Philosopher's Stone0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

With the feat. Panacea, once a long rest: a fragment ends poison, wither, weakness, slowness, blindness, hunger and bad omens on the ally you look at within 4 blocks, or on you. With Variant, Chrysopoeia, once a long rest: up to 8 iron or copper ingots in your hand become gold.

6th

Toxic Alchemy3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks): 27 poison damage, and poisoned for a minute (a hardy foe may halve it and shrug off the poison).

11th

Cursed Energy Transmutation2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour CE: a 36-block line of raw force along your look, 9 damage per 2 CE.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Transmutation Sphere10 CE · Maximum (X)

A 12-block sphere where you look is unmade and remade: 53 force damage to everything in it, stunned 2 s.

Domain

18th

Mandala of Dependent Origination

The whole Domain is transmuted at once in the chosen form, on every chosen foe: fire (41 damage), water (swept and slowed) or stone (27 damage and held 1.5 s).
