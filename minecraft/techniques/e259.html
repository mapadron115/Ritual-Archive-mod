Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Transmute2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Reshape a 12-block cube of matter where you look (18 blocks) into the chosen form (Technique Key 1): a weapon, your weapon blows add 9 damage for a minute · fire, 20 damage to everything in it and the ground alight · water, everything in it swept back and slowed, and fire put out · stone, a wall that stands a minute.

6th

Alchemy of the Soul1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour CE into your own flesh: 9 damage mended per 2 CE.

6th

Toxic Alchemy3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (9 blocks): 27 poison damage, and poisoned for a minute (a hardy foe may halve it and shrug off the poison).

11th

Cursed Energy Transmutation2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour CE: a 18-block line of raw force along your look, 9 damage per 2 CE.
