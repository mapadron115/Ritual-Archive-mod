Black Flash Chaining

Not a technique but a rhythm: chain hit into hit until the distortion answers in red, again and again.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

11th

Step Inside8 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute you fight inside the distortion: the Chain never breaks, and your first critical each 3 s is a sure Black Flash.

3rd

See the Gap1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute you see the distortions: every creature within 16 blocks shows to you, and each blow builds the Chain twice.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Crimson Distortion10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you are the distortion: the Chain is full, every critical you land is a Black Flash, and more of your blows land critically. Afterwards you're spent: weak and slow-handed for a minute.

Passives

1st

The Chain

Every blow you land builds the Chain (up to 5); 3 s without one breaks it. Each link: blows 3% harder, 2% likelier to be critical, and a critical 6% likelier to be a Black Flash.

18th

The Spark Remembers

The Chain never breaks, and your blows land critically more often.

Technique feats

3rd

The Gap Wears You

Once every 3 s, a creature's melee blow slips 2.5 microseconds sideways (2 CE): it lands at half, and the attacker takes 18 damage.

6th

Spark-Sight

Once every 2 minutes, a blow that would land was seen coming in the distortion: it misses.
