Mandate of the First Emperor

The First Emperor reads the Crux Stars where power converges, breaks its flow, takes tribute from every failed blow, and raises the terracotta legions.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Drifting Tortoise1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A Crux strike at the foe you look at (36 blocks): it's Flow-Broken for a minute. Its blows on you land a fifth softer and everything of yours lands a fifth harder on it. 6 foes at a time.

3rd

White Tiger Crescent Moon2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (6 blocks): 33 damage, and it's thrown 9 blocks (a strong foe holds).

11th

Swallow Slash2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A cut on the foe you look at (9 blocks): 40 damage, and 13 damage more for each Tribute you spend, up to 3.

11th

Terracotta Legions10 CE · Cast (Z)

6 terracotta soldiers rise and fight for a minute: each strikes for 21 damage once every 3 s. They can be struck down.

3rd

Mount Tai Dragon Claw2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at (9 blocks): 33 damage. A Tribute, if you have one, also slows it for 3 s.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: King of All Beginnings10 CE · Maximum (X)

Needs full Tribute. Every foe within 36 blocks is Flow-Broken, and for a minute Heavenly Phoenix costs nothing. Your Tribute is kept.

Passives

1st

Heavenly Phoenix

Once every 3 s (2 CE), a creature's blow on you is cut by 25% of your health (34% from a Flow-Broken attacker), never below a quarter of the blow.

1st

Tribute

Up to 11: earned when the Phoenix turns a blow from a Flow-Broken foe, or cuts 10% of your health off any blow.

Domain

18th

All Under Heaven

Throne of the First Emperor. Each pulse the Imperial Meridian Flow-Breaks every chosen foe and takes tribute: 40 damage each, and a Tribute.
