❖ e288 · Jujutsu Kaisen ❖

Teleportation

Marks, blinks, and sending others where you please: a blink to where you look, marks to return to from any distance, a dodge, and a soul swap.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Teleport2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You vanish and reappear where you look (up to 24 blocks, never inside a wall). With Variant: to your chosen mark within 930 blocks, if no foe is watching you; allies beside you come along.

6th

Mastered Teleport8 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

To your chosen mark (Technique Key 1 picks it) at any distance in this world. Or, with a foe in your sights (within 6 blocks), it's sent there instead (a strong will may resist).

1st

Teleport Mark1 CE · Quick (G)

A mark at your feet to return to (up to 15; the oldest fades when you place too many). It costs nothing.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Soul Swap10 CE · Maximum (X)

You and the foe in your sights (within 12 blocks) trade places, soul first: it reels, held for 3 s, and takes 89 damage through armor as its soul tears loose.

Passives

3rd

Improved Teleport

Once every 3 s, a blow that would land on you is dodged: you blink out of the way (1 CE).

11th

Improved Marking

Marks cost nothing, and you can hold three for each point of CE power.

Technique feats

11th

Warded Marks

Your marks watch as well as carry: you're told when a foe comes within 2 blocks of one, and it glows.
