Jajanken

An honest game: Rock charges a fist while you stand glowing, Paper throws it, Scissors cuts. You always say which one is coming. No Domain, only a vow that spends your future.

6arts
3passives
5technique feats
1stMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Rock1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to charge (a charge every 0.75 s, 1 CE each, up to 6; you're glowing, half as fast and take 15% more), release: 26 damage per charge to the foe in front of you.

3rd

Paper2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Conviction thrown 20 blocks: 27 damage.

6th

Scissors2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

An edge of aura (5 blocks): 27 damage to what's in front of you.

20th

Everything I Have0 CE · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest, a minute: instant charges (still 1 CE each), free Paper and Scissors, and charging no longer slows or exposes you. Then your Cursed Energy empties and stays empty until a long rest.

1st

Forbidden Vow: The Future, Spent0 CE · Cast (Z)

Cast twice to swear. A minute grown, as if at level 20: six instant charges, free arts, and 15% of Jajanken's blows land half again as hard. Then Jajanken leaves you for good.

Maximum

1st

All-In Rock8 CE · Maximum (X)

Six charges at once: 159 damage, the foe thrown 10 blocks and knocked down, and the ground cratered.

Passives

1st

Declared Aloud

Every art announces itself, and Rock's chant comes a line a charge. While charging Rock you glow for all to see and take 15% more damage.

11th

Held Gambit

If a foe hits you or comes within 4 blocks while you're charging Rock, the punch releases into it at once.

18th

Crater

Rock with 4+ charges also hits everything within 3 blocks of the target for half, knocks it down and craters the ground.

Technique feats

3rd

Ground Slam

Rock with no foe in front of you slams the ground: everything within 5 blocks takes it and is knocked down.

3rd

Aftershock

A landed Rock drives on into the foe behind it for half (1 CE).

3rd

Signal Paper

Variant + Paper fires straight up: no damage, 1 CE less, a pillar of gold aura seen from far off for 5 s.

6th

Sunder

Scissors strips its foes' armor for 10 s, cuts the cobwebs in its arc and frees you and the allies in it from holds.

6th

Paper Mine

Technique Key 1 (1 CE): press a Paper charge into the ground where you look. The first foe to come near sets it off. Up to three.
