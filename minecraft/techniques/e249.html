Cursed Corpse Creation

Cores, bodies, mutations, the Prized Creation and Perfect Creations: Yaga's cursed-corpse construction and command.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Cursed Corpse0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Sew a core into a body of the chosen size (Variant + Technique Key 1): Small (1 CE, 9 damage a blow) · Medium (2 CE, 18 damage) · Large (4 CE, 27 damage) · Huge (6 CE, 36 damage) · Gargantuan (10 CE, 53 damage). It fights for 10 minutes (an hour from 18th), up to 6 corpses.

Passives

3rd

Corpse Tactics

Technique Key 1: Cooperative (one blow in four lands a quarter harder) · Offensive (9 damage more a blow) · Defensive (blows land 15% lighter on you while a corpse stands).

6th

Cursed Corpse Mutation

One corpse in twenty comes out mutated: half again as big and twice as mean.

18th

Perfect Creations

Your corpses outlast the fight: they stay an hour.

Technique feats

1st

Expert Maker

Every corpse costs a third less CE.

3rd

Patchwork Salvage

When a corpse falls, a third of its strength is grafted onto another of yours.

6th

Mutate Specialist

The first corpse you sew after each long rest always comes out mutated.
