Skinless Blight

Create Blight patches that wither and suppress healing, with fresh water a hard weakness that cuts safe lanes through your territory.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Blight Patch2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 6-block patch of blight where you look (18 blocks) for a minute (up to 6; never on water): foes in it Wither each 3 s, 7 damage per Wither (up to 5), and most of what they heal is taken back. Plants in it rot away and grass dies to dirt.

1st

Blight Breath2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A 6-block cone of rotten breath: 20 damage and a Wither.

Passives

1st

Binding Weakness: Fresh Water

While you stand in water your blight hits at half. A foe standing in water can't be withered by a patch, and the water washes a Wither off it each 3 s.

The whole shore is blight: every chosen foe Withers twice and takes 27 damage.
