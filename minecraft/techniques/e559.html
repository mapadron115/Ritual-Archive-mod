The Immortal Game

Chess with full-scale shikigami: pawns screen and promote, knights leap the line, rooks own the hallway, and a king who castles out of every blow.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Set the Board1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Set the piece Technique Key 1 picked where you look (9 blocks) for a minute: pawn 1 CE, knight 2, bishop 2, rook 3, queen 6 (her arrival lets every piece strike at once). You field up to 6. Pieces can be taken.

1st

The Move0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The piece that can reach the foe you look at leaps onto it and strikes (pawn 7 damage, up to the queen's 27 damage). Once every 3 s; 2 CE buys another.

Passives

3rd

Castling

Once every 3 s, a blow meant for you is Castled away (2 CE): you and your nearest piece within 18 blocks swap places.

6th

Check and Promotion

Foes within 4.5 blocks of a piece are in check: they take a fifth more from you. A pawn that takes a foe is promoted to the best piece you can field.
