The Immortal Game

Chess with full-scale shikigami: pawns screen and promote, knights leap the line, rooks own the hallway, and a king who castles out of every blow.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Set the Board1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Set the piece Technique Key 1 picked where you look (9 blocks) for 10 minutes: pawn 1 CE (free on an empty board), knight 2, bishop 2, rook 3, queen 6 (her arrival lets every piece strike at once). You field up to 6. Pieces can be taken, and a taken piece is gone until a long rest: the set is eight pawns, two knights, two bishops, two rooks and a queen.

1st

The Move0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Your pieces move as in chess, one block a square: the pawn one forward (two on its first move), taking one diagonally forward; the knight's L, over anything; the bishop diagonally, the rook straight and the queen either way, up to 8 squares, stopped by the first thing in the way. Along the way a piece leaps a rise up to its height (pawn 1 block, knight 5, bishop 6, queen 10; the rook climbs nothing) and drops any height. If a piece's move reaches the foe you look at, the strongest such takes its square and strikes (pawn 5 damage, up to the queen's 16 damage); if none can, the best placed piece moves toward it without a blow. A foe off every line is safe. Once every 3 s; 2 CE buys another (free inside your own Domain).

Maximum

13th

Maximum: The Immortal Sacrifice10 CE · 30 s cooldown · Maximum (X)

With a pawn, a knight, a bishop, a rook and a queen all on the board, they close on the foe you look at (27 blocks). Each leaps to a square its own move attacks from, the foe is held for 1.5 s, and they take in turn: the pawn's 12 damage, the knight's 16 damage, the bishop's 20 damage, the rook's 24 damage and the queen's 28 damage. The mate is paid for with the queen: she leaves the board, not taken, and can be set again. A piece with no square to strike from sits it out.

Passives

3rd

Castling

Once every 3 s, a blow meant for you is Castled away (2 CE): you and your nearest piece within 18 blocks swap places.

6th

Check and Promotion

Foes within 4.5 blocks of a piece are in check: they take a fifth more from you. A pawn that takes a foe is promoted to the best piece you can field.

Domain

18th

Checkmate

The board captures every chosen foe: 37 damage, half again for a foe in check.
