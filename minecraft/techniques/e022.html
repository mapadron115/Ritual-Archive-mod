Pachinko Fever

Idle Death Gamble as a playable loop: balls that feed the expectation meter, a bet, three jackpot reels, and a Jackpot that won't let you die.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Green Ball1 CE · Cast (Z)

A ball at the foe you look at (6 blocks): 11 damage. In your Domain, a hit raises expectation 10%.

1st

Red Rod3 CE · Cast (Z)

A rod at arm's length (3 blocks): 16 damage. In your Domain, a hit raises expectation 25%.

1st

Gold Doors4 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Train doors of gold slam on the foe you look at (6 blocks): 20 damage, and it's held. In your Domain, a hit raises expectation 50%.

1st

Great Luck / Fever0 CE · Quick (G)

Great Luck, once a long rest: your next melee blow lands half again as hard. During a Jackpot (from 11th): spend a Fever point instead, a hard blow and every art ready at once, once every 3 s.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Love Train10 CE · Maximum (X)

All three at once on the foe you look at: they raise expectation even outside your Domain, and since all three strike the same foe, the bet is placed on the spot.

Passives

3rd

The machine

Technique Key 1, in your Domain: bet all your expectation (up to 190%). A number from 1 to 20 (plus 1 per 10%) picks the event: the better it is, the fewer faces on the reels, 12, 10, 8 or 6. It runs for 2–8 s, then a second number sets your rerolls: 1–12 three, 13–15 four, 16–17 five, 18–19 ten, 20 a Rainbow (an automatic win). Three reels spin and reroll toward a match; three of a kind pays. A miss wipes the expectation.

3rd

Jackpot

The Domain ends and for 12 s: your CE refills every 3 s, you heal fast, and nothing can take you below half a heart. Your free Domain comes back.

6th

On a Roll

After a Jackpot, every number the machine draws is drawn twice, keeping the better, until you log off.

11th

The Fever

During a Jackpot, every death it refuses earns a Fever point (up to 6), spent on the quick key.

18th

Luckiest Sorcerer Alive

The number that sets your rerolls is drawn three times, keeping the best. The Jackpot lasts twice as long, and it heals twice as fast.

Domain

3rd

Idle Death Gamble

The floor plays itself: every 3 s a green ball lands on each chosen foe, raising expectation. It deals no damage: this Domain is all rules. Free once a long rest (and again after a Jackpot), then 25 CE.
