Techniques / Jujutsu Kaisen

❖ e021 · Jujutsu Kaisen ❖

New Shadow Style

A practiced art: a Simple Domain circle where the sword answers on its own, an instant Batto draw, and the Evening Moon that cuts whatever steps in.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

3rd

Batto Sword Draw3 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

One draw: you cross up to 5 blocks where you look in an instant, and your edge tastes every foe along the way (up to 5): twice your weapon's blow and 9 damage more, often a critical.

6th

Evening Moon: Cut / Guard0 CE · Cast (Z)

Toggle what the Evening Moon does when a foe steps into your circle (once a second, sword in hand): Cut, 1 CE, a hard blow on the newcomer; Guard, no CE, the newcomer is thrown back out.

11th

Moonlight Veil2 CE · Cast (Z)

A minute with your energy along the blade: melee blows add 7 damage, and one in five lands half again as hard.

1st

Simple Domain0 CE · Quick (G)

Draw a 4.5-block circle where you stand for 30 s (free 6 times a long rest, then 2 CE): shots that cross its line are cut down (one every half second), you take 15% less inside, its strength takes 80 of the blows on you before it breaks, and a hostile Domain's sure-hit can't find you there. While a free use is left, it draws itself when a hostile Domain starts forming around you or its sure-hit is about to land. Stepping out ends it (not any more: Perfect Shadow Style).

Passives

1st

True New Shadow Style

The circle is drawn before thought can intervene: it's instant, and free a few times a rest.

18th

Perfect Shadow Style

The circle costs nothing, has no limit, and walks with you.

Technique feats

3rd

Instantaneous Attack

Each circle you draw holds one prepared draw: the first foe to step inside meets a Batto-strength cut.

6th

First Strike

Even without the circle, the Evening Moon waits: after 5 s with no foe near, the first one to come within 4 blocks is cut.
