Presentation

Presentation is everything: holographic doubles that eat the blows, a dehydration gun that does exactly what it says, and a speech nobody escapes.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

6th

Dehydration Gun3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) is dehydrated: half its size, slowed, its weapon blows half as hard, for a minute (a player 10 s; a hardy foe 3 s; a great foe only hits a fifth softer). Water undoes it at once.

11th

The Speech5 CE · 12 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every foe within 36 blocks stops to listen: held for 3 s and fighting weakened for a minute (a steady mind is only weakened).

1st

Presentation!2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

5 holographic doubles stand round you for ten minutes: half the time, a creature's blow aimed at you lands on one instead, popping it.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The Grand Presentation12 CE · Maximum (X)

Every foe within 72 blocks: 71 damage and frozen in awe for 3 s (a steady mind takes half and isn't). Your doubles return in full.

Misdirection
