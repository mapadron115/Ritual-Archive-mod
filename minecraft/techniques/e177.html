World Coil

Lay spectral serpent coils across the field, close them into Rings, and decide whether each circle protects, poisons, or constricts.

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Close the Ring2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A serpent Ring closes on the ground where you look (12 blocks; 8 across) for a minute, up to 6. Ward: you take 30% less inside. Venom: foes inside take 14 damage every 3 s. Constrict: foes inside are slowed and take 18 damage every 3 s.

1st

Coil Segment1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A spectral serpent 12 blocks long lies across the ground where you look (36 blocks) for ten minutes, up to 12. A foe crossing it stumbles. Three of them near where you Close the Ring are bound into it: the Ring is free and lasts ten minutes.

1st

World Fang0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The serpent strikes the foe you look at (6 blocks): 9 damage of poison; one on a Segment is poisoned (a resist holds it).

1st

Tighten1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Every Ring tightens at once: every foe in one takes 18 damage and is held 2 s. Nested World: the coils walk inward, dragging each foe 3 blocks toward the heart.

Passives

1st

Ring Mode (Technique Key 1)

Picks what your next Ring does: Ward (you and your allies inside take 30% less), Venom (foes inside also can't heal for a 3 s; a resist halves it) or Constrict.

6th

World Venom

Venom lingers three 3 ss. Once every 3 s, a foe crossing a Segment is bitten by World Fang from the coil (1 CE).

Domain

18th

Midgard Encircled

The World Serpent closes round the Domain: every chosen foe is constricted (held 1.5 s, 36 damage) and poisoned.

Ocean Coil

The serpent's home water: you swim swiftly and breathe underwater.

3rd

Closing Snap

A Ring that closes snaps shut on the foe nearest its heart: it's dragged toward the centre and held 1.5 s (a strong one may hold its ground).
