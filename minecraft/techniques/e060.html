❖ e060 · Anime & Manga ❖

Battle Waltz

Spinning tops with brutally simple orders. Their randomness is area denial, and the dancer becomes a top too.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (Y) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

3rd

Battle Waltz3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

12 tops swarm a 4-block spot where you look for 30 s; foes in it stumble. At most 24 swarm tops at once; the oldest stop.

6th

Tornado Top3 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute you spin your own body: 4 more armor, nothing knocks you back, and whatever strikes you takes 26 damage (once every 3 s). Spinning Guard (2 CE, once every 3 s) lessens a heavy blow by 59% of your health.

11th

Shotgun Blues5 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your tops fire as one down a 12-block line along your look: 66 damage, and they land at its end.

1st

Dancing Top1 CE · 0.3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Throw a top along your look; it spins, ricochets off every surface and strikes whatever's near for 13 damage. Up to 9 loose tops; the oldest stops. They spin 45 s.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Fifty-Top Funeral10 CE · Maximum (X)

Fifty spectral tops in five swarms where you look (24 blocks) for 30 s: 13 damage to whatever they touch. A new Funeral ends the last.

Passives

1st

Tops

Each top strikes for 13 damage, harder at 6th, 11th and 18th. They fall, roll and bounce like real tops.

Technique feats

3rd

Low Waltz

Technique Key 1 (2 CE) sends a top within 6 blocks of the foe you look at spinning into its feet: 13 damage, and it trips (a nimble foe keeps its feet).

11th

Blue Shot

Shotgun Blues shatters one of its tops for 26 damage more.
