Magnetic Manipulation

Polarize, Attraction and Repulsion, magnetic flight, induction bolts, field deflection, the railgun and Iron Requiem.

7arts
4passives
3technique feats
11thMaximum
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Attraction / Repulsion1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every foe within 6 blocks of where you look is pulled to that point. Hold Variant for Repulsion: every foe within 6 blocks of you is thrown away. Polarized foes answer from anywhere within 20 blocks, and opposite charges that meet slam together for 22 damage each.

3rd

Induction Bolt1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A bolt of current, 20 blocks: 22 damage (11 damage more on a polarized foe).

11th

Polarity Field3 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute, a 5-block field around you: hostile shots that enter it are torn apart, every foe in it is polarized, and loose metal drifts to your hand.

11th

Railgun4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A slug driven down a 40-block line by the field: 66 damage to everything on it. Carry an iron nugget or ingot and it's the slug: 22 damage more (33 damage for an ingot), and it's spent.

6th

Field Compression2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The polarized foe you look at (20 blocks) is squeezed: 18 damage, held 3 s. A foe wearing metal can't resist; any other may.

1st

Polarize1 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Mark the foe you look at (20 blocks) with a polarity for a minute, + and − in turn (up to 7 marks). Marks are magnets: within 5 blocks of each other, opposites are hauled together and slam for 22 damage each, and like charges are flung apart.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Iron Requiem10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every foe within 40 blocks takes the mark at once, and is drawn together where you look and crushed, 89 damage each.

Passives

1st

Signature

Variant + Polarize tells you where every mark is: how far and which way.

3rd

Magnetic Flight

You fly on the planet's field.

11th

Electromagnetic Perception

Every foe within 18 blocks glows: you see their current through walls and smoke.

6th

Field Deflection

Once every 3 s (1 CE): a shot that would hit you is turned aside, and, for 1 CE more, sent back.

Domain

18th

The Master's Field

Every chosen foe takes the mark, and each pulse 66 damage: it's drawn toward the Domain's heart and held until the next pulse.
