Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Séance2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Channel the freshest body you've killed within 18 blocks (30 s): Physical, more life, armor and strength, the bigger it was; or with Technique Key 1, Both (2 CE more): its soul too, one of its traits (flight, fireproof, immovable, aquatic or swift). 1 CE every 6 s to keep. With Variant the ally you look at (9 blocks) is the vessel instead, and must stay within 48 blocks of you. A soul whose body was stronger than its vessel may take the reins for 3 s each time you pay: the vessel can't cast, and strikes harder.
