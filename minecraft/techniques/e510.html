Emperor's Chains

Five chains: a jailer, a judge, a dowser, a healer and the scarlet-eyed emperor. Chain Jail binds the sworn kind absolutely, and every 3 s of Emperor Time burns an hour of your life.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Swear the Vow0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Name your sworn group, once per character, for good: the kind of creature you look at, or a player's team (that one player if they have none).

1st

Chain Jail2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The middle chain lashes your sworn foe where you look (36 blocks): held 3 s and its output sealed (its shots and arts shut off, and a sorcerer's CE can't refill) for 9 s. The vow narrows the chain: it never closes on anyone else.

1st

Dowsing Chain0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Attune to the creature or player you look at: until a long rest you know how far it is and which way, within 1600 blocks (your sworn group at any distance in the same world).

1st

Holy Chain0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The thumb mends the ally you look at (4.5 blocks), or you: up to 5 CE, 10% of their health per CE (15% in Emperor Time).

1st

Steal Chain2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The syringe tip, 9 blocks. A sorcerer's CE is sealed for 3 s and one use of their technique's first art is yours: Variant + Technique Key 1 casts it once, at its own cost (while you hold it, Emperor Time can't be cooled). Anything else: its strength drains for 3 s, and the stolen aura comes back to you (3 CE, once every 3 s).

3rd

Judgment Chain3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Stake a foe within reach (4.5 blocks) with a rule: "do not strike me" (with Variant: "use no technique": a player spending CE or a creature loosing a shot breaks it). If the rule falls, so does the stake: a foe 10 levels under you, or 5 to 9 under and already low (half its health at 5, more as the gap grows), player or creature but never a boss, dies; anything else is torn for a tenth of its health.

6th

Emperor Time2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Scarlet eyes: the chains at full power and your hands faster, but every 3 s burns an hour of your life. Press again to cool them.

Maximum

1st

The Unappealable Sentence8 CE · Maximum (X)

Burn 12 hours: your sworn foe where you look (36 blocks) is held and judged, its output sealed for a minute.

Passives

1st

Binding Vow of the Designated

Chain Jail, its snares and the Sentence close only on your sworn group. The same stake rests in your heart.

6th

Lifespan Ledger

Every 24 hours burned by Emperor Time lowers your max health for good.

11th

Twin Fetters

Chain Jail reaches 36 blocks and also binds a second of your sworn kind near the first (2 CE).

20th

Post-Mortem Protocol

Once a long rest, falling leaves you standing for a minute in free Emperor Time.

Domain

18th

The Scarlet Courtroom

The vow executed perfectly: at each pulse your sworn group is jailed in full and staked ("do not strike me") and takes 66 damage; anyone else the chains pass through like smoke, lashing for 44 damage.

The Snare Left Behind

Technique Key 1 (2 CE) lays a loop of Chain Jail where you look: the first of your sworn group to step in is bound in full. Up to three.

6th

Borrowed Hours

In Emperor Time, once every 10 s an affliction on you is thrown off at once (2 CE and six more hours of life).
