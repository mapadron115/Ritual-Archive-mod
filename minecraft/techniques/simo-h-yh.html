Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Isanmaalle: Flesh Round1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A rifle shot along your aim (180 blocks), paid with 6% of your health: 29 damage, half again if you haven't moved since your last shot. A loaded organ round fires instead.

3rd

Sniper's Nest1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Make a nest where you stand: while you're within 6 blocks of it, creatures lose track of you.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The 543rd Bullet5 CE · Maximum (X)

The heart round: your health and most of your max health are gone until a long rest restores you. One shot along your aim (240 blocks): 221 damage through armor, piercing everything in the line.

Passives

6th

Organ Rounds

Technique Key 1 loads an organ round for your next shot, each paid in max health until a long rest: Kidney (10%: 41 damage more, pierces), Liver (15%: a line through walls and everything, 20 damage more on the first), Pancreas (20%: 54 damage more, through armor). At most 80% of your max health goes this way.
