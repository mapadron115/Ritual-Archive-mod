Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Sound Blast1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A blast of sound along your look (18 blocks): 66 damage, shoving the foe back, and glass and ice shatter where it strikes. Hold for a longer volley: up to 3 blasts, 1 CE each beyond the first.

6th

Sound Wave5 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A wall of sound, a 9.0-block cone: 133 damage, shoved far and deafened (reeling a moment, and hitting softer). Hold a second first, the Musical Climax: throw it as an 18-block line from where you look instead.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Epic Solo8 CE · Maximum (X) · hold to pour

A minute on stage: your blows add 22 damage (hold to pour up to 3 CE more, 22 damage each), and every foe within 4.5 blocks of you takes 15 damage every 2 s.

Passives

6th

Emotional Melodies

Technique Key 1 changes the vibe (5 CE, a minute). Your Sound Blasts carry it: Happy charms (it stops fighting you), Sad pins it down, Angry turns it against its own side. Strong foes shrug most of it off.

Domain

18th

Hall of Fame

The encore nobody asked for: every chosen foe takes your Sound Blast (66 damage) and is deafened for a minute (it hits softer).
