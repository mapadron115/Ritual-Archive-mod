Pain Packer

Heat-proof armor drinks every wound and banks it as heat, then gives it all back as a rising sun. The more it hurts, the bigger the dawn.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Vent2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The bank bursts 4.5 blocks around you: 9 damage per 10 Pain (at least that much), and the ice and snow there melt. The bank empties.

11th

Slow Burn2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute: each 3 s, foes within 3 blocks take 7 damage per 20 Pain, and the bank drains 20.

3rd

Heat Sense0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Pain radiates: every creature within 9 blocks, and how much it's hurt.

1st

Pain Packer1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Conjure (or dismiss) the heat-proof armor: you can't be set burning, and every wound is banked as Pain (up to 200).

Maximum

1st

Rising Sun5 CE · Maximum (X)

The bank becomes a sun over you: 11 damage per 10 Pain (at least 33 damage) within 18 blocks, then 22 damage each 3 s for a minute; what's under it melts.

Passives

1st

The bank

Pain banked is the damage you take while armored (as a share of your health), up to 200. Hurt with the bank already full, it Overflows: a free Vent, on its own.

6th

Backdraft

Once every 3 s (1 CE), a melee attacker eats up to 30 Pain back, 7 damage per 10.

18th

Deep Bank

The bank holds twice as much, and the sealed armor can't be blinded.

20th

Solar Core

The first wound each 3 s banks double.

Domain

18th

High Noon

The sun doesn't miss: every chosen foe takes 9 damage per 10 Pain each pulse, and the bank doesn't empty.

Banked Light

Cauterize

Solar Forge
