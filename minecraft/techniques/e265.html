Explosive Flesh

Turn your own body into the bomb: detachable explosive anatomy from tooth to leg, thrown or detonated on the spot.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Explosive Flesh0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Tear off the chosen part (Technique Key 1) and throw it; it detonates where it lands: Tooth (1 CE, 14 damage, 4.5 blocks) Finger (2 CE, 20 damage, 6.0 blocks) Hand (3 CE, 34 damage, 7.5 blocks) Arm (5 CE, 54 damage, 10.5 blocks) Leg (6 CE, 68 damage, 13.5 blocks). Each costs some of your health (it grows back); hands and bigger blow a pit in soft ground. A nimble foe halves it. Bites the Dust: with Variant the part lies where it lands for up to a minute (3 at most), and Variant + Technique Key 1 sets them all off at once, half again as hard.

3rd

Payload0 CE · Quick (G)

With Elemental Bomb: choose what your thrown parts carry, plain · fire (it burns and catches) · cold (it freezes and slows) · lightning (the wound jumps to a second foe), at twice the part's CE.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Full Body Detonation10 CE · Maximum (X)

Everything at once: you detonate where you stand, 108 damage to everything within 12 blocks and a pit in the ground, and a third of your health goes with it.

Passives

3rd

Faster Throws

Tearing and throwing is quick.

6th

Guerilla Tactics

Once every 3 s, when a melee blow lands on you or you land one, the chosen part goes off on your body (its CE and health): everything near you takes its blast.

11th

Stronger Explosions

Blasts are 1.5 blocks wider, their pits half a block wider, and what they catch is dazed and blinded a moment.

Domain

18th

Detonation Field

You choose a limb on every chosen foe, and detonate it: 54 damage. Inside it your own parts cost no CE, only the flesh.
