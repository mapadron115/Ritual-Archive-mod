Seven Terrors of the Cedar Forest

Manifest one of seven Terror Faces at a time; cedar groves extend your aura, so the forest itself carries the fear you choose.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Cedar Grove2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Three cursed cedars grow where you look (18 blocks) for a minute (up to 6 groves): the grove carries your Terror Face 6 blocks around it.

11th

Sevenfold Guardian2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For 2 rounds you wear your Terror Face and the next one together: every pulse carries both fears.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Cedar Forest Before the Axe10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you wear all seven faces at once, in your aura and every grove.

Passives

1st

Terror Faces (Technique Key 1)

Each 3 s your aura (6 blocks) and your groves strike foes for 9 damage and carry your Face's fear: Dread (they hit 25% softer) · Stillness (slowed) · Flame (burning) · Blindness · Rot (poisoned) · Flight (they flee) · Silence (one in three held a moment). Strong foes shrug the fear off some of the time.

The whole forest wears your face: every chosen foe suffers its fear and takes 36 damage.
