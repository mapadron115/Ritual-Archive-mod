The Hut Turns

The witch of the deep forest: a fence of bones, a flying mortar, grasping roots, and the hut on chicken legs that comes when called.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

The Fence of Bones2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A fence of real bone, 7 wide and 3 high, rises where you look (36 blocks) for a minute. A foe pressed against it takes 7 damage once every 3 s.

3rd

Root Grasp3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Roots seize every foe within 9 blocks of where you look (36 blocks): 14 damage and held for 3 s (a strong foe takes the damage only).

6th

The Hut Turns5 CE · Cast (Z)

The hut on chicken legs comes where you look (36 blocks) and stalks your foes for a minute, trampling them for 20 damage once every 3 s. It can be struck down, and there's only ever one. With Variant, it settles where you look instead: it turns its door to you, kneels, and stands as a real cabin you can walk into, with a bed, a double chest, a furnace and a hearth (it needs open, fairly level ground, 10 by 11 blocks). It keeps what you leave in the chest and furnace wherever it settles next. Variant again and it rises and walks out the rest of its minute. It also packs up if you go more than 64 blocks away. Settling and rising cost nothing while it's out.

11th

Eat the Path8 CE · 12 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute 36 blocks round where you look (72 blocks) is hungry forest: foes inside are slowed, lose sight now and then, and hit you 15% softer.

3rd

The Mortar Flies3 CE · 12 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Climb into the mortar and fly for 6 s.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Wild Takes Back10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute 72 blocks round where you stand is old-growth forest. Foes inside are slowed, hit you softer, and take 27 damage once every 3 s, 3 times at most on each foe, from lashing branches (a nimble foe takes half); a branch may hold one for 1.5 s, and you mend a little for each foe lashed.
