The Thirty Ride3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Two warriors of Ling muster at your side for a minute: each strikes for 11 damage once every 3 s. They can be struck down.

3rd

Demon-Slayer's Grip2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Seize the foe you look at (6 blocks): held fast for 1.5 s, a Named foe for 3 s (a strong foe slips free; a great foe can't be held).

1st

The Naming2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at (36 blocks) is Named for a minute: it's slowed by your name, and one weapon blow in four on it lands half again as hard.
