Iron Sorcery

Doom armor and gauntlet, arcane bolts, counterspells, Doombots, armor modules, the Time Platform and Castle Doom.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Gauntlet0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) and Quick (G)

The armor's integrated gauntlet: a blast of force, 30 blocks, 18 damage.

3rd

Arcane Bolt1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A seeking bolt of sorcery at the foe you look at (30 blocks): 22 damage.

6th

Doombot3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A Doombot decoy for a minute: it draws foes and fires its own gauntlet, 18 damage every 3 s (2 at a time).

1st

Trap Scheme2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

An arcane mine where you look (18 blocks): the first foe within 1.5 blocks of it takes 36 damage. It waits until a long rest; 6 at a time.

15th

Time Platform6 CE · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest: you go back six seconds, to where you stood and the health you had (if it was more).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Power Cosmic Theft12 CE · Maximum (X)

A minute: the next great blow against you (a tenth of your health or more) is caught and stolen. You take none of it, and it's fired back along a 30-block beam at twice the force.

Passives

1st

Doom Armor

Always worn: +6 armor and +2 toughness, and sealed: you never run out of air.

3rd

Counterspell Scheme

Once every 3 s (2 CE): a projectile that would hit you is unmade before it lands.

9th

Latverian Arsenal

Two armor modules. Variant + Technique Key 1 swaps the older one for the next. Flight (you fly), Force Shield (once every 3 s a blow is cut by a third), Tractor Beam and Micro-Missiles (Technique Key 1, 2 CE: the foe you look at is pulled to you, or four seeking missiles, 9 damage each), Mystic Sensor (foes within 24 blocks glow, and you see in the dark).

11th

Power Siphon

Each shot the Counterspell Scheme unmakes leaves a Siphon token (one at a time, for a minute): your next Gauntlet or Arcane Bolt adds 33 damage.

Domain

18th

Castle Doom

Every 3 s the castle runs one Scheme in turn: a gauntlet blast on every foe it chose (66 damage), binding them until the next pulse, or clearing every hostile shot from the air.

Technique feats

3rd

Mystic Armor

Below 11th, the Counterspell Scheme leaves a Siphon token too (1 CE more).

6th

DOOM Does Not Beg

Once a minute, a blow from a player sorcerer is filed as intelligence: you learn their technique, and for a minute their blows land 15% softer on you.

6th

Hidden Doombot

Once a long rest, a blow that would kill you while a Doombot is out fells the Doombot instead: you were standing where it stood, at a third of your health.
