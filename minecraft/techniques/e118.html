Carnage

A red arsenal of blades, shard volleys and tendrils driven by the Kill Chain, ending in the Carnage Storm.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Red Weapon0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Reaper Blade: a red scythe sweeps everything within 4.5 blocks in front of you, above and below too (9 damage, plus the Chain). Technique Key 1 switches the arsenal: Cleaving Axe (the foe in reach, 13 damage plus the Chain), Harpoon Spear (a foe at 6 blocks, or thrown 18, 11 damage plus the Chain), Maw Tendrils (a foe within 4.5 blocks is grabbed and held 3 s instead of hurt), Shard Volley (2 CE): three red shards fly 20 blocks where you look, 18 damage each. The chosen weapon and the shards strike together, for the shards' 2 CE.

6th

Autonomous Tendrils2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute: once every 3 s, a tendril lashes the nearest foe within 5 blocks, from any side, and drags it a little closer, building Chain.

18th

Carnage Unleashed8 CE · Cast (Z)

A minute: both configurations at once, tendrils every second out to 10 blocks, the Red Feast doubled, and going cold never drops the Chain.

1st

Red Rain1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Feat. Shards form in the air and rain down on up to two foes within 10 blocks: 9 damage each, building Chain.

Maximum

1st

Maximum: Carnage Storm10 CE · Maximum (X)

You blur through everything within 12 blocks: spikes erupt under each foe and throw it up, 133 damage each and held for 3 s (a strong one tears free and takes half). +2 Chain for each one held.

Passives

3rd

Kill Chain

Each hit builds 1 Chain (2 on an unhurt foe), up to 12. 3 s without a hit drops it to 0. At Chain 3+ one weapon blow in ten is a critical; at Chain 6+ you're 15% faster.

6th

Free Tendrils

At Chain 4+, once every 3 s a tendril strikes a foe within 5 blocks on its own.

9th

Red Feast

Once every 3 s, a Red Weapon hit heals you 6% of your health. Fire or a sonic blast on you spoils it for 3 s.

13th

Red Finale

At Chain 6+, your next Red Weapon hit spends the whole Chain: 11 damage more per link.

15th

Carnage Rules

A kill keeps the Chain warm for another 3 s: it carries to the next one.

20th

Red Event

Once every 3 s, a Red Weapon hit splashes 44 damage onto every other foe within 3 blocks of its target.

Technique feats

1st

Red Rain

The quick key rains symbiote shards on up to two foes within 10 blocks.

3rd

Wall of Teeth

Once every 3 s, a creature that hurts you within 6 blocks is snared in the symbiote's maw and held for 3 s (a strong one tears free).

1st

Blood Trail

The last creature your Red Weapon wounded is tracked for an hour: its distance and direction show every 2 s, within 1600 blocks. Wounding another moves the trail.
