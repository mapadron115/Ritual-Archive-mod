Living Lightning

The Word, the wisdom of Solomon, the strength of Hercules, the power of Zeus, the speed of Mercury and the courage of Achilles.

11thMaximum

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Champion's Fist0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Champion only: a lightning fist at the foe in front of you, 25 damage (44 damage as the Living Lightning).

11th

Power of Zeus2 CE · Cast (Z)

Champion only, once a transformation: lightning down a 30-block line, 66 damage. As the Living Lightning, Variant + this, once in the minute, is the discharge: 89 damage to every foe within 18 blocks.

1st

The Word2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

"SHAZAM!" Lightning strikes you and you're the Champion for ten minutes: far stronger, lightning can't hurt you, and your fists crackle. Speak it again (free) to turn back.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The Living Lightning10 CE · Maximum (X)

A minute: you are the bolt, not the boy. Technique Key 1 crosses up to 100 blocks where you look in a flash; your fists hit 44 damage; and once every 3 s, Variant + Technique Key 1 to call lightning where you look, 66 damage within 6 blocks.

Passives

3rd

Wisdom of Solomon

Once a long rest, nausea or darkness on you breaks at once.

6th

Strength of Hercules, Stamina of Atlas

Champion only: your fists hit harder, and becoming the Champion gives you temporary health (your level and proficiency).

11th

Speed of Mercury

Champion only: you fly, and you're much faster.

18th

Courage of Achilles

Champion only: you can't be frightened, and transforming frees allies within 9 blocks of fear. Once a long rest, a blow that would kill you leaves you at half a heart, still the Champion.

Technique feats

3rd

Lightning Rod

As the Champion, once every 3 s you may catch a shot that would hit you (1 CE, about a third of the time) and hurl it back as lightning: 22 damage to the nearest foe within 40 blocks.

3rd

Seven Thrones

Technique Key 1 calls one patron for 3 s (1 CE); Variant + Technique Key 1 picks which. Solomon clears nausea, blindness, darkness, slowness and weakness; Hercules adds a die to your fists; Atlas halves weapon and shot blows; Zeus adds a die to your next bolt; Achilles gives temporary health; Mercury doubles your speed.

3rd

Wisdom's Price

As the Champion, a hold on you breaks at once, but the Word spends itself and you turn back.

3rd

The Family Shares

Variant + Champion's Fist on an ally within 4 blocks (3 CE, Champion or not): for a minute their bare fists hit 16 damage and lightning can't hurt them.
