Twin Moon Armament

One hand draws moonlight, the other living flame: alternate them in rhythm, hang glintblades in the air, and raise twin moons over the field.

8arts
7passives
4technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Carian Phalanx0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every stored glintblade looses along your look, seeking a foe: 22 damage each.

3rd

Flame Spiral3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A spiral of flame around you (3 blocks): 44 damage (a nimble foe takes half), then you dart 3 blocks along your look.

11th

Carian Sovereignty6 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A great cut at the foe you look at (5 blocks): 89 damage, then a 6-block cone of 44 damage.

3rd

Moonlight Wave2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A wave of cold moonlight from the Moon Blade, 18 blocks along your look: 33 cold damage to every foe it crosses, and they freeze. 1 CE less in the Sorcerous stance.

6th

Carian Stance2 CE · Cast (Z)

For 3 s, take a stance; casting again steps to the other. Sorcerous: the Moon Blade reaches 1.5 blocks farther and Moonlight Wave costs less. Infernal: Flame blows add 11 damage and carry you through the foe you hit.

11th

Twin Aspect Guard3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For 3 s, the first blow on you is cut by 66 damage: the Moon turns a technique's blow, the Flame a weapon's.

1st

Manifest Twin Blades1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute your weapon blows alternate Moon (the foe freezes) and Flame (it burns), each 11 damage harder. A blow within a second of the last adds as much again and stores a Carian glintblade (up to 6).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Twin Moons10 CE · Maximum (X)

You rise and raise two moons: three shockwaves run out along the ground (5 blocks): 44 damage, 44 damage, then 66 damage. Foes caught are knocked down, and those caught twice are hurled up. Third Moon: with Variant, 5 CE more brings a fourth shockwave (66 damage), and the four pass each other's guard.

Passives

1st

Knightly Footwork

While your blades are out, you have 4 more armor.

6th

Perfect Alternation

Once every 3 s (twice from 20th), a quick follow-up blow also looses one stored glintblade at its foe, for nothing.

11th

Dancing Knight

Two quick follow-ups within 3 s quicken you, and the next hold on you slips half the time.

11th

Rellana's Flame

A Flame blow that ends a quick follow-up leaves a burst that goes off a moment later: 22 fire damage within 2 blocks of where it struck.

18th

Moonfire Sovereignty

Your blades stay out until you sheathe them (cast again). With Variant, once every 3 s a blow carries both the Moon and the Flame, and both their dice (2 CE).

20th

Moon and Fire, Always Together

A quick follow-up adds 22 damage more.

20th

Sovereign Knight

Once every 3 s, a blade blow strikes a second time, as hard, a moment later.

Technique feats

3rd

Carian Dueling Form

Technique Key 1 (1 CE) marks the foe you look at (8 blocks) as your duelist for 10 s: your blade blows on it add 11 damage.

6th

Phalanx Reserve

Your glintblades screen you: once every 3 s, a shot about to hit you may be caught by one of them (half the time), spending it.

3rd

Messmer Ember

Flame Spiral's dart sets alight each foe you pass, for 11 fire damage.

11th

Moon Princess's Guard

Twin Aspect Guard also turns the first blow on an ally within 3 blocks of you.
