Scarlet Bloom

An undefeated blade that drinks from every clean cut, and a scarlet rot that blooms in whatever it touches.

7arts
8passives
4technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

3rd

Waterfowl Dance3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You leap, hang in the air, then dash at the foe you look at (12 blocks) three times, cutting everything near you: three flurries of 22 damage, each drinking life and perhaps planting rot. Each dash takes its line as it starts, so a foe can get clear of it.

6th

Scarlet Aeonia6 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You leap high and come down where you look (12 blocks), and a scarlet flower blooms: 53 damage within 4 blocks, and two Rot Marks on everything in it. For 3 s the flower stays, and a foe who comes into it takes a Rot Mark. The plants around it wither.

11th

Scarlet Wings4 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute you fly on scarlet wings, and your weapon blows bite 11 damage harder.

3rd

Scarlet Thrust1 CE · Cast (Z)

Your next weapon blow thrusts deep: 11 damage more, and a Rot Mark.

3rd

Aeonian Phantom2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For 3 s, the first blow about to land on you misses half the time, and when it does you step 3 blocks aside.

18th

Scarlet Phantoms6 CE · Cast (Z)

Up to 6 phantoms of your blade cut the foes within 27 blocks, shared among them: 22 damage and a Rot Mark each. They drink no life.

1st

Kick of the Severed0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Right after a weapon blow: the foe is kicked two blocks back (a strong foe holds). With 1 CE to spare, it's knocked down 1.5 s as well.

Passives

1st

Blade Lifesteal

Each weapon blow heals you 8% of your health, up to 6 times every 3 s. It drinks even through a shield.

1st

Rotting Edge

Once every 3 s a weapon blow plants a Rot Mark, and a critical blow always plants one more (up to 5; they last a minute). Every 3 s, each mark rots its foe for 7 damage.

1st

Flowing Sword

While you fight in melee you move faster.

6th

Unalloyed Pride

Your own rot never touches you, and fear or charm slides off you half the time.

18th

Goddess Form

Once a long rest, a blow that would fell you blooms instead: you rise at half your health, your Scarlet Wings spread for nothing, and a Scarlet Aeonia opens where you stand.

20th

True Goddess

After you've bloomed three times (0 so far), poison and withering can't take hold of you.

20th

Aeonia Without Collapse

With Variant, Scarlet Aeonia lands you at the flower's edge, not its heart.

20th

Rot Is Rebirth

Once every 3 s, when your rot hurts a foe you gain 6 temporary health.
