Glistening oil remakes your body piece by piece while infection spreads through everything you touch. All will be one.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Oil Lash1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A whip of glistening oil at the foe you look at (9 blocks): 18 damage, and it's infected. With the Cannon limb, a 27 damage shot twice as far.

6th

Proliferate2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every infection within 9 blocks spreads a step (the foe you look at is infected even if it wasn't), and +1 Oil.

15th

Corrupted Conversion5 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at turns on its own kind for 3 s: surely at 3 Infection, otherwise a strong will resists.

1st

Self-Modify2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

You graft yourself a little further: +1 Oil. An iron ingot from your pack is grafted in too: +2 Oil instead (it's used up). From 13th, Variant + Self-Modify picks the Praetor Evolution instead.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: March of the Machine10 CE · Maximum (X)

At Oil 10: for a minute every blow infects for free, the infections near you spread each 3 s, your plating thickens, and you grow large.

Passives

1st

Oil

Oil builds as you're hurt (once every 3 s). At 3 your plating hardens (4 armor); at 6 your blows bite 7 damage deeper.

1st

Infection

Once every 3 s a blow infects its target (1 CE, up to 3 for a minute): it rots for 7 damage per Infection each 3 s and hits 8% softer per Infection. A carrier that dies bursts, infecting the foes within 3 blocks.

3rd

Adapt

At Oil 3, Technique Key 1 picks an adaptation: Plating (2 more armor), Climber (you climb walls), Sensors (night vision, and foes within 12 blocks shown to you), Edge (7 damage on a blow once a 3 s), or Resistance (one kind of harm lands at 80% until your next long rest).

9th

Compleat Limb

At Oil 5, Variant + Technique Key 1 picks a limb: Blade (9 damage on your blows), Cannon (Oil Lash becomes a shot), Shield (once a 3 s a blow lands at 80%), or Injector (your blows slow a foe a moment).

11th

Perfected Form

Resistance is always worn beside your adaptation.

13th

Praetor Evolution

At Oil 7, Variant + Self-Modify picks an evolution: Wings (flight), Regeneration, Reach (+1 block), Ward (technique blows on you land at 85%), or Two Limbs.

20th

All Will Be One

Three adaptations and the evolution at once. Once a long rest, a killing blow (1 CE) makes you a cocoon instead: 1 health and held for 3 s, then you reform at half health.

Domain

18th

New Phyrexia

The clinic opens: each pulse every infection in it spreads a step for free, then every chosen foe takes 55 damage per Infection (111 damage at most; a hardy one halves it).

Technique feats

3rd

Gitaxian Probe

Variant + Oil Lash probes instead (1 CE): how hurt the foe is, its armor, what it shrugs off; Resistance may then answer its kind of harm.

3rd

Steel Thane

At 3 Oil or more, once every 3 s a creature's blow locks your plating rigid: it lands at half, but you're nearly rooted for a moment.

6th

Toxic

Past Oil 5 you exude a miasma: each 3 s, foes right beside you gain an Infection (a hardy one resists).

9th

Viral Drake

Proliferate leaps: each infected foe passes the oil to one uninfected foe within 3 blocks (a hardy one resists).
