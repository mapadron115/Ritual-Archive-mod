Skill Hunter

Bandit's Secret: witness a creature's attack, then press the bloody palm print to its flesh within the hour; the attack is yours, and it can't use it while your page holds it.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Read the Open Page2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Fire the attack on the open page along your look: the real projectile, now yours, 33 damage more on a hit. A stolen art reads as that art, cast for its own cost on top and with its own cooldown. With Two Pages Open, the bookmarked page fires too (2 CE more).

1st

Bandit's Secret: The Palm0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Press the palm print to a creature within reach (3.0 blocks) that you watched attack within the hour: its attack becomes a page, and it can't use it again. On a sorcerer you watched cast one of their arts (never a Maximum), that art is the page, and they can't cast it while you hold it.

3rd

Bookmark0 CE · Cast (Z)

Mark the open page.

11th

Due Diligence0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Study whoever you look at: a sorcerer's arts, named; a creature's attack, if you've seen it.

1st

Turn the Page0 CE · Quick (G)

Open the next page of the book. With Variant, the Catalogue: every page, and whether each stolen art's owner is about.

Maximum

1st

Imprimatur10 CE · Maximum (X)

A minute: every attack you see a creature make is inscribed at once, no palm needed.

Technique feats

6th

The Placed Ribbon

When a blow drops you below a quarter of your health, the bookmarked page reads itself along your look (once a minute).

1st

Palimpsest

Variant + Bookmark erases the open page: a stolen art goes back to its owner, and the impression tells you their level and technique.

6th

Footnote

Reading a stolen art tells its owner it was just used, and how far away and which way.

1st

Sun and Moon

Technique Key 1 (2 CE) stamps a sun, then a moon, on the creature you look at (12 blocks); one of each at a time. When the two marked creatures touch, they detonate: 44 fire damage to every foe within 6 blocks.
