Long Step Procession

Turn distance into a resource: uncanny strides make Steps that dodge blows, bridge gaps, and leave glowing processions behind.

4arts
3passives
4technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Stilt Kick1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A kick from far too high (3 blocks, 6 holding a Step): 18 damage and a shove. Spend a Step to knock the foe down 2 s too (a strong foe keeps its feet). Once every 3 s, a bare-handed blow holding a Step does it for 1 CE: a shove of 6 blocks and, unless it holds, the foe is knocked flat.

3rd

Procession Path1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

2 Steps, after walking 12 blocks this 3 s: the ground you crossed glows for 10 s. Allies on it are quick; foes on it are slowed, take 14 damage the first time they cross, and are knocked flat unless they hold.

11th

Echo Swap0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

2 Steps: trade places with the Echo nearest your look, up to 36 blocks away.

1st

Long Stride0 CE · 0.3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

1 Step: one impossible stride, 6 blocks along your look, through creatures and over a low wall.

Passives

1st

Impossible Stride

Every 6 blocks walked earn a Step (up to 6, two every 3 s); two rounds standing still and they fade. Holding a Step, you step over full blocks and your reach is 6 blocks.

6th

Between the Frames

Once every 3 s, a blow on you can be stepped out of (2 Steps, 1 CE): you're 6 blocks to the side, and the blow lands on a frame you've left.

11th

Processional Echoes

Walk 18 blocks in a 3 s and up to 6 Echoes stand where you were, for a 3 s. While one stands, a blow from more than 6 blocks away misses 1 time in 4, and once every 3 s a miss swaps you with an Echo for free.

Domain

18th

Footage That Should Not Exist

Every chosen foe leaves a frame where it stood, and at each pulse is snapped back to its last frame, unable to react for 1.5 s. Only where it stands rewinds: never its health.
