Judgement Cut

An unbroken mind with a katana: Concentration builds with every clean cut, until space itself is severed.

8arts
3passives
4technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

6th

Judgement Cut3 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Space is cut where you look (36 blocks): 44 damage within 6 blocks (a nimble foe may halve it), and 11 damage more on your marked foe. A rift opens there, a dark sphere full of cuts, and shatters like glass; leaves, plants and glass in it fall apart.

9th

Mirage Blades2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Summon 6 Mirage Blades, kept until a long rest. Each weapon blow spends one for 9 damage more; Technique Key 1 fires one, 16 damage, up to 36 blocks.

18th

Sin Devil Trigger8 CE · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest, for a minute: Devil Trigger's gifts and more. Blows land far softer on you, your blade bites 18 damage harder, a Trick each 3 s is free, and you gain Concentration each 3 s. At 10 Concentration it costs nothing.

11th

Doppelganger4 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute a spectral double repeats your weapon blows and Judgement Cuts for half.

13th

Devil Trigger5 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute: flight, speed, blows land softer on you, you heal every 3 s, and your blade bites 9 damage harder.

15th

Judgement Cut End8 CE · Cast (Z)

At 10 Concentration: everything within 24 blocks is cut, 111 damage (a nimble foe may halve it). Concentration falls to 5.

3rd

Trick1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Vanish and reappear 12 blocks where you look (18 at 5+ Concentration), or beside your marked foe.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Dimensional Judgement10 CE · Maximum (X)

A cut aimed at distance itself: a 108-block line where you look, 133 damage (a nimble foe may halve it).

Passives

1st

Concentration

A weapon blow landed while unhurt for a second raises Concentration (up to 10); being hit drops it by 2. Each point makes your blows 2% harder. The last foe you struck is marked for 10 s.

1st

Focused Draw

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow draws 1 CE for 9 damage more.

20th

Motivated

Concentration holds 12; the first blow each 3 s doesn't shake it, and at 10 or more the first Judgement Cut each 3 s costs nothing.

Technique feats

1st

Rapid Slash

Your first weapon blow within 2 s of a Trick also cleaves a second foe within 3 blocks for half.

3rd

Perfect Sheathe

A blow that drops a foe spends 2 Concentration on one perfect sheathe: foes within 3 blocks freeze for 3 s.

9th

Summoned Sword Master

Variant + Technique Key 1 spends 2 Mirage Blades on a spinning guard: for 3 s shots land on you at half.

11th

Dark Slayer

At full Concentration, once every 10 s a blow about to land meets only the air you left (1 CE): you Trick away.
