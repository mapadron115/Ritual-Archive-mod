Arts

1st

Piercing Schrift1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A seal-shot along your look that passes through walls and every foe in its line (27 blocks): 25 damage, a sure critical on a foe standing still. In Multi-Shot (Technique Key 1), one seal at each of up to 5 foes near where you look.

11th

Intangibility6 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute with your body phased: blows pass mostly through you (three-quarters less).

6th

Critical Mark3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at (9 blocks): your next hit on it is a sure critical.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Sacrosanct10 CE · Maximum (X)

The phased body at its height: a minute of Intangibility, and through it every shot of yours is a sure critical.
