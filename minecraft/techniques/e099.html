Vector Authority

You don't create force: you rewrite direction and magnitude. Nothing touches you unanswered, once you've calculated it. Do not strike him; you're only lending him your weapon.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Vector Touch0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Toggle. Your weapon blows add 13 damage and shove the foe away, or drag it to you with Variant held (1 CE, at most once a second).

3rd

Vector Rewrite2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute: gravity is a suggestion. You're faster, you leap far higher, you walk up any wall you walk into, and falls don't hurt. Borrowed Vector (feat): with Variant, the ally you look at within 18 blocks walks on it instead.

6th

Atmospheric Weapon3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 36-block line of compressed air where you look: 53 damage, and everything on it is blown along. Bioelectric Rewrite (6th): with Variant, a foe within 3 blocks has to resist or takes 40 damage and can't counter, dodge or parry for 3 s.

11th

Vector Transformation: Repel5 CE · 1.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The point you look at (54 blocks) violently repels: 79 damage to everything within 12 blocks, flung away from it, up and down too. Items fly with them.

11th

Vector Transformation: Attract5 CE · 1.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The same, but the point violently attracts.

13th

Black Wings6 CE · Cast (Z)

A minute (any number of times): you fly, one reflection each 3 s is free, and Vector Touch adds three dice instead of one. Platinum Wings (20th): with Variant, for 4 CE more, every Vector Die of yours deals 2 more while they last.

15th

Plasma Geometry7 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A sphere of plasma at the point you look at (72 blocks): 106 damage within 5 blocks, and blinded. It needs air: nothing forms under water.

18th

One-Way World: PUSH / PULL / REVERSE0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

What your Domain declares each pulse.

1st

Vector Filter1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Raise (or lower) the Filter. A shot you've calculated is turned around at the border of your body and flies home as yours (or where you look, with Variant held), and a blow is returned to the one who threw it (1 CE each, any number).

Maximum

1st

Maximum: Celestial Vector Collapse10 CE · Maximum (X)

The point you look at (108 blocks): everything within 13 blocks is dragged to it, 159 damage, then blasted straight up. Hostile shots in it are torn apart, and the ground at its heart gives way.

Passives

1st

Calculation

Something you haven't seen needs calculating: about 85% of the time it's solved and known until a long rest; otherwise the Filter drops. A reflected blow is never reflected again, and a Domain's sure-hit passes.

9th

Advanced Filter

A long rest keeps your 6 newest calculations, and once every 3 s a miscalculation doesn't drop the Filter.

Domain

18th

One-Way World

Each pulse the world declares what you chose: PUSH or PULL (106 damage, flung away from or toward the centre) or REVERSE (106 damage, slammed into the ground and held).

Technique feats

3rd

Passive Filter

While the Filter is up, allies within 9 blocks are sheltered: blows land softer on them, and once every 3 s the Filter turns a shot heading for one of them (it counts as one of your reflections).

9th

Borrowed Vector

Variant + Vector Rewrite lends the Rewrite to the ally you look at within 18 blocks for the same minute: the speed, the leap, no fall damage, and walking up walls.

6th

Kihara Counterstudy

Returned blows come back at three times their force instead of twice.

11th

White Wings

When Black Wings end, they shed in one vector discharge (4 CE): 53 damage to everything within 12 blocks, flung away.
