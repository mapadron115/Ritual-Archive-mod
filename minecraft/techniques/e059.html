Deep Purple

An army, exhaled: smoke soldiers that fight, screen and grapple while the general hides somewhere in the fog.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Smoke, Obey2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Exhale 6 smoke soldiers where you look (12 blocks). Each strikes for 14 damage a second; one hit dissipates it, and it disperses after a minute.

1st

Dismiss the Ranks0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Let every soldier drift away.

20th

The Fog Itself2 CE · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest, for a minute: you are smoke. Nothing can touch you, and you fight only through your soldiers.

18th

The General Vanishes3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Dissolve and re-form beside the soldier nearest where you look (24 blocks).

Maximum

1st

Maximum: The Smoking Parade5 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute the whole army is out: 60 soldiers, and every 3 s each foe hemmed in by three of them takes 53 damage. You're exhausted after.

Passives

1st

Soldiers

Up to 30 soldiers stand at once (five a level from 6th, at most 30).

6th

Screen

Once every 3 s, 1 CE: a soldier beside you steps in and takes the blow.

11th

Hands of Smoke

Your soldiers grapple: their blows hold foes 1.5 s (a strong foe less), and once every 3 s a foe held by two or more is dragged 6 blocks toward you (never a boss).

Domain

18th

The Barracks of Fog

Nowhere to Stand: soldiers condense out of the fog round every chosen foe for 36 damage, and a soldier forms beside it. Every foe inside can't see while it stays inside, and your ranks may swell to 60 while the Barracks stands.

Technique feats

1st

Secondhand Lungs

A soldier fitted as a breathing mask: while any soldier stands, you never run out of air, and poison doesn't take.

3rd

False General

One soldier wears your face and gear, in plain sight: while any stand, creatures more than 6 blocks away lose track of you and go for it instead.

3rd

Dead Drop

Variant + Technique Key 1 (1 CE): a soldier takes the stack in your hand to the player you look at (within 64 blocks), drifting there as smoke, and puts it in their pack (at their feet if it's full).

6th

Fog of War

Technique Key 1 (2 CE): your soldiers breathe out a 6-block cloud of heavy smoke where you look (12 blocks) for a minute; every foe inside is blinded.

11th

The Last Soldier

Once a long rest, when you'd fall, you dissolve instead and re-form beside your farthest soldier (within 60 blocks) at 1 health.
