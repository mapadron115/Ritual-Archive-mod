Gravity Sovereignty

Violet marks that make everything answer to your gravity (pull, push, orbit) until the stars themselves fall on command.

9arts
6passives
4technique feats
11thMaximum
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Vector Command1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every foe within 4 blocks of where you look is hurled: toward you (Pull), or along your aim in any direction (Push: look down to drive them into the ground, up to fling them skyward), and takes 18 damage (a strong foe may hold). Marked foes answer from anywhere in range, can't hold, and take 27 damage. Technique Key 1 switches Pull and Push.

3rd

Cragblade2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute, a weapon blow on a marked foe also pushes it 3 blocks along your look (Push) or pulls it 3 blocks to you (Pull).

6th

Meteor Sling4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Up to 6 marked things in orbit fly at the foes where you look: 27 damage each.

6th

Weightless Step0 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A short, weightless leap along your look, and you drift down after it. Once every 3 s, free.

18th

Consort's Meteor Rain8 CE · 20 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You vanish upward, and 3 s later you fall on the point you looked at (up to 90 blocks): every foe within 9 blocks takes 71 damage and is thrown. With Variant, Heaven-Shattering Reentry: 10 CE more, 18 blocks and 107 damage, and the landing costs you 36 damage.

3rd

Starcaller Cry3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 4-block sphere where you look (12 blocks) collapses: every foe in it is dragged to its heart and takes 27 damage (a strong foe is dragged less).

6th

Collapsing Stars2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

6 violet gravity orbs seek foes (18 blocks): 18 damage each, and each drags its target toward you.

1st

Gravity Mark1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at (30 blocks) wears a violet mark for a minute (a strong foe may resist): it's slowed, and it answers your Vector Command from anywhere in range. Looking at no foe, it marks a loose item, a falling block or a shot in flight instead (up to 6). Greatsword Conductor: once every 3 s a weapon blow marks its target for 1 CE.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Festival of Falling Stars10 CE · Maximum (X)

A minute: three meteors form in orbit over you every 3 s (up to nine). Variant + Technique Key 1 hurls them all where you look: 36 damage each in a 2-block burst (a nimble foe halves it), and each one craters the ground.

Passives

1st

Gravity Collision

A foe you hurl that strikes a wall or another creature stops dead and is knocked down a moment.

6th

Conqueror's Orbit

Marked things circle you. Once every 3 s (2 CE) one is thrown into the path of a shot coming at you, and stops it.

11th

Absolute Vector

Vector Command throws farther.

11th

Orbital Arsenal

Each weapon blow or arrow that lands also launches a thing from your orbit at its target: 18 damage.

18th

Perfect Gravity

You may fly: you stand and run on open air.

20th

General of the Stars

Mass Bows to the General: one Vector Command each 3 s is free. Army of One: below half health, nothing resists your gravity. Heaven-Shattering Reentry: see Consort's Meteor Rain.

Domain

18th

Wailing Firmament

Heaven Falls: every 3 s each foe it chose takes 53 damage, is dragged toward the centre, and hangs in the air until the next.

Technique feats

1st

Redmane Greatbow

You mark the ammunition as you fire it: once every 3 s, an arrow that hits a marked foe drags it toward you.

3rd

Starcaller's Mark

What the cry cannot crush, it claims: a foe that holds against Starcaller Cry is marked for a minute.

6th

Lion's Gravity

Anchored to your own law: nothing knocks you back, and once every 3 s the foe that struck you is pulled to you.

11th

Grand Conjunction

Variant + Technique Key 1 merges the meteors in orbit into one greater star: 36 damage per meteor in a 3-block burst, and a far greater crater.
