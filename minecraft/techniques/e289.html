Clone

Split yourself into an army: clones that fight, shield, and trade places with you, until Divide and Conquer.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

One Man Army3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A clone of you for 10 minutes, 25 damage a blow. It takes blows for you until it has soaked all of your health, then breaks (up to 6 at once).

11th

Body Swap1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

You and your farthest clone trade places.

Maximum: Four is a Party10 CE · Maximum (X)

Your whole roster splits off at once, fresh, and each clone sets on the nearest foe with a 36 damage opening strike.

Technique feats

1st

Long Leash

Your clones step out where you look (within 9 blocks), not just at your side.

3rd

Shell Game

Technique Key 1 trades places with the clone nearest where you look (within 18 blocks).

6th

Meant to be Expendable

Once every 10 s, when a blow is about to land, a clone splits off mid-blow (4 CE): it shoves you aside and takes it.

Shared Burden

Every few seconds while a clone stands, the poisons, withering, burns and slowing on you pass to it instead.
