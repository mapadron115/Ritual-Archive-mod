Hundred-Demon Revel

Switch visible Oni Masks, red for force, blue for endurance, black for terror, and let the Revel of takedowns power physical dominance.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Oni's Club1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (3 blocks): 22 damage, and 11 damage more for every Revel you spend (all of it).

3rd

Dread Laugh1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

With the feat, behind the Black Mask: the foe you look at (6 blocks) reels away from your laugh and stands cowed for 3 s (a strong will may hold).

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Hundred-Demon Night Parade10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you wear all three masks at once: force, endurance and terror together.

Passives

1st

Masks (Technique Key 1)

Red: your blows 25% harder. Blue: blows on you 25% softer, and you mend 6% of your health every 3 s. Black: foes within 4 blocks hit 25% softer. Swap once every 3 s, or anytime for 1 Revel.

1st

Revel

Each kill gives a Revel (up to 5).

Technique feats

1st

Red Horn

Behind the Red Mask, once every 3 s a blow landed at a full run bowls the foe over (a strong one may keep its feet).

3rd

Dread Laugh

The quick key laughs behind the Black Mask (1 CE).
