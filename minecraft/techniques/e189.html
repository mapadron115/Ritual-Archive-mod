Hundred-Demon Revel

Switch visible Oni Masks, red for force, blue for endurance, black for terror, and let the Revel of takedowns power physical dominance.

noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Oni's Club1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (3 blocks): 22 damage, and 11 damage more for every Revel you spend (all of it).

11th

Night Parade3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute three oni silhouettes stand where you look (18 blocks). Once every 3 s the Oni's Club strikes a foe within 3 blocks of one, wherever you stand. A foe that walks into one scatters it; a kill, or a Mask swapped for a Revel, calls them all back.

3rd

Dread Laugh1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

With the feat, behind the Black Mask: the foe you look at (18 blocks) reels away from your laugh and stands cowed for 3 s (a strong will may hold).

Maximum

18th

Maximum: O-Oni10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you are the great oni: half again your size, 3 blocks more reach, a Revel every 3 s, and all three masks at once. The Mask you wore as you cast it rules the others, its gift doubled: Red blows 50% harder, Blue blows on you 40% softer, Black foes hitting 50% softer.

Passives

1st

Masks (Technique Key 1)

Red: your blows 25% harder. Blue: blows on you 25% softer, and you mend 6% of your health every 3 s. Black: foes within 4 blocks hit 25% softer. Swap once every 3 s, or anytime for 1 Revel.

1st

Revel

Each kill gives a Revel (up to 6).

3rd

Kanabo Principle

Whatever you hold strikes like an iron club: your critical weapon blows hurl the foe 6 blocks.

Technique feats

1st

Red Horn

Behind the Red Mask, once every 3 s a blow landed at a full run bowls the foe over (a strong one may keep its feet).

3rd

Dread Laugh

The quick key laughs behind the Black Mask (1 CE).

3rd

Iron Skin

Behind the Blue Mask, once every 3 s: a foe's blow on an ally within 6 blocks lands on you instead, softened by the Mask.

11th

Parade Master

Variant + Technique Key 1 steps into the nearest silhouette (it's spent): for 3 s you also wear the Mask you'd swap to next.
