EMETH, the Truth

Clay given the word: it grows as the fight goes on, swallows blows into itself, and when it finally speaks, nothing false stands before it.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Clay Fists1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A slam on the foe you look at (6 blocks, 3 more for each step you've grown): 15 damage, and 5 damage more a step. It knocks the foe down and back and cracks the ground under it (a strong foe holds; a great foe isn't moved).

11th

Shem of Mending4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The clay knits closed: regain 31% of your health. Twice a long rest.

11th

Unstoppable Guardian5 CE · Quick (G)

For a minute nothing moves you, and you trample the foes you walk through: your slam's damage, once every 3 s, 2 times at most on each foe.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Golem Speaks10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute the clay embodies Truth: your slams deal double dice, every creature within 18 blocks is shown to you and can't stay unseen, and their blows on you land 15% softer. Once a long rest.

Passives

1st

The Word on the Brow

Nothing frightens or muddles you: weakness, nausea and darkness slide off.

3rd

Growing Truth

Every 3 s you stay in a fight you grow, up to two steps: a quarter larger, a tenth more health and 3 blocks more reach each. It settles back between fights.

6th

Swallow the Blow

Once every 3 s (1 CE) a blow is swallowed into the clay: halved.
