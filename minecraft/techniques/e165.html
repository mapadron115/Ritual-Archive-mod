Leeds Infernal

Build Pursuit by running down fleeing prey, then spend it on dive-bombs, horn launches and impossible aerial strikes.

5arts
2passives
3technique feats
18thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Dive Bomb1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You hurl yourself at the foe you look at (12 blocks): 18 damage, and 9 damage more for every Pursuit you spend (all of it).

3rd

Marked Quarry1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Mark the foe you look at (36 blocks) for a minute: each 3 s it pulls 6 blocks or more farther from you, you gain 2 Pursuit. Free on a foe you struck in the last 3 s.

3rd

Devil's Cry2 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The shriek of the Pine Barrens: a 18-block cone where you look (36 as the Devil). What can't steel itself is frightened for 6 s.

1st

Horn Toss1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

1 Pursuit: your next blow launches its target into the air (a strong foe holds).

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Leeds Devil Takes Flight10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you grow huge and winged: swift flight, Pursuit up to 12, and two more every 3 s.

Passives

1st

Pine Barrens Predator

You glide (falls are slow unless you sneak). Pursuit (up to 12) builds as you run, fall and strike on the move; standing still for 3 s lets it cool away.

6th

True Flight

You fly. Dive Rake: a Dive Bomb knocks the foe flat and frightens it (a strong foe may hold).

Technique feats

1st

Roofline Hunter

Walls become roads: you cling to and run up them.

3rd

Hellish Whinny

A foe Devil's Cry frightens betrays itself: it glows for a minute, through walls.

3rd

Broken-Bridle Pursuit

Held, slowed or snared, the hunger only sharpens: gain 2 Pursuit when it takes hold.
