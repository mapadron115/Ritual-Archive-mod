Kinetic Transmission

A ten-gear gearbox of stored momentum: gain Gears by moving and striking, then spend only what each Shift needs, braking, throwing, and finally redlining.

5arts
6passives
4technique feats
11thMaximum
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Engage Transmission1 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute your body is a gearbox, starting at Gear 6: every 3 s, a Gear for moving 3 blocks (any way: falling and climbing count) and a Gear for landing a blow (three for both), up to 10.

6th

Suspended Interval1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Feat. Up to 6 shots within 6 blocks stop in the air; cast again to let them go along your look, as yours. With no shot near, the marked foe you look at is pinned 1.5 s.

18th

Archive: ZERO / TERMINAL GEAR0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Which Gear the Zugzwang Engine forces on its pulse.

1st

Impact Shift0 CE · Quick (G)

Each tap sets one more Gear for your next blow (past what you hold, it resets). The blow spends those Gears and 1 CE: 13 damage per Gear, and the foe is pushed 3 blocks per Gear (a strong foe holds). Use it as often as you like. Variant + this key readies Burn Out (with Reverse Cursed Technique).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Redline10 CE · Maximum (X)

At Gear 10: your Gear is held at 10 for 6 rounds (18 s). Triple speed, you run up walls, nothing shoves you, you swing faster, and one Velocity Override every 3 s is free. When it ends you pay: 26 damage every 3 s for 3 rounds, through anything (never below half a heart), then Burnout. In the Zugzwang Engine it's free and costs nothing after.

Passives

1st

Acceleration Shift

Technique Key 1, engaged: two Gears for a burst of speed along your look, and falls don't hurt you for 3 s.

3rd

Brake Shift

A blow that lands on you while engaged spends a Gear to brake it: 20 damage less, and nothing knocks you back while you hold Gears.

6th

Velocity Override

Your blows mark foes for a minute (up to 6). Variant + Technique Key 1 at a marked foe within 36 blocks spends 3 CE and the Gears you've set: it's thrown 6 blocks a Gear along your look, and a wall it hits deals 26 damage a Gear. Aim at its legs to Down-Shift instead: it's slowed for 3 s, and held there at 4 Gears or more. Once every 3 s.

11th

Perfect Transmission

Engaged, Strength and Dexterity +2 (and their caps +2), and once every 3 s an Impact Shift gives you a Gear back.

18th

Burn Out

With Reverse Cursed Technique: Variant + the quick key (4 CE and every Gear) readies it for a minute. The next art a marked player casts within 36 blocks fails with its CE spent, their technique is out for 3 s, and they take 13 damage per Gear. A strong will holds: the art goes off and they take half. Readied at Gear 10, it stops a Domain as it opens (Dead Ignition), and they suffer its Burnout.

18th

Drive Die d12

Your Drive Die is a d12.

Domain

18th

Zugzwang Engine

Open, centred where you open it. Inside, Redline is free and has no recoil, every foe counts as marked, and Velocity Override costs no CE. Each pulse forces a Gear: Zero Gear, 132 damage and held still 1.5 s (great foes only slowed); or Terminal Gear, 132 damage and thrown 36 blocks along your look, then 53 damage each time it swings or moves 3 blocks before the next pulse.

Technique feats

3rd

Flywheel Counter

Engaged, once every 3 s a blow on you is spun into the flywheel (2 CE): it lands 86 damage softer and you gain 6 Gears.

11th

Vector Skip

Acceleration Shift skips instead (1 CE more): you blink 8 blocks along your look, and your next blow is an Impact Shift of every Gear set.

11th

Relativistic Break

The Maximum again during Redline (10 CE): you cross 90 blocks along your look in an instant, and every foe on the line takes 55 damage. Then Redline ends, with its recoil.

6th

Suspended Interval

Gives you the Suspended Interval art.
