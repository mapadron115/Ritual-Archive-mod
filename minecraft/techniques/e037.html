Kinetic Transmission

A ten-gear gearbox of stored momentum: gain Gears by moving and striking, then spend them braking, accelerating, and finally redlining.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Engage Transmission1 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute your body is a gearbox, starting at Gear 6: every 3 s, a Gear for moving 3 blocks (any way: falling and climbing count) and a Gear for landing a blow (three for both), up to 10.

1st

Impact Shift0 CE · Quick (G)

Your next blow dumps every Gear into it: 11 damage per Gear.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Redline8 CE · Maximum (X)

At Gear 10, all ten Gears at once: an impact of 132 damage within 3 blocks that craters the ground and throws everything it reaches far away (a strong foe holds its ground and takes half).

Passives

1st

Acceleration Shift

Technique Key 1, engaged: two Gears for a burst of speed along your look, and falls don't hurt you for 3 s.

6th

Brake Shift

A blow that lands on you while engaged spends a Gear to brake it: 18 damage less, and nothing knocks you back while you hold Gears.

11th

Perfect Transmission

Engaged, you're stronger and faster: Strength and Dexterity +2 (and their caps +2), blows 10% harder, 15% faster.

Domain

18th

Open Circuit

Compulsory Gear: every chosen foe is forced to Zero Gear, 132 damage and held still for 1.5 s (great foes are only slowed).
