Conduit Mimic

Touch a power and it's yours (smoke, neon, video, concrete), each with its own shot, movement and finisher, until they erupt together.

11thMaximum

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Mode Shot1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your Mode's shot (18 blocks). Smoke: 18 damage and it burns. Neon: 18 damage, often a critical. Video: 18 damage and it loses track of you. Concrete: 22 damage and a shove.

15th

Karmic Finisher6 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once per Mode per long rest, where you look. Smoke, Orbital Drop: you rocket up and slam down, 89 damage and knocked down. Neon, Overcharge: 89 damage and blinded. Video, Angelic Storm: 89 damage and confused. Concrete, Fortress: 89 damage and walled in with conjured concrete.

1st

Mode Movement1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Smoke: dash 6 blocks through foes as smoke. Neon: run as light for 3 s. Video: fly on wings for 3 s. Concrete: harden, so blows land far softer for 3 s.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Conduit Symphony10 CE · Maximum (X)

All four erupt together: everything within 8 blocks takes 107 damage and is blinded or bound (a nimble foe halves it and keeps its feet).

Passives

1st

Sources

Technique Key 1 switches to the next Mode whose source is within 6 blocks. Smoke: fire, lava, a lit furnace or campfire. Neon: glowstone, sea lanterns, lanterns, or bright light. Video: the open night sky. Concrete: standing on concrete or stone.

13th

Source Cache

Each Mode stores one charge: switch to it anywhere once, refilled when you touch its source.

Technique feats

1st

Smoke & Mirrors

Smoke Dash leaves a thick cloud where you stood: foes within 2 blocks are blinded for 3 s.

3rd

Neon Runner

Running as neon, you pass through foes as light: each one you run through takes 18 damage, once per run.

6th

Digital Angel

Taking Video flight with 2 CE to spare hard-lights the wings into blades: everything within 3 blocks takes 27 damage.

11th

Concrete Skin

Once every 3 s, a blow that lands is petrified shut (1 CE): it lands softer by 11 damage plus your level.
