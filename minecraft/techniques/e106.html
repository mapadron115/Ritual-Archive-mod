Overhaul

Touch gives complete authority over matter's arrangement, not its soul: execution, healing, terrain, and horrifying fusion. He doesn't heal or harm. He revises.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Disassemble1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Touch (4 blocks): 26 damage to the foe you look at (a golem or anything conjured takes 32 damage), or the block you look at comes apart (it drops, like a mined block).

1st

Reconstruction1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 AP: heal 20% of your health (about). With Variant, the ally you look at within 4 blocks instead.

3rd

Ground Spikes2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The floor is a draft: a 9-block line of stone spikes along your look, 40 damage to each and knocked down. Living Architecture (feat): with Variant, 1 CE more, the last spike stays a minute and slams the nearest foe within 6 blocks every 3 s for 26 damage.

6th

Biological Repair3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

2 AP: heal 44% of your health (about), and take poison, wither, blindness and nausea apart. With Variant, the ally you look at within 4 blocks instead.

11th

Fusion5 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

4 AP: fuse with a held foe in reach, if it's a foe 10 levels under you, or 5 to 9 under and already low (half its health at 5, more as the gap grows), player or creature but never a boss, It's absorbed, and half its health becomes yours as absorption for 10 minutes.

13th

Terrain Dominion5 CE · 1.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Restructure the ground where you look (20 blocks): stone erupts across 9 blocks, 79 damage and thrown up.

18th

Theater: Disassemble / Reassemble0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Choose what your Domain declares each pulse.

Maximum

1st

Maximum: City Block Reconstruction10 CE · Maximum (X)

5 AP: reshape everything within 20 blocks. 132 damage to every foe, walled in by conjured stone and held for 3 s. The walls come down after 10 s.

Passives

1st

Assembly Points

14 AP. They return after a long rest or two minutes out of a fight.

9th

Instant Reassembly

Automatic, 2 AP + 3 CE, once every 3 s: after a heavy hit (a fifth of your health), your body is put back together (poison, wither and blindness undone) and your worn armor is mended a tenth. It answers for an ally within 3 blocks too.

15th

Complete Rebuild

Once a long rest, 4 AP + 6 CE: rebuilt from 0 health to 1. You, or an ally within 4 blocks who would die.

Domain

18th

Operating Theater of Perfect Matter

Each pulse it declares what you chose: DISASSEMBLE (106 damage to every chosen foe, and a player's held item is worn a tenth) or REASSEMBLE (you and your allies inside heal 49% and are cleansed).
