Prayer Song

A ten-beat dance that lifts you and takes the floor out from under one foe, more with every beat.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Prayer1 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute, dance against the foe you look at (12 blocks). Every 3 s is a beat, and they add up: 1 you're harder to hit (6 armor) · 2 its guard drops · 3 your blows on it add 5 · 4 you're a third faster · 5 it fights weaker · 6 harder still to hit · 7 your blows strike true more often · 8 your blows on it add 9 damage · 9 they strike true more often still · 10 it can barely act. The song stops if your partner is out of sight or past 36 blocks when a beat falls.

11th

Maximum: Fortissimo10 CE · Maximum (X)

The dance leaps to its height: Prayer begins (or jumps) straight to its last beat.
