Illusive Mirage

Curse creatures and make them see illusions: distorted faces, false bodies, false terrain, until the House of Mirrors makes the lie inescapable.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Cursed Illusion2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) sees distorted faces: it reels and loses track of you, hits 40% softer for 6 s, and glows for a minute so it can't hide from you (a strong will may shake it off). From 6th, up to 5 foes near where you look at once (2 CE each beyond the first).

3rd

Phase Fist3 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your next weapon blow strikes beside a false double: a sure critical.

3rd

Distorting Disguise3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For 5 hours you look like someone else: creatures not already hunting you don't know you, until you strike.

11th

Illusive Terrain10 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute, the ground 45 blocks around you lies: every foe in it is slowed to a crawl, and the creatures there lose track of you.

3rd

Blurring3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For 5 rounds your body smears on the eye: about a third of the blows aimed at you miss (not a blind attacker's).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Absolute Perspective10 CE · Maximum (X)

With the feat: the foe you look at is fooled in every sense for a minute. Each 3 s it's blinded, reeling and weakened, and a harmful illusion bites it for 36 damage.

Domain

18th

House of Mirrors

Every chosen creature falls under an illusion and turns on the nearest of its own for 3 s. Mirror's Spite: half the blows aimed at you find another foe instead.
