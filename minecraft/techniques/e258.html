Cursed Circuitry

Cursed Circuitry, Advanced Circuits, Linked Circuits and Circuit Mastery: lay invisible patterns and detonate them as traps.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Lay Circuit2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Lay the chosen pattern (Technique Key 1) on the floor, wall or ceiling where you look (9 blocks); it waits, unseen by foes, until one comes on it, and remembers the way you looked. Combustion: 20 fire damage in 6 blocks · Lightning Surge: 18 damage in a 9-block cone the way you looked, stunned 2 s · Stasis Hold: held 3 s in 6 blocks · Burst Leap: everything on it thrown the way you looked when you laid it (you, the way you look now) · Shockwave Blast: 14 damage down a 12-block line the way you looked, thrown back · for 2 CE more: Binding Chains (held 3 s in 9 blocks), Energy Discharge (27 damage in 12), Energy Drain (14 damage in 12, CE back, and a sorcerer loses up to 10 CE to you unless they resist), Cursed Detonation (66 damage in 18 and a crater, once a long rest). Up to 8 at a time.

1st

Activate0 CE · Quick (G)

Set off the pattern nearest where you look (within 9 blocks of you; any of yours from 11th). In Living Circuitry, with none in reach, it's a Burst Leap of your own the way you look, once every 3 s.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Living Circuitry10 CE · Maximum (X)

With the feat: for a minute the circuit is drawn into your own body. Your weapon blows add 14 damage lightning (once a second), and once every 3 s one stuns the foe a moment.

Passives

6th

Linked Circuits

A pattern going off sets off every other pattern within 36 blocks of it, one after another.

11th

Circuit Mastery

Patterns deal 14 damage more, and Activate reaches any of them. Variant + Technique Key 1, Cursed Overclock: spend up to 5 CE; for a minute your patterns deal 7 damage more per CE.

Domain

18th

Deus ex Machina

The whole Domain is your circuit: every chosen foe's energy is drained through it, 66 damage, and 1 CE back to you for each (up to 5). A sorcerer among them also loses up to 5 CE to you.
