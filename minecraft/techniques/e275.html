Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Missile Impact1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Two giant fists of cursed energy hurled at the foe you look at (27 blocks): 42 damage for the first on each foe, 42 damage for each after. Hold to pour CE in: a fist more per CE (up to 5), the Devastating Barrage.

6th

Unrelenting Fury3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 9-block cone of fists falling from the sky: every foe in it takes 53 damage (a strong one may halve it) and is knocked down.

6th

Armored Missile Fist2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

With the feat: a minute with the fists worn as gauntlets, 4 armor more, a longer reach, and 36 damage on your weapon blows (up to three times a second).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Brutal Bombardment6 CE · Maximum (X)

A wave of fists on one foe within 15 blocks, then the crush: 205 damage, knocked down, the ground under it caved in, and everything near it takes the wave too.

Passives

3rd

Devastating Barrage

Technique Key 1: grappling fists. They deal no damage but seize the foe (1.5 s; a strong one may break free), and a seized foe struck again is pinned (3 s).

Domain

18th

Unrelenting Ring of Spirit

Giant fists fall on every chosen foe: 71 damage and knocked down. Inside, Missile Impact costs nothing and reaches any foe in the Domain.
