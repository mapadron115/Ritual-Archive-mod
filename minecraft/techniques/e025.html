Puppet Manipulation

A modular construct workshop: four chassis grades built where you look, cursed-energy cannons, and the Ultimate Puppet.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Build Puppet0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The chosen chassis stands where you look (18 blocks) for 10 minutes and strikes once every 3 s. Technique Key 1 picks the chassis. Basic (1 CE): 9 damage. Lesser (3 CE): 18 damage, and while it stands, blows land 15% lighter on you. Advanced (6 CE): 33 damage, and a bolt of 22 damage at the nearest foe within 36 blocks once every 3 s. Absolute (10 CE): 55 damage, it guards you, and its bolt is 44 damage. Up to 3 puppets; a new one past that takes the oldest's place. They can be struck down.

1st

Puppet's Eyes1 CE · Cast (Z)

See through the puppet or messenger nearest your look, up to 64 blocks away, and walk it with your own keys; it still strikes what comes in reach. Your own body stands where it is, open to blows. 1 CE every 6 s; cast again to come back.

3rd

Communication Puppet0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A tiny messenger stands where you look for 5 minutes: it can't fight and falls to a single blow, but it remembers every foe it sees within 16 blocks. When it ends, its memories come home: each of those foes glows for you for 30 s. Up to 6 after each long rest.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Ultimate Puppet10 CE · Maximum (X)

Needs the feat. Your two strongest puppets fuse into one Ultimate chassis for 10 minutes: both their blows and both their health, it guards you, and it fires two bolts of 44 damage once every 3 s. Half again the size. Cast it again beside your Ultimate to climb inside and drive it with your walk keys (sneak climbs out): every blow on you lands on it instead.

Puppet Show

Velvet curtains part on a stage that runs to the horizon, and strings come down from the dark. Each pulse every chosen foe dances on your strings: it strikes the nearest other foe with its own blow. A foe with no one to strike is held for 1.5 s.
