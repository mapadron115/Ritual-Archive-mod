Ice Formation

Encasing cold that holds foes in visible ice, frozen ground, a rain of spikes, and a Maximum that shatters every shell at once.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Ice Creation1 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 6-block cone of cold where you look: 66 damage, and the ice covers them, holding them for 1.5 s. With Variant it lays a path of ice instead, two blocks wide and 14 long the way you look, through the air if need be, for 20 s. Fire melts your ice: a burning foe is let go.

6th

Frost Calm4 CE · 5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Freezing mist on a spot within 36 blocks: 62 damage to everything within 18 blocks, held by the ice for 1.5 s, and the ground stays frozen for 10 s: water skins over with ice, snow settles, and foes on it are slowed.

11th

Ice Fall8 CE · 10 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You drop to the ground for a second, and ice falls on every foe within 18 blocks: held by the ice, then the spikes, 118 damage.

Maximum

11th

Shatter6 CE · Maximum (X)

Every foe your ice is holding (within 24 blocks) shatters with it: 111 damage through armor, and the ice is gone.

Passives

1st

Born of Winter

Cold can't touch you: freezing (powder snow, frost) does you no harm, and the water you walk on freezes under you.

3rd

Extreme Cold

A foe held in your ice is worn down: its blows on you land 15% softer while it's held. Foes within 3 blocks of a shell of your ice are slowed.

Domain

18th

White Abyss

Winter declared as law: every foe it chose takes 89 cold damage every 3 s and is held by the ice for 1.5 s.

Technique feats

1st

Iceshape

Ice Creation also forms a blade of ice in your hand: for 10 minutes your melee blows deal 7 damage more and slow the foe.

6th

Permafrost

When your ice would let a foe go, its feet stay frozen: every hold lasts half a second longer.

6th

Frostbite

The cold of your ice goes deeper: a held foe's blows land 30% softer instead of 15%, and it takes 7 damage every second it's held.
