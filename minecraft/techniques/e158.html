With Many Voices

Archive perfect voices, breathe away short-term memory, and lure the confused toward false cries in the dark.

3arts
4passives
4technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Amnestic Breath2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 18-block cone of amnestic mist: foes in it lose a Moment (up to 3; a strong foe may resist). One lost: it can't react against you. Two: a creature loses track of you past 8 blocks. Three: once you've moved 6 blocks you're hidden from it until you strike it or it bumps into you. Each 3 s a foe may shake one off.

11th

AMN-C227 Saturation4 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute, a 9-block aura of mist: foes that stay in it lose a Moment every 3 s.

1st

Thrown Voice1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A recorded voice cries out where you look (36 blocks) for a minute: foes that have lost a Moment go looking for it (1 CE every 3 s while it lures). Inside your Domain it comes from anywhere you look, free once every 3 s.

Passives

1st

Voiceprints

You record the voice of a creature you strike, or a player you hear speak within 36 blocks: up to 6 voices, the newest first. Thrown Voice speaks in the newest.

1st

Predatory Strike

Once every 3 s, your blow on a foe with a Moment lost that's lost you or can't react: it has to resist or be frightened of your nearest voice (or you) for a minute, unable to get farther from it, and held 1.5 s.

3rd

Troglobitic Senses

You see in the dark, sense creatures within 18 blocks, and climb walls.

6th

Borrowed Distress

Your voices lure as they always have, for 1 CE every 3 s while a foe is drawn.

Domain

18th

Corridor Without Memory

Memory Break: every chosen foe loses a Moment.

Technique feats

1st

Perfect Voiceprint

Technique Key 1 (1 CE) throws a second voice, your second-newest, where you look: lured foes follow whichever is nearer.

6th

Dark Hunter

Variant + Thrown Voice (1 CE more): for 3 s, foes within 18 blocks of the voice show themselves to you.

6th

No New Memories

A creature that fails to resist your Breath forgets you for a minute: it drops you as a target (not a strong one).

11th

Once every 3 s, an ally's blow on a foe with 2 or more Moments lost lands half again as hard (1 CE).
