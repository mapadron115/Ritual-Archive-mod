Hive Evolution

A brood of Slivers that share every trait the hive has learned, and a queen who is all of them at once.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Sliver Brood2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Five Slivers hatch beside you for a minute (up to 12 at once): 15 damage a bite, wearing every hive trait. With Variant, the brood comes back to you instead.

15th

Sliver Queen6 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The Sliver Queen, as big as a cow, joins the brood for a minute: 33 damage a blow, and she wears every trait. The first each long rest is free.

1st

Component Mana0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Your nearest Sliver dissolves back into 1 CE, spraying 9 damage on the foes beside it.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Sliver Legion10 CE · Maximum (X)

For 9 s every trait the hive knows is worn, and five more Slivers hatch. Afterwards the hive wears only two traits for 5 minutes.

Passives

1st

Hive Traits

The hive wears 6 traits at once (Technique Key 1 looks through them, Variant + Key 1 wears or takes off the one shown); every Sliver and the Queen share them all: Claw, +1d6 on a Sliver's first bite each round; Carapace, +2 armor; Swift, +20% speed; Sinew, +mastery on a Sliver's first bite each round; Flying, they leap at prey and take no fall damage; Venom, their bites poison; Reach, +1 block of bite reach; Regeneration, each heals 2 a round; Ward, holds, slows and weakness on them are halved; Double Strike, one extra bite a round for the hive; Leech, the hive's bites heal you once a round; Psionic, their bites pass armor; Diffusion, a Sliver's first area blow each round is lessened.

1st

The Hive Is Many

While your brood is out, players on your team within 50 blocks of you or of a Sliver wear the hive's traits too: Carapace's armor, Swift's speed, Reach, Regeneration, Ward, Flying (no fall damage), Venom on their blows, Claw and Sinew (and Psionic) on their first blow each round, Double Strike's second blow, Leech and Diffusion. One hive at a time: the nearest caster's.

3rd

Hive Mind

The brood shares one target: the foe you last struck, shot or were struck by.

11th

Adaptive Sliver

When a Sliver or you take a kind of harm (2 CE, by itself, once every 3 s), the brood takes half from that kind for 3 s.

20th

Sliver Overlord

Six traits at once, Sliver Brood hatches five, and the first Sliver Queen each long rest is free.

Domain

18th

The First Hive

Each pulse a Sliver forms beside each of up to 6 chosen foes and bites once for 27 damage with the hive's traits, then dissolves at the next pulse.
