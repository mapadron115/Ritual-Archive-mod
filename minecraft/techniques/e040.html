Kinetic Imbuement

Convert the potential energy of inorganic matter into a deliberate kinetic release: loaded cards, a bound conduit, laced charges, and Fifty-Two Pickup.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Loaded Toss1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Charge a piece of matter from your off-hand or hotbar: hold to load it (a Charge every 0.6 s, 1 CE each after the first, up to 5, and no more than its size holds: a token such as a button or nugget 1, small such as an ingot or gem 2, a block or ore 3, a storage block 4, obsidian or netherite 5). Thrown, it detonates on what it hits: 22 damage per Charge, a block and a half of blast per Charge.

3rd

Cardsharp Barrage2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A fan of 5 cards, one Charge each, thrown as one motion.

6th

Lace Charge1 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Plant a 2-Charge object where you look (36 blocks; up to 5 at once). Technique Key 1 detonates every laced Charge at once.

6th

Armed Ally2 CE · 20 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Needs the feat Armed Ally. Hand the teammate you look at (6 blocks) a charged piece of matter. It keeps its 2 Charges in their hands for a day, and goes off where they throw it: 44 damage.

1st

Bound Conduit0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Charge your weapon as a conduit: once every 3 s a weapon blow discharges, 22 damage in a small blast (1 CE). Press again to let it go.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Fifty-Two Pickup10 CE · Maximum (X)

The whole deck at once: charged cards rain across 10 blocks where you look, each a blast of 44 damage.

Passives

3rd

Vector Discharge

Variant + Technique Key 1 (1 CE): a small blast at your heels throws you the way you look.

3rd

Kinetic Footwork

Faster on your feet.

6th

Chain Reaction

A blast sets off every charged thing lying in its reach, laced or not, each its own explosion (1 CE each).

6th

Saboteur's Touch

A weapon blow (1 CE) charges the foe's held weapon or worn armor, up to 6 foes for a minute. Technique Key 1 sets them off on their wearers: 44 damage each.

11th

Remote Charge

Lace Charge reaches 18 blocks, and 36 from 18th.

11th

Advanced Agility

Faster still. A sprint carries you up the wall it runs into, and blasts hurt you half as much.

Domain

18th

House of the Loaded World

No blow when it lands: the debtors detonate themselves. Every 3 s, each foe it chose takes on a debt until the next. A foe holding something has a Loaded Hand: its next swing or shot blows up in its grip, 66 damage, and knocks what it held to the ground. Any other has a Loaded Floor: when it has moved 3 blocks the ground goes off under it, 66 damage, and it's thrown about 9 blocks and held a moment.
