Dragonheart Incarnation

Eat the heart, inherit the dragon: claws, scales and lineage breath in installments, until the debt comes due and the human outline goes.

8arts
10passives
4technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Dragonclaw1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A dragon's claw rakes the foe you look at (3 blocks; 5 as a dragon): 16 damage (25 damage as a dragon).

3rd

Dragon Breath3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your lineage's breath down a 18-block cone: 45 damage (a nimble or strong foe takes half), and the world takes it too. Greater Breath: with Variant, a 27-block line instead. As a dragon: 71 damage, free every 6 s, and each 3 s it may come back sooner. Primeval Breath: as a dragon with your breath come back, Variant spends 5 CE more on a 36-block cone of 107 damage, and it costs you 18 damage.

3rd

Dragon Roar2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A dragon's roar: foes within 4.5 blocks are frightened for 3 s. One that shrugs it off is safe from it for a day.

6th

Dragon Wings3 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute charcoal wings spread from your back and you fly.

11th

Dragonstorm6 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A storm of your lineage on everything within 9 blocks of you: 62 damage and its affliction.

3rd

Communion Bite3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A dragon's maw closes on the foe you look at (3 blocks): 45 damage, and it's knocked down 2 s (a strong foe keeps its feet).

1st

Heartfire Surge1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For 3 s your blows burn with your lineage, 9 damage more and its affliction, and you move faster.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: True Dragon12 CE · Maximum (X)

For up to ten minutes you become a dragon: your body swells, you fly, your scales thicken, your claws rake twice as hard, nothing knocks you back, and Heartfire Surge and Dragon Breath come free. Cast again to take back your human shape.

Passives

1st

Gravel Scales

Dragon scales harden your skin: 6 more armor.

1st

Draconic Senses

You see in the dark.

1st

Draconic Lineage

Technique Key 1 picks your lineage: Flame, Ancient Lightning, Glintstone Frost, Magma or Scarlet.

1st

Dragon Hearts

Each kind of dragon you slay brings your lineage's steps (Greater Breath, Lineage Ascension) one level sooner, up to 5. Hearts taken: 0.

6th

Tail or Horn

Variant + Technique Key 1 chooses, once after each long rest. Tail: a foe that strikes you is swept back. Horn: a Dragonclaw after a 6-block straight run knocks the foe down and throws it 3 blocks. Now: Tail.

11th

Greater Scales

Your scales thicken further, and weapon blows on you land a tenth lighter.

11th

Lineage Ascension

Your breath also carries the affliction of the lineage you held before this one.

18th

Dragon Fear

As a True Dragon, a foe that first comes within 9 blocks of you is frightened (a strong will shrugs it off).

20th

Beyond Time

Once a transformation, Variant + Dragonclaw vanishes into a rift and comes out beside the foe you look at, up to 90 blocks away, with a claw that can't miss.

20th

Elder Heart

As a True Dragon, your own lineage's harm can't touch you.

Technique feats

6th

Winged Predator

A weapon blow landed while diving down knocks the foe flat (a strong one keeps its feet) and you rebound safely (once a 3 s).

11th

Dragon Communion Savant

For 1 CE more, your breath also carries the lineage you held before those two.

6th

Scale Shelter

Your scales spread over an ally within 1.5 blocks: they take less harm.

11th

Dragonlord's Echo

Dragonstorm breaks where you look, up to 27 blocks away.
