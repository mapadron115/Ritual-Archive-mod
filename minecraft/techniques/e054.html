Dragonheart Incarnation

Eat the heart, inherit the dragon: claws, scales and lineage breath in installments, until the debt comes due and the human outline goes.

5arts
3passives
1technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Dragonclaw1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A dragon's claw rakes the foe you look at (3 blocks; 5 as a dragon): 16 damage (25 damage as a dragon).

3rd

Dragon Breath3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your lineage's breath down a 12-block cone: 45 damage (a nimble or strong foe takes half), and the world takes it too. As a dragon: 71 damage, free every 6 s.

3rd

Communion Bite3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A dragon's maw closes on the foe you look at (3 blocks): 45 damage, and it's knocked down 2 s (a strong foe keeps its feet).

1st

Heartfire Surge1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For 3 s your blows burn with your lineage, 9 damage more and its affliction, and you move faster.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: True Dragon12 CE · Maximum (X)

For up to ten minutes you become a dragon: your body swells, you fly, your scales thicken, your claws rake twice as hard, nothing knocks you back, and Heartfire Surge and Dragon Breath come free. Cast again to take back your human shape.
