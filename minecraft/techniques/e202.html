Man-Eater's Ballistics

A finite tail magazine: fire specialised quills at range, then close with the lion's maw to reload. Volley, feed, disengage.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Quill Volley1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Three spines (or what you have) fly where you look (48 blocks): 14 damage each, and poison. A quill stays in the foe for 10 s (up to three), each one slowing it, and carries the tail's payload (Technique Key 1): Barbed (slowed more) or Heavy (thrown back); or, for 2 Spines a quill, Venom (a deep poison), Screamer (it can't answer blows for 3 s) or Tether (it can't go more than 9 blocks from where it was struck for 6 s). A quill that strikes a wall glances off it once.

1st

Rattle the Barbs1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every foe with a quill of yours in it, within 48 blocks, is held for 1.5 s unless it resists.

1st

Lion's Maw1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A bite on the foe you look at (3.5 blocks): 22 damage, you heal a little, and 2 Spines regrow. With Variant you worry it instead: no Spines, and a foe with a quill in it flees for 3 s unless it resists.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Sky Black With Quills10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every spine at once (six at least) rains out of the sky where you look, on every foe within 18 blocks: 61 damage, three quills and no answering blows for 3 s, or 20 damage and one quill if it resists. 6 quills stand in the ground for a minute and stick the first foe to step on them.

Passives

1st

Tail Magazine

Your tail holds 10 Spines. One regrows every 4 s; the lion's maw reloads faster.

Technique feats

1st

Quill Catcher

Once every 3 s, a shot that would hit you may be snatched out of the air (about a third of the time), loaded as a Spine, and a quill flies back at whoever shot it for 14 damage.

3rd

Scorpion Tail

Lion's Maw spends a Spine to bite with venom: 7 damage more, and the foe is envenomed.

6th

Hunter's Mouth

Lion's Maw on a foe at death's door (under a quarter of its health) or one that is held regrows a third Spine.

6th

Compound Payload

Variant + Quill Volley, 2 Spines a quill: each quill carries your payload and the next one after it.
