Man-Eater's Ballistics

A finite tail magazine: fire specialised quills at range, then close with the lion's maw to reload. Volley, feed, disengage.

Quill Volley1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Three spines (or what you have) fly where you look (27 blocks): 14 damage each, and poison.

Lion's Maw1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Maximum: The Sky Black With Quills10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every spine at once (at least six, regrown on the spot) rains out of the sky where you look: 20 damage each, in a 6-block storm.

Passives

1st

Tail Magazine

Your tail holds 8 Spines. One regrows every 4 s; the lion's maw reloads faster.

Technique feats

1st

Quill Catcher

Once every 3 s, a shot that would hit you may be snatched out of the air (about a third of the time) and loaded as a Spine.

3rd

Scorpion Tail

Lion's Maw spends a Spine to bite with venom: 7 damage more, and the foe is envenomed.

6th

Hunter's Mouth

Lion's Maw on a foe at death's door (under a quarter of its health) regrows a third Spine.
