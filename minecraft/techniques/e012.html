❖ e012 · Jujutsu Kaisen ❖

Disaster Tides

Hydrokinesis with a 3rd-level Domain, a roster of sea shikigami, a defensive water ring, flooding, and a Maximum tsunami.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Tides of Disaster1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Tap: water thrown where you look (36 blocks): 36 damage. Hold a second, then release: a high-pressure lance 18 blocks long instead (+1 CE), 22 damage to everything on it, and a foe that can't take it (60%) is knocked down and takes it again.

3rd

Death Swarm: Sperm Whale10 CE · Cast (Z)

The largest of the drowned rushes the foe you look at, or the nearest: one strike, 100 damage, and it's gone (inside your Domain; anywhere from 11th). The others answer to Technique Key 1.

6th

Water Defensive Ring6 CE · Cast (Z)

A ring of water around you for 2 minutes: shields 60% of your max health, taking blows first; while it holds, cold can't touch you and you take 15% less from everything. It rises by itself when a heavy blow comes in (8 CE, once every 30 s).

11th

Flash Flood8 CE · Cast (Z)

The foe nearest where you look (within 18 blocks): 89 cold damage and swept about 24 blocks away. Hold Variant as you cast for the whole flood instead (+2 CE): 106 damage to everything within 6 blocks of a point 18 blocks away.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Tsunami10 CE · Maximum (X)

The sea itself: a wall of water rises behind you and rolls the way you look for 4 s, 66 damage a second to everything it carries.

Passives

1st

Tide-born

You breathe water and swim like you were born in it.

3rd

Death Swarm

Technique Key 1 calls a sea shikigami (Variant + Key 1 picks which): Eel (2 CE, 20 damage), Piranhas (3 CE, 33 damage), River Serpent (4 CE, 41 damage), Killer Whale (6 CE, 62 damage). Each rushes the foe you look at or the nearest, strikes once and is gone. Up to 3 on their way at once; inside your Domain only, until 11th level.

Domain

3rd

Horizon of Captivating Skandha

The spectral school engulfs every foe it chose: 89 damage every 3 s, held by the feeding current for 1.5 s. Two free openings a long rest, then 20 CE.
