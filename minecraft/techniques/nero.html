The Burning Lyre

The emperor plays while the city burns: a dirge that sets the audience alight and an anthem that wards the player.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

The Audience Gathers2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Begin a minute's performance: once every 3 s the Dirge burns every foe within 36 blocks (7 fire damage) and the Anthem wards you against 5% of your health. Each beat after the first costs 1 CE; with none left, the music stops.

6th

Encore of Embers5 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The three foes nearest where you look, within 36 blocks of you: 34 fire damage, and they burn for 9 s (a nimble foe takes half and doesn't catch).

11th

Symphony of Ash8 CE · Cast (Z)

For 3 s the audience is everything within 72 blocks, the Dirge burns 20 fire damage, and those it burns are frightened: they hit you 20% softer for 3 s (a steady mind resists). Once a long rest.

3rd

Crescendo3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For 3 s the Dirge burns 14 fire damage and the Anthem wards twice as much, and you hear every foe in the audience. Starts the performance if it isn't playing.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Eternal City Aflame10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute burning Rome moves with you: once every 3 s foes within 36 blocks take 27 fire damage (a nimble foe takes half), and the flames part for you.
