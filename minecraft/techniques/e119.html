Invisible Force

Invisibility, force screens, constructs, flight, the Absolute Sphere and the Event Horizon Shell.

6arts
2passives
3technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

6th

Force Shapes2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A wall of force where you look (20 blocks) for a minute: it stops foes and their shots. With Variant, a 3x3 platform of hard light there instead. Either draws 10 Integrity. Technique Key 1 (2 CE) is a ram instead: 33 damage to the foe you look at, shoved back.

11th

Internal Bubble5 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A field inside the chest of the foe you look at (18 blocks): 55 damage, and a sorcerer can't cast for 3 s.

11th

Flight2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Ten minutes: you fly on a bubble of force.

18th

Absolute Sphere8 CE · Cast (Z)

Seal the foe you look at (20 blocks) in a sphere of force: it can't move or act for 9 s (a nimble foe may slip it).

1st

Invisibility2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Ten minutes: light bends around you and foes lose you. Attacking shows you until the next 3 s. Cast again to drop it. With Variant (3 CE) the ally you look at goes unseen instead, or every ally within 6 blocks.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Event Horizon Shell10 CE · Maximum (X)

A 10-block sphere around you for a minute: shots from outside can't cross it, foes inside can't leave, and every 3 s it closes in, 55 damage to every foe at its edge.

Passives

3rd

Force Screen

An Integrity pool of 40 that refills slowly. Once every 3 s (1 CE), a blow that reaches you is caught on a panel: Integrity is spent to stop it. When the last of it goes, every wall and platform collapses and your technique burns out for 3 s.

20th

Unseen Architect

Integrity doubles, and the first Force Screen each 3 s costs no CE.

Technique feats

3rd

Hard-Light Step

While you hold Integrity: no fall damage, no knockback, and sneaking as you fall lets you walk down the air (it drains Integrity).

3rd

Mother's Shield

The Force Screen also catches a blow on an ally within 6 blocks (the same once every 3 s), and slides them 2 blocks clear of the attacker.

6th

Fine Control

Variant + Technique Key 1 works the lever, button, door, trapdoor or gate you look at, up to 18 blocks away.
