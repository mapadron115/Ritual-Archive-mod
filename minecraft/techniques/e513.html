Hide and Seek

A fourth-dimensional mansion entered through doors only the keyholder can hang, and Scream, a window that closes on whatever it touches and files it away.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

The Mansion Door2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Hang a real trapdoor where you look, flat on the ground or against a wall (9 blocks; 6 at a time). Each door opens into a room of its own in your mansion, and only for you and your allies. Variant, looking at one of your doors, leaves it open to anyone, or locks it again: an open door lying on the floor is a pitfall, and a foe that steps on it drops into its room. Anyone can break a door, and when your last door falls, everyone in your mansion is put out.

1st

Enter / Leave the Mansion0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Step through your door (or right-click it), with any ally within 2.5 blocks, into that door's room: the mansion is a hall, a corridor and 20 rooms outside the world. A foe you look at within 3 blocks is dragged in with you unless it resists (2 CE): a player can leave by the trapdoor in that room, back out of the door it belongs to, and a creature goes back where it stood once nobody walks the mansion. Every room is shut by an iron door that only your hand or an ally's opens, so a foe stays in the room it came into. Nothing outside can reach you, and nothing can dowse you. Leave by a room's trapdoor, or with this art, out of the door nearest where you look. Variant at a door: straight through it and out of your next door.

3rd

Scream3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A portal snaps shut on a foe within 9 blocks: it's filed into a sealed room of its own, a player for 3 s and a creature for a minute, then put back where it stood. Its output is sealed for a minute. You're told which room: you and your allies can walk in on it. The vast are only held where they stand, 3 s.

18th

Keyholder's Step1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Step out of the door nearest where you look (within 150 blocks), from wherever you are, with any ally at your hand (2.5 blocks). Variant: step into your hall from anywhere.

Maximum

1st

Reversal: Seek and Find2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Maximum (X)

Tear the hiding off: the foe you look at within 18 blocks is dragged out beside you (not the vast ones), and for a minute it glows and can't turn invisible. Aimed at another keyholder's door, it drags out everyone hiding behind it. With nothing in your sights, it pulls your own filed foes out beside you.

Doormat

1st
