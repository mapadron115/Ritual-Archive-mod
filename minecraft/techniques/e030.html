Arts

6th

Shooting Star5 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

While mass is up: kick the ground's loose stone down a 10-block line where you look, twice your mass's blow bonus, to everything on it.

1st

Virtual Mass Increase1 CE · Quick (G) · hold to pour

Hold to pour mass in, a CE a point (up to 12): for 30 s your melee blows add 5 damage to 18 damage by your mass (a step up per 2 mass) and half the mass again, and hit far harder. It can't be ended or renewed early.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Black Hole20 CE · Maximum (X) · hold to pour

Hold the key for 2 s to commit (let go early and nothing happens): you become the singularity. Within 20 blocks, a foe 10 levels under you, or 5 to 9 under and already low (half its health at 5, more as the gap grows), player or creature but never a boss, is destroyed outright, unless it holds its ground (3 in 10). The rest take 443 damage and a fifth of their health through all defense, and are dragged in. The ground around the singularity is swallowed as it grows, and loose things are drawn in. Two seconds later, you die.

Passives

1st

Mass Impact

A critical melee blow (a falling one) with mass up detonates: 1+ mass knocks the foe down, 2+ tears a cone of the same blow out behind it, 3+ slows it, 4+ holds it for 2 s. It's shoved 3 blocks too.

3rd

Improved Duration

Virtual mass lasts twice as long.

Domain

18th

Event Horizon

Everything inside is crushed by mass: every step costs four. Every 3 s, each chosen foe is knocked flat and takes 71 damage.
