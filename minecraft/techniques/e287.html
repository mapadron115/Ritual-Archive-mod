Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Devour1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your maw opens for 3 s: every creature you kill in it is devoured and its nature stored (up to 5 kinds; a new one digests the oldest).

3rd

Regurgitation4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For an hour you wear the chosen devoured thing's nature (Technique Key 1 picks): a flyer's wings (flight), a fire-proof thing's flame (no burning), a water thing's gills (breath and swimming), a spider's climb (up walls; crouch to cling), a heavy thing's hide (12 armor, no knockback), or anything else's fangs (your blows bite harder by its strength).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Body of the Devourer10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you become the chosen devoured thing: every nature at once, and its bite on every blow twice over.
