Braxton Veil

Flood the battlefield with a nauseating, electrically wrong atmosphere whose modes reshape sight, weight, sensing and stamina.

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Atmospheric Pulse3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The Veil exhales: everything in it (or within 3 blocks of you without it) takes 14 damage, gains Exposure and is poisoned (a strong foe may resist).

1st

Unnatural Atmosphere2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute the Veil hangs round you (18 blocks; with Variant it hangs still where you look, 18 blocks off). Every 3 s foes in it gain Exposure (up to 5): 1 slowed, 2 weakened, 3 sick and reeling, 4 poisoned and rotting (7 damage an Exposure each 3 s).

Passives

1st

Modes (Technique Key 1)

Sulfur: exposed foes heal at half. Crimson: you're hard to see. Gravitic: foes inside are dragged slow. Static: exposed foes lose their aim and hit softer.

6th

Floating Apparition

You hover (fly) while the Veil is up.

The Hill Beneath the Red Star

The whole barrier becomes the Veil, every Mode at once: every chosen foe gains Exposure and is poisoned.
