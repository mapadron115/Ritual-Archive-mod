Synovia Manipulation

Liquefy the bones where they stand: soften the frame so every blow afterward lands deeper, and plant mites that eat the marrow.

noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Synovia Manipulation4 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (9 blocks): 53 damage (twice on a creature of bone), and its frame softens: your blows on it land 25% harder for 3 s (a tough foe halves it and stays firm).

6th

Bone Broth10 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Every foe within 9 blocks is liquefied at once.

Passives

3rd

Devour the Marrow

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow plants marrow mites (3 CE): the limb breaks, slowed and hitting 20% softer for a minute.

11th

Softened Frame

Softened foes take even more from every blow.

Technique feats

1st

Ossuary Sense

You feel the architecture of bodies through stone: a mote shows over every creature within 16 blocks.

3rd

Joint Lock

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow on a softened foe seizes the hinge: it's held a moment (a strong one may tear free).

6th

Broth of the Fallen

Bone Broth feeds you: a little health for each foe it catches (up to five).
