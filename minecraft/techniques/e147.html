Faultline Ledger

Mark faults in bodies, let ordinary movement and impacts pile up Strain, then cash it out in controlled ruptures.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Rupture1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (12 blocks) ruptures: 7 damage a Strain banked (at least one), and it staggers. 2 or more knocks it flat; 4 or more opens a fissure under it and holds it 3 s; 6 or more throws it back; 8 cracks its armor too.

1st

Survey1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at (12 blocks) gains 1 Strain (and a Fault, if it had none).

Passives

1st

Accumulate Strain

A foe you hurt gains a Fault for a minute. Once every 3 s each, it gains Strain when it moves 2 blocks, when it's knocked about, and when you strike it (up to 8).

6th

Aftershock

Rupturing 4 or more Strain passes 1 Strain to another Fault within 6 blocks.

11th

Deep Fault

Strain banks up to 8, and the Cataclysm opens.
