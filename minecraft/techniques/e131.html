Pyromantic Soul

Ember rises only from self-harm and reckless acts, and the hotter the forge, the more it burns everything, you included.

7arts
4passives
4technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Pyrobolt1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A bolt of fire (18 blocks): 18 damage, 27 damage at 3 Ember, 36 damage at 6.

6th

Ignition4 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You explode: 45 damage to everything within 6 blocks, the ground catches, and you take some too (+2 Ember).

9th

Spontaneous Combustion3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) bursts into flame: 45 damage, then 9 damage more a 3 s later as it burns.

13th

Elemental Incarnation6 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute you become living flame: you fly, fire and lava can't hurt you, your blows carry 11 damage more, and everything within 4.5 blocks burns each 3 s.

15th

Inferno6 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

An inferno 9 blocks across where you look (27 blocks): 89 damage (a nimble foe halves it), and the ground burns. Stand in it yourself for +3 Ember.

3rd

Flame Dash1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Dash 9 blocks along your aim, leaving a burning path: 18 damage to everything you pass, and real fire behind you.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Incandescent Cataclysm10 CE · Maximum (X)

At Ember 10: the forge empties down an 18-block cone, 159 damage (a nimble foe halves it), burning the ground. Ember is a gate, not a cost: it stays, and the heat is quiet for 3 s after.

Passives

1st

Ember

Rises only from reckless acts: taking hits, casting below half health, and standing in fire or burning. Cools a point every 5 s you play it safe.

11th

Regulator Off

Taking a hit head-on gives 2 Ember instead of 1.

18th

Emblem of Flame

The first fire art you cast each 3 s rolls one more die.

20th

Torch of Defiance

Ember never falls below 3. Once a long rest, the Inferno costs 3 CE less and every die lands at its top.

Technique feats

3rd

Young Pyromancer

The first fire art of 3 CE or more you cast in a minute sheds a spark: for a minute it strikes the nearest foe within 9 blocks each 3 s for 7 damage and sets it alight, and it winks out when it finds no one.

3rd

Shared Flame

Technique Key 1 lends your flame to the ally you look at (9 blocks): their next weapon blow within two rounds carries 9 damage more fire.

6th

Reckless Spark

Cast a fire art with Variant: it burns you a little (nothing reduces it, never below 1) for an Ember, and its dice hit a quarter harder.

6th

Can't Be Extinguished

Once a minute, struck while Ember runs at 5 or more, you erupt: the attacker takes 41 damage and burns, and you gain 1 Ember.
