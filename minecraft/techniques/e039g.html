Sunshine: Solar Grace

The same furnace as Pride, circulated with angelic precision: flight in daylight, Solar Infusion on every blow, lines of sunlight, delayed judgments, suns from orbit, and the Greatest Sun.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Cruel Sun1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour 1 to all your CE: a miniature sun flies 120 blocks where you look and bursts on what it meets, 16 damage at night to 33 damage at noon for one CE, a Solar Die more for each CE poured. It falls from orbit, 6 blocks above you.

6th

Crazy Prominence2 CE · Cast (Z)

Your Mastery in CE (6): 6 homing suns fall from orbit among the foes within 120 blocks, 14 damage at night to 40 damage at noon each.

6th

Sunspot2 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The next foe your Sunshine burns carries a sunspot (6 at a time). It detonates when the foe moves 3 blocks, or after 3 s: 28 damage at night to 79 damage at noon, and it's blinded for a second.

1st

Solar Infusion0 CE · Cast (Z)

Toggle. A melee blow or Cruel Sun that lands spends 1 CE for 14 damage at night to 40 damage at noon more, once every 3 s (twice, three times at noon).

1st

Solar Memory0 CE · Cast (Z)

The rite: stand still out of a fight for 10 s under a Low, High or Near-Noon sun to store it inside you. Release (2 CE): for a minute you burn as if under the stored sun, if it's higher. Each true noon stores itself.

3rd

Solar Fist2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A 12-block line of concentrated sunlight where you look: 21 damage at night to 46 damage at noon to every foe on it.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Greatest Sun10 CE · Maximum (X) · hold to pour

A miniature sun grows as it crosses the sky to where you look (up to 64 blocks) and bursts: 80 damage at night to 218 damage at noon to every foe within 6 blocks (12 if it lands more than 30 blocks away). Hold to pour more: a Solar Die per CE, and a block wider per 5.

Passives

1st

Solar Station

Your sun hits harder as the day climbs: Night (weakest), Low Sun, High Sun, Near-Noon (11:00 to 14:00), Zenith (12:00 to 13:00, strongest).

1st

Solar Grace

While the sun is up you fly, faster as it climbs. At night (or when the memory runs out) you fall.

3rd

Fine Control

Your Sunshine never touches what you don't mean it to: villagers, pets and grazing animals pass through it unharmed.

6th

Solar Sovereignty

By day nothing can set you burning, and flames don't hurt you.

11th

Perfect Grace

Flight half again as fast; every Solar Die burns a little hotter; Solar Infusion rides two blows every 3 s at noon; your Sunshine cuts through armor.

18th

Sun Without Horizon

Your sun never sinks below High Sun, even at night, and each true noon stores itself in your Solar Memory.

18th

Absolute Circulation

Solar Infusion rides two blows every 3 s at any hour (three at noon); you keep 6 sunspots, and an armed Sunspot marks every foe a Crazy Prominence hits (2 CE each).

Domain

18th

Empyrean of the Greatest Sun

A weightless golden firmament of orbiting suns, where it is always noon. Every 3 s, Celestial Procession: each foe it chose takes 99 damage and carries a Procession Sun; if it moves 3 blocks before the next, the sun detonates, 79 damage, and blinds it for a second.
