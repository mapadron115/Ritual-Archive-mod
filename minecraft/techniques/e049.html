Flame of Frenzy

A self-burning meter of Madness feeds a yellow flame that frightens, kindles and spreads, until the boundary between you and everything melts.

8arts
6passives
4technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Frenzied Burst1 CE · Cast (Z)

+1 Madness. A gout of yellow flame along your look (24 blocks): 18 damage (more with Madness). A foe struck twice is frightened: it hits softer.

3rd

Frenzy Gaze2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

+1 Madness. A foe within 18 blocks that is looking you in the eye takes 27 damage and can't land a blow for 3 s.

3rd

Flame Fan3 CE · Cast (Z)

+1 Madness. A 9-block cone of yellow fire: 36 damage to each foe in it, half to a strong will.

6th

Face of Chaos3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

+1 Madness. A held foe within reach (4 blocks) sees the face: 45 damage, and it's stunned a moment.

11th

Apparition of the Lord6 CE · Cast (Z)

+2 Madness. For a minute a flaming head hangs over you: every 3 s it looses 6 bolts at foes within 27 blocks, 18 damage each.

20th

May Chaos Take the World5 CE · Cast (Z)

You become the Lord of Frenzied Flame, by choice.

11th

Unendurable Frenzy6 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

+2 Madness. The Lord's apparition breathes: a 8-block cone of 53 damage, and everything in it is frightened.

1st

Howl of Frenzy2 CE · Quick (G)

+1 Madness. For a minute your weapon blows burn: 9 damage more, and they set foes alight.

Passives

1st

Madness

Frenzy arts add Madness (0-10); it ebbs a point every 20 s you go without. Each point makes your Frenzy 5% stronger. From 7, blows on you land 15% harder; at 10 the flame takes you: your mind reels for 5 s and Madness falls back to 7.

1st

Burn the Pain

Nausea and anything holding you burn away the moment they take you (1 CE, +1 Madness).

6th

Contagious Despair

A frightened foe your flame hurts makes the foes beside it weaker for 3 s.

11th

Madness Feeds Madness

A foe that fails against your Frenzy gives you 6 temporary health.

18th

The Lord of Frenzied Flame

At 10 Madness the flame crowns you instead of taking you: for a minute your Frenzy Dice are d12s, you fly, and the flame's reeling can't take you. Every 3 s you must hold on, or the flame turns you on the nearest creature and you charge it. Lord's Emergence: once a long rest, falling at 8 Madness or more leaves you at 1 health, crowned. Melt Distinction: crowned, your flame burns what fire can't. Last Ember of Self: once a crowning, a hold you'd lose is kept for 53 damage to yourself, never more than half the health you have.

3rd

Kindled Contagion

From 3 Madness, a foe your Frenzy frightens is Kindled: it burns, and when it dies the flame leaps to the foes beside it.

Domain

18th

One Great, No More Distinction

Melt the Boundary: every chosen foe takes 106 damage, and every blessing on it burns away. It opens only at 6 Madness or more.

Technique feats

1st

Feed the Flame

Technique Key 1: take a point of Madness on purpose. Your next Frenzy blow adds 9 damage.

3rd

Shabriri's Cry

While Howl of Frenzy runs, once every 3 s the foe you look at (6 blocks) hears your cry: it freezes in fear for 1.5 s and is frightened.

6th

Yellow-Eyed Prophet

You see creatures around you through walls.

11th

Lord Candidate

Once a long rest, when the flame would take you, it crowns you instead: you keep your mind and are mended.
