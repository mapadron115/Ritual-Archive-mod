Arts

1st

Vermin of Disaster2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

An insect swarm boils out beside you and hunts for 10 minutes: 14 damage a bite each 3 s (one swarm at a time; 6 from 3rd). Kill its insects and it's gone.

3rd

Earthen Insect Trance2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A pouch shikigami crawls out for 10 minutes (up to 2): a heavy beetle, 27 damage a bite each 3 s.

11th

Parthenogenesis1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

8 CE, 1 less for every creature your vermin have devoured in the last 480 s: an egg where you look (18 blocks) hatches a swarm every 3 s for a minute.

Maximum

Every swarm you can hold boils out at once, and the whole cloud falls on the foe you look at: 53 damage from each swarm.

Reinforced Vermin

When you can spare 2 CE, your swarms come out hardened: larger insects, 27 damage a bite.
