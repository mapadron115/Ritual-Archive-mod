Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

3rd

Snowball Barrage2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A handful of three snowballs along your look (36 blocks): 27 cold damage, and what they hit is chilled and slowed for 3 s. Snow settles where they land.

11th

Whiteout5 CE · Cast (Z)

Snow swallows 36 blocks round you for a minute and settles on the ground: foes inside are slowed, and now and then blinded.

1st

Wind-Rider1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The wind carries you: flight for 3 s, or a full minute while your Belief is 3 or more. Water freezes under your feet while it has you.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Eternal Snow Day10 CE · Maximum (X)

A blizzard 72 blocks round you for five minutes: every foe in it is slowed and chilled to the bone, and you ride the wind freely through it.

Passives

1st

Belief

Belief (up to 11) grows when your snow hits and while another player is within 72 blocks; it fades slowly out of a fight.

14th

The Wind Remembers

Once every 3 s (2 CE), a blow on you blows you aside: it lands a third softer and you're carried away from it.
