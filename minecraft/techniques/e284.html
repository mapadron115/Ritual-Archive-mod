Star Dictator

Command star shikigami whose orbits trace a pseudo-domain: from a lone orbiting light to an Army of the Dictator under a Divine Mandate.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Minor Star Shikigami1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour up to 6 CE: a minor star shikigami per CE for 10 minutes, 7 damage a strike (up to 18 at once).

6th

Major Star Shikigami6 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A major star shikigami for 10 minutes, 36 damage a blow (one at a time; two from 11th). Struck down, it can't be called again for an hour.

3rd

Pseudo-Domain Constitution0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute your stars orbit the point where you look (18 blocks): each 3 s every foe inside the orbit (3 blocks for each star in it) is cut 11 damage per orbiting star (a nimble foe may halve it). Press again to release them.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Divine Mandate10 CE · Maximum (X)

The Army of the Dictator: your star roster fills at once, and for a minute the orbit cuts twice as deep.

Technique feats

3rd

Constellation Network

Every minor star is an eye: a mote shows you each creature within 9 blocks of any of them.

6th

Keystone Star

Your Major Star holds the center: while it stands, the orbit follows it and doesn't break.

11th

Judicial Amputation

A foe that fails badly against your orbit loses a limb: slowed hard and hitting 30% softer for a minute.
