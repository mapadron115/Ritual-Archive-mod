Seven Sacred Coils

Wrap friends and foes in living cursed coils: friendly coils ward, heal and shed ailments; hostile coils constrict, poison and drag.

3arts

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Hostile Coil1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A coil wraps the foe you look at (36 blocks) for a minute, unless it tears free (up to 3 on one foe): every 3 s it's squeezed (26 damage a coil), poisoned, and dragged toward you.

1st

Tighten0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Spend a coil on the foe you look at (36 blocks): 13 damage, and it's held a 3 s unless it tears free. River Venom: 1 CE more poisons it.

1st

Friendly Coil1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A coil wraps the ally you look at (36 blocks), or you, for a minute: blows land a quarter softer (a third from 11th), the bearer mends 6% of their health every 3 s, and ailments slough off.

Passives

1st

Seven Coils

You hold 7 coils in all (or your mastery plus CE power, if more); those on foes come free when they die.

1st

Interpose

Once every 3 s, a blow on an ally wearing your coil is taken by the coil whole: it's spent, and the striker is caught 1 s unless it tears free.

6th

River Venom

While a coil is out you swim swiftly and breathe water.

Domain

18th

Palace Beneath the Sacred River

The river's coils rise: every chosen foe is wrapped by a coil, past your cap, and squeezed (53 damage, poisoned, held 2 s); you and your allies inside are wrapped by friendly coils, and you mend.

1st

Jeweled Hood

While any of your coils is wrapped, a mote shows over every creature within 6 blocks of each bearer, and over those near you.

3rd

Constriction Expert
