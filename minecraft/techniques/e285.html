Starlight Symphony

Mold shining cursed fog, raise fox shikigami, and become the mist itself: a control package crowned by the Fog of Exorcism.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Cursed Fog1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour CE (up to 15): shining fog 3 blocks around you per CE, for a minute, following you; foes inside can't see.

3rd

Kitsune Creation2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A fox shikigami for a day, 14 damage a bite (up to 6).

6th

Living Fog3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Up to an hour: you become a drifting cloud. You fly slowly, can't be seen, and blows pass mostly through you (three-quarters less), but you can't strike. Cast again to take shape.

11th

Inari8 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The great fox Inari for a minute, 53 damage a blow.

6th

Fox Fire3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

With the feat: your fog erupts in a vapor explosion, 89 cold damage to every foe in it, chilled, and the fog is spent.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Fog of Exorcism10 CE · Maximum (X)

The fog detonates down a 18-block cone: 177 cold damage (a hardy foe may halve it).

Technique feats

3rd

Fox in the Mist

While the fog stands, you can keep one more Kitsune out.

6th

Fox Fire

Unlocks the quick key: detonate your fog.
