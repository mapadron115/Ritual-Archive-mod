A counter-flow art: when a blow lands on you, blunt it and channel the residue into your next strike.

Flowing Palm1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Passives

1st

The River Answers

Once every 3 s a technique, magic or a sorcerer's blow on you is caught (1 CE): it lands a third softer, and the residue waits two rounds for your next blow.

6th

Ripples

The residue spreads: each weapon blow carries half of what's held, not all of it.

Technique feats

Still Water
