Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Super Strength0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A fist at the foe in front of you: 9 damage (11 damage in Solar Body; more with World of Cardboard and Sun-Dipped), knocking it far.

3rd

Heat Vision1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 CE and 1 Charge: a 30-block line of heat from your eyes, 33 damage, and it burns: what it lands on catches fire, and ice and snow melt.

6th

Flight1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 CE and 1 Charge: ten minutes of flight. While flying, Technique Key 1 is Super Breath (2 CE): a 10-block gust that blows foes away and puts out fires.

13th

Solar Flare5 CE · Cast (Z)

Spend all your Charge (at least 5): everything within 10 blocks takes 22 damage per Charge.

15th

World of Cardboard1 CE · Cast (Z)

1 CE and 2 Charge, a minute: your fists hit 22 damage harder.

18th

Sun-Dipped8 CE · Cast (Z)

+5 Charge, a minute: fast flight, blows barely touch you, your attacks deal 33 damage more, and you heal every 3 s.

1st

Solar Body1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

1 CE and 1 Charge, a minute: blows land far softer on you, your bare fists hit 11 damage, and you jump much higher.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: World of Cardboard10 CE · Maximum (X)

The day he stops holding back: one blow where you look and a 10-block shockwave, 26 damage per Charge (all of it).

Passives

1st

Solar Charge

Up to 15 Charge. You gain 1 every 10 s under the open daytime sky; each morning you start with at least 5.

11th

Man of Steel

Once every 3 s (1 CE and 1 Charge), a heavy blow is halved.

20th

Last Son

Once a long rest, a blow that would kill you leaves you at half a heart instead (2 CE).

9th

Super Senses

You see in the dark, and every foe within 24 blocks shows through walls.

Technique feats

3rd

Sun-Drinker

Fire is income, not harm: burning can't take hold of you, lava scorches you less, and the first fire to touch you each 3 s banks 1 Charge.

3rd

Faster Than a Bullet

Once every 3 s (1 CE), a blow on an ally within 6 blocks: you're at their side and take it instead, halved while your Solar Body burns.

1st

Hope

Variant + Solar Body (1 CE + 1 Charge): every ally within 9 blocks sheds blindness, darkness, nausea and weakness, and stands firm for 3 s.

6th

Freeze Breath

Super Breath learns winter: foes in the heart of the gust are frozen in place for 3 s and take 27 damage, and water in its path freezes over.
