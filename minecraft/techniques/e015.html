Heart Catch

Two great hands of love at your shoulders: they strike, seize, shield you and charm.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Heart Grasp1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A hand closes on the foe you look at within its reach: held for 3 s (a strong foe may slip free; the hand has the better grip).

1st

Hands of Love1 CE · Quick (G)

Two hands for ten minutes, never more than 18 blocks from you: they strike what comes within 6 blocks of you (14 damage). Calling them again replaces the pair.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Love at First Sight10 CE · Maximum (X)

A foe within a hand's reach is charmed at first sight, no blow needed (a strong will may resist): for a minute it forgets the fight.

Passives

3rd

Handy

The hands hit harder, guard 6 blocks round you and reach 18 blocks from you.

6th

Hand Shield

Once every 3 s, a hand throws itself between a blow and you, or an ally within the hands' reach: it lands at a quarter.

11th

Cutie Honey

Technique Key 1 (5 CE): the next blow a hand lands charms its foe for a minute (a strong will may resist).

Domain
