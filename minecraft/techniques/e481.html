████ and Light

An innate technique that forges killing intent into bars of light: thrown, rained down, and finally ridden as the beam itself.

3arts

11thMaximum

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Light Construct2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A bar of light thrown along your look (18 blocks), through every foe in its path: 18 radiant damage. You flash after it on a hit, and holding pours up to 5 CE for 27 damage more each.

3rd

Rain of Light3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G) · hold to pour

Bars of light fall within 3 blocks of you: 111 radiant damage, and what they hit is pinned 3 s (a nimble foe slips free). They leave real light behind for half a minute. Hold to pour more.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Light Speed8 CE · Maximum (X)

You become the beam: a 60-block line along your look, 199 radiant damage to everything on it, and you arrive at the end.

Passives

6th

Hyperspeed

Light can't burn the hand that holds it: you can't be set burning.

Technique feats

1st

Dazzling Glare

Once every 3 s, a creature within 9 blocks striking you meets a flare of light (2 CE): about half the time its blow misses, and it's dazzled.

3rd

Irradiated Light

Once every 3 s, a construct or rain bar that hits leaves radiation (1 CE): 7 damage more, and the foe is poisoned.
