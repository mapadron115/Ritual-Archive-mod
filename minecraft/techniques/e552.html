The Tithe

Sacrifice is currency: pay in your own blood to strike harder, and make your enemies pay into the plate so you can take the house cut.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

3rd

The Collection Plate2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) pays: 9 damage, 20% softer and slowed for 3 s (a strong will pays half). You take the house cut: some life, or 1 CE if you're whole.

11th

The Revival8 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once a minute: the plate passes to every foe within 18 blocks at once.

1st

The Offering1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Pay a little of your own life (no armor softens it), and your next blow lands 18 damage harder. Made at an altar (an enchanting table or a lectern within 4 blocks), the blessing is doubled.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The Grand Tithe10 CE · Maximum (X)

Pay a great price in life, then regain all of it, shed every ailment and refill your CE. For every foe within 36 blocks, the congregation strikes one of them for 22 damage.
