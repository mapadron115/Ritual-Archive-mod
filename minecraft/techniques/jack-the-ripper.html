Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Malicious Blessing1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Throw a blessed object along your aim (36 blocks): 20 damage. A real object in your off hand is the one thrown: it's spent, and adds 8. A blessing that lands skips on to a second foe within 9 blocks.

6th

Grand Blessing4 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Bless the ground where you look (36 blocks): everything within 12 blocks turns on the foes there, 46 damage each (a nimble foe takes half).

3rd

Prepared Murder1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A blessed trap where you look (36 blocks) springs on the first foe within 6 blocks of it: 33 damage. Up to 12 traps, and each waits ten minutes.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Dear God10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute everything you throw or strike with carries every Murder: twice a second at most, a weapon blow lands 26 damage harder; your blows and blessings leave foes bleeding for 9 s; and thrown blessings fly on through every foe in their path.

Passives

3rd

London Bridge

Once every 3 s, a blow on you finds you a step away: you slip 3 blocks clear and it lands a quarter softer.
