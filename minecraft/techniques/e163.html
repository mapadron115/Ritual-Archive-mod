Red-Eyed Omen

See disasters before they fully exist, stockpile Omens, and spend those warnings to step out of harm or make a foretold opening unavoidable.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Red-Eyed Glare1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The red eyes fix the foe you look at (12 blocks): 18 damage, and its Disaster Clock ticks.

6th

Winged Intercession2 CE · Cast (Z)

Black moth wings for ten minutes: you fly, and when you warn an ally within 12 blocks you fly to them and take the blow yourself.

1st

Omen Sight1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Watch the foe you look at (36 blocks) for a minute: its blows on you yield Omens, and its Disaster Clock fills each 3 s.

Passives

1st

Eyes Before the Storm

A blow about to land on you is foreseen (an Omen and 1 CE): you're already 2 blocks to the side. Up to 11 Omens; a heavy blow, or any from the foe you watch, gives one.

1st

Warning

Once every 3 s, a blow about to land on an ally within 36 blocks is foreseen too (an Omen and 1 CE): they're already 3 blocks aside, and it misses.

3rd

Disaster Clock

The watched foe's Clock fills each 3 s (to 3). At 3, your next blow on it lands half again as hard and the Clock resets.

Domain

18th

Point Pleasant

Red-eyed afterimages of near futures on every surface: every chosen foe's Disaster Clock ticks, and you gain an Omen.
