Shadow Killer

Kill without touching: strike through shadows, bind bodies to their own darkness, and darken the sky itself.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Shadow Strike1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks; in pitch black it has no shadow) is struck through its own shadow: 25 damage, a sure critical on a foe that isn't hunting you, or under the dark sun, or that hasn't been hurt yet. It costs nothing.

1st

Shadow Bind2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at is bound to its shadow for 3 s: in darkness there's no escape; in light it may tear free.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Heavenly Star of Darkness10 CE · Maximum (X)

A dark sun hangs over where you look for a minute (9 blocks): every foe under it takes 27 damage each 3 s, can't see, and is always in shadow. The campfires and candles under it go out.

Passives

6th

Void Steps

You see in the dark, and in darkness creatures more than a few blocks off lose you. Crouched in the dark, you're invisible until you strike, rise or step into light.

11th

Shadows of Mortality

Shadow Strike costs nothing, and is a sure critical on a foe that hasn't been hurt yet.

Technique feats

3rd

Shadow User

Technique Key 1 (1 CE) sinks you into the darkness where you look (18 blocks) and you step out of it there.

6th

Lingering Bindings

Shadow Bind can't be torn free even in light (2 CE more).
