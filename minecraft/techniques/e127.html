Martian

Shapeshifting, Solid and Light density, phasing, telepathy, the True Martian form and Psychic Communion.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Martian Strike0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) and Quick (G)

A blow at the foe in front of you: in Solid form 20 damage (and once every 3 s, 9 damage more); in Light form 11 damage.

6th

Phase2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For 3 s nothing solid can touch you (only shots can), you can't be seen and creatures lose track of you, and you can't strike. Every ally within 2 blocks phases with you.

9th

Mind Link3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute: you read the surface thoughts of the foe you look at (30 blocks; a resolve check). It's weakened, and its blows land a quarter softer on you.

13th

Deep Telepathy5 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Psychic intrusion: the foe you look at is stunned for 3 s (a resolve check).

18th

True Martian6 CE · Cast (Z)

A minute in your true shape, a head taller: you fly fast, your strikes deal 22 damage more, and density changes cost nothing.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Psychic Communion10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every foe within 30 blocks is linked to your mind: 111 damage (a resolve check halves it), and for 3 s you know everything each one intends: they're weakened and their blows land softer.

Passives

1st

Shapeshift

Solid form: heavy fists, and you can't be knocked back.

3rd

Light Density

Technique Key 1: Light form. You fly, fall gently and move a quarter faster, but hit lighter.

15th

Selective Phasing

Once every 3 s (2 CE): a blow that would land passes through you.

Technique feats

6th

Ghost

While Phased, one hand can solidify for a single Martian Strike without ending the Phase.

9th

Detective Mind

While you hold a foe's thoughts through Mind Link, you read its intent: once every 3 s, its blow on you is sidestepped.
