Mythical Beast Amber

Lightning on every blow, charges that discharge as real lightning, and a once-in-a-lifetime Release that grows past human limits and ends in collapse.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

6th

Lightning Discharge1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Lightning falls on the foe you look at (within 64 blocks): 22 damage for every charge on it (at least one's worth), all discharged at once.

11th

Loud Blast0 CE · 5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Released, from its second minute: a blast from your mouth at the foe you look at (within 18 blocks), 66 damage, and 41 damage and a daze to everything within 9 blocks of it.

11th

Electric Beam10 CE · Cast (Z)

Released, from its fifth minute: a huge beam of cursed electricity down a 6-block line, 111 damage, and everything it hits is blinded a moment.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Mythical Beast Amber Release20 CE · Maximum (X)

ONCE IN THIS CHARACTER'S LIFE: your body fills with electric cursed energy for up to five minutes, growing every minute: stronger, faster, quicker blows, and lightning can't hurt you. From the second minute, Loud Blast. From the third, armor of lightning: every blow lands 5 softer. From the fourth, you see every foe within 72 blocks through walls. From the fifth, Electric Beam. Cast it again to end it early. When it ends, the body collapses (weakened for up to half an hour, longer the longer you burned), and it can never be used again.
