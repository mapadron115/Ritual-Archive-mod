Black Flame Apostasy

The godskin hunt: the Apostle's reach or the Noble's mass, black flame that burns late, and a Sabbath swept across the field.

8arts
8passives
4technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

3rd

Black Flame Orb2 CE · Cast (Z)

An orb of black flame along your look (18 blocks): 27 damage, and it Kindles what it hits.

3rd

Ritual Circle3 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute, a 3-block ring of black flame where you stand: every foe that crosses its edge is Kindled. In the Noble's skin its pillars ward you: weapon blows from foes inside the ring land at half.

3rd

Scouring Black Flame3 CE · Cast (Z)

A 9-block cone of black flame: every foe in it is Kindled and blinded for 3 s. A nimble foe may slip it.

6th

Noble Roll2 CE · Cast (Z)

In the Noble's skin, roll 6 blocks along your look through your foes: each one you cross is knocked down for 1 s and dragged 3 blocks with you.

11th

Skinbound Partner8 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute, your other skin stands beside you as a body of its own, with 160 health. Once every 3 s it strikes your foe (25 damage) and Kindles it for nothing. It can be struck down.

11th

Black Flame Tornado7 CE · Cast (Z)

A whirl of black flame: every foe within 4.5 blocks is pulled 3 blocks toward you and Kindled.

1st

Godskin Stance1 CE · Quick (G)

For a minute, wear a skin and its armament. Apostle: a fifth faster, a longer reach and the Twinblade. Noble: your body swells, 6 more armor, nothing knocks you back, and the heavy Stitcher. Technique Key 1 swaps them for free.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Godskin Sabbath10 CE · Maximum (X)

You and a phantom of your other skin sweep 12 blocks along your look: every foe crossed is knocked down and Twin-Kindled (the flame bursts in it twice). A nimble foe is only Kindled.

Passives

1st

Kindling

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow Kindles its foe (1 CE): 3 s later the black flame bursts inside it (53 damage). Until it bursts, the foe can't heal.

1st

Swaddling Recovery

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow wraps you in a little life (6% of your health).

3rd

Twin Detonation

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow on a foe Kindled by your other skin bursts its flame at once and knocks it down for 1 s (1 CE).

6th

Apostle Extension

In the Apostle's skin your reach grows by 1.5 blocks more, and once every 3 s a weapon blow lets you step 3 blocks back from your foe.

11th

Shared Health Rite

While your partner stands, once every 3 s it takes up to 30 of a blow on you.

18th

Resurrection Chant

A fallen partner reforms 6 s later, for 5 CE, unless you're struck while you chant.

20th

True Black Flame

The black flame's burst grows by half.

20th

Gloam Hunt

When you and your partner strike one foe within 3 s, its Kindling bursts at once and it can't heal for 3 s.

Technique feats

1st

Apostle's Reach

In the Apostle's skin, Kindling costs no CE.

3rd

Shared Vestments

While you wear a skin, the ally nearest you within 3 blocks shares its grace: tougher and faster.

6th

Swaddling Cloth

Swaddling Recovery wraps the most hurt ally within 3 blocks instead of you, when they need it more.

11th

Choir of Two

Foes within 9 blocks of your partner glow, as if you saw them through its eyes.
