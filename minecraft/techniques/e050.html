Black Flame Apostasy

The godskin hunt: the Apostle's reach or the Noble's mass, black flame that burns late, and a Sabbath swept across the field.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

3rd

Black Flame Orb2 CE · Cast (Z)

An orb of black flame along your look (18 blocks): 27 damage, and it Kindles what it hits.

3rd

Ritual Circle3 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute, a 3-block ring of black flame where you stand: every foe that crosses its edge is Kindled.

1st

Godskin Stance1 CE · Quick (G)

For a minute, wear a skin. Apostle: a fifth faster and a longer reach. Noble: your body swells, 6 more armor, and nothing knocks you back. Technique Key 1 swaps them for free.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Godskin Sabbath10 CE · Maximum (X)

You and a phantom of your other skin sweep 12 blocks along your look: every foe crossed is knocked down and Twin-Kindled (the flame bursts in it twice). A nimble foe is only Kindled.

Passives

1st

Kindling

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow Kindles its foe (1 CE): 3 s later the black flame bursts inside it (36 damage).

1st

Swaddling Recovery

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow wraps you in a little life (6% of your health).

Technique feats

1st

Apostle's Reach

In the Apostle's skin, Kindling costs no CE.

3rd

Shared Vestments

While you wear a skin, the ally nearest you within 3 blocks shares its grace: tougher and faster.

6th

Swaddling Cloth

Swaddling Recovery wraps the most hurt ally within 3 blocks instead of you, when they need it more.
