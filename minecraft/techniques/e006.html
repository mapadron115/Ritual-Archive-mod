Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Construct1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour 1 to 6 CE into something real (Technique Key 1 chooses). A weapon: a real blade in your hand for 30 s, a diamond sword's blow and 10% harder per CE. It's yours alone: it can't be dropped, and it's gone when its time is up, if you die, or when you build another. Matter: a column of solid stone where you look (within 12 blocks), a block per CE, that crumbles after 10 minutes.

3rd

Cursed Creation3 CE · 5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A cursed item, made for the moment: a random blessing (toughness, mending, speed, strength, a shield, quick hands, life force or a spring in your step) for 10 minutes.

6th

Liquid Metal1 CE · Cast (Z)

Your constructed weapon flows: each use (up to 4) adds half a block of reach and 5% more force, for 30 s.

11th

Insectoid Armor10 CE · Cast (Z)

A minute in insectoid armor: much tougher (+8 armor, +4 toughness), 20% stronger and 30% faster, and blows land 30% lighter.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Perfect Sphere10 CE · Maximum (X)

A perfect sphere nothing can break, moved by a thought for 30 s: it hangs where you look (scroll to set how far, up to 12 blocks) and crushes every foe it touches, 44 damage every 1.5 s.

Domain

20th

Threefold Affliction

A law, not an event: there is no pulse. Inside it every construction costs 2 CE less.
