Amazon Champion

Divine arms and bracelets, the Lasso of Truth, Amazon leaps, flight, Godkiller training and the Godwave Counter.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Divine Weapon0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) and Quick (G)

A sweep of your divine sword: everything in a 3.5-block arc in front of you takes 18 damage. It counts as a weapon blow (Favor and the rest).

3rd

Lasso of Truth1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The golden lasso, 15 blocks: the foe you look at is roped and bound for 6 s (a strength check; bigger foes slip it more). Bound in truth, it fights at half its heart (weakened), and the rope stays tied to you.

15th

Bracelet Shockwave4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

3 Favor: clash your bracelets. A 15-block cone, 77 damage, everything in it thrown far.

18th

Champion of Themyscira6 CE · Cast (Z)

A minute: blows land far softer, you fly fast, your weapon deals 22 damage more, and your Favor can't fall below 1.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Godwave Counter10 CE · Maximum (X)

Spend all your Favor: for 3 s, the next great blow against you is met with crossed bracelets. 98% + 20% of your health of it is stopped, and if that stops it entirely, a 30-block wave of the stopped force goes back the way it came (up to 159 damage).

Passives

1st

Bracelets of Submission

Once every 3 s (1 CE and 1 Favor), a shot that reaches you is struck aside and flies back the way it came, as yours.

1st

Favor

Your first weapon blow each 3 s earns 1 Favor (up to 10).

6th

Amazon Leap

You jump far higher and never take fall damage. After sprinting 20 blocks, your next weapon blow deals 18 damage more.

11th

Flight

You fly, and once every 3 s your weapon deals 9 damage more.

9th

Divine Guard

Once every 3 s (1 CE), a blow on an ally within 3 blocks meets your shield: it lands on you instead, and you step to their side.

13th

Godkiller Training

Against a boss, anything with twice your health or more, or a sorcerer above your level, one blow in ten strikes half again as hard, and your first blow each 3 s on one earns a second Favor.

Technique feats

3rd

Amazon Grappler

A roped foe is hauled up to 7 blocks toward you (1 CE; not a boss).
