Wildspeaker

Call the pack, mark the prey, grow primal yourself, and when the forest answers, it answers all at once.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Beast Call2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A beast of the land answers for a minute (a wolf in the biome's coat, or a polar bear on snow): 18 damage a bite (up to 3). It wears a trait from your trophies. The first call each minute is free.

9th

Primal Might3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute you're a beast yourself: faster, a little larger, and your weapon blows bite 9 damage harder. Your beasts run faster too.

15th

Overrun5 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You and the whole pack charge: every beast strikes its nearest foe for 27 damage, and you dash 9 blocks.

3rd

Hunter's Mark1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at (27 blocks) is your quarry for a minute: it glows for you, and your beasts bite it 8 damage harder.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Caller of the Pack10 CE · Maximum (X)

Five spectral beasts join the hunt for three 3 ss, beyond your usual pack, and charge as one swarm: once a 3 s they strike your quarry (or the nearest foe) for 89 damage (a nimble foe halves it).

Passives

1st

Trophies

Each kind of creature you kill leaves a trophy, and your beasts wear its trait: Flight (fliers), Venom (spiders, bees, witches), Armor (golems, shulkers, the plated), Charge (ravagers, hoglins, goats, horses), Amphibious (sea creatures), Reach (endermen, the tall) or Pack Tactics (wolves, raiders). From 6th, Technique Key 1 picks the trait.

13th

Apex Trophy

A boss, or a creature of 100 health or more, earns an Apex for your lead beast, picked with Variant + Technique Key 1: Breath (a 66 damage cone once a minute), Regeneration, or Huge (larger, longer reach, 9 damage more a bite).

20th

Apex Predator

Three beasts; the first Beast Call each minute is free, and your lead beast wears two trophy traits and the Apex.

Domain

18th

The Great Hunt

Each pulse a stampede of spectral beasts runs a 6-block line from you to the Prey (your quarry, or the nearest chosen foe): every foe in it takes 77 damage and is knocked down. Only the Prey can't halve it.

Technique feats

6th

Pack Bond

Two beasts on one foe combine their attack: each bite deals 7 damage more.

11th

Trample

A beast wearing Charge that bites at the run throws its foe aside (a strong foe holds).

3rd

Primal Hunter

Your quarry can't hide from your beasts: they find it through walls and invisibility, twice as far, and never lose it.

6th

Unnatural Predation

Once a minute, when a creature hurts an ally within 18 blocks of one of your beasts, that beast answers it at once: 18 damage.
