Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

3rd

Ley Line Command3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

On the ground: draw on the earth for a minute. Every blow you take is 8 damage lighter, and a heavy one is braced against (1 CE: half of it). You can't be held, slowed or frozen. It lapses while your feet are off the ground.

6th

Ground Control3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Stomp, and the ground within 18 blocks answers in the way Technique Key 1 picks. Heave: every foe on it is thrown off its feet (held 1 s). Mire: every foe on it is slowed for 6 s. Rampart: a real wall of earth, 7 wide and 2 high, stands up before you for 10 s. Wave: the foe you look at on the ground is carried about 12 blocks the way you look unless it resists, or an ally you look at is, willingly.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Dragon Pulse8 CE · Maximum (X)

Stomp the world: every foe within 36 blocks on the ground, or hanging high above it, takes 111 damage, knocked down and blinded 2 s (some keep their feet: half, no stun). The earth guards you for each one that falls.

Passives

1st

Flow of the Dragon

Feet on the ground: your melee blows add 4 damage (up to three times a second) and nothing knocks you back.

6th

Earth Dragon Soul

While the Ley Line holds, a melee blow draws 27 damage more from the earth once a second. Stand still for a second first and it can do so twice, but the earth then holds you in place for a second.

11th

World Anchor

On the ground, every few seconds the earth guards you against the next 15 damage of harm (replaced, not stacked). Below half health with the Ley Line up: 3 CE for half damage from everything for 6 s.

Domain

18th

Of Primordial World

The ground is the only law: anything inside that flies more than 3 blocks above the ground is crushed down into it for 55 damage (once a second). You are always under the Ley Line here.
