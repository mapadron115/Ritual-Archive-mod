Siphon

Every clean hit is a debt. Steal the cursed energy you run on, wager it in an arena where no one dies, and be the one the crowd pays.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Every foe within 6 blocks is siphoned at once, and takes 36 damage for the wound it leaves.

Passives

1st

Siphon

2 clean blows steal 6 CE. Changing targets keeps the chain, and it ends only after 30 s without a hit. Only a nearly full swing that truly lands counts, or a fully drawn shot. A sorcerer you steal from loses it.

1st

Stolen Reserve

Your own reserve is 5 CE: only that comes back by itself. You can hold 100 stolen CE above it, until you spend it or take a long rest.

3rd

Marked Prey

The foe you siphon becomes your chosen opponent for 6 s: your chain on it needs one blow less, your blows on it land 7 damage harder, and it hits anyone else a quarter softer.

6th

Blood on the Sand

Technique Key 1 in your own arena turns stolen CE into Tribute: it feeds the Pool and tears Gladiator health from the foe, not your reserve.

6th

Colosseum Spoils

Each victory wins a Spoil at random: a real weapon, a piece of arena armor, or an Arena Beast, for 60 s. Weapons can't be dropped or kept. A beast fights by itself and can be struck down. From 18th you win two at once.

11th

Roar of the Crowd

A victory's Encore starts with 5 in the Pool, and Tribute adds your Favor to it.

18th

Emperor of the Living Dead

Once every 3 s a siphon gives you a free blow. Inside your arena a siphon pays you and the Pool both, and an Encore starts with 10 plus your last Favor.

Domain

6th

Amphitheater of the Living Dead

Needs 3 stolen CE, then costs 6. Everyone inside, you too, fights on 100 Gladiator health, which takes all damage instead of their real health. At nothing they're sent to the stands, alive and out of the fight. Every 3 s each competitor pays 3 CE into the Pool, and whoever can't pay loses Gladiator health. The Pool lifts your Favor, which hardens your weapon. Last one standing: you cash the Pool out, with no Burnout, and take a title, the crown and a Spoil. If anyone else is last, or time runs out, the Pool is lost.
