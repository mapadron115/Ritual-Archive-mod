Faceless God

Infect minds with escalating Madness and Corruption; even the user is not exempt from the madness they spread.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Madness Inducement5 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every foe within 36 blocks that can see you takes 10 Madness (a strong will may resist, and is then safe from it for a day). At 10 a creature goes berserk: it turns on whatever is nearest it and wears itself down. Madness fades a minute after it was last fed.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Rumination5 CE · Maximum (X)

The foes within 9 blocks glimpse the Faceless God: 15 Corruption each. Every 3 s each one either resists or takes 11 damage per 10 Corruption and sinks deeper.

Passives

6th

Obsession and Insanity

Madness is contagious: each 3 s, a berserk foe gives 5 Madness to the foes within 9 blocks of it.

Domain

18th

Theia Mania

Every chosen foe takes 10 Madness. If more than half of them are berserk or corrupted, the whole Domain screams: 111 damage (221 damage to those who break).

Veil of the Faceless God

While any foe carries your Madness, you sense every creature within 16 blocks: a mote shows over each.

Hands of Madness
