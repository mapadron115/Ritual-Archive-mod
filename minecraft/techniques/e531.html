The Downward Stair

Open a stairwell that descends impossibly far. Something knocks from below; use sparingly.

3arts
1passives
3technique feats
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Descend3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A stairwell opens at your feet for 10 minutes (two at a time). Technique Key 1 (1 CE) moves the newest one's mouth to the ground you look at.

18th

The Bottom2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest: a foe trapped in your stairwell falls through the bottom, 68 damage, and surfaces far away (a strong will clings on).

1st

Go Down0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Go down the stairwell you stand at, or come back up: below, nothing can see or harm you, but you can't act.

Passives

6th

The Trap

A foe that steps onto an open mouth falls in: 41 damage, and it's stuck climbing out for 6 s.

Technique feats

1st

Down Only

Within 3 blocks of your stairwell, nothing can knock you back.

1st

Watch Your Step

Near your stairwell you move silently: creatures more than 6 blocks off don't notice you.

3rd

Emergency Exit

You can keep one more stairwell open.
