Directive Arrows

Write glowing arrows into the air, ride their instructions at impossible speed, and turn a carefully drawn route into one decisive strike.

6arts
7passives

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Directive Inscription0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 CE inks 6 arrows. Each cast hangs one where you look (24 blocks), and the one before it turns to point at it; hold Variant to point it along your look. Up to 18 hang at once, for a minute.

3rd

Acceleration Sphere0 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

6 CE: you ride 6 legs around the foe you look at (12 blocks), striking it on each for 22 damage plus Vector Edge.

6th

Imposed Directive0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Toggle. Once every 3 s, a weapon blow spends 2 CE to drive the foe 8 blocks away from you; a wall in the way turns it into a collision (41 damage, stopped 1.5 s).

6th

Acceleraise Boost2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The ally you look at (24 blocks) is faster for 3 s, and once in it, after riding at least one of your arrows, their weapon blow adds 11 damage per arrow ridden (up to 3).

1st

Erase Route0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Clear your arrows and start a new chart. Inside your own Domain: ride a virtual arrow along your look instead.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Dominion of Borrowed Speed10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute, an 8-block field moves with you: foes in it are slowed and you gain their speed, and every 3 s 6 free arrows bloom around you.

Passives

1st

Arrowpath

Touching an arrow carries you 8 blocks along it, in any direction; passing through another chains into its heading. 6 rides for 3 s.

1st

Vector Edge

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow adds 11 damage for every arrow ridden in the last few seconds, up to 6.

3rd

Quick Inscription

A weapon blow drops a free arrow at your feet, pointing along your look (6 a 3 s).

6th

Projectile Redirection

A hostile shot passing within 2 blocks of an arrow is bent along it and becomes yours (1 CE, spends the arrow).

11th

Persistent Line

Once every 3 s, an arrow you ride stays.

18th

Routebreaker

After a chain of 3 or more arrows, a free strike on the foe nearest the end: 66 damage.

6th

Shared Route

Your allies ride your arrows too, 6 a round each, with no Vector Edge of their own.

Domain

18th

Palace of the Predestined Road

Every point is an arrow: your arrows never fade, rides are unlimited and the quick key rides along your look. Every chosen foe is driven down the road you drew, and breaks on what it hits (66 damage, held 1.5 s).

Technique feats

6th

Rescue Vector

Once every 3 s, 2 CE and the arrow: an ally struck within 3 blocks of one of your arrows is carried along it, and a melee blow on them misses.

6th

Double Bank

A shot you've redirected bends a second time at another arrow in its line, for 1 CE more.

6th

Shared Vector Edge

Once every 3 s, 1 CE: an ally's weapon blow after they ride your arrows adds 11 damage per arrow ridden (up to 6).

1st

Snare Glyph

Your first 6 arrows are snares too: a foe that steps into one is held 1.5 s (a strong one tears free).
