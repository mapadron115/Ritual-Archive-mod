Cursed Cannon

A one-man battery: beams that blow holes in the world, a shot that splits, a shell that hunts, and a held nuclear charge.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Granite Blast1 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A beam of cursed energy along your look (30 blocks), through every foe on it: 133 damage. It blows a hole where it lands and kicks you back. Once every 2 s. Hold Variant as you fire: Grape Shot (1 CE more), split among up to 6 foes near where you aim. Hold the key a second, then release: Guided Shell (1 CE more), a shell that hunts the foe you looked at.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Nuclear Shell10 CE · Maximum (X) · hold to pour

Hold the key 4.5 s to build a loud, obvious charge, then release: a 30-block cone of Granite Blast with 55 damage more. Hold Variant as you release: the Antimatter Bomb, all of it within 20 blocks around you instead. Let go early and it fizzles, free.
