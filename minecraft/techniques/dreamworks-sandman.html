Golden Slumber

Golden dreamsand that whips, guards and soothes, and puts every nightmare on the field to sleep.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Dreamsand Whip2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A whip of golden sand along your look (18 blocks): 8 damage to everything along it. The sand lights the dark round you for a minute.

3rd

Good Dreams3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A good dream wraps you: a golden ward that soaks 18% of your health in blows, and fear, weakness and nausea lift. With Variant, it wraps the ally you look at (18 blocks) instead.

11th

Dreamcraft4 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A manta of golden sand glides beside you for a minute and lashes your foes, 15 damage once every 3 s. It can be struck down.

1st

Lullaby Sand3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) falls asleep for 3 s, and any blow wakes it (a steady mind resists; a great foe only drowses).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The Golden Dawn10 CE · Maximum (X)

Golden sand rises like sunrise: every foe within 36 blocks falls asleep as under the Lullaby (a steady mind drowses instead), and you're warded.
