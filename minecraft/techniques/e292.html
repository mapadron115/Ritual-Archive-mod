Mister Fantastic

A rubber body with long reach, wraps and holds, reshaped limbs, Colossus Coil and the Infinite Coil.

4arts
8passives
3technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Elastic Strike0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your fist stretches 5 blocks at the foe you look at: 16 damage, plus 9 damage per Stretch (mallet fists hit 25 damage; whip-limbs reach 10 and the foe can't react for a moment).

3rd

Wrap & Hold2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Wrap a foe within reach: held fast for 3 s (a strong one may break free), and you gain 1 Stretch.

18th

Colossus Coil4 CE · Cast (Z)

Coil around up to 6 foes within reach: held, and each takes 27 damage every 3 s for three.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Infinite Coil10 CE · Maximum (X)

A minute: your body unfurls into a 20-block web of bands where you look. Foes in it are held each 3 s (a strong one may break free) and squeezed for 27 damage.

Passives

1st

Rubber Body

Blows from foes land softer, and your reach is longer.

1st

Stretch

Wraps and soaked blows wind it; 3 s with neither unwinds 1. At Stretch 5+ your holds can't be broken and you reach a block farther. At Stretch 10 every foe you hold is squeezed each 3 s, but blows on you land 20% harder. Variant + Elastic Strike releases 2 (free).

9th

Contortionist

A hold on you slips off within half a second.

13th

Living Shield

Once every 3 s (2 CE), a blow on an ally within 4.5 blocks lands at half as you wrap them.

20th

Apex Plasticity

Stretch never falls below 3.

6th

Kinetic Soak

A blow that lands on you is reduced by 7 damage more, and feeds 1 Stretch (once every 3 s).

11th

Malleable Arsenal

Technique Key 1 (1 CE, a minute): mallet fists, whip-limbs or a parachute (no fall damage, a slow glide), in turn.

15th

Soak & Rebound

The damage a blow did to you is added to your next Elastic Strike (up to your level).

Technique feats

3rd

Snapback

A foe that breaks your Wrap takes 18 damage as the limb snaps back, and you try the hold once more.

1st

Reed's Calculus

Variant + Technique Key 1 (6 times a long rest): the creature you look at is analyzed, its health and armor in chat, and your next blow on it within 3 s hits 25% harder.

3rd

Elastic Rebound

Once every 3 s, a shot that hits you meets a catching membrane (2 CE): it lands much softer.
