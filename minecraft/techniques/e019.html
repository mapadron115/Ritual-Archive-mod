Passives

1st

The Southern Cross

The stars by distance: Imai, Acrux, Mimosa, Ginan, Gacrux. Bearers of the same star within 8 blocks are drawn together (the one with more cursed energy pulls) and collide, each hurt by the other's size. A bearer can only approach the next star in the order; any other star within 3 blocks pushes it away. You bear Acrux: only an Imai bearer can reach you.

3rd

Effective Marking

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow sets the chosen star on its target for free.

6th

Gravity

Collisions ignore armor.

18th

Universal Force

Collisions become pure force: half again as hard.

Domain

18th

Star Crossed Lovers

Every chosen foe is given your chosen star, whatever a star can usually hold, and its bearers are hurled into each other. One alone is slammed down for 22 damage.
