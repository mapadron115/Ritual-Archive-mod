Destined Death

The rune of death: every clean blow brands a Fate Scar, and the scarred take more from you until they can't heal at all.

Arts

1st

Black Blade Wave1 CE · Cast (Z)

A black crescent flies along your look (8 blocks), through everything in its way: 18 damage and a Fate Scar on each.

1st

Final Black Blade10 CE · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest, on a foe within 12 blocks bearing 5 scars: a foe 10 levels under you, or 5 to 9 under and already low (half its health at 5, more as the gap grows), player or creature but never a boss, it dies. Anything else, or a strong will, is stunned for 3 s.

11th

Black Blade Eclipse8 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute every weapon blow brands a Fate Scar for nothing, but the healing you receive is halved.

Maximum: Thousand Black Blades10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every foe within 6 blocks is scarred twice and knocked down (a nimble foe takes one scar and keeps its feet).

Passives

1st

Deathbrand

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow brands its foe with a Fate Scar (1 CE). Scars last a minute, and up to 5 stack. Each takes 10.0% of the foe's max health away while it lasts (never below 10% of it), and cuts its healing by a tenth, half at most. The bar comes back as the scars go; the health doesn't. You strike no harder.

1st

Beast Step

A weapon blow on a scarred foe springs you 3 blocks back from it.

6th

Mortal Wound

A foe with 3 scars or more can't heal past them: whatever would heal it clears a scar instead, Reverse Cursed Technique included.

11th

Death Denies Death

A scarred foe saved from death (a totem, an art that cheats death) keeps a scar, and the minute starts over.

20th

True Destined Death

Five scars, a tenth of the bar each, down to a tenth of it, and the Final Black Blade.

1st

Death in Motion

You jump higher.

Domain
