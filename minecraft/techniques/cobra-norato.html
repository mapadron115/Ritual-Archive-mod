The River That Walks

The great serpent of the Amazon: own the crossings, drag what wades into the deep, and become the Cobra Grande itself.

4arts
2passives

18thMaximum

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Coil and Strike2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A serpent's lunge at the foe you look at (12 blocks): 25 damage, and it's dragged toward you (42 damage as the Cobra Grande).

11th

The Flood Comes Up8 CE · 12 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute 36 blocks round where you look (54 blocks) floods: it counts as water for everything you do, and foes in it are slowed.

3rd

The Drowned Rise3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Up to three drowned shades rise under foes in water within 36 blocks (or the three nearest, if none): 14 damage each, and they're dragged down, held for 1.5 s.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Cobra Grande10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you are the great serpent: nothing holds or shoves you, you swim through everything, blows on you land a quarter softer, and Coil and Strike bites twice as deep. Once a long rest.

Passives

1st

The Deep Place

In water you breathe and swim freely, and blows on you land a third softer.

3rd

The River Decides

Once every 3 s, while you're in water, the nearest foe within 12 blocks is pulled under: dragged toward you and slowed (a strong foe is only slowed).
