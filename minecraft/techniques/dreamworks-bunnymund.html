Warrior of Spring

Tunnels under every battlefield, boomerangs that always come home, and painted war-eggs that don't hatch anything nice.

3arts
2passives
0technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

3rd

Twin Boomerangs2 CE · 1.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Two boomerangs along your look, 24 blocks out and home again, cutting through everything: 9 damage on the way out and 5 damage on the way back.

1st

The Warren Below1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

From the ground, dive under for a moment (untouchable) and burst out where you look, up to 36 blocks away: 5 damage to every foe within 6 blocks of the hole, thrown upward.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The Great Eruption10 CE · Maximum (X)

You dive and erupt under up to six foes within 72 blocks in turn: 21 damage to every foe within 6 blocks of any of them, thrown upward. You come up at the last.

Passives

6th

War-Eggs

Technique Key 1 sets a war-egg where you look (3 CE, 18 blocks; up to 6, five minutes). A sentry spits yolk at a foe within 24 blocks once every 1.5 s, 5 damage, 5 times, then cracks. A bomb bursts when a foe comes within 6 blocks: 27 damage. Variant + Key 1 switches which you set next.

18th

The Burrow Remembers

Once a long rest, a blow that would fell you dives you to safety with a sliver of health.
