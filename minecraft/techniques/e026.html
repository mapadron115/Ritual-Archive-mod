Ratio Technique

Every body has a weak point at seven to three: criticals that come more often and cut through armor, a ground-splitting Collapse, and Overtime.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Ratio1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Arm the 7:3: your next melee blow within 3 s gets two tries (hold to pour 1 CE more per extra try, up to 5); each has a 1 in 5 chance of a critical that lands half again as hard, cuts through armor and carries 9 damage more. (Golden Ratio: every blow already is.)

6th

Collapse4 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Strike the ground where you look (within 12 blocks): it caves in 6 blocks around, its surface cracking, shaking and then falling away (nothing harder than ore, and nothing that holds anything). Everything there takes 25 damage and wades through rubble for 30 s.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Overtime10 CE · Maximum (X)

A minute past the end of the workday: your blows land half again as hard, and Ratio costs nothing.

Passives

1st

Critical Focus

One blow in ten finds a weak point on its own: half again as hard, and 9 damage more.

18th

Golden Ratio

Every melee blow is a 7:3 critical, without rolling or spending.

Technique feats

3rd

Measured Guard

You read incoming blows too: once every 3 s, a blow on you tries the 7:3 for 1 CE: 1 time in 5, it's halved.

11th

Fatal Ratio

A 7:3 critical takes 2 CE more and cuts at a limb: the foe is slowed and weakened for 5 s.
