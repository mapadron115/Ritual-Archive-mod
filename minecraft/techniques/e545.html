Develop0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Replay your oldest captured moment on the foe you look at (27 blocks): the captured blow lands again, on it. Two at once (Contact Sheet).

Exposure1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute your lens sees the truth: you see in the dark, and creatures within 16 blocks and ore within 10 show to you alone.

3rd

Flash2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)
