Grave-Mound Tenacity

Choose a Grave Anchor. Trespassers, and those who flee your reach, feed Restlessness that fuels resistance and retaliation.

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Barrow Strike1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (3 blocks): 18 cold damage, and 9 damage more for each Restlessness you spend (all of it).

3rd

Mound-Dweller2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute a Grave Mound rises round your Anchor: the guarded ground is 9 blocks, and foes on it are mired.

6th

Corpse-Swell2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute the corpse swells: half again your size, 3 blocks more reach, and a foe in your reach that is snatched or blinks away is dragged back unless it breaks your grip.

1st

Grave Anchor1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Your Anchor rises where you stand: 6 blocks of guarded ground. On it, blows land 20% softer.

Passives

1st

Restlessness

Once every 3 s each: a foe stepping onto your guarded ground, a foe tramping 6 blocks about inside it, and each foe slipping out of your reach. Up to 6.

1st

Restless Corpse

Once every 3 s, a foe's weapon blow or shot of a tenth of your health or more spends 1 Restlessness and lands at half. A weapon blow of yours with Variant held spends 1 for 9 cold damage more.

11th

Too Angry to Die

Once a long rest, on your guarded ground: a blow that would kill you spends all your Restlessness (2 at least) and 4 CE instead. You stand at 1 health and strike the nearest foe in reach for 18 damage.

Domain

18th

Barrow That Refuses the Dawn

The barrow closes round your Anchor, and all of it is your guarded ground: every chosen foe is held by grave-hands (36 damage, 1.5 s), and one more than 9 blocks from the Anchor is dragged toward it and feeds Restlessness.

1st

Corpse Weight

Swollen, or full of Restlessness, nothing can knock you back.

3rd

Anchor's Pull

A foe slipping out of your reach can be escorted back (1 Restlessness): it's hurled toward your Grave Anchor (a strong foe may hold).

3rd

Burial Goods

Variant + Grave Anchor buries a second Anchor where you stand. Technique Key 1 (1 CE) moves your guarded ground to the other one.
