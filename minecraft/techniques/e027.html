Shrine

Invisible cuts at range, the adaptive Cleave, Divine Flame, a perfect form, and Malevolent Shrine: a Domain with no barrier at all.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Dismantle1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

An invisible cut flies 30 blocks straight where you look, through walls, into the first thing it meets. Tap for 1 CE (9 damage); hold to pour up to 6 CE (53 damage at full). Hold Variant as you release: Spiderweb, the cut fans out across the 6 blocks in front of you, into everything there (+1 CE).

1st

Dismantle on the Swing0 CE · Cast (Z)

Toggle. While it's on, each swing of your arm also sends a Dismantle where you look (1 CE, 9 damage), once a second, two at a time with Perfect Form.

3rd

Cleave1 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z) and Quick (G) · hold to pour

The adaptive cut. Hold to pour 1–10 CE, then the foe nearest your crosshair within 30 blocks takes 77 damage to 177 damage. It reads the target's structure for a minute: the next Cleave on it cuts clean through armor.

6th

Divine Flame: Open1 CE · Cast (Z) and Technique Key 1 (C) · hold to pour

The one thing you can see coming. Hold to pour 1–12 CE, then release a fire arrow where you look (up to 60 blocks): it bursts 1 block wide per CE, burning everything in it for 159 damage at full pour, and sets it alight. The blast burns a crater into the world and leaves the ground on fire.

Maximum

18th

World Cutter15 CE · Maximum (X) · hold to pour

Hold the key through the whole chant (1.5 s); let go early and it breaks, free. A cut through space itself, 40 blocks where you look, through walls: 177 damage to everything on the line, through armor. The world along it is cut too.

Passives

1st

Invisible

Only you can see a Dismantle in flight; it passes through anything between you and where you point.

11th

Perfect Form

+15% attack speed, +5% melee damage, +2 hearts. Pouring CE takes half as long, and your swings Dismantle twice.

Domain

18th

Malevolent Shrine

A Domain with no barrier: nothing is held in and there's nothing to break, but it reaches 72 blocks. Every 3 s it cuts every foe it chose that's still inside: a full Dismantle (53 damage) and a full Cleave (177 damage, through armor on a foe already read).
