Sunshine: Pride Incarnate

A furnace in the chest that grows with the sun. Cruel Sun, Pride Flare, Super Slash, and at true noon, The One. At 18th, a fatal vow refuses the end.

10arts
9passives
3technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Cruel Sun1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour 1 to all your CE: a miniature sun flies 120 blocks where you look and bursts on what it meets, 16 damage at night to 33 damage at noon for one CE, a Solar Die more for each CE poured. Hold a second past full for Maximum Output: the sun stays 10 s, following your aim (scroll to set how far out), and burns what it touches every 1.5 s. Pride Flare sets it off early.

6th

Crazy Prominence2 CE · Cast (Z)

Your Mastery in CE (6): 6 homing suns among the foes within 120 blocks, 14 damage at night to 40 damage at noon each.

6th

Super Slash2 CE · Cast (Z)

Your Mastery in CE (6): surge forward 8 blocks, striking everything in your path 6 times with your weapon's force.

11th

Divine Sword5 CE · Cast (Z)

The One only: a 6-block cone of sunlight, 103 damage, through armor.

11th

Divine Spear5 CE · Cast (Z)

The One only: a 4-block thrust into the first foe, 93 damage, through armor, and it's stunned for a second.

1st

Solar Memory0 CE · Cast (Z)

The rite: stand still out of a fight for 10 s under a Low, High or Near-Noon sun to store it. Release (2 CE): for a minute you burn as if under the stored sun, if it's higher.

1st

Lion's Heat0 CE · Cast (Z)

Toggle. Once every 3 s, a melee blow spends 1 CE for 14 damage at night to 40 damage at noon more (as The One, 2 blows every 3 s).

18th

Refuse the End0 CE · Cast (Z)

While The One: the fatal vow, The One Ultimate. It is noon for you whatever the hour, but your max health falls every 6 s, and when it ends, so do you. Press again to let the noon go.

18th

Final Prominence15 CE · Cast (Z)

The One Ultimate only: all your CE (15 at least) into a final sun that falls where you look, 30 blocks of burning noon, 463 damage and more for every CE beyond 15. Then the end.

3rd

Pride Flare / Sun-Rider2 CE · Quick (G)

Pride Flare while a Cruel Sun is in the air, held, or just landed (3 s): it detonates, 23 damage at night to 66 damage at noon in a burst that widens with the sun; a held sun goes up far bigger. Otherwise Sun-Rider: ride solar fire 6 blocks (more as the sun climbs) where you look, untouchable.

Passives

1st

Solar Station

Your sun hits harder as the day climbs: Night (weakest), Low Sun, High Sun, Near-Noon (11:00 to 14:00), Zenith (12:00 to 13:00, strongest).

1st

Pride Incarnate

Your melee hits harder and knocks back further as the sun climbs, and when a fight begins in daylight your pride shields you (absorption, more as the sun climbs).

1st

The Body Rises with the Sun

Each step of the sun: a little faster on foot, higher jumps, and quicker mining.

3rd

Sun-Wrought Stature

High Sun and up: heavier knockback. Near-Noon and up: you reach further with your blows.

6th

Solar Sovereignty

By day nothing can set you burning, and flames don't hurt you.

6th

Prideful Endurance

On its own, once every 3 s (2 CE): a heavy blow is blunted by your Solar Dice.

11th

Solar Authority

Cruel Sun reaches 60 blocks and takes twice the CE; Prominence suns hit harder; your Sunshine cuts through armor.

11th

The One

At Zenith: half damage taken, immune to fire and knockback, faster, +50% attack speed, Lion's Heat on 2 blows every 3 s, and a great shield of pride.

18th

Unbounded Sunshine

Cruel Sun reaches 120 blocks with no limit on the CE poured; Prominence suns hit harder still.

Domain

18th

Throne Beneath a Merciless Noon

Inside it is always noon: Zenith, and The One. Every 3 s, Sovereign Pressure bears down on each foe it chose, 113 damage, crushing it to the ground and pinning it there.

Technique feats

1st

Night-Prowling Lion

The sun's weakest hour is the lion's: at night creatures more than 8 blocks off never notice you, and your first blow every 3 s lands harder.

6th

Bestowed Memory

Solar Memory's other form gives the rite to the ally beside you (4 blocks), holding a weapon: the stored sun goes into the weapon, not you. They release it once, with Variant + use, for 2 CE: for a minute its blows burn with that sun (18 damage at High Sun, once every 3 s) and set the foe alight. You can't release it yourself.

3rd

Corona of Command

Technique Key 1 (3 CE, with the sun High or higher): a corona pulse; every foe within 3 blocks is blinded for 3 s.
