Gate of the Protective Colossus

Designate thresholds: hostile crossings generate Vigilance that lets the guardian intercept and, at last, account for every crossing.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Raise Threshold1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A gate stands where you look (18 blocks), facing the way you look, for 10 minutes (up to 6). A foe that walks through it takes 18 damage once every 3 s, is slowed for 3 s and shown for 3 s, and unless it resists is stopped for 1 s and takes 18 damage more. The first crossing every 3 s gives you a Vigilance (up to 6; it's lost when no gate of yours stands).

6th

Five-Legged Step2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Standing within 3 blocks of one of your gates, you stand at another: the one nearest your look, within 72 blocks.

1st

Intercept1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Spend all Vigilance: you're beside the foe you look at (18 blocks), guarded for 3 s (blows on you land at half), and you strike it for 9 damage per Vigilance spent (up to 6).

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Gate No Enemy Crosses Uncounted10 CE · Maximum (X)

Four great gates rise in a square around you for a minute: crossing one costs a foe 53 damage and holds it 2 s, and no foe's shot passes through them.

Passives

3rd

Bull-Lion Intercession

Once every 3 s, for a Vigilance: when a foe's blow lands on an ally within 18 blocks, you and the ally change places and the blow lands on you. Within 6 blocks of one of your gates, it lands at half.

6th

Five-Legged Perspective

Every creature within 6 blocks of one of your gates is shown to you alone.

11th

Name on the Gate

Allies who walk through your gates are quickened for 3 s; a stranger who fails to resist a gate takes 18 damage more.

Domain

18th

Gate No Enemy Crosses Uncounted

Four great gates stand 12 blocks from its heart while it's open. Every chosen foe is counted: 36 damage, held 2 s, and a Vigilance for you. One that walked through any gate of yours since the last count takes 18 damage more.
