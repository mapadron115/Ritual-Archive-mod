Dreadhorde

Keep the dead as an account: raise zombies from the graveyard, sell the fallen twice, and spend everything on a single Dreadhorde army.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Raise Dead2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A zombie rises from the ground you stand on (a drowned from water, a husk from sand). From a Corpse card it lasts 10 minutes and strikes for 25 damage; with no cards, a feeble one for a minute. Up to 12 at once.

11th

Mass Reanimation5 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Up to 6 zombies at once (Corpse cards make them stronger).

6th

Raise Wight3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 Corpse card: an elite Wight rises, twice a zombie's health, striking for 34 damage. It lasts 10 minutes. Wights take no place under your cap.

3rd

Dark Bargain0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Sacrifice your nearest undead: 1 CE back, or below half health, some life instead. Its corpse bursts for 18 damage on foes beside it.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Rise of the Dreadhorde10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every Corpse card becomes one great Army for a minute: its blow is 33 damage for every 3 cards spent (up to 4 times; at least once).

Passives

1st

Graveyard

Every foe you kill adds a Corpse card (up to 32).

6th

Death's Majesty

Your undead hit harder.

9th

Diabolic Tutor

Technique Key 1: 2 Corpse cards for 4 CE, once every 5 minutes.

13th

Contract of Four Demons

Variant + Technique Key 1 chooses a pact until your next long rest (a few seconds to settle on it): the Legion's, 2 more undead; the Wound's, your dead hit a foe under half health a fifth harder; the Feast's, the Bargain heals 4d8; the Iron's, your dead's blows pierce armor.

20th

Death Cannot Hold Me

Once a long rest, 4 Corpse cards keep you at 1 health when you'd die.

Domain

18th

The Chain Veil

Each pulse, every chosen foe within 2 blocks of one of your undead takes 66 damage, and the grave recruits at the scene: a 1-health zombie of yours rises beside each one struck, until the next pulse.
