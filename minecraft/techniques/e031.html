Straw Doll Technique

Nails, a hammer and a straw doll: nails that bind a foe to the doll, Resonance that strikes it from anywhere, and hairpins that wait to go off.

5arts
2passives
3technique feats
11thMaximum

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Nail Projectile1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Two nails hammered where you look (24 blocks), 14 damage for the first on each foe, 14 damage for each after. Hold for two more (3 CE more).

3rd

Resonance3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Strike the straw doll and use up its piece: the foe bound to it takes it wherever it is. A minor piece: 41 damage, and it fights weaker for 3 s. A medium piece: 98 damage, and its technique is shut for 1.5 s. A major piece: 177 damage, and it's held for 3 s. Its max health falls by as much as the blow took, to half at most, for five minutes.

6th

Hairpin1 CE · Cast (Z)

Plant up to 6 nails where you look (1 CE each beyond the first). Technique Key 1 detonates every planted hairpin: 14 damage per nail to every foe within 2 blocks of it.

11th

Improved Resonance6 CE · Cast (Z)

The foe within arm's reach that's held, bound or stunned: knocked down, 53 damage, and its cursed energy suppressed for a minute: half a sorcerer's casts fizzle, their CE spent, and a creature fights weaker.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Last Nail in the Coffin10 CE · Maximum (X)

(The Last Nail feat) While a foe is bound to the doll: every planted hairpin tears loose, streaks to it wherever it is and goes off as one, 14 damage per nail, and it's held for 3 s. The link ends.

Passives

1st

Nail Pouch

20 nails; one comes back every 3 s.

3rd

Resonance Link

A wound you deal with a blade, a nail or an arrow takes a piece of the foe for your doll (3 CE), if no other foe is bound: minor under a quarter of its max health, medium under half, major from half. A bigger wound on the same foe takes a bigger piece (3 CE more). The link lasts a minute, and a red thread shows it.

Domain

18th

Grand Effigy Rite

Every 3 s, every foe it chose is nailed: 89 damage, and bound to an effigy for 1.5 s.
