Contact Decay

Whatever the hand touches should not exist: Decay Marks that pile up and crumble, blocks that tremble and fall to dust, and ruin that spreads through the ground itself.

3arts

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Five-Finger Decay1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Touch the foe you look at (3 blocks): 13 damage and a Decay Mark (two at 20th); at 3 Marks it Crumbles. Touch a block instead and it trembles and falls to dust, and the decay runs through up to 60 blocks joined to it.

11th

Awakening6 CE · 5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Hand to the ground: ruin runs 72 blocks through it (36 at 13th, 54 at 15th, 72 at 20th), and the nearest 20 blocks of its surface tremble and crumble. Everything standing on that ground, not in the air or in water, takes 66 damage and 2 Marks, and is knocked down for 2 s.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Deika Collapse10 CE · Maximum (X)

48 blocks of ground unmade, two layers deep, and the ruin runs 90 blocks: everything standing on that ground takes 132 damage, 3 Marks, and is knocked down for 2 s. A closed Domain's barrier in the ruin takes its hardest blow. Recoil: you lose 53 damage (never below half a heart), and an arm breaks (Weakness for a minute).

Passives

1st

Decay Marks

Up to 3 on a foe; they fade 30 s after the last if it survives.

1st

Touch Goes Both Ways

Whatever hits you with its body has touched your hand: 13 damage and a Mark (1 CE, once every 3 s).

3rd

Crumbling Hold

A marked foe that's held, within 3 blocks of you, gains a Mark every 3 s.

1st

Crumble

A foe that reaches 3 Marks Crumbles: 2 CE (free from 15th) for 66 damage (more as you master it), and its Marks clear. Without the CE, it Crumbles at the next Mark.

9th

Four Fingers

Every 3 s, a marked foe touching another passes a Mark to it (1 CE).

15th

Avalanche

Awakening also knocks its victims down.

Technique feats

6th

Chain Reaction

A foe your Decay reduces to nothing passes a Mark to everything touching it.

11th

Ruined City

You shape the ruin as you make it: Awakening also heaps a rubble rampart where you look (1 CE), 3 wide and 2 high, which decays in its turn after a minute. With Variant it opens a pit there instead.

1st

Dustproof

Your Decay knows its maker: Deika's recoil no longer hurts you (the arm still breaks), and with Variant, Awakening or Deika carries you up to 6 blocks along your look, riding the crumble.

6th

Selective Hand

Variant + Five-Finger Decay stills the hand: Touch Goes Both Ways stops until you do it again.
