Auspicious Beast Summon

Four beasts: a golden horn that follows your blows, dark water that carries you, Kirin's endurance, and Ryu, a dragon whose lightning you command.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Kaichi1 CE · Cast (Z)

Summon Kaichi's golden drill horn, which stays until you dismiss it (your art's other form). Cast it at the foe you look at (30 blocks), or land a weapon blow, and the horn flies at that foe, through walls, and doesn't stop until it strikes or 5 s pass: 20 damage, once every 3 s.

3rd

Reiki1 CE · Cast (Z)

Dark water carries you: twice as fast for 3 s. On its own, once every 3 s (2 CE), a blow about to land may be turned aside (half the time).

6th

Kirin1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour 1 to 6 CE: Kirin's endurance shields you, 5% of your health per CE, but you stand rooted for 3 s.

11th

Ryu10 CE · Cast (Z)

Summon Ryu, the dragon, for an hour: it flies beside you and fights. Technique Key 1 commands its lightning breath (once every 3 s): 53 damage down a 12-block line. If it falls, it's gone until a long rest.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Kirin's Flames10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute Kirin's endurance burns inside out: every blow that reaches you returns as fire on whoever struck it, three times as hard.

Domain

18th

Auspicious Beasts' Realm

A law, not an event: inside, every beast answers for 2 CE less, and Kaichi strikes with every blow.
