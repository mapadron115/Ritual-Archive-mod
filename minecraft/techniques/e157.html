Corrosive Threshold

Infect surfaces and prey with Corrosion, turn the stains into doors, and drag degraded targets into a private hunting space.

4arts
2passives
4technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Corroding Touch1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you touch (2.5 blocks): 16 damage of rot and a Corrosion Mark (up to 3, each slowing it, a minute after the last). Dragging Hand (6th): with Variant, on a foe with 2 or more Marks, for 3 CE more, it has to resist or you both sink below for 3 s, where nothing touches either of you; you rise at your stain nearest your look (within 36 blocks) with it beside you. Never a boss.

1st

Slough1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks), with 2 or more Corrosion Marks, rots open: 7 damage a Mark, and its Marks are spent. If it resists it takes half and keeps them (resisting is harder at 3).

11th

Rotting World6 CE · 12 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you reach for (6 blocks), with 3 Corrosion Marks, is dragged down into your pocket dimension for 3 s. It rots there and comes back up broken: 66 damage. 3 CE inside your Domain.

1st

Threshold Walk1 CE · 0.3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Sink into the ground and rise from your stain nearest where you look (within 72 blocks), or else from the ground where you look (18 blocks). A stain lit bright, or rising while blind, can push you out beside it, and then you can't Walk for 3 s. Rising from a stain under water costs 2 CE more. Free once every 3 s. Inside your Domain every wall is a mouth: you rise from any surface you look at, and with Variant you carry an ally within 2 blocks (1 CE).

Passives

1st

Stains

Technique Key 1 stains the surface where you look (12 blocks), up to 8 stains, each for 10 minutes: doors for Threshold Walk.

3rd

Predatory Emergence

Your first melee blow within 3 s of a Walk deals 18 damage more.

Domain

18th

House Beneath Every Surface

Predigestion Tide: every chosen foe gains a Corrosion Mark and is pulled toward the floor; one already at three sinks and is held 1.5 s. Every wall is a mouth: Threshold Walk needs no stain here.

Technique feats

1st

Black Mucus

Your stains are sticky ground: foes standing near one are slowed (not a strong one).

6th

Liquefying Grip

The third Corrosion Mark seizes its foe: it's held fast and can't react for 3 s (a strong one may tear free of the hold).

6th

Predator Beneath

Within 3 s of a Walk, Variant + Walk keeps you half sunk until 3 s has passed: shots on you land at half, and you can't move.

11th

Recall Protocol

Once every 5 minutes, a blow that drops you below a quarter of your health pulls you (2 CE) through your farthest stain within 72 blocks.
