Corrosive Threshold

Infect surfaces and prey with Corrosion, turn the stains into doors, and drag degraded targets into a private hunting space.

4arts
1passives
3technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Corroding Touch1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you touch (2.5 blocks): 14 damage of rot and a Corrosion Mark (up to 3, each slowing it).

1st

Slough1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (6 blocks) rots open: 7 damage a Corrosion Mark (at least one's worth) and a little more (a strong foe may hold together). Its marks are spent.

11th

Rotting World6 CE · 12 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you touch is dragged down into your pocket dimension for 3 s (each Corrosion Mark makes it surer; at 3 it can't resist). It rots there and comes back up broken: 66 damage.

1st

Threshold Walk1 CE · 0.3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Sink into the ground and rise from your stain nearest where you look (within 24 blocks), or else from the ground where you look (6 blocks). Free once every 3 s.

Passives

1st

Stains

Technique Key 1 stains the surface where you look (12 blocks), up to 5 stains: doors for Threshold Walk.

Predigestion Tide: every chosen foe gains a Corrosion Mark and is pulled toward the floor; one already at three sinks and is held 1.5 s.
