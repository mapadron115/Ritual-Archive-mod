Arts

11th

Soul Bound Treasure10 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your Regalia leaves your hand and fights on its own for a minute: 27 damage a blow.

1st

Regalia2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Your soul takes the shape of the weapon in your hand for 5 hours: every blow with that kind of weapon lands 5 damage harder, and once every 3 s it can take 1 CE for 9 damage more. With the feat, pressing it again while the Regalia stands readies a Technique Release.

Passives

6th

Divine Protection

The Regalia has a life of its own (135 damage): a blow on you is thrown onto it (1 CE) until that's spent. It mends on a long rest.

Domain

18th

Cursed Soul Armory

Lesser regalia hang in the air of the armory and strike every chosen foe: 89 damage.

Technique feats

3rd

Regalia Versatility

Technique Key 1 turns the Regalia between weapon and shield: as a shield it lends 10 armor instead of its blows, and Divine Protection takes blows for free.

6th

Celestial Raiment

Manifesting also drapes a heavenly scarf around you while it lasts (3 CE more): 5 armor and 10% less from blows.

11th

Technique Release

Pressing the quick key while the Regalia stands (2 CE) readies a release: your next blow within 10 s is a critical that knocks the foe down, and the Regalia breaks with it.
