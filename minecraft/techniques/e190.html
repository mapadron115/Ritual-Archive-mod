Earth-Spider Loom

String cursed Anchor Threads between surfaces: movement through the network is taxed and can trigger pulls, restraints and geometric traps.

3arts

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Anchor Thread1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A thread from you to the surface you look at (18 blocks) for a minute (up to 6): foes that cross it are cut (9 damage), slowed, and caught fast 1.5 s unless they slip it.

6th

Loom Trap2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

With three threads strung: their far anchors close into a triangle for a minute. Each foe that sets foot in it is bound 3 s unless it slips the silk, once each.

1st

Draw Taut2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Every thread snaps taut: each foe touching one is yanked to its middle, cut for 18 damage and caught 2.5 s. With Variant, no snap: you ride the thread you touch to its far end.

Passives

3rd

Ceiling Hunter

You climb walls (sneak to cling) and hang from a ceiling while sneaking. Variant + Draw Taut rides the thread you're touching to its far end.

11th

Buried Spider

Once every 3 s, your weapon blow on a held foe spends your oldest thread: 18 damage more, and it's dragged 6 blocks to you.

Domain

18th

Castle Under Eightfold Silk

Silk fills the castle: every chosen foe is wrapped fast (held 1.5 s, 27 damage).

Technique feats

1st

Silk Architect

Variant + Anchor Thread ties the thread to the ally you look at (18 blocks): it moves with them, and you're told how far and which way they are.

1st

Tremor Reader

Within a block of one of your threads, you hear through the web: a mote shows over every creature near you.

3rd

Spider Drop

Once every 3 s, a blow landed while dropping onto a foe pins it 3 s (a strong one may tear free).

11th

Eightfold Knot

Technique Key 1 (1 CE): collapse your newest thread and re-string it centred where you look (6 blocks).
