Serpent Against the Dawn

Track Night Depth: put out light, blind, and press the dark to deepen it, while the sun, if it overcomes you, drives it back.

4arts

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Devouring Dark1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The dark swallows the foe you look at (12 blocks): 18 damage, 9 damage more per Night Depth, and it's blinded (a strong foe may see through it). Lit campfires and candles near it go dark.

1st

Swallow the Light1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Where you look (36 blocks), 9 blocks of unnatural dark for two 3 s periods: fires, lit campfires and candles in it go out, and foes inside see only darkness. At Night Depth 2 or more they're blinded unless they see through it.

6th

Solar Struggle2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

At Night Depth 3, the Eclipse Pulse: foes within 18 blocks take 36 damage and can't be healed for 3 s unless they endure it (then half, and they heal). Your Night Depth falls to 1.

3rd

Coil of Night2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A coil of shadow wraps the foe you look at (12 blocks): 25 damage, held and blinded for 3 s (the greatest aren't held).

Passives

1st

Night Depth

0–3. Deepens once every 3 s for each of: a light put out, a foe blinded, a foe wounded by the dark. It pales every 5 s under the open daytime sky or in bright light, and when fire burns you. Each step: your blows 10% harder, blows on you 8% softer.

11th

Uncreated Darkness

At Night Depth 3 fire burns you half as much, though it still drives the night back; and when it does, you move like a struck snake for 2 s.

Domain

18th

Twelve Gates Before Sunrise

The night has twelve gates and no dawn: every chosen foe is blinded and takes 36 damage, and Night deepens. At every third gate they can't be healed for 3 s either.

Technique feats

1st

Light-Eater

When Swallow the Light puts out a light, the eaten light is thrown in the eyes of the nearest foe within 18 blocks of it: blinded for 3 s unless it looks away.

3rd

Enemy of Sunrise

When a foe's fire drives your night back, the dark lashes it: 18 damage unless it endures.

6th

Endless Coil

At Night Depth 3, Coil of Night strikes from any darkness: it reaches 36 blocks.

11th

Chaos Before Creation

Once a long rest, when your Night Depth would fade to nothing, its dregs erupt: foes within 6 blocks take 36 damage and are blinded 3 s.
