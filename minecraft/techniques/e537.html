Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Water Shaping1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A wave toward where you look, 3 blocks: 16 damage, shoving foes 3 blocks, and fire in its path goes out. With the trident in hand it thrusts instead (the same blow, one foe).

6th

Marine Telepathy2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Water within 8 blocks answers: 6 dolphins fight for you for a minute, 9 damage a bite.

11th

King of the Seven Seas4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once a minute, picked with Variant + Technique Key 1: a 18-block tidal wave (22 damage, swept 9 blocks and held 2 s), or a whirlpool 6 blocks across where you look for a minute (each 3 s, what's in it is held and drawn in).

18th

The Trench Answers6 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A leviathan breaches where you look (36 blocks): everything within 18 blocks of it takes 89 damage, and the ground floods for a minute: foes wade through it.

3rd

Trident of the King2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The trident in your hand, or back to the sea. Technique Key 1 throws a real trident, 16 damage, that the sea takes back.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Leviathan's Wake10 CE · Maximum (X)

The ocean stands up: a 36-block wave along your look, 89 damage, everything swept 18 blocks. The ground floods for a minute, and while you stand in it your arts cost 2 CE less.

Passives

1st

Atlantean Blood

You breathe water, swim and mine underwater as you would on land, and the cold of the deep can't touch you.

Technique feats

1st

Pressure King

Once every 3 s, your weapon blow on a foe in the water while you're in it adds 9 damage, and chills and slows it.

3rd

Tidecaller's Step

Variant + Water Shaping (2 CE), standing in water: you come up in the water you look at, up to 18 blocks away.

3rd

Stormcaller's Wake

Variant + Trident of the King with the trident in hand (2 CE): lightning where you look (36 blocks), 22 damage to foes within 3 blocks of it.

1st

Abyssal Gaze

Variant + Marine Telepathy (2 CE): the foe you look at within 9 blocks is frightened of you for 10 s.
