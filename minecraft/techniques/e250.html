Blood Techniques

Blood shaped into rings, nets, crystals, rain and beams, every art paid in CE and your own blood. Convergence compresses it to the limit.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Slicing Exorcism1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A spinning ring of blood flies 36 blocks where you look, slicing through everything on its way: 18 damage. Costs 1% of your health.

1st

Crimson Binding1 CE · 5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A red net flies 18 blocks; whatever it catches is bound where it stands for 3 s, or 4 s after charging 1 s and 5 s after charging 2 s. Costs 1% of your health.

1st

Blood Construct1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Blood drawn out and held in shape: a wall three blocks wide and two high where you look (6 blocks), for a minute. Costs 1% of your health. With the Blood Crystallization feat, Variant (2 CE more) hardens a plank instead: six blocks straight out from your feet the way you look, for 20 minutes.

1st

Blood Meteorite1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A hurled crystal of blood that arcs and hits hard: 25 damage, knocking the foe back. Costs 3% of your health.

6th

Crimson Rain3 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Spikes of blood rain on a 3-block circle where you look (up to 36 blocks): 33 damage in all; converged, they pin foes down. Costs 2% of your health.

6th

Blood Geyser3 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Saturate the ground where you look (up to 6 blocks): the first foe to step on it in the next 30 s is hit from below by a geyser of blood, 55 damage, and launched. Costs 3% of your health.

11th

Blood Burst0 CE · Cast (Z)

Toggle. While it's on, once every 3 s a melee blow explodes with blood (1 CE and 1% of your health): 27 damage to everything within 2 blocks.

11th

Piercing Blood8 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A supersonic beam of compressed blood, 60 blocks where you look, through every foe on the line: 89 damage. Converged, it lingers 2 s and sweeps with your aim, striking each foe on it once more. Costs 5% of your health.

3rd

Flowing Red Scale3 CE · Quick (G)

A minute with your blood running fast: much faster on foot, +3 armor, +25% attack speed, higher jumps, and nothing knocks you back.

Maximum

11th

Wing King Imitation10 CE · Maximum (X)

Maximum (the Wing King Imitation feat): a minute on veined wings of blood. You fly, and every 2 s a line of blood rains on the foe nearest below you, 18 damage. Piercing Blood sleeps while you fly.

Passives

6th

Convergence

Hold the cast key to compress the blood, rooted where you stand: each second adds a stage (two at most), and each stage hits much harder, reaches 6 blocks farther and costs a little more CE and blood.

Domain

18th

Bloodbath

Every 3 s, the blood of each foe it chose turns on them from within: 53 damage. While it stands, your blood costs are paid from them, not you.

Technique feats

1st

Blood Production

Cursed energy spun into blood: when a blood cost would come out of your body, 1 CE pays for it instead (up to ten times as much blood as CE).

1st

Blood Crystallization

Variant + Blood Construct: blood taught to be stone, a plank to cross a gap.

6th

Crimson Afterimage

Under Flowing Red Scale you leave a red ghost behind: once every 10 s, a blow finds the afterimage instead of you (3 CE).

11th

Supernova

Piercing Blood seeds three small suns of blood along its line: for a minute they wait, then burst like buckshot (27 damage) when a foe comes near.

11th

Wing King Imitation

Gain the Maximum: a minute of flight on wings of blood, raining lines of ruin below.
