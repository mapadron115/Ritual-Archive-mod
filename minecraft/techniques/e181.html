Starvation Colossus

Violence feeds Hunger. Spend it to assemble giant skeletal arms, ribs, jaws, and finally a towering cursed exoskeleton.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Bone Arm1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A giant skeletal hand slams down on the foe you look at (12 blocks): 22 damage, and 11 damage more for every 2 Hunger you spend (all of it). With Variant, 1 Hunger: the arm stays for a 3 s, 6 blocks more reach and 5 damage on your next blow.

1st

Bone Jaw1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

2 Hunger: a skull's jaw bites the foe you look at (6 blocks): 12 damage, and it's held a 3 s unless it tears free.

6th

Gnashing Jaws3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A skull's jaws snap down a 4-block cone: 36 damage, and a Hunger for each foe caught.

3rd

Rattle of the Giant2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The ringing of the bones: foes within 12 blocks are frightened a 3 s unless they steel themselves, and each that isn't feeds a Hunger (up to 6). Once every five minutes.

6th

Skeletal Armor2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

3 Hunger: for a minute the skeleton walks round you: +9 armor, larger, 3 blocks more reach, and the Arm's empowering costs no Hunger once a 3 s.

11th

Torso of a Nation2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

4 Hunger: a rib-and-spine tower twelve blocks tall rises round you for a minute (6 blocks across): shots from outside land a quarter softer on your allies inside, and a foe stepping in is shoved 6 blocks out unless it holds.

1st

Rib Cage1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

3 Hunger: ribs close round you for 3 s; blows land at half.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Gashadokuro10 CE · Maximum (X)

With 6 or more Hunger, all of it: for a minute the starving skeleton rises round you. You tower, gain 14 damage of temporary health for each Hunger, your reach grows by nine blocks, your first blow each 3 s also bites and holds, a round with a heavy wound near you gives back a Hunger, and every 3 s the ground shakes for 44 damage within 4 blocks.

Passives

1st

Hunger

Once every 3 s each: blood spilled within 36 blocks, a foe falling below half health, a death (two). Up to 18. End a 3 s full without spending any and you starve: 7 damage.

Technique feats

1st

Bone Chime

You hear the half-dead rattle: a mote shows over every creature below half its health within 18 blocks.

6th

Jaw of Famine

Gnashing Jaws feeds you: 5% of your health for each foe caught (up to three).

3rd

Starving Maw

When you'd starve, a foe in your Jaw takes it instead.
