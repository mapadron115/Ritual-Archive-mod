The Crimson Bath

Years flow toward the Countess: every blow sips life, every death fills the bath, and the last bath drinks the whole room.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

6th

The Bath Overflows4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every living foe within 9 blocks: 20 damage as its years flow to you (a hardy foe half), and you regain half of it all.

3rd

Drink Deep2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Your next weapon blow on a living foe also wards you for the damage it deals, up to a third of your health.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Last Bath10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every living foe within 18 blocks: 54 damage (a hardy foe half), and you regain all of it. A foe it fells is two Years.

Passives

1st

First Sip

Once a second a weapon blow on a living foe sips: you regain 1% of your health, and 1% more for each Year Drunk. Each living foe you fell adds a Year (up to 12) until you next rest. The undead, golems and conjured things have no years to give.

11th

The Countess's Due

Once every 3 s (5 CE), a living foe you fell wards you for 18% of your health.
