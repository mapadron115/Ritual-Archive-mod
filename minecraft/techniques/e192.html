Sun-Eater Wings

Earn Pinions through sustained flight and by breaking poison and bindings, then spend them on radiant stoops that devour serpents.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Radiant Stoop1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You dive on the foe you look at (12 blocks) from above: 18 damage of radiance, and 9 damage more per Pinion spent (all of them).

6th

Wingbeat Deliverance2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 Pinion: the ally you look at (36 blocks) is swept to your side, free of poison, slowing and holds. With Variant: the foe you look at is hurled 6 blocks away unless it holds its ground.

1st

Sun-Eater Wings2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute great golden wings carry you: you fly, and every 3 s aloft earns a Pinion (up to 8).

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Wings That Eclipse the Serpent10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute your wings blot out the sun: poison and bindings can't take you, and every 3 s radiance falls on the foes beneath you (36 damage). The first Deliverance every 3 s costs no Pinion, and the Stoop deals 27 damage more to a foe beside a poisoned or held ally.

Passives

1st

Pinions

Earned by staying aloft and by breaking free of poison, slowing or being held (each is cleansed the moment it takes you). A weapon blow with Variant held spends one for 9 damage of radiance.

3rd

Enemy of Venom

Once every 3 s, 1 CE: an ally within 18 blocks who is poisoned, slowed or held is torn free, and you earn a Pinion.

11th

Sunward Ascent

Aloft under the open sky you earn Pinions twice as fast.

Technique feats

1st

Feather Shield

Once every 3 s, 1 Pinion: a foe's blow on an ally within 9 blocks lands a quarter softer behind your feathers.

1st

Venom Breaker

You tear free of sickness too, and so do the allies you free: withering, weakness and hunger are cleansed for a Pinion each.

11th

Solar Plumage

Under the open daytime sky, poison and slowing can't take hold of you at all.
