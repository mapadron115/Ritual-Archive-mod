Blind Spot

Tax enemy attention to keep you still, then explode through any lapse in line of sight with impossible movement and a punishing strike.

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

6th

Peripheral Failure2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Up to 6 foes within 18 blocks blink: blinded 3 s, and they can't Observe you for 3 s even once they see again (a strong foe may keep its eyes open).

1st

Blind Spot Step1 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Only while no foe Observes you: you're simply somewhere else, up to 18 blocks along your look. Your next weapon blow within a second is a Snap Strike: no more harm, but the foe can't react (a player's shield is knocked down) for 6 s, and if it fails to resist it's also held there and blinded for 6 s.

Passives

1st

Observation

A foe within 18 blocks whose eyes are turned on you, with nothing in the way, Observes you. While any do, you can't Step.

1st

Blink Advance

Once every 3 s, when a foe watching you strikes or shoots, your next Step within a second works even while you're watched (up to 4 blocks).

3rd

Blink Reflex

The moment the last Observer looks away, your next Step costs nothing (it reaches half as far).

Domain

18th

Containment Chamber: DON'T BLINK

Biological Blink: every chosen foe's eyes close at once (1.5 s), and each one you close gives a Blink Window (up to 6): a Window pays a Step instead of CE, one every 3 s, until the next pulse.

Blink Hunter
