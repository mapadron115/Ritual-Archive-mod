Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Excavation1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour: a row of stone cubes (one per CE, up to 7) rises where you look, within 18 blocks. They stop shots, foes can't pass, anything standing there is thrown up, and they crumble after 30 s.

3rd

Rock Blast1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A rock torn from the ground and thrown at the foe you look at (18 blocks): 20 damage.

3rd

Earthen Armor1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour up to 6 CE: earth worn for a minute, 2 armor for each.

3rd

Tremor3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The ground bursts around you: everything within 9 blocks takes 55 damage (more at 6th, 11th and 18th) and is knocked down.

6th

Wall of Stone4 CE · Cast (Z)

A wall 6 wide and 4 high stands where you look, within 36 blocks, for a minute. Technique Key 1 (1 CE) shoves it 10 blocks the way you face, ramming foes for 22 damage.

6th

Rock Burst1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

(Feat: Rock Burst) The stone nearest you explodes: 22 damage to everything within 3 blocks of it.

6th

Shove Wall1 CE · 2 s cooldown · Technique Key 1 (C)

Your newest wall is shoved 10 blocks the way you face, ramming every foe in its path for 22 damage and carrying it along.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Stone Arms8 CE · Maximum (X)

The giant's arms rise and close on the foe you look at (24 blocks): 66 damage, and it's held. Every 3 s after, 1 CE crushes it for 26 damage; it lets go when you can't pay, after 12 s, or when you cast it again.
