Shadow Eminence

By day, an unremarkable face in the crowd. By night, a slime-clad god of theatrics who announces his apocalypses before he performs them.

11thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Slime Sword0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

With the suit on: the slime as a blade, 18 damage to the foe you look at within 3.5 blocks.

1st

Overcharged Strike0 CE · Cast (Z)

A toggle: slime blows spend 1 CE and 1 Atomic for 9 damage more.

3rd

Atomic Discharge3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

2 Atomic or more: a small apocalypse where you look (18 blocks), 3 blocks around, 22 damage per Atomic, and the soft ground craters.

6th

Slime Threads2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Threads fine as lies: the foe you look at within 9 blocks is held for 3 s (a strong one may tear free).

6th

Slime Wings2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Fly for a minute.

20th

True Eminence10 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest: open I am Atomic at once, past Burnout, its opening detonation at its peak.

1st

Slime Bodysuit1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Put the slime suit on, or take it off: 4 armor, and whatever you swing is slime too, so weapon blows build Atomic.

Passives

1st

Mob Camouflage

Creatures more than 8 blocks off overlook you unless you've struck them, and your first blow in each fight is a critical.

1st

Atomic

Up to 8: +2 per slime blow, once every 3 s.

Open, with no barrier. Opening: 177 damage to every foe in range, and the ground at the heart craters. Then atomic fire, 22 damage each pulse.
