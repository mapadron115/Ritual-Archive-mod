Wind Between Heaven and Earth

Draw directional Wind Roads: you ride the flow, enemies fight against it.

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Wind Road2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A road of wind from you to where you look (18 blocks, any direction) for a minute (up to 6). On it you and your allies are carried swiftly along it and don't fall (sneak to step off), and so is everything loose on it. Foes on it are swept along it, slowed, and buffeted for 7 damage each 3 s.

3rd

Serpent of the Updraft2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute you fly, as long as you keep within 6 blocks of one of your Wind Roads; a 3 s away from them and you sink gently down.

6th

Breath of Civilization2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The Wind Road nearest where you look breathes out: every foe on it is hurled 9 blocks along it unless it braces, and allies on it are quickened for 3 s.

1st

Gust1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Everything within 4.5 blocks is blown 6 blocks away (9 damage to foes), shots and loose things too, and fire there goes out.

Passives

1st

Plumes

Riding a Wind Road from one end to the other earns a Plume, once every 3 s (up to 6). Variant + Technique Key 1 spends one: for 3 s your roads carry their riders twice as fast.

11th

Morning-Star Crossing

Where two of your roads cross is a Crossroad. Once every 3 s, you or an ally standing at one and looking toward another within 36 blocks is carried there.

Domain

18th

City Beneath the Feathered Sky

Six Wind Roads open out from you, beyond your usual count, and every road runs through the city: every chosen foe is hurled by the wind (36 damage) and slowed for 3 s.
