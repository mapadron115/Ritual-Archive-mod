Arts

1st

Wind Road2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A road of wind from you to where you look (18 blocks, any direction) for a minute (up to 6). On it you're carried swiftly along it and don't fall (sneak to step off), and so is everything loose on it. Foes on it are swept along it, slowed, and buffeted for 7 damage each 3 s.

1st

Gust1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Everything within 4.5 blocks is blown 6 blocks away (9 damage to foes), shots and loose things too, and fire there goes out.
