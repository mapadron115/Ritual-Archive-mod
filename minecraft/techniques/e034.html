Vengeful Spirit Manipulation

A spirit bound to your ring: her arm tears out of the air, her voice holds foes still, and in full she fights beside you.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Partial Manifestation3 CE · Cast (Z)

Her arm tears out of the air at the foe you look at (6 blocks): 66 damage.

6th

Borrowed Word2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

She speaks a word she once learned, "Don't move": the foe you look at (12 blocks) is frozen for 3 s (a strong will may hold).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Full Manifestation10 CE · Maximum (X)

Rika in full, for five minutes: she fights beside you (66 damage a blow), and while she's here you're stronger and your cursed energy runs higher.

Passives

1st

The Ring

Your weapon is enhanced by the spirit bound to you: weapon blows 5% harder.

3rd

Involuntary Manifestation

A blow of more than a quarter of your health calls her out in full on her own, for a minute, to protect you (once a minute).

3rd

Mimicry

When Rika in full strikes a sorcerer, she copies the first art of their technique (never a Maximum or a Domain), up to 5 arts. While she's out in full, Technique Key 1 casts the one you've picked, at its own cost; Variant + Technique Key 1 picks the next.

11th

Stronger Bond

While she's manifested, your blows land 15% harder, you have 6 more armor, and your reserve holds 2 more CE: you gain 2 when she comes and lose 2 when she goes.

Domain

18th

True and Mutual Love

A field of swords, one for each copied technique: every 3 s, each chosen foe is struck by a copied art, free. With nothing copied, or an art that can't reach, the swords fall instead: 89 damage.
