Royal Marionette

A surgical theater of three dolls: Doctor Blythe repairs bodies, the Puppeteer turns corpses into soldiers, and Terpsichora makes your own body dance past its limits.

7arts
4passives
4technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Doctor Blythe3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The surgical doll stands beside you for ten minutes. Surgery and Emergency Stitch need her.

1st

Surgery0 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

With Blythe on stage: spend up to 5 CE, each healing 8% of your health.

1st

Predatory En0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Toggle: you sense every living presence within 64 blocks, even through walls (1 CE a minute). It grows with you.

3rd

Puppeteer3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

String up the freshest corpse of something you killed within 6 blocks (in the last 30 s): the same creature rises and fights for you for 180 s, striking for 7 damage a second (or half its own blow, if harder). Up to 20 puppets. Technique Key 1 sets their order: follow, guard where they stand, or attack the foe you look at. Puppet Memory: one that shot in life (a skeleton, a pillager, a blaze) keeps its shot, and its hits land as yours.

6th

Snap the Strings1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Destroy the puppet nearest where you look: foes within 2 blocks of it are snared 3 s (a strong foe less). Variant: the Suture Surge instead, its stitches mending you and your allies within 3 blocks of it by a tenth of their health.

6th

Terpsichora5 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute the ballerina strings your body: 40% faster, higher jumps and quicker swings. Variant while she dances (2 CE): Dance Past the Limits, +4 armor for 3 s, then the strings cost you 5% of your health.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Puppet Master's Serenity10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute Terpsichora dances without end and one blow in five misses you. You're exhausted after.

Passives

1st

One doll at a time

Royal Theater: all three dolls are on stage at once.

1st

Emergency Stitch

While Blythe is on stage, once every 3 s, 2 CE: a heavy blow is lessened by 24% of your health.

11th

Fifty Strings

Up to 20 puppets at once.

18th

Last Dance

If you'd die while Terpsichora dances, the doll keeps your body moving for one more 3 s.

Technique feats

1st

Field Surgeon

While Blythe stands, every 5 s she mends the most hurt ally within 6 blocks for 16% of their health (2 CE).

3rd

Invisible Strings

Your puppets wait in the wings, unseen: invisible until they strike, and again after 3 s without striking.

1st

Feral En

Your En also finds danger and treasure: creatures hunting you glow red to you, and chests and ores within 8 blocks sparkle.

11th

Dance Beyond Death

The strings hold even when the dancer cannot: Last Dance works from 11th.
