Hundred-Handed Arsenal

A pool of spectral hands, each committed to offense, defense or grappling, and none free for the others until you change your mind.

4arts
4passives
4technique feats
18thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Hundred Hands2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Offense: every free hand strikes the foe you look at (6 blocks), 7 damage each (up to 11). Grapple: the free hands pin it, half a second a hand up to 3 s (a strong foe less), from 12 blocks. Guard: the hands are busy guarding. Every hand used is gone for 3 s.

3rd

Stone Barrage2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Up to 6 free hands throw a stone each at the foe you look at and those beside it (36 blocks), two at most at any one: 9 damage a stone, and 7 damage more on each foe struck.

11th

Briarean Barrage3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

With the feat: every free hand fuses into one massive fist on the foe you look at (18 blocks): 9 damage a hand (up to 98 damage), and it's knocked down unless it holds.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Three Hundred Stones10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every hand hurls boulders at once: 18 stones rain down where you look (36 blocks), 18 damage each within 2 blocks, and the rubble slows whoever they strike. It needs a free hand and takes them all.

Passives

1st

The Hand Pool

11 hands. A hand given a job comes back after 3 s.

1st

Commitment (Technique Key 1)

Offense: 3 blocks more reach while a hand is free, and a free hand adds 9 damage to each weapon blow. Guard: a free hand turns aside a fifth of a foe's blow, two hands two fifths of a heavy one. Grapple: the art pins.

6th

Fifty Heads, Hundred Tasks

While a hand is free you work blocks from 6 blocks farther, and Grapple reaches 12 blocks.

11th

Titan-Burying Volley

In Offense, once every 3 s, your weapon blow on a creature much larger than you spends 1 CE and 3 hands: stones bury it for 3 s unless it breaks out (the greatest are only slowed).

Technique feats

1st

Catch the Sky

In Guard, once every 3 s a free hand may snatch a shot out of the air (about a third of the time).

1st

Ambidexterity Is Meaningless

In Offense, a free hand guides each ally's first weapon blow every 3 s within 18 blocks of you: 9 damage more.

3rd

Jailor of Titans

Grapple pins two foes at once: the one you look at and the next nearest in reach.

11th

Briarean Barrage

The quick key fuses every free hand into one massive fist (3 CE).
