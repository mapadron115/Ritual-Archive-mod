Hundred-Handed Arsenal

A pool of spectral hands, each committed to offense, defense or grappling, and none free for the others until you change your mind.

3arts

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Hundred Hands2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Offense: every hand strikes the foe you look at (6 blocks), 7 damage each (11 hands). Grapple: the hands pin it 3 s (a strong foe less). Guard: the hands are busy guarding.

11th

Briarean Barrage3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

With the feat: every hand fuses into one massive fist, 98 damage on the foe you look at.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Three Hundred Stones10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every hand hurls boulders at once: 18 stones rain down where you look (18 blocks), 18 damage each within 2 blocks.

Passives

1st

Commitment (Technique Key 1)

Offense, Guard (each hand turns aside 4% of every blow, at most 60%) or Grapple.

Technique feats

1st

Catch the Sky

In Guard, once every 3 s the hands may snatch a shot out of the air (about a third of the time).

3rd

Jailor of Titans

Grapple pins two foes at once: the one you look at and the next nearest within 6 blocks.

11th

Briarean Barrage

The quick key fuses every hand into one massive fist (3 CE).
