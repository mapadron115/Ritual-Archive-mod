Dark Dimension

A swordsman's darkness: read every intent in the room, swallow what you read, and cut where the reading ends, until space itself stops being an obstacle.

7arts
4passives
4technique feats
1stMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Dark Cloaked Slash0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Toggle. Your weapon blows carry the darkness: +13 damage (1 CE, at most once a second).

6th

Avidya Wild Slash3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A sword stroke through everything within 4 blocks, above and below you too: your weapon's blow and 26 damage to each, and you end beside the last one cut (a step of up to 6 blocks). In Mana Zone it can be centred on the foe you look at.

9th

Mana Zone4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute: a 36-block zone around you. You sense everything that moves in it (they're outlined, walls or not), your Dimension Slash reaches half the zone farther, and Avidya Wild Slash can strike around a foe you look at inside it.

11th

Dimension Slash6 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 72-block cut through space along your look, through walls: 93 damage to everything on it. A closed Domain's barrier it crosses takes its hardest blow, and conjured blocks on it are unmade.

13th

Black Moon3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

With Mana Zone up: for 3 s, every hostile arrow, trident or fireball within 9 blocks is erased.

1st

Ki Read1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A minute: you read the room. Every foe within 24 blocks is outlined, walls or not, and 1 blow in 20 lands as a critical. Ki Savant (feat): with Variant, the foe you look at is read (what it's casting or holding, its cursed energy), and your next Black Hole on its shot within 3 s is free.

Maximum

1st

Maximum: Dark Cloaked Dimension Sever10 CE · Maximum (X)

A 180-block cut through space, 159 damage to everything on it. It splits the ground and walls it crosses. The Equinox also cuts open any Curtain or Barrier it crosses.

Passives

3rd

Black Hole

Once every 3 s (2 CE), the darkness swallows a hostile arrow, trident or fireball about to reach you (within 6 blocks).

15th

Iai: Death Draw

In Mana Zone, once every 3 s (4 CE): whoever strikes you, or steps into your reach, is cut first: your weapon's blow and 53 damage.

20th

Captain's Black Moon

Mana Zone reaches 36 blocks, one Black Hole or Iai each 3 s is free, and a Dimension Slash on a foe inside the zone lands half again as hard 1 time in 10.

1st

The Dark Blade

With an empty hand the darkness is your sword: bare-handed blows hit like an iron sword.

Technique feats

1st

Ki Savant

Variant + Ki Read reads the foe you look at: what it's casting or holding and its cursed energy. Your next Black Hole on its shot within 3 s costs nothing.

3rd

Dark Cloaked Blade

Your weapon is cursed steel: its blows bite through armor, a quarter harder on an armored foe.

6th

Black Hole Mastery

You catch rather than erase: what your Black Hole swallows is thrown back where you look, twice as fast, as your own shot (2 CE more).

11th

Surpass Your Limits

At 0 health, before you fall, everything left (5 CE or more) goes into one last Dimension Slash.
