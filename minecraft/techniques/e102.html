Dark Dimension

A swordsman's darkness: read every intent in the room, swallow what you read, and cut where the reading ends, until space itself stops being an obstacle.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Dark Cloaked Slash0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Toggle. Your weapon blows carry the darkness: +11 damage (1 CE, at most once a second).

6th

Avidya Wild Slash3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Cut everything within 4 blocks, above and below you too: 33 damage each.

9th

Mana Zone4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute: a 20-block zone around you. You sense everything that moves in it (they're outlined, walls or not), and your Dimension Slash may begin anywhere inside it.

11th

Dimension Slash6 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 40-block cut through space along your look, through walls: 77 damage to everything on it.

13th

Black Moon3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

With Mana Zone up: for 3 s, every hostile arrow, trident or fireball within 5 blocks is erased.

1st

Ki Read1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A minute: you read the room. Every foe within 24 blocks is outlined, walls or not, and 1 blow in 20 lands as a critical.

Maximum

1st

Maximum: Dark Cloaked Dimension Sever10 CE · Maximum (X)

A 100-block cut through space, 133 damage to everything on it. It splits the ground and walls it crosses. (Equinox.)

Passives

3rd

Black Hole

Once every 3 s (2 CE), the darkness swallows a hostile arrow, trident or fireball about to reach you (within 4 blocks).

15th

Iai: Death Draw

In Mana Zone, once every 3 s (4 CE): whoever strikes you is cut first, 44 damage.

20th

Captain's Black Moon

Mana Zone reaches 20 blocks, and one Black Hole or Iai each 3 s is free.

Technique feats

3rd

Dark Cloaked Blade

Your weapon is cursed steel: its blows bite through armor, a quarter harder on an armored foe.

6th

Black Hole Mastery

You catch rather than erase: what your Black Hole swallows is thrown back where you look, twice as fast, as your own shot (2 CE more).

11th

Surpass Your Limits

At 0 health, before you fall, everything left (5 CE or more) goes into one last Dimension Slash.
