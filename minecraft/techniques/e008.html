Cursed Speech

Ten commanding words that bend whoever hears them, paid for in Cursed Energy and the speaker's own throat. The stronger you are than the listener, the harder the word lands.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Speak0 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Speak the chosen word (Technique Key 1 picks it) at the foe you look at, within 24 blocks, paying its CE: Sleep (2), Return (1), Run Away (1), Blast Away (2), Plummet (2), Don't Move (2); from 3rd Get Twisted (3); from 6th Get Crushed (4); from 11th Explode (5); from 18th Die (10). Hold to pour up to 4 more CE: 11 damage more each.

11th

Resounding Voice0 CE · Cast (Z)

Toggle. Your words cost 5 CE more and fall on every foe in a 12-block cone (with Megaphone, hold Variant for a 28-block line).

3rd

Interjection0 CE · Quick (G)

Your bound word (Variant + Technique Key 1 binds the chosen one), spoken instantly at the foe you look at: once every 3 s.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The Last Word10 CE · Maximum (X)

The chosen word, spoken to every foe within 24 blocks at once, at no strain.

Passives

1st

Vocal Cords

Every CE a word costs is taken from your throat too (60 at your level). It heals a point a second; when it can't pay, you can't speak.

1st

Weight of the Word

A word lands by how much stronger you are than who hears it (players: their level; creatures: their toughness). Each level they have over you strains your throat 20% more and shortens what the word holds; they recoil it on you, and at 5 levels over you the word chokes. Each level you have over them holds it longer.

3rd

Interjection

The quick key speaks your bound word instantly, once every 3 s.

18th

Die

On a foe 10 levels under you, Die is death. 5 to 9 under, it kills a foe already low (half its health at 5, more as the gap grows) and otherwise hits for 166 damage. Closer than that, it's that hit and a heavy strain. Bosses never just die.

Domain

18th

The Last Word

Every 3 s you speak the chosen word, and every foe it chose falls under it, at no cost and no strain.
