Nine Lives

A ledger of nine lives, a riposte that punishes every blow, and the courage to burn three lives for one perfect cut.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

The Legend's Blade1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A rapier thrust at the foe you look at (6 blocks): 9 damage, twice that within 1.5 s of a blow landing on you.

14th

The Duelist's Challenge2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Single combat with the foe you look at (36 blocks) for a minute: your blows on it land a third harder, its blows on you a third softer.

3rd

Fear Me, If You Dare2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) is frightened of you: it flees for 10 s and fights weakened for a minute (a steady mind resists).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Nine Lives, One Cut5 CE · Maximum (X)

Burn up to three lives (one always stays): a cut for each on the foe you look at (6 blocks), 21 damage each, and 21 damage more if all three are burned.

Passives

1st

Nine Lives

A blow that would fell you burns a life: you're back at a quarter of your health. One life returns each long rest, and the last can't be burned.

6th

Softpaws

You land on your feet (no fall damage) and climb walls (sneak to cling).

11th

Lives Behind Him

Burning a life makes you much tougher for 3 s.

18th

Death Keeps His Word

Once a long rest a burned life is spared, and with three lives or fewer your blows land a fifth harder.
