Symbiote

A living suit: tendrils and web-lines, ablative weave, predatory morphologies, camouflage, recovery and the King in Black's echo.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

3rd

Web & Tendril1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A tendril lashes out 12 blocks where you look (twice as far at Bond 3): a light foe is dragged to you, a heavy one drags you to it, and at a wall, a ceiling or a branch it's a web-line that zips you there.

9th

Adaptive Camouflage3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute: the suit takes the colour of everything behind it. While you hold still you can't be seen and creatures lose you; attacking shows you for a moment.

11th

Symbiotic Recovery3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The suit knits you back together: you heal 38% of your health (about).

18th

King in Black Echo6 CE · Cast (Z)

A minute: wing-like tendrils (you fly), blows land softer on you, and fragments of the symbiote lunge as one swarm at the foe nearest you every 4.5 s, 44 damage.

1st

Living Suit0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The symbiote covers you (press again to pull it back): you grow to Venom's size (as far as the space around you allows; sneak to squeeze through), tougher, and once every 3 s a weapon blow can take 1 CE for 9 damage more. Ablative Weave: once every 3 s a blow on you lands softer.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: We Are Venom10 CE · Maximum (X)

9 s: every morphology at once, a tendril lashes the nearest foe every second (18 damage), and the Ablative Weave doubles. Afterwards you're exhausted.

Passives

1st

Bond

The partnership's ledger, 0 to 6: +1 for feeding (a kill in the suit) and for protecting at cost (an interception, or the Weave catching a blow while you're low), each at most once a minute. Fire or a sonic blast tears the suit off you, -1. At Bond 3 the tendril reaches twice as far.

6th

Predatory Morphology

Technique Key 1 (suit on) cycles: Claws (weapon blows hit harder), Blade Tendrils (you reach farther), Rampart (much tougher, and once every 3 s for 1 CE a blow on an ally within 4.5 blocks lands on you instead), Bulk (you grow, hit heavier, and nothing knocks you back).

13th

Host Override

In the suit, when you're held, the symbiote moves your body: the hold is shaken off (3 CE, once every 5 minutes).

15th

Perfect Bond

At Bond 6: in the suit you're faster and tougher still, the Ablative Weave is twice as thick, and Rampart reaches 9 blocks.

20th

Lethal Protector

Bond never falls below 3. Once a long rest you survive a killing blow at 1 health and heal 41% of your health; once a long rest the suit takes a blow meant for an ally within 9 blocks by itself.

Technique feats

1st

Spider-Sense Echo

In the suit, the symbiote watches your back: a third of the blows from behind you are slipped.

1st

Living Parachute

In the suit you never take fall damage, and sneaking while you fall snaps out web-wings to glide.

3rd

Anti-Sonic Conditioning

Fire and sonics never tear the suit off you: a fragment swarm detaches instead and lashes the nearest foe every 4.5 s for a minute (22 damage).
