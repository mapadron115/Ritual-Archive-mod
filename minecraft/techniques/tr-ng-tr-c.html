The Sisters' Rebellion

Trung Trac and her sister's echo fight as one rebellion: every blow is answered by the other, and the war-elephants ride again.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Sister's Blade1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A cut at the foe you look at (6 blocks): 16 damage, and 9 damage more if your sister just struck it.

6th

The Queen's Elephant4 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A war-elephant of cursed energy charges 36 blocks along your aim: 36 damage to each foe it runs through, trampled flat (a strong foe takes half and stands).

11th

Sixty-Five Citadels8 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute you wear 6 more armor, and once a second a weapon blow of yours is answered by hers, 16 damage, while she stands. Once a long rest.

1st

The Second Blade3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Your sister's echo fights beside you for a minute: she strikes for 18 damage once every 3 s, and 9 damage more on a foe you just struck. She can be struck down. Technique Key 1 swaps your places (2 CE).

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Queen's Answer10 CE · Maximum (X)

You and your sister strike the foe you look at (9 blocks) as one: 103 damage, and it's stunned for 2 s. Once a long rest.

Passives

1st

The Answer

Your blow on a foe sharpens your sister's next one on it, and hers sharpens your Sister's Blade.

6th

The Fallen Sister's Share

If the echo is struck down, for a minute once a second a weapon blow of yours lands 9 damage harder, and calling her back costs 1 CE.
