Passives

3rd

Adaptation

The first blow of a kind of harm (a kind of creature, of shot, or of hazard) costs 1 CE to adapt to. Then that kind hurts you a quarter as much for a minute. Each player is a kind of their own.

11th

Divine Adaptation

Poison, wither, blindness, darkness, weakness or slowness that has been on you for 3 s is adapted to (3 CE): it ends, and can't take hold again for a minute. And once every 3 s a claw blow on a kind of foe you're adapted to adds 18 damage: the wheel has turned on it.

Domain

18th

Hall of the Five-Clawed Dragon

Your claws stab through space at the healthiest chosen foe: a critical blow, 85 damage, distance ignored.

3rd

Close Combat Specialist

Once every 3 s, a claw blow at arm's length grips the foe: held 2.3 s, and torn again a second later (16 damage).

3rd

Wheel of Adaptation

Each time you adapt, the teammate nearest you within 18 blocks takes 20% less from blows for a minute (2 CE).

Mahoraga's Fury
