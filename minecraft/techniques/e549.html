Hourglass

The Domain is the technique. Trap the battlefield inside an hourglass whose conditions judge everyone, then settle the Final Accounting.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Passives

1st

Domain

18th

The Hourglass

Five 3 ss of judgment. A chosen foe that keeps still earns a Grain; one that doesn't is judged for 18 damage. When the sand runs out, the Final Accounting: the foe with the fewest Grains takes 89 damage, and you regain 10 CE and health.
