Hundred Seals Physique

The peerless rikishi moves his strength around his body like weights on a scale: all arms, all legs, or all frame, never more than he has.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Harite1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

An open-palm slap on the foe you look at (6 blocks): 9 damage and more for each Band in your Arms. With Arms 5 or more it stuns for 1.5 s (a hardy foe shrugs).

11th

Hundred Seals Release4 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute a region holds up to 8 Bands.

3rd

Sabaori2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Crush the foe you look at (6 blocks): 18 damage and more for each Band in your Arms, knocked down. With Arms 7+ and Frame 3+ it's pinned for 3 s (a strong foe holds).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Yatagarasu10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every Band in your Legs rushes into your Arms as you charge the foe you look at (24 blocks): 66 damage and 4 damage more for each Band in your Arms, thrown far. Afterward the Bands settle back.

Passives

1st

The Muscle Map

Twelve Bands. Each in the Arms past four: weapon blows 6% harder. In the Legs: 6% faster and a higher jump. In the Frame: 1.5 armor, and at six nothing knocks you back. Each one short of four takes the same away. Technique Key 1 moves 6 Bands to the next region; Variant + Key 1 evens them out.

6th

Full Muscle Control

A region holds 6. Using a region past 5 strains you a little afterward.
