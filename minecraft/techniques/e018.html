Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

3rd

Lapse Blue1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour 1–6 CE: a sphere of Blue rolls 30 blocks toward where you look, through walls, dragging in everything within 1 block per CE and crushing it for 71 damage (at full pour) every 1.5 s. Hold a second past full for Maximum Output: it stays a minute, following your aim (scroll to set how far out it hangs, 2 to 30 blocks), crushing for 13 damage every 1.5 s. The chant runs as you hold: Phase, daybreak, arms of compassion.

6th

Reversal Red2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Hold to pour (2 CE a step, up to 6 steps): a red point flies 12 blocks; the first foe it meets takes 89 damage (at full pour) and is flung 4 blocks per step. If it hits a wall first, the distance it didn't get to travel hits it again. Hold a second past full for Maximum Output: flung twice as far. The chant runs as you hold: Phase, twilight, eyes of wisdom.

1st

Infinity1 CE · Quick (G)

Raise Infinity (1 CE, then 3 CE a second to keep it, or 1 CE every 6 s with the Six Eyes). Each attack that reaches you has a 100% chance never to arrive (bosses' and sorcerers' 95%); projectiles slow to a stop at its edge. A harmful effect or a shove someone else lays on you gets the same chance, and nobody you could harm can come within 1 block of you. A Domain's sure-hit passes through. Press again to drop it (or, with the Six Eyes, to spend 6 CE on Automatic Infinity first: every attack stops, at 1 CE a second).

Maximum

11th

Hollow Purple10 CE · Maximum (X) · hold to pour

Press once to draw Blue and Red apart (free; you're slowed), press again within 6 s to let them meet: a slow sphere of imaginary mass rolls 60 blocks over 8 s, erasing everything it passes, 265 damage every half second to all inside it, through armor. What it catches is dragged along. Or hold the key through the chant (2 s; let go early and it's only drawn): 200% Hollow Purple, half again as wide, rolling twice as far (120 blocks), 529 damage every half second.

Passives

1st

What Infinity can't stop

A Domain's sure-hit, poison already in you, falling, fire and lava, starving, being teleported, what you do to yourself, and anything that isn't an attack.

11th

Teleport

Technique Key 1: Blue's logic applied to yourself. Blink toward where you look, 2 blocks per CE (up to 6 CE).

11th

Delayed Purple

Fire Red into your own held Blue: it detonates at the Blue for Red's cost plus 10 CE, one blow to everything within 8 blocks: 972 damage, through armor.

14th

Automatic Infinity

With the Six Eyes, Infinity rises on its own the first time you're hit with it down (once every 3 s, 1 CE).

15th

The Filter Completes

Infinity stops every attack except bosses' and other sorcerers'.

Domain

18th

Unlimited Void

Everything at once, with no filter. Every 3 s, each foe it chose that isn't touching you takes 107 damage and stands paralyzed, unable to move or act, until the next one (a full minute from the first; bosses shake it off sooner).

Technique feats

1st

Offensive Infinity

The wall that refuses becomes the wall that crushes: while Infinity is up, a foe pressed against it takes 41 damage, is thrown back, and if a wall is behind it, it's pinned there for a second (once every 3 s per foe). Walls and ceilings the field presses on crack and break as they would under an iron pickaxe; sneak to spare them.

3rd

Spatial Skirmishing

Once every 3 s, a melee blow carries Blue (1 CE): it lands as a critical and the foe is pulled in and held for half a second. Every other one carries Red instead: the foe is thrown back.
