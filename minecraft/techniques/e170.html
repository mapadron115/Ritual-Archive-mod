Red Sand Below

Build a hidden network of Tremor Paths beneath the fight, then erupt with acid, lightning, dragging strikes and collapsing ground.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Erupt1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The ground bursts under the foe you look at (12 blocks): 26 damage of acid and lightning, and the soft ground cracks. On a Tremor Path, 53 damage and it's dragged down 2.5 s (a strong foe holds).

1st

Burrow2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute you sink into the ground: unseen, 30% faster and taking half damage, leaving a Tremor Path every 2 blocks (up to 12). Striking surfaces you.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Olgoi-Khorkhoi10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you're the great worm: blows on you land a quarter softer, every Erupt also strikes everything within 3 blocks of its target, and every foe on a Path burns for 26 damage a second and a half.

Passives

1st

Tremor Paths

Paths last ten minutes (up to 12); you see a mote over every creature standing on one.

Technique feats

3rd

Venom Static

Erupt's acid and lightning short-circuit the nerves: its foe is stunned 1.5 s (a strong foe may shrug it off).

6th

False Tremor

Technique Key 1 (1 CE): lay a false Tremor Path on the ground where you look (12 blocks).
