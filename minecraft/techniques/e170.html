Red Sand Below

Build a hidden network of Tremor Paths beneath the fight, then erupt with acid, lightning, dragging strikes and collapsing ground.

5arts
3passives
4technique feats
18thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Erupt1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The ground bursts under the foe you look at (12 blocks): 26 damage of acid and lightning, and the soft ground cracks. On a Tremor Path, 53 damage and it's dragged down 2.5 s (a strong foe holds). Drag Under: a foe within 3 blocks of a Path is pulled onto it and held a 3 s for 2 CE more (a strong foe holds). As the great worm, a 9-block burst that knocks foes down.

1st

Live Wire2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Charge the Path segment nearest your look (36 blocks): everything on it takes 26 damage of lightning, half if it resists.

11th

Network Predator2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Burrowed on a Path: come up from the segment nearest your look, up to 72 blocks away.

1st

Burrow2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute you sink into loose ground (earth, sand, snow, gravel; never worked stone): unseen, 30% faster and taking half damage, leaving a Tremor Path every 6 blocks (up to 18). Striking or leaving loose ground surfaces you. Caustic Tunnel: holding Variant, the segments you lay are acid, 1 CE each (up to 6).

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Olgoi-Khorkhoi10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you're the great worm, huge: weapon blows and shots on you land at half and all else a quarter softer, you burrow through any ground, every Erupt is a 9-block burst that knocks foes down, and every foe on a Path burns for 26 damage a second and a half.

Passives

1st

Tremor Paths

Segments last ten minutes (up to 18); you see a mote over every creature standing on one within 72 blocks.

3rd

Caustic Tunnel

The first foe on an acid segment each 3 s takes 26 damage of acid and is slowed (half and no slow if it resists).

11th

Network Predator

Once every 3 s, a foe tripping an acid segment also gets a Live Wire there, for 1 CE.

Technique feats

3rd

Venom Static

Erupt's acid and lightning short-circuit the nerves: its foe can't react for a 3 s.

6th

False Tremor

Technique Key 1 (1 CE): lay a false Tremor Path on the ground where you look (36 blocks).

3rd

Sand Listener

Variant + Technique Key 1: read the memory of the segment you stand on or look at: how many crossed it in the last ten minutes, and what.

6th

Buried Ambush

An Erupt from underground before you've fought for 10 s is free.
