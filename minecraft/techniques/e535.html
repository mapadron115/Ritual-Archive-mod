Explosion

Palms that sweat nitroglycerin: blasts, piercing AP Shots, blinding Stun Grenades and the diving Howitzer Impact, and the longer the fight runs, the more sweat feeds them.

6arts
3passives

noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Explosion2 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A blast where you look (up to 18 blocks): 20 damage, plus 7 damage per Sweat fed (hold to feed). It blows a pit in soft ground; the big ones leave craters.

1st

Explosion (Cone)2 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A 9-block cone of fire from your palms, the same blast.

3rd

AP Shot3 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A 36-block piercing line that punches through a block of cover: 27 damage, plus up to 2 Sweat.

6th

Stun Grenade2 CE · 5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A blinding flash where you look (12-block radius, up to 36 blocks off): blinded, reeling and weakened for 3 s.

11th

Howitzer Impact6 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Spin-dive up to 36 blocks where you look and detonate (12-block radius): 54 damage, plus 7 damage per 2 Sweat fed.

1st

Blast Dash1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Ride the recoil a dozen blocks the way you look. Looking straight down, it's a Blast Jump: you're thrown up and whatever's under you takes 7 damage.

Passives

1st

Nitroglycerin Sweat

Sweat holds 7: +1 every 3 s (2 while sprinting), +1 when you're hurt (once every 3 s). Hold the cast key to feed it into a blast.

6th

Growing Arsenal

Explosion deals 20 damage before Sweat.

18th

Cluster

No more recoil. Once every 3 s, an explosion that connects pours the rest of your Sweat in: 7 damage each.

Technique feats

1st

Grenadier Gauntlets

Your gauntlets keep what your palms make: Sweat holds 4 more, and every fight starts with at least 2.

1st

Point-Blank

An explosion within 3 blocks of you adds 14 damage and throws the foe back.

11th

Final Howitzer

Once a long rest, Howitzer Impact costs 2 CE less and deals 14 damage more. For one dive, the tornado is bigger and the argument is over.

3rd

AP Shot: Auto-Cannon

Technique Key 1 (1 CE and 1 Sweat): a quick AP Shot, 18 blocks, 14 damage.
