Resonance

Every structure has a resonant frequency: flesh, stone, cursed energy. Tune your foes blow by blow, then break them all at once.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Tuning Pulse1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A ringing pulse along your look, 18 blocks: 9 damage, and everything it touches is tuned once more. Glass and ice in its path shatter.

6th

Breaking Point2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) shatters: 18 damage, 36 damage at 3–4 attunement, 53 damage at 5 or more (a tough foe halves it); its attunement is spent. Aimed at a block no harder than stone, it sings the block apart, as if mined.

3rd

Listen1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute you hear every frequency: foes within 16 blocks and ore within 10 show to you alone, and your blows on a foe tuned 2 or more find its thin spots (more often critical).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Perfect Pitch10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every tuned foe within 36 blocks takes 22 damage per attunement; every untuned one 44 damage (a tough foe halves it). All attunement is spent, and every pane of glass around you bursts.

Passives

1st

Attunement

Each blow you land tunes the foe (up to 8; it fades a minute after your last hit, never from 18th). At 3 or more its flow jams: it hits 15% softer, and a sorcerer pays 1 CE more for every spend. Breaking a foe tuned 5 or more cascades to every tuned foe within 9 blocks, for half.

Technique feats

1st

Sympathetic Ear

You hear cursed energy flow like footsteps: every creature within 16 blocks shows to you.

3rd

Dissonance

Once every 3 s, a creature that hurts you meets a counter-frequency (2 CE): 18 damage, and it hits 30% softer for 3 s.
