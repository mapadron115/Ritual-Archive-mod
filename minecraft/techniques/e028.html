Sky Manipulation

Grab the sky as a sheet: roll foes along it, bend shots through it, wear it as armor and fly, shatter it like thin ice, and fling everything skyward.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Grip the Sky2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Pinch the sky and pull it out of your hand: a sheet 6 blocks along your look for 3 s, following your hand. Foes it sweeps over are rolled along the pull (14 damage), and shots that fly into it bend through its fold and leave along your aim, yours now.

3rd

Sky Armor1 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute the sky hangs on you like a cloak: 17 more armor, and it carries you (you can fly).

11th

Thin Ice Breaker8 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 18-block line of sky shatters like thin ice where you look: 55 damage to everything on it, and fliers are knocked out of the air.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Hurricane10 CE · Maximum (X)

The sky rolls up from your feet and exhales: every foe within 18 blocks that can't hold its ground is hurled high into the air, and comes down hard. Needs the feat Maximum: Hurricane.

Passives

1st

Sky Shape

Once every 3 s (1 CE), a blow about to land on you, or on an ally within 4.5 blocks, may glance off bent sky (about a third of the time). A weapon blow that glances off leaves the arm that swung it numb for 3 s.

6th

Sky Shape Improvement

Once every 3 s (6 CE), a shot that would hit you is bent back the way it came, at whoever fired it.

Domain

18th

Heaven's Gate

Every chosen foe that can't fly is falling: the ground is very far down and coming up fast (41 damage).

Cloak of Invisibility

Still water, unseen: under Sky Armor, after 2 s without attacking you turn invisible and creatures lose track of you, until you strike.

11th
