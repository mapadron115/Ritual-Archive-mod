Nine Shadows of the Court

The nine-tailed fox of the Heian court: foxfire that burns the idea of you, shadows that take your place, and a smile that turns kingdoms.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Foxfire1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Foxfire along your aim (72 blocks): 11 fire damage, double under the Ninth Tail.

3rd

The Court Wants3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (36 blocks) sees what it most desires: a creature turns on whatever is nearest it for 9 s (a steady mind resists). A player or a great foe isn't turned: it fights weakened for a minute.

6th

Sessho-seki's Breath5 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The Killing Stone's vapor down a cone before you (18 blocks): 53 poison damage and poisoned for 10 s (a hardy foe takes half and isn't).

11th

The Smile That Ruins Kingdoms8 CE · 12 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every foe within 36 blocks may be turned, as by The Court Wants.

1st

Nine Shadows2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A fox shadow of your court fights beside you for a minute, biting for 18 fire damage once every 3 s. Up to nine; they can be struck down. 1 CE under the Ninth Tail.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Ninth Tail10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute all nine tails show: Foxfire deals double dice, a Shadow costs 1 CE, and blows from foes within 36 blocks land a fifth softer on you.

Passives

3rd

Never Where You Think

Once every 3 s (2 CE), a creature's blow meant for you lands on one of your Shadows within 36 blocks instead: you change places.

11th

Tails as Lives

Once a long rest, a blow that would fell you severs a tail instead: your Shadows vanish, you're at half health, and you're gone in foxfire.

Domain

18th

The Gilded Cage of Nine Tails

Every chosen foe sees its most trusted beloved. Each pulse it burns in foxfire, 33 fire damage, and a creature is turned on its own side until the next.
