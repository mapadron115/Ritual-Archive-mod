Hair Manipulation

Stingers, explosive drills, and helicopter or jet hair-forms, crowned by a Domain that shreds everything that moves.

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Hair Stingers1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Three needles of hardened hair along your look (18 blocks, 1.5 more): 18 damage for the first on each foe, 18 damage for each after.

3rd

Explosive Drill3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A spiraling drill of hair that bores through soft blocks and bursts where it stops: 71 damage within 3.0 blocks (a nimble foe may halve it).

6th

Aircraft Hair3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute of flight: your hair becomes a Helicopter (hover; a melee striker meets the blades for 14 damage) or a Jet (Technique Key 1: fly fast and ram what you pass for 18 damage, throwing it along your line and knocking it down unless it resists).

6th

Mega-Drill4 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

With the feat: one drill made enormous, a 24-block line of piercing ruin along your look, through walls, 66 damage to everything on it; it bores a tunnel through the soft ground it crosses.
