❖ e009 · Jujutsu Kaisen ❖

Cursed Spirit Manipulation

An unlimited stored collection of the creatures you've beaten, a bounded active roster, sacrificial defense, Uzumaki and Womb Profusion.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Release Spirit0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) and Quick (G)

Call a stored spirit of the chosen kind, the very creature you beat, to fight beside you for ten minutes (CE by its grade; up to 5 at once; a boss answers only alone). It fights the way it always did. Technique Key 1 chooses the kind, strongest first (Variant goes back); the summons answer faster. Variant calls every spirit out back into your collection, free and unspent.

1st

Absorb1 CE · Cast (Z)

Swallow the hostile creature you look at (16 blocks) without killing it, if its grade is below 6: it resists with its will, and if it fails it becomes a black orb in your collection. A boss, whatever its grade, once it's beaten down to a tenth of its health. Never a player, a pet or a conjured creature. Variant turns absorbing on or off: on, whatever you kill becomes an orb you swallow (a boss's is hard to keep down: you're weakened a while); off, kills stay dead.

11th

Extracted Action4 CE · Cast (Z)

Use what Uzumaki pulled out of a spirit it spent, the chosen kind if it was among them, else the strongest. You hold one at a time, and it's gone once used. A shot or a fist, 40 damage; a blast, fire or venom, 26 damage with what it brings.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Uzumaki4 CE · Maximum (X)

Every active spirit spirals into one blast: a 15-block cone, 26 damage, plus 13 damage and 2.5 blocks for every spirit beyond the second. The spirits are spent, and one of their actions stays in your hand. With Variant, up to 10 stored spirits of the chosen kind are poured in as well, and they don't come back.

Passives

3rd

Curse-Infused Spirits

Your energy runs through what you call: a spirit gets 50% more health, 5 more melee damage and 10% more speed.

3rd

Sacrificial Defense

Once every 3 s, a heavy blow on you is thrown onto your nearest spirit (CE by its grade): it lands softer by d20s by its grade (as % of your health), and the spirit is destroyed.

6th

Perfect Control

Your spirits keep closer, and switch to the foe you strike at once.

Domain

18th

Womb Profusion

A procession of curses engulfs every foe it chose: 89 damage every 3 s, and a weak undead creature is swallowed into your collection outright. Inside, releasing spirits costs nothing.

Technique feats

6th

Defensive Summoning

With no spirit out, Sacrificial Defense calls one from your collection to take the blow (5 CE plus its grade; it's spent).

11th

Minimum: Uzumaki

With fewer than two spirits out, the Maximum without Variant spends two stored spirits of the chosen kind instead: a small, seeking Uzumaki at the foe you look at, 13 damage per point of their grade (twice it, up to 20).

11th

Uzumaki Versatility

A Minimum: Uzumaki with no foe in sight waits in your hand for up to a minute and fires itself at the first foe within 30 blocks.
