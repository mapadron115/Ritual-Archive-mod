Anti-Cursed Grimoire

Zero cursed energy, and swords that unmake the premise curses are built on. Anti-Energy fuels the Demon-Slayer, Black Form, and the devil's union.

Maximum

Maximum: Infinite Slash Equinox0 CE · Maximum (X)

Passives

1st

Zero CE

You have no cursed energy to spend: Anti-Energy (52) runs everything. It returns on a long rest, or after two minutes out of a fight.

1st

Demon-Slayer Sword

Your melee blows bite 11 damage deeper into anything that casts. Once every 3 s (1 Anti-Energy), a shot is blocked with the flat: it lands at half.
