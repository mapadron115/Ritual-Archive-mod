Anti-Cursed Grimoire

Zero cursed energy, and swords that unmake the premise curses are built on. Anti-Energy fuels the Demon-Slayer, Black Form, and the devil's union.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

6th

Demon-Dweller Slash0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 Anti-Energy: a 36-block anti-magic slash where you look, 26 damage to everything on it. It cuts down any block a technique conjured. With Variant and an ally within 3 blocks, it wears their technique's colour.

9th

Demon-Destroyer0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

2 Anti-Energy: cut every harmful effect off yourself, or off the ally you look at; and every effect off the foe within 4 blocks you look at (it's weakened for a 3 s too). A conjured creature in reach with under 50 health is unmade outright; a bigger one takes 106 damage instead. Free once every 3 s.

11th

Devil Union0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

4 Anti-Energy, a minute (any number of times): you fly, you're fast, shots and every technique's damage land at half, and your blows leave a foe unable to counter, dodge or parry for 3 s. True Devil Union: once every 3 s, a blow on a sorcerer who fails to resist puts the art they last cast on cooldown for 3 s.

13th

Black Divider0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

2 Anti-Energy: the sword lengthens into a 12-block arc in front of you, above and below too: your weapon's blow to each, and 40 damage more to anything that casts or was conjured. Conjured blocks in it are cut down.

15th

Demon-Slasher Katana0 CE · 1.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

3 Anti-Energy: a selective 36-block line, 66 damage, and what it cuts is weakened.

3rd

Black Form0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

2 Anti-Energy, a minute: faster, a far higher jump, and once every 3 s your blow on a sorcerer breaks their concentration (a Domain being cast, a chant, a held art).

Maximum

1st

Maximum: Infinite Slash Equinox0 CE · Maximum (X)

Every Anti-Energy (at least 6), once a long rest: a 72-block line that walls don't stop, 106 damage to every foe on it, cutting down every conjured block in its way. A closed Domain's barrier it crosses takes its hardest blow, and every sorcerer it cuts loses their concentration.

Passives

1st

Zero CE

You have no cursed energy at all: none to spend, none to steal. Anti-Energy (52) runs everything. It returns on a long rest, or after two minutes out of a fight. A fight starts you with 6 or more.

1st

Demon-Slayer Sword

Your melee blows bite 13 damage deeper into anything that casts or was conjured. Once every 3 s (1 Anti-Energy), a shot or a technique's flying shot is blocked with the flat: it lands at half.

Technique feats

1st

Anti-Magic Swordsman

Once every 3 s (1 Anti-Energy), a shot or a technique's flying shot about to hit an ally within 3 blocks is blocked with the flat: it lands at half.

3rd

Bull Thrust

In Black Form, a melee blow at a full sprint drives the sword home: 26 damage more, and it's thrown back (once every 3 s).

6th

Sword Flight

You ride the sword: while Black Form lasts you can fly.

11th

Liebe's Partner

When Devil Union ends, the departing devil discharges: every foe within 18 blocks loses every effect it carries, and fights weaker for 10 s.
