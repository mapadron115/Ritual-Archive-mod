Stone Cutting Style

A martial technique that turns the body into a chisel: blows tear through stone, and two forms focus that breaking force.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Stone Cutting Strike2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (3 blocks): 33 damage, and the stone around it cracks apart, mined loose.

1st

Swift Fury1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Three chisel blows on the foe you look at (3 blocks): 9 damage each.

Passives

1st

The Body Is the Tool

Your weapon blows bite 9 damage harder (once a second), and you break stone twice as fast.

Technique feats

3rd

Keystone Break

When the Stone Cutting Strike cracks stone apart, the break keeps going (1 CE): foes within 3 blocks take 26 damage (a nimble one may halve it).

6th

Living Quarry

Golems, shulkers, silverfish and other things of stone or metal are quarry to you: everything you deal them lands half again as hard.
