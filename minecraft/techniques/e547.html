Hemline

The threaded needle: stitch foes to the floor, pull them where you want them, and dress the whole battlefield to your measurements.

5arts
1passives
1technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Needlework1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A cursed needle (36 blocks): 18 damage, and what it hits is Threaded for as long as it lives.

3rd

Hemming2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) has its shadow sewn to the floor: it can't move for 3 s, and it's Threaded.

6th

Stitch2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at is stitched in place for 1.5 s (a strong foe may hold); a threaded foe can't, and is held for 3 s.

11th

The Full Wardrobe8 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once every 5 minutes: every foe within 18 blocks is yanked 9 blocks toward you and stitched for 3 s, no holding out.

1st

Pull1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at is yanked 4.5 blocks toward you (a strong foe may hold); a threaded foe is caught from anywhere in reach, and can't. With no foe in view, the loose item, boat or cart you look at is reeled in.

Passives

Needlework is free, and threads hold until the foe dies.
