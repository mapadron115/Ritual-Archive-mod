❖ e161 · SCP Anomalies ❖

Refining Clockworks

Feed things into a portable cursed clockwork and choose how aggressively it interprets 'refinement', then decide if one more Very Fine cycle is worth the risk.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Rough1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The clockwork grinds the foe you look at (2.5 blocks; 6 with Decoupled Line): 18 damage, and 1 Scrap (2 if it's ground to nothing). With no foe there, the block you look at is ground one step rougher.

6th

Very Fine5 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

2 Scrap: a far greater refinement for a minute (your blows hit half again as hard), but one cycle in four the machine overdoes it and your weapon is ruined for that minute instead (30% softer).

3rd

Fine3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

1 Scrap: your weapon is refined for a minute: 10% harder-hitting and 10% quicker.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Masterwork Cycle10 CE · Maximum (X)

3 Scrap: a flawless Very Fine for ten minutes, no risk.

Passives

1st

Scrap

Rough yields Scrap, up to 11. A long rest empties the hopper.

1st

1:1 (Technique Key 1)

1 CE: the plain item in your hand becomes its equivalent, one for one: the next wood, the next colour, or the next stone. Items with enchantments, names or other data are refused.

Technique feats

1st

Salvage Intake

A foe you kill within 6 blocks yields 1 Scrap about half the time.

3rd

Decoupled Line

Intake here, Output there: Rough reaches 6 blocks.

6th

Restorative Refinement

Very Fine on a ruined weapon runs backward: it's restored and refined to Fine for a minute, no risk.
