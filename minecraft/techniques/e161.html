Refining Clockworks

Feed things into a portable cursed clockwork and choose how aggressively it interprets 'refinement', then decide if one more Very Fine cycle is worth the risk.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Portable Clockworks2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Two frames stand where you look, an Intake and an Output, for ten minutes: throw a plain item into the Intake and it comes out of the Output refined by the dial (Technique Key 1: Rough, Coarse, 1:1, Fine, Very Fine). It takes whole stacks, twice as fast. The frames fall if you go more than 36 blocks from them. Items with enchantments, names or other data are refused.

1st

Rough1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The clockwork grinds the foe you look at (2.5 blocks; 6 with Decoupled Line): 18 damage, and 1 Scrap (2 if it's ground to nothing). With no foe there, the block you look at is ground one step rougher.

11th

Very Fine5 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

2 Scrap: the item in your hand is read far too well. Roll a d12 less your Risk (on your status line), then Risk rises by 1: 1 or less, Misread: ruined (30% softer) for a minute, and you take 10% of your health plus 5% per Risk, Risk +2 more; 2, Inverse: one Fine, and its swings are 20% slower; 3-4, Oblique: one Fine and a strange property (it glows, or knocks back hard); 5-7, Superior: two Fines for ten minutes; 8-9, Anomalous: two Fines and lifesteal 10% or a burning edge; 10 or more, Impossible: blows half again as hard for a minute. A roll of 5 or more also leaves a random enchantment the item can take (not a curse), kept for good, in place of any enchantments it had. With Risk and 2 Scrap to spare, 2 of it takes 1 Risk off first. A long rest sets Risk to 0. Master Dial: once a long rest, Variant sets the roll to 10 (then Risk becomes 5).

3rd

Fine3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

1 Scrap: the item in your hand is refined for 600 s, one Fine per item, chosen by what it is: Keen on a weapon or tool (10% harder-hitting), Reinforced on armor or a shield (+2 armor while you hold or wear it), Efficient on anything else (while you carry it, your first Clockworks art once every 3 s costs 1 CE less).

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Interpretation Engine10 CE · Maximum (X)

3 Scrap: the item in your hand is Impossible for ten minutes, ignoring Risk (blows half again as hard). Afterwards Risk is 5, and you're in Burnout for 3 s.

Passives

1st

Scrap

Rough yields Scrap, up to 11. A long rest empties the hopper.

1st

Thrift

With 2 Scrap to spare beyond what an art needs, the machine spends it to take 1 CE off a Clockworks art (never below 1; not Rough or Very Fine).

1st

The dial (Technique Key 1)

Technique Key 1 turns the dial. Rough grinds an item into Scrap by its size: 1 for most, 2 for tools and blocks, 3 for armor and workstations, 4 for anvils and furnaces. Coarse (1 CE) takes an item apart into its crafting ingredients, minus one of the most numerous; it needs the recipe's full output count, and an item made from one ingredient gives Scrap instead. 1:1 (1 CE) makes it its equivalent, one for one: the next wood, the next colour, the next stone. Fine and Very Fine refine it as the arts do, at their price. Variant + Technique Key 1 is 1:1 on the item in your hand.

6th

Compound Cycle

The machine takes whole stacks and works twice as fast.

18th

Master Dial

Very Fine rolls a d12, and once a long rest Variant + Very Fine sets the roll to 10 (then Risk becomes 5).

Technique feats

1st

Salvage Intake

A foe you kill within 6 blocks yields 1 Scrap about half the time, and a block broken within 18 blocks of your Intake yields 1 Scrap (once a second).

3rd

Decoupled Line

Intake here, Output there: Variant + Portable Clockworks moves the Output to where you look, up to 18 blocks from the Intake. Rough reaches 6 blocks.

11th

Restorative Refinement

With the dial on Very Fine, a worn plain tool in the Intake comes out fully repaired, no roll and no Risk, for 5 CE and 3 Scrap.

3rd

Hot-Swap Tuning

Variant + Fine moves the Fine on the item in your main hand to the item in your off hand.
