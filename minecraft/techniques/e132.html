Worldwaker

Claim patches of the world as Lands, draw on them, wake them into elementals, and finally make every claim erupt at once.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Vine Lash1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks): 18 damage; on one of your Lands, 27 damage and bound for 3 s (a strong foe may tear free).

3rd

Awaken Land3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The nearest Land (or the ground where you look) rises as an elemental for 10 minutes: 22 damage a blow (two at a time).

13th

Animist's Awakening5 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Up to 6 of your Lands rise for a moment: each slams the nearest foe on it, 27 damage.

11th

World Shaper3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A wall of the ground's own earth rises where you look for a minute.

1st

Claim Land1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The ground where you look (18 blocks) becomes your Land for 10 minutes, 6 blocks around, and what grows there grows (up to 10; the oldest returns to the wild).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Worldwake10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every Land erupts (or, with none, the ground where you look): 55 damage to everything within 4.5 blocks of each, thrown up (a nimble foe halves it).

Passives

9th

Elemental Bond

Two elementals at once, and when one lands a blow you move freely for a moment.

15th

Vastwood

You and your allies standing on your Lands are quicker.

20th

Soul of the World

Up to 10 Lands. Once a long rest, Variant + Technique Key 1: for a minute everything within 36 blocks of you is your Land.

1st

Leyline

Standing on one of your Lands, one cast every 3 s costs 1 CE less.
