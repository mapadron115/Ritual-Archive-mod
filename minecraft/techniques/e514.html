Crazy Slots

A clown, a lottery, nine weapons: spin and fight with whatever the clown deals. A drawn weapon can't be dismissed until it's used. And there's a number you've never rolled.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Use Weapon0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Attack with the weapon the clown dealt: the Daggers (2 seeking throws), the Scythe (3 sweeps), the Bow (3 arrows), the Hammer (2 blows that crack the ground), the Gun (3 shots), the Shield (blocks one blow), the Whip (one grab) or the Mace (2 blows that knock the foe away).

3rd

Second Spin2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest: spin twice, and you must take the second.

11th

Double Feature2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest: deal two weapons at once.

18th

The Clown's Contract2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Pay some of your health: spin twice and keep the one that fits the fight (a close weapon if a foe is near). With Variant you name it defensive: the Shield, if either roll gives it.

1st

Spin the Clown2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Spin the clown's reel (it never lands on 9) and draw that weapon.

Maximum

1st

Full House5 CE · Maximum (X)

A minute: all nine at once, no conditions. Use Weapon swings a random one. When it ends the clown collects: slowed and weakened for 30 s.

Passives

6th

Encore

Once a long rest, when a weapon is used up, the clown spins again at once (2 CE).

20th

The Number You Never Rolled

Once a long rest, at 0 health: 3 CE, back to half health, holding the Survivor, which deals double to whatever brought you down.

Domain

18th

The House Always Deals

Everyone plays: each chosen foe juggles the clown's prop and hits 30% softer until the next pulse.

Technique feats

1st

The Clown Knows

When you spin, the clown whispers where the foe you look at is soft: its armor is halved for a minute.

3rd

Rigged Applause

Technique Key 1 (1 CE): flourish the weapon in your hand; foes within 9 blocks that fall for it stop to watch for 3 s.

6th

Curtain Call

Once a long rest, within a minute of a weapon being used up, Variant + Use Weapon hurls its echo: one more attack with it (1 CE).

11th

Prop Master

Variant + Technique Key 1 (1 CE): your last used-up weapon is left where you look as an innocent prop for an hour; the next foe to touch it takes one blow of it. One at a time.
