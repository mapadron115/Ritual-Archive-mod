Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

3rd

Energy Blast1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Energy Blast form: hold to pour 1 to 6 CE, a blast of force along your look (24 blocks): 7 damage per CE, bursting where it lands.

3rd

Defensive Ward1 CE · Quick (G) · hold to pour

Defensive Form: hold to pour 1 to 6 CE, a ward of 5% of your health per CE that soaks blows. It stacks to half your health, and fades after 30 s out of a fight.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Core Release5 CE · Maximum (X)

Depends on the form that's awake. Unblockable: Crippling Strikes, 10 s of free, harder Unblockable Blows. Energy Blast: Blast Away, the recoil hurls you toward where you look and knocks down everything behind you. Defensive: Fortress, the ward bursts outward, what's left of it as a blow to everything within 2 blocks.

Passives

1st

First Core

Your body keeps a backup: 20% more health.

3rd

Second Core

Variant + Technique Key 1 steps through the combat forms; Technique Key 1 claims the one shown as a core and wakes it. Unblockable Blows: after a blow lands, 1 CE (once a second) makes it ignore armor and land a quarter harder. While it's awake, your body grows a quarter bigger.

6th

Third Core

Claim a second form. One burns at a time; Technique Key 1 switches.

11th

Insurance

Struck down: a heart stops instead. That core seals for 7 days and you rise at half health. With both sealed, nothing catches you.

Domain

18th

Rule of Three

A law, not an event: while it stands, all of your unsealed hearts burn at once.
