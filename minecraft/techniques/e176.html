Gleipnir Breaker

Begin under self-imposed chains: keep them for stability, snap them when their trigger comes to release Ragnarök, and turn every restraint into fuel.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Fenris Bite1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (3 blocks): 22 damage, and 11 damage more for every Ragnarök token you spend (all of them).

1st

Wolf's Lunge0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

1 Ragnarök token: 9 blocks along your look, and slowness and holds fall off you. Ending a hold this way gives you temporary health.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Ragnarök Unbound8 CE · Maximum (X)

With 3 or more Bonds broken, for a minute, spending every token: you're huge, 10% faster and 11 damage of temporary health a token, once every 3 s a blow gains 5 damage a token (32 damage at most), every third shakes the ground for 11 damage, and nothing frightens or holds you. When it ends every Bond is broken until a long rest, and you're spent.

Passives

1st

Bonds

5 Bonds, prepared out of a fight with none broken (Technique Key 1 picks one, Variant + Key 1 prepares or drops it): Tyr's Hand (no healing from others: regeneration can't take hold on you; snaps when you fall below half health); Silent Fang (a whisper: your chat reaches only 8 blocks, no chant, no spoken words; snaps when you're frightened or gagged, or an ally names you while you're hurt); Moon Chain (no art that costs more than 2 CE; snaps when a foe near you spends 5 CE at once or opens a Domain); Dromi (no reactions; snaps when an ally within 18 blocks is struck); Gleipnir (30% slower; snaps when a foe you're fighting stands 18 blocks off or more); Laeding (no weapons: weapon blows do nothing; snaps when a weapon wounds you). A Bond snaps only when its trigger comes: a shockwave (18 damage within 3 blocks), a Ragnarök token (up to your Bonds), and its restriction is gone. While Bonds hold, blows on you land 5% softer for each and nothing throws you. Broken Bonds reform after two minutes out of a fight; tokens keep until a long rest.

3rd

The Chain Remembers

Being slowed or held gives you a Chain token for two 3 ss (one at a time), spent like a Ragnarök token.

6th

Wolf at the Rope

A Bond snapping frightens foes within 6 blocks (a strong will may hold). Variant on a blow: 2 tokens and 1 CE for 11 damage more, and the foe is knocked flat.

11th

Five Impossible Bindings

Holding 3 Bonds or more, one art a 3 s costs 1 CE less. The third Bond to snap in a fight frees you of holds and throws you 12 blocks along your look.

Technique feats

3rd

Slipped Leash

Once every 3 s, when something slows or holds you, you slip it at once, 6 blocks along your look.

6th

Moon-Eater

When Moon Chain snaps, the foe who snapped it takes 7 damage as the chain lashes back.

6th

Unbroken Oath

Finish a fight with no Bond broken: the next time your picked Bond's trigger comes, it holds (no token).
