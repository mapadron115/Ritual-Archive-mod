Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

3rd

Empathic Bolt2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A bolt of someone's pain, 22 damage (44 damage as the Daughter of Trigon). Hold it a second to pour 2 CE more for 9 damage more.

1st

Soul-Self1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For 10 minutes your soul-self, a raven of shadow, flies beside you (36 blocks). It strikes a foe near you once a 3 s for 22 damage.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Daughter of Trigon10 CE · Maximum (X)

Stop filtering, for a minute: you fly, blows on you land at half, your bolts hit harder, your bare fists hit 44 damage, and foes within 18 blocks are afraid (they hit 25% softer). Taking the form, a weak will lets Trigon steer: you strike the nearest creature within 6 blocks, friend or foe. Once a 3 s, Technique Key 1 is a hellfire wave, 66 damage within 9 blocks, and the ground there catches fire.

Passives

1st

Empathic Sense

You feel every creature within 16 blocks: a mote shows over each, to you alone.

11th

Shared Pain

Once every 3 s (2 CE), a creature that hurts you takes half of it back; or, when one hits an ally within 9 blocks, you take the blow in their place and the attacker takes half of it back.

18th

Azarath Ascendant

Your arts cost 1 CE less, and your soul-self ranges 90 blocks. Outside the Daughter of Trigon, Technique Key 1 is White Raven (1 CE, a minute): your empathic arts deal 9 damage more, and nothing frightens you.

Technique feats

3rd

Trigon's Bargain

Once every 3 s, one of your arts costing 2 CE or more takes its price in pain instead: 2 CE less, paid in health (never below a quarter).

3rd

Still Point

Variant + Technique Key 1 (2 CE): for a minute creatures past 6 blocks or out of sight lose you, and nothing sees you glow through walls.

6th

Grief Eater

Once every 3 s (2 CE), a blow that would kill an ally within 18 blocks leaves them at 1 health, and you take 22 damage that nothing reduces (never below 1).

6th

Nevermore

Projecting your soul-self unfurls the dread: foes within 9 blocks hit 20% softer for a minute (a strong will holds).
