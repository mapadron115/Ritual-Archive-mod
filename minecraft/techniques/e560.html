Revenge Counter3 CE · Cast (Z)

Everything you've endured since your last Revenge, on top of your weapon's blow, to the foe you look at in reach (4.5 blocks). Past 270 the excess costs you half of itself. A long rest empties the ledger.

1st

Full Counter2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A split-second guard. A technique, magic, fire, lightning or a blast that lands in it is caught: you take nothing, and it returns to its sender twice as hard. A physical blow (a weapon, a fist, an arrow) can only be parried: half lands, and all of it goes to Revenge. With Variant the guard is amplified: a caught blow also spends up to 6 more CE, 13 damage more each. The Captain's Counter: the guard also catches blows on an ally within 18 blocks.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Absolute Counter15 CE · Maximum (X)

Once a long rest, for a minute: every blow that reaches you is countered on its own (1 CE each), returned two and a half times as hard, and Counter Vanish costs 1 CE. After it, the arm needs 3 s before it counters again.
