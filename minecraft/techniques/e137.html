Nightmare Weaver

Plant fear, harvest it, and sculpt it into nightmares shaped like what your enemies dread most.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Nightmare Touch1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks): 18 damage, and if its nerve breaks, a Fear Mark (up to 3): each makes it hit 10% softer for a minute. A player bearing all three is haunted: the phantoms of the sleepless night come for them until they sleep.

9th

Nightmare Shikigami4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A full nightmare for a minute: 27 damage a bite (two at a time).

11th

Exile Thought3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The creature you look at (18 blocks) is shelved for 3 s: a conjured one is held and unseen; a player can't cast.

3rd

Dream Token1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A small nightmare springs up beside the foe you look at and hunts for 6 s (9 damage a bite). If the foe bears a Fear Mark it feeds on one and lasts 20 s.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Nightmare Incarnate10 CE · Maximum (X)

Your fears fuse into one Huge nightmare for a minute: 55 damage a bite, and a die more for each stored Dream token it spends (up to 3).

Passives

1st

Fear Marks

A marked foe hits 10% softer per Mark, and your nightmares bite it harder. Nightmare Touch with Variant consumes its Marks instead, 9 damage more each.

6th

Blueprint

Technique Key 1 picks the shape your Nightmare Shikigami wakes in. Hunter: a die harder on a marked foe. Cage: its first bite on a foe each 3 s holds it 1.5 s. Abyss: foes within 4.5 blocks of it are in darkness and shaken.

13th

Fear Made Flesh

Your Nightmare Shikigami bites a die harder.

15th

Nightmare Harvest

A marked foe that dies leaves its Marks as stored Dream tokens (up to 6).

20th

Fear Has No Face

Two Nightmare Shikigami. Once a long rest, Variant + Nightmare Shikigami costs 1 CE and a stored Dream token.

Domain

18th

The Unwritten Nightmare

The gallery opens: each pulse every chosen foe meets its fear. Reveal: 66 damage, and its fear is named to you. Run: thrown 9 blocks toward you and marked.
