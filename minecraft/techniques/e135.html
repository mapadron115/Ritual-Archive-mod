Silver Golem

A silver body that needs no breath, an unmaking touch, and a workshop of artifacts that wake at your word.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Unmaking Touch1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe in reach (3.5 blocks): 18 damage as you take it apart.

9th

Null Rod3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) has its ranged powers extinguished for a minute: its shots fizzle and it can't cast (a strong presence resists).

6th

Liquimetal2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Metal flows into cover where you look: a wall of iron bars, 5 wide and 2 high, for a minute.

13th

Legacy Weapon5 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Three of your constructs fuse into one great construct for a minute: 60 health, 55 damage a blow.

15th

Creator's Touch3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You mend, and your constructs are renewed for another minute.

3rd

Animate Artifact2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The metal block you look at wakes as a construct for a minute (27 damage a blow; the block returns when it ends). With none in view, a plain one (18 damage). Up to 10.
