Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Immobilizing Eyes2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Look at the foe nearest where you look within 18 blocks: while you keep it in sight it moves at half speed, hits softer and its guard drops; 1 CE every 3 s per foe to keep it. Hold the cast first to sweep up to 5 foes, 1 CE each beyond the first. A foe that resists wounds your eyes.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Paralyzing Gaze6 CE · 6 s cooldown · Maximum (X)

One foe you can see within 36 blocks is paralyzed, frozen even in mid-air, for 3 s, and stays under your gaze after. Straining against you, it may wound your eyes twice over.

Passives

1st

Your eyes

Your eyes have their own life (half of yours). Each foe that resists the gaze wounds them; they recover slowly. At zero you're blind, and they won't recover until a long rest.

6th

Reactive Eye Movement

A foe within 18 blocks that strikes at you meets your eyes (2 CE, every 3 s): a gazed foe always misses; another misses unless it resists. Technique Key 1: Eye Drops, 1 CE, close your eyes a moment and they recover.

Domain

18th

Chamber of Fractured Reflections

Mirrors on every side: your gaze can't be broken inside, and three chosen foes are held by it at every sure-hit, at no cost to your eyes.
