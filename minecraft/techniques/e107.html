Crimson Legion

A walking graveyard. Consumed souls are extra lives, summonable familiars, and the fuel for your most horrifying release.

8arts
4passives
4technique feats
1stMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Blood Drinker1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Drink the soul of a fresh body within 6 blocks (bodies you walk over are drunk on their own).

3rd

Black Hound2 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute: a black hound pours out of your shadow and hunts beside you (26 damage a bite). While it's out, once every 3 s your blow lands 26 damage harder. Baskerville (feat): with Variant, a foe one of your hounds holds within 12 blocks is dragged to your feet (a soul).

9th

Named Familiar3 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute: raise your finest stored soul as the creature it was, fighting for you, with 120 health or its own if that's more. A shooter keeps its weapon and its shot. Named Dead (feat): with Variant you commune with it instead, and every creature of its kind within 48 blocks glows for a minute.

11th

Vampiric Mist2 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Until the next 3 s: you're mist. Nothing touches you, you're hard to see, and you can't strike.

13th

Restriction Level One5 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A minute: release up to 6 familiars at once, your named souls first, then hounds. While it lasts, Extra Life costs 2 souls.

18th

Restriction Level Zero10 CE · Cast (Z)

Ten minutes: the court deployed. 10 swarms of bats and hounds (a soul each) and every named familiar. No Extra Life while they're out.

1st

Feed1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Spend a soul: heal 20% of your health (about).

Maximum

1st

Maximum: Sea of the Dead12 CE · Maximum (X)

30 souls released as a tide out of the ground within 36 blocks: 132 damage to every foe, held by the dead for 3 s.

Passives

1st

Soul Stock

Up to 80 souls; a long rest starts you with at least 20. Up to 6 elite souls (a creature with 40+ health) are kept by kind.

6th

Extra Life

Automatic, 3 CE + 3 souls, once every 3 s: at 0 health you stand at 1 and heal 20%. Not while Level Zero is out.

11th

Vampiric Body

Blows land 15% softer, and you climb any wall you walk into.

15th

True Vampire

Once every 3 s, a kill feeds you its soul as it falls (1 CE).

Technique feats

3rd

Baskerville

Your hounds hunt with a body now: their bites seize what they hit, holding it a moment.

3rd

Casull & Jackal

Technique Key 1 (1 CE): both cursed pistols fire where you look, 18 damage each.

9th

Named Dead

Variant + Named Familiar communes with your finest stored soul instead of raising it: every creature of its kind within 48 blocks glows for a minute.

6th

No Life King

When Extra Life saves you, one more soul lets the moment land: foes within 6 blocks that see it freeze in dread for 3 s.
