Empty Herd

Open feeding wounds and drain blood. Every feeding drives an automatic metamorphosis: gaunt predator, fed hunter, swollen apex.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Feed1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Drain the foe you touch (2.5 blocks), once every 3 s a creature: 18 damage, and you heal half of it; a wounded foe gives 27 damage and its wound closes. A sorcerer also loses 3 CE. Every feeding advances your body. Pounce: after running 12 blocks, a blow on a wounded foe makes your next Feed free.

6th

Goat-Sucker Leap2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

You pounce onto the foe you look at (6 blocks) and Feed.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Night of the Empty Pens10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you sense every living thing within 36 blocks, run 30% faster and climb freely, and every blow opens a wound. Feed reaches 9 blocks on a spectral tether, the first each 3 s is free, and each feeding counts twice and heals 7 damage more. When the night ends you're Gaunt again.

Passives

1st

Feeding Wound

Once every 3 s (1 CE), a weapon blow opens a wound (up to 11 at once): it bleeds 7 damage every 3 s for a minute.

1st

Metamorphosis

Gaunt (0–1 feedings): blows on an unhurt foe add 7 damage. Fed (2–3): 20% faster, and Feed heals all of it. Swollen Apex (4+): every blow adds 7 damage, but you're 15% slower. Gaunt in the dark, creatures lose you past 8 blocks. A long rest with no feeding since the last resets it.

3rd

Night Scent

Every wounded creature within 180 blocks shows to you alone, through walls.

11th

Empty the Pen

Once every 3 s, a wounded sorcerer within 18 blocks who spends 3 CE or more at once is fed on where it stands. One fed on at 0 CE gets none back for a 3 s.

Technique feats

1st

Purging Molt

Technique Key 1: regress one stage to slough tainted flesh; poison, withering, nausea, weakness and slowness fall away.

3rd

Spraying Wound

Once every 3 s, a critical blow sprays: a second foe beside the target gains a Feeding Wound, free.

6th

Packless Hunter

With no ally within 9 blocks, once every 3 s your blow on a wounded foe drives it from the herd: it reels away frightened and hits 30% softer 3 s (a strong will may hold).

1st

Livestock Sense

The creature you look at within 18 blocks: you smell whether it carries CE and whether it's below half health.
