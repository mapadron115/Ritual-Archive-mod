The Possessive Mask

Wear one body until it rots, tempt another into accepting the mask, and move your will across hosts.

3arts
3passives
4technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Temptation1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (36 blocks) is tempted by the mask: one that gives in gains Temptation (up to 3). At 1 the Sweetened Offer can't be refused; at 2 it's slowed going away from you; at 3 it can be worn. It fades after a minute, and each 3 s a foe may shake one off. A player sorcerer at 3 shows you the last art they cast.

3rd

Host Transfer3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The mask settles on the creature you touch (2.5 blocks) with 3 Temptation: your host, turning on its own kind, for up to a minute (from the second 3 s it may throw you off). Its wounds land on you instead (Shared Vitality). Players and great foes can't be worn. Borrowed Bodies (6th): with Variant, something you killed in the last 10 s rises as your host, no resist, with 80 health, for 5 minutes. Inside your Domain, a foe at 3 Temptation within 36 blocks can be worn without a touch.

1st

Sweetened Offer0 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at lowers its guard and goes docile for 3 s (any blow wakes it). A tempted foe can't refuse and stays docile twice as long (it spends a Temptation).

Passives

1st

Corrosive Secretion

Once every 3 s, 1 CE, a melee blow seeps: the foe has to resist or be held for 3 s. Technique Key 1 corrodes the soft block you look at within reach.

1st

Manifest Mask

A porcelain mask: you see a mote over every mind within 72 blocks.

11th

Mind Under the Mask

When your host falls, the mask leaps (2 CE) to another foe within 18 blocks that could be worn.

Domain

18th

Theater of the Anguished Mask

Curtain Call: the whole house is tempted at once, every chosen foe gains a Temptation.

Technique feats

1st

Honeyed Words

A Sweetened Offer on an untempted foe that accepts it also gives it a Temptation (1 CE).

6th

Black Ichor

A block you corrode away leaves a black patch for 3 s that slows foes who walk into it.

6th

Borrowed Aptitude

While you wear a host, you keep the best of its training: your blows hit 10% harder and you have 4 more armor.

11th

Never Without a Face

Once a long rest, a blow that would kill you while you wear a host is paid by the host instead: it dies, and you stand at a quarter of your health.
