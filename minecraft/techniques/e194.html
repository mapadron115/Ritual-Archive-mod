Red Lioness Fever

Consecutive slaughter builds Heat: speed and damage that, at its height, burns the lioness too. Escalate, or cool.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Lioness Pounce1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You pounce onto the foe you look at (6 blocks): 18 damage, and 9 damage more per Heat.

3rd

Breath of Pestilence2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 9-block cone of plague wind: 18 fire damage, or half to one that endures it. Those who don't lose half of any healing for 3 s.

6th

Blood-Red Draught1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once every five minutes: Heat falls by 3, you mend 16 health, and fear leaves you.

1st

Vent0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The fever breaks outward: 14 damage of fire per Heat to everything within 3 blocks, and Heat falls to 0.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Lady of Slaughter, Lady of Healing10 CE · Maximum (X)

The Lady of Slaughter: for a minute Heat is locked at 5 and its gifts are doubled, but it burns you only half as much. With Variant, the Lady of Healing: for three 3 s periods Heat is locked at 0, and every 3 s you and allies within 9 blocks mend 33 health and are cleansed of poison and fear.

Passives

1st

Heat

Each 3 s you deal damage, Heat +1 (up to 5); 3 s without, -1. Per Heat: 4% faster and 6% harder blows. At 4 or more, you burn every 3 s.

11th

Eye of Ra

At Heat 5, whenever the fever burns you it burns every foe within 6 blocks too: 9 fire damage unless it gets clear.

Technique feats

1st

Lion's Pursuit

At Heat 3 or more, sprinting leaves a trail of fire behind you: foes crossing it burn (7 damage).

3rd

Fever Claw

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow delivers the plague (1 CE): the foe is poisoned, set alight, hits 15% softer and loses half of any healing for 3 s.

6th

Red Beer Ritual

Variant + Blood-Red Draught hands it to the ally you look at within 4 blocks: they get the mending and lose their fear; your Heat still falls.

11th

Two Faces of the Sun

Once in each Maximum, Technique Key 1 turns the sun's other face: Slaughter becomes Healing, or Healing Slaughter, for three 3 s periods.
