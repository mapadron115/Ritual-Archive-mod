Elder Futhark

Carve the old alphabet in cursed energy: thorn, ice, torch, journey, ward, birch, and at the end, the Norns' own hand.

2arts
0passives
1technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Carve a Rune0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Carve the chosen rune (Technique Key 1 picks), on the foe you look at (18.0 blocks), on you, or on the block you look at: Kenaz, fire: 26 damage and burning, or a block kindled (1 CE) · Thurisaz, thorns: 40 damage and bleeding for 3 rounds (2) · Isa, ice: 26 damage, frozen and pinned 3 s, or water frozen (2) · Raidho, a swift journey for a minute (1) · Algiz, a ward on you of 79 damage (2) · Berkano, birch-healing, or plants grown (2) · Hagalaz, hail 9 blocks around where you look: 53 damage, and glass and ice crack (4) · Sowilo, a sun-ray: 53 damage to everything along it, blinded unless it resists (4) · Tiwaz, Tyr's spear: 79 damage and its armor opened for 2 rounds (6) · Wyrd, any rune at all (3). The rest of the alphabet, on you or on the ally you look at: Fehu, up to 6 of your CE given to an ally (2) · Uruz, a minute of strength: a melee blow once every 3 s adds 13 damage (2) · Ansuz, a word of prohibition on a foe: it can't answer blows or strike true for 3 s, and a sorcerer can't cast (6) · Gebo, a gift of temporary health (1) · Wunjo, fear lifted (1) · Nauthiz, need: a foe is starved and slowed, and a sorcerer loses 6 CE (4) · Jera, harvest: strike within 3 s and be healed (2) · Eihwaz, a minute of yew: once every 3 s the kind of harm that strikes you is learned and halved (4) · Perthro, a minute of lots: once every 3 s a heavy blow on you is cast again, and half the time misses (6) · Ehwaz, a horse's bound forward (1) · Mannaz, a bond with an ally: each knows where the other is for 10 minutes (1) · Laguz, a current where you look: foes within 6 blocks are swept the way you face (2) · Ingwaz, a seed: part of the next blow on you is kept and rides your next melee blow (4) · Dagaz, daybreak: blindness, poison, weakness, slowness and holds ended, and healing (6) · Othala, a homeland 9 blocks round where you stand for a minute: you and allies in it take a fifth less, foes are slowed (6). Kenaz carved on nothing is a torch: every creature within 18 blocks is shown. Variant + Technique Key 1 steps a whole aett..
