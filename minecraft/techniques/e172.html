Visitors at the Window

Call an almost indestructible nuisance swarm that steals, clings, rattles defenders, and keeps coming back.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

They Came Back2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute a swarm of 6 Visitors besets your foes: 7 damage a nip, and what they nip hits 15% softer for a few seconds. Any that vanish come back while the swarm lasts.

1st

Swarm Trick0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Technique Key 1 picks the trick, this plays it on the foe you look at with a Visitor within 6 blocks of it. Rattle: it can't react for a 3 s. Grab: its held item is pulled away and dropped (or handed to you). Cling: slowed for a 3 s. Hurl Clutter (18 blocks, any Visitor): its next blows land at half for a 3 s. Sabotage (3rd), on the block you look at: lights within 3 go out or fall, doors fly open, riders are thrown from carts and boats. Every Window: each play also Rattles. A foe that fails to shrug a trick off banks a Mischief (up to 12). Variant: Pile On, 1 CE, every 2 Mischief plays the trick on one more foe. The first extra trick each 3 s is free.

1st

Rattle the Doors0 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Every Visitor rushes the foe you look at (18 blocks) at once, and it's rattled: 20% softer for 4 s.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Siege at Kelly-Hopkinsville8 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute, an 18-block Visitor Perimeter where you look (36 blocks): every 3 s up to 6 foes inside are nipped (14 damage), rattled and can't react; the bank has no cap.

Passives

11th

Every Window

A shot from a foe beside a Visitor goes wide 1 time in 2.

Technique feats

1st

Shiny Eyes

While the swarm is out, its eyes glow and you see a mote over every creature near you; the invisible near a Visitor glow.

1st

Roof Scratcher

A foe with a Visitor right above it, once every 3 s, hits at half until the next.

3rd

Tiny Thieves

Grab on a player takes a small thing instead: a potion, pearl, golden apple or firework from their hotbar, never armor.
