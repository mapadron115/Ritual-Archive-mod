Palimpsest

Record an earlier layer of a place or a person, then selectively restore it: red ink on the present.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Margin1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

First press: record where you stand. Next press: return there and regain 15% of your health (a Revision Die is spent; with none left you only return). You have 11 Revision Dice a long rest.

11th

Redaction5 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every hostile shot within 6 blocks of where you look (12 blocks) is struck from the page, and foes there lose their footing.

1st

Position Note1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at (12 blocks) is noted: 3 s later it's pulled back to where it stood and takes 13 damage (a nimble or strong foe is only stopped, for half).

Maximum

18th

Maximum: First Draft12 CE · Maximum (X)

Everything within 6 blocks returns to where it stood five seconds ago: you regain 39% of your health, and foes are pulled back and take 53 damage (a nimble foe takes half and stays).
