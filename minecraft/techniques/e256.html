Centipede Shroud

Cursed Centipede, Insect Storage, Earthquake and Vermin of Disaster: a living shikigami shroud that fights with you, stores for you and devours for you.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

6th

Earthquake4 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The centipede bursts out of the ground where you look (18 blocks): 44 damage to every foe within 9 blocks, knocked down, and the soft ground there caves in. A foe off the ground is spared.

11th

Vermin of Disaster8 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Up to three giant centipedes launch at the foes nearest where you look (72 blocks): 89 damage each, dragged toward you (hurled away with Variant) and held 2.5 s (a strong foe may tear loose).

1st

Cursed Centipede0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The centipede wraps around you as a shroud (free the first time each long rest, then 3 CE): your weapon blows add 11 damage and you have 6 armor more, half of every blow on you lands on the centipede until it's killed (50% of your max health, half again with the second centipede), and it carries you up walls (sneak to hang). Press again to send it away.
