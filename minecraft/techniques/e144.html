Golden Fault

Damage creates Fractures. Mend your own breaks into stronger seams, or strike through the enemy's before they heal.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Gilded Seam1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (6 blocks): 16 damage, and a Fracture opens in it.

1st

Mend the Break2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Regain 10% of your health (twice that if you spend a Lacquer). You harden for 3 s after: 6 more armor, and nothing knocks you back.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Golden Reassembly12 CE · Maximum (X)

You regain 25% of your health. Every foe within 6 blocks has its Fractures detonate: 22 damage, plus 7 damage a Fracture, and it's knocked flat (a hardy foe stays standing).

Passives

1st

Fracture Sense

Once every 3 s, a foe you hurt gains a Fracture (up to 5) for a minute, a gold seam on its body.

1st

Exploit the Break

A weapon blow on a fractured foe spends a Fracture and a Lacquer (1 CE): 7 damage more, up to three at once, and three cracks its armor.

1st

Lacquer Pot

You hold 11 Lacquer, refilled by a long rest.

Technique feats

1st

Beautiful Scar

Mending yourself leaves a golden scar: for 6 s, poison, withering, burning, nausea, slowing and weakness can't take hold.

3rd

Shatterpoint

When a foe reaches its most Fractures, you see the moment it will fall: your next weapon blow on it lands half again as hard.
