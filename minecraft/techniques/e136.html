Bloodlord

An old vampire's appetite: drink from every blow, store the blood, and spend it on spawn, sorcery and one exsanguinating feast.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Blood Lance1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A piercing lance of blood (18 blocks): 18 damage; it drinks up to 6 Blood for 9 damage more each.

9th

Vampire Spawn4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A blood-born spawn for a minute: 27 damage a bite, and each bite heals you a little. It spends 3 Blood if you have it (otherwise it lasts only 20 s).

6th

Aristocrat's Command2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 Blood: the foe you look at (18 blocks) is dismissed for 3 s: a creature turns away from you, a player is shaken (a strong will may hold).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Exsanguinate10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every foe within 9 blocks: 89 damage, and 11 damage more for every 2 Blood spent (all of it); a tough foe halves it. You drink half of what it takes, up to your full health.

Passives

1st

Vampiric Thirst

Once every 3 s a weapon blow drinks deep (1 CE and 1 Blood): 9 damage more, and you heal.

1st

Blood

Once every 3 s your blows gather a Blood; each kill gives another.

13th

Markov Immortality

Variant + Technique Key 1 (1 CE and 3 Blood): you heal 55 damage, half if fire touched you in the last 3 s.

15th

Lord of Innistrad

Two spawn at once.

20th

Ancient Vampire

Blood holds 15 and rises to 3 after half a minute out of a fight. At 10 Blood or more you fly, and blows land 25% softer on you. Once a long rest, a killing blow leaves you at 1 health for 1 CE and all your Blood (5 at least).

Domain

18th

Castle Markov

The banquet hall opens: each pulse every chosen living foe loses 22 damage (a tough one half), and you and your spawn drink the total, 66 damage at most.
