Bloodlord

An old vampire's appetite: drink from every blow, store the blood, and spend it on spawn, sorcery and one exsanguinating feast.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Blood Lance1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A piercing lance of blood (18 blocks): 18 damage; it drinks up to 6 Blood for 9 damage more each.

9th

Vampire Spawn4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A blood-born spawn for a minute: 27 damage a bite, and each bite heals you a little. It spends 3 Blood if you have it (otherwise it lasts only 20 s).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Exsanguinate10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every foe within 9 blocks: 89 damage, and 11 damage more for every 2 Blood spent (all of it); a tough foe halves it. You drink a share.

Passives

1st

Vampiric Thirst

Once every 3 s a weapon blow drinks deep (1 CE and 1 Blood): 9 damage more, and you heal.

1st

Blood

Once every 3 s your blows gather a Blood; each kill gives another.

20th

Ancient Vampire

At 10 Blood or more you fly, and blows land 25% softer on you.

Technique feats

6th

Sanguine Bond

Technique Key 1 (1 Blood) bonds the foe you look at for 12 s: each time Vampiric Thirst heals you on another foe, the bonded one bleeds too.
