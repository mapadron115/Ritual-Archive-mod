War God's Household

Give a wandering soul a name and it joins the household as family: each Regalia a weapon, garment, guide, shield, scale or sacred beast.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Battle Muster1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Call or dismiss the highlighted Regalia (dismissing is free), up to 8 active at once.

1st

Regalia Art0 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The highlighted Regalia's own move (it must be active): Pinpoint Order marks a foe so your next blow on it lands half again as hard; Rending Arc is a 3-block cone of 47 damage; Crossfire fires two shots of 14 damage; Capture drags a foe to you and holds it 3 s; Pounce leaps 6 blocks onto a foe for 47 damage. Another active Regalia answers it (1 CE; the first every 3 s is free from 6th).

3rd

Borderline2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A line of light across the ground where you look (12 blocks long, a minute): the first foe to cross it takes 49 damage, is thrown back and stopped 3 s.

11th

Sevenfold Muster5 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Up to 8 active Regalia each fly at a foe within 24 blocks: 9 damage each.

1st

Name the Next0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Highlight the next Regalia in the household.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Heaven's Grand Muster8 CE · Maximum (X)

Every unbroken name answers once, active or not (up to 8): 18 damage each on foes within 24 blocks.

Passives

1st

Household

Eight Regalia, one of each form; 8 may be active at once (5 at 3rd, 7 at 6th, all at 11th).

1st

Guide Charm · Battle Garb

Charm: you see a spark on every creature within 16 blocks. Garb: 3 more armor (6 Blessed), and once a 3 s, 1 CE, Take the Wound lessens a blow by 35% of your health.

1st

Greatblade · Twin Sidearms · Binding Lash

Weapon blows add 9 damage once a second (twice that Blessed); shots add 7 damage once a second; the Lash adds a block of reach and drags what it strikes toward you.

1st

Sacred Bulwark · Sky Lion · Judgment Scales

Bulwark: 6 more armor, and Stand Between (2 CE) lessens a blow as the Garb does. Lion: faster, higher, no fall damage, and from 6th you fly. Scales: whoever hurts you is marked (2 CE, once every 3 s) and your next blow on it adds 41 damage.

6th

Coordination

The first Household Answer each 3 s is free.

11th

Hafuri Vessel

When you'd fall, the highlighted active Regalia risks its name for you: it becomes Blessed and you stand at 1 health (two may be Blessed). After that, once a long rest, a Blessed Regalia breaks to keep you up.

Domain

18th

Golden Citadel of Ten Thousand Names

Every Regalia is active. Testimony of Every Name: every chosen foe takes 47 damage; the Accused (whoever hurt you in the citadel) take double, and are dragged inward and held 1.5 s.

Technique feats

6th

Line That Casts Out Evil

Your Borderline may run 24 blocks long.

6th

Many Names, One Banner

Battle Muster in its other form calls the highlighted Garb, Bulwark, Lash or Sky Lion into the hands of the ally you look at, within 18 blocks. It still counts against your Active Limit, and it gives them its armor, reach, speed and the Lion's soft landing instead of you. Muster it again to call it home; it comes home by itself past 36 blocks.

6th

Cavalry of Heaven

While you fly on the Sky Lion, up to 6 allies within 3 blocks who hold sneak ride with you, and land softly when they let go.
