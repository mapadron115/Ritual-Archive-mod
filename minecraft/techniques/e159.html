Adaptive Hatred

Survive something once, record what hurt you, and spend limited Adaptation slots deciding which threats your body is allowed to stop caring about.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Hateful Maul1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (2.5 blocks): 26 damage, and 13 damage more for every Adaptation installed.

1st

Install Adaptation1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Spend 1 Data of the chosen kind: that harm lands at half. With 2 Data it's installed Hardened: a quarter. You have 4 slots; when they're full the oldest is replaced.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Total Adaptation10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you grow two extra slots, and every kind you hold Data in installs itself at once.

Passives

1st

Exposure Record

Whatever hurts you is recorded (1 CE): +1 Data in its kind (Blows, Shots, Elements or Hazards), up to 3. Technique Key 1 picks the kind.

6th

Reactive Morphology

Each installed Adaptation makes you 4% faster and gives 2 armor.

18th

Termination Failure

Once a long rest, a blow that would kill you leaves you standing, mends you 44% of your health, and fills every kind's Data.

Technique feats

1st

Hostile Evolution

Once every 3 s, a creature that hurts you with a harm you've adapted to takes 26 damage back.

6th

Keter Genome

Once every 3 s, being hurt by a harm you hold Data for installs that Adaptation on the spot (1 Data).

11th

Consume and Rebuild

Each long rest adds 1 Data to every kind.
