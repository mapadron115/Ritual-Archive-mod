Adaptive Hatred

Survive something once, record what hurt you, and spend limited Adaptation slots deciding which threats your body is allowed to stop caring about.

18thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Hateful Maul1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (2.5 blocks): 26 damage, and 13 damage more for every Adaptation installed.

1st

Install Adaptation1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Spend 1 Data of the chosen Threat Tag: that harm lands at half, or that effect lasts half as long. With 2 Data it's installed Hardened: a plain harm (fire, a fall, a blade and the like) or an effect can't touch you at all, while a technique's, magic's or a Domain's stays at half, and once every 3 s one such blow is cut by 79 damage more. You have 4 slots; when they're full the oldest is replaced. With Variant it shapes your body instead (Reactive Morphology).

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Abhorrent Evolution10 CE · Maximum (X)

Consume all the Data of at least two Threat Tags: each is installed at once, in two extra slots, for a minute. You grow larger, 10% faster, and your bare hands deal 13 damage more. When it ends those slots and that Data are gone, you're slowed and sluggish for a minute, and you record nothing for 3 s.

Passives

1st

Exposure Record

Whatever hurts you is recorded by its exact Threat Tag (1 CE): +1 Data, once every 3 s a Tag, up to 3, kept until a long rest. Each kind of damage is a Tag, and so is each harmful effect (poison, slowness, weakness, blindness, being held). Technique Key 1 cycles the Tags you hold Data for.

3rd

Regenerative Hate

Below half health, once every 3 s, a harm you're adapted to mends you 15 health.

6th

Reactive Morphology

Each Adaptation brings one shape, picked with Variant + Install, each shape once: Swift (+10% speed), Climbing, Swimming, Long Reach (+1), Plated (+2 armor), Crushing Hands (13 damage more bare-handed).

18th

Termination Failure

Once a long rest, a blow that would kill you leaves you standing and mends you 44% of your health; what nearly killed you gets 3 Data and a free Hardened Adaptation until the fight ends.

Technique feats

1st

Hostile Evolution

Once every 3 s, a creature that hurts you with a harm you've adapted to takes 26 damage back.

6th

Keter Genome

Once every 3 s, a harm you hold Data for but haven't adapted to swaps out an Adaptation for it on the spot, spending no Data.

6th

Lent Loathing

Once every 3 s, Variant + Technique Key 1 (1 CE) lends your newest Adaptation to the ally you look at (within 18 blocks) until 3 s has passed.

11th

Consume and Rebuild

Once a minute, eating mends you 79 and gives 1 Data to the Tag of the last harm that hurt you.
