Pestilence Doctrine

Diagnose a target, gather its Symptoms, cash them into a brutal Cure, then turn the dead into crude Patients.

2arts
2passives
3technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Palliative Harvest1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every Symptom on the foe you look at (6 blocks) is consumed: 13 damage each. On a healthy foe, that much once, and its first Symptom.

3rd

The Cure2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A touch (2.5 blocks) that consumes every Symptom: 33 damage, plus 13 damage a Symptom. A foe it kills rises as your Patient (up to 6, for ten minutes).

Passives

1st

Diagnosis

Once every 3 s, 1 CE: a weapon blow infects its foe with a Symptom (up to 8). Every two Symptoms, it heals a quarter less and hits 5% softer.

11th

Systemic Pestilence

Foes hold 8 Symptoms, and the Cure raises Patients.

Domain

Perfect Diagnosis: every chosen foe gains two Symptoms.
