Pestilence Doctrine

Diagnose a target, gather its Symptoms, cash them into a brutal Cure, then turn the dead into crude Patients.

3arts
3passives
4technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Clinical Examination0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at within 18 blocks: whether it's above or below half its health, whether it's poisoned or withered, and a sorcerer's Cursed Energy, above or below half. Inside your Domain it reaches anywhere, and once every 3 s its other form plants a Symptom on a foe you can see (1 CE).

1st

Palliative Harvest1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every Symptom on the foe you look at (18 blocks) is consumed: 13 damage each. A foe with none can't be harvested.

3rd

The Cure2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A touch (2.5 blocks) that consumes every Symptom: 33 damage, plus 13 damage a Symptom. Three or more consumed: it can't heal for 3 s. All it could hold, with the foe at a quarter of its health or less: it has to resist or drop dead (a player or a great foe takes 53 damage more instead). A foe it kills rises as your Patient (4 CE). Its other form is Crude Surgery: a creature you killed in the last 10 s rises as a Patient (5 CE).

Passives

1st

Diagnosis

Once every 3 s, 1 CE: a weapon blow infects its foe with a Symptom (up to 8). Symptoms fade a minute after the last one lands. From 18th it infects twice a round, on two different foes.

1st

Symptomatic Decline

Every two Symptoms, a foe heals a quarter less and resists this technique 10% worse.

11th

Systemic Pestilence

Foes hold 8 Symptoms, and the Cure raises Patients: up to 6, each with 105 health, ten minutes. Among them all they strike once every 3 s: 33 damage.

Domain

18th

Ward of the Final Physician

Perfect Diagnosis: every chosen foe gains two Symptoms. Operating Staff: your Patients move faster inside. Sterile Field: Clinical Examination reaches anywhere inside.
