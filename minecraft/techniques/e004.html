Body Manipulation

Move into the bodies you kill: you wear them, their size, strength and toughness become yours, each leaves a stolen trait, and a backup body waits if you die.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Graverobber1 CE · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest: move into the freshest body you've killed (within 10 s and 6 blocks), a sorcerer's too. Your body becomes it: everyone sees you as it, at its size, and with more health, armor and strength the mightier it was. It stays until you take another.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Backup10 CE · Maximum (X)

Set where you stand as your only backup: the next time you'd die, you wake there instead, at half your health.

Passives

1st

The New Body

A taken body keeps its might until you take another.

3rd

Skill Theft

Every body you take leaves you one trait it had (wings, fireproof hide, gills, thick hide, speed, venom, a predator's strength). You keep 4; the oldest goes first.

18th

Immortality

Every body is a new age: weakness and slowness never touch you.
