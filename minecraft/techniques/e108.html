Undead

The technique negates approaching death. You fight with your own body, fingers for bullets and blood for propulsion, and a Repair Reserve pays the invoice.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

3rd

Parts Bullet1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Tear free a finger and fire it on a falling arc (36 blocks): 26 damage. Costs a little of your life.

6th

Dead Line2 CE · 1.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Lose 5% of your life: a 36-block line of blood where you look, 53 damage to everything on it. The blocks it crosses are splashed red.

9th

Repair Boost0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Toggle. Once every 3 s, a melee blow drives a shard of you in: lose 6% of your life and 1 CE, for 79 damage more.

11th

Division3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Lose 10% of your life: 6 severed parts (twice as many under Victor) crawl after your foes for a minute, biting for 40 damage between them.

13th

Dead Road5 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Lose 10% of your life: a 54-block road of you, 93 damage to everything on it.

18th

Victor6 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest, for a minute: Repair doubles, every Undead blow hits harder, and your own costs can't drop you below 1.

15th

Interpretation Shift0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Death is a definition; choose which one you rule out. Age: wither can't take hold and stolen years come back. Stasis: you can't be bound or frozen. Dismemberment: a fall costs 5 Reserve, not 10. Unconsciousness: no blindness, darkness or nausea while the Reserve lasts. You hold two: Variant chooses the second.

6th

Blood Propulsion1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Shed 6% of your life as a jet of blood: you're thrown along your full look, up a cliff or over a ravine, and land unhurt.

Maximum

1st

Maximum: Dead Quartet10 CE · Maximum (X)

Lose 25% of your life: skin, blood, bone and muscle lash up to four foes within 36 blocks for 40 damage each (159 damage on a lone foe). Then you reassemble, repairing up to 25% from the Reserve; with Variant, beside the first foe it struck.

Passives

1st

Negate Death

While the Repair Reserve (200) lasts, death doesn't take: you keep going at 1 health, slowed and weakened, until you've repaired a tenth of yourself. Each fall costs 10 Reserve.

1st

Repair

Each 3 s, heal up to 6% of your life from the Reserve. It refills on a long rest, and whenever you're out of danger.

15th

Interpretation Shift

One death you've ruled out of order, and a second (the art chooses; it starts as Stasis).

20th

Undead Completion

The Reserve doubles, and once a long rest even an empty Reserve can't kill you.

Technique feats

3rd

Crimson Bullet

Plant rather than fire: Technique Key 1 sets a finger in the block you look at (36 blocks, a little life). The first foe to come near it is hit for 40 damage. Up to three.

6th

Bloody Arts

Your propulsion carries more than you: an ally within 2 blocks is thrown along with you, and lands unhurt.

11th

Never Stop

Conditions are suggestions: once a minute, 10 Repair throws off slowness, nausea, blindness, mining fatigue and binding at once.
