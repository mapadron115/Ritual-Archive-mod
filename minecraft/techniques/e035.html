Techniques / Jujutsu Kaisen

❖ e035 · Jujutsu Kaisen ❖

Ten Shadows

One shadow given ten bodies. Begin with the Divine Dogs; tame the rest in trials you must win alone. A fallen shadow does not return, but its strength can pass on.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Manifest0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Call the selected shadow, or send it back if it's out. It costs its own CE (the Divine Dogs 1 each, Mahoraga 20). Shadows fight on their own for 180 s, biting once every 3 s and doing their move every fourth bite. The quick key selects.

3rd

Taming Trial0 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Challenge the selected untamed shadow: it rises against you, and you must strike it down yourself within two minutes to tame it. Its CE is spent, win or lose.

8th

Shadow Wearing0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Wear the selected tamed shadow's function for a minute, for half its cost: its gift, and its move on Technique Key 1. It can't be manifested meanwhile. Wheel Wearing (Mahoraga): 10 CE, once a long rest.

6th

Shadow Storage0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Sink the item in your hand into your shadow, or draw the stored one out into an empty hand. It stays there through death.

3rd

Shadow Skulker0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

With the Shadow Skulker feat: on solid ground, sink into your own shadow (2 CE, then 2 CE every 6 s). You're unseen and untouchable, no cursed energy comes back to you, and your shadows fight on. Cast it again or swing a weapon to rise.

11th

Wells Unknown Abyss0 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Fuse the selected manifested shadow with the other one you called last: one body that bites with both, 30% harder, does both their moves, and keeps the gift of what was poured in (Nue: it flies and its bites spark; Mourn Tiger: a claw in every bite; Piercing Ox: 15% faster; Rabbit Escape: it takes half of every blow; Great Serpent or Gama: its bites slow). They share one fate, and count as one. With Totality, Max Elephant or Nue selected and its three kin out, they become a chimera for a minute instead: Zagroth (Piercing Ox, Mourn Tiger, Great Serpent), Hageshi (Round Deer, Nue, Rabbit Escape) or Agito (Great Serpent, Mourn Tiger, Round Deer). If a chimera falls, all four fall.

1st

Dismiss0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every manifested shadow returns to your shadow.

1st

Select Shadow0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The next shadow on the roster (with your variant held, the one before).

Passives

1st

Death is permanent

A shadow struck down is gone for good. If one Dog falls, the survivor can be called as Divine Dog Totality. Shadows mend a little every 3 s, more out of a fight.

1st

Manifestation capacity

4 at once (2 from 3rd, 3 from 11th, 4 from 15th; one more in your Domain). The Dogs and Gama each count as one; Mahoraga takes two.

1st

Rabbit Escape

While the rabbits are out, blows on you land 15% softer.

17th

Wheel Wearing

With Mahoraga tamed: Shadow Wearing on Mahoraga, 10 CE once a long rest. A second blow of the same kind turns the wheel; the third lands half, and every one after a quarter.

Domain

18th

Chimera Shadow Garden

The field becomes living shadow: each pulse every chosen foe sinks, held 1.5 s, and takes 53 damage. Inside, one more shadow may stand, shadows rise for half their CE, and they rise where you look.
