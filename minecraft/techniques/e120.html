Flame On! A body of living fire: bolts, sculpted flame, plasma, flight, and the Supernova when the Heat runs full.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Fire Bolt0 CE · 0.3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

While aflame: a bolt of fire, 20 blocks, 18 damage (+1 Heat).

6th

Fire Sculpting2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 5-block cone of flame, 36 damage (+1 Heat). Technique Key 1 (2 CE) shapes it into a 20-block line instead.

11th

Plasma Burst4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

White flame where you look (30 blocks): everything within 6 blocks takes 66 damage (+2 Heat); sand there fuses to glass and ice melts.

1st

Flame On!1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A minute aflame: fire can't hurt you, foes that strike you burn (once every 3 s), and you fly. With Variant, vent 2 Heat instead (free).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Supernova10 CE · Maximum (X)

At Heat 10 only: everything within 13 blocks takes 133 damage as you go nova, and the sand around you turns to glass. Heat drops to 0 and you're exhausted.

Passives

1st

Heat

Every fire art raises Heat by 1 (Plasma Burst by 2), up to 10, and so does each 3 s aflame in fire or lava. Out of combat it cools by 1 every 3 s.

3rd

Jet Flight

While aflame you fly, and sprinting leaves a burning trail.

6th

Superheated Body

At Heat 5+, your weapon blows add 9 fire damage and set the foe alight.

18th

Nova Instability

At Heat 10 your fire arts carry two more of their dice, but the heat burns you for 6% of your health every 3 s (Variant + Flame On vents 2 Heat).

20th

Living Star

While aflame your Heat can't fall below 3, and you fly faster.

Technique feats

3rd

Hotshot

At Heat 5+, Fire Bolt flies as a focused lance: faster, 40 blocks, and through everything in its line.

1st

Skywriter

Variant + Technique Key 1 (1 CE): a flame copy of you for 3 s; the first blow meant for you strikes it instead.

6th

Flame Cage

Variant + Fire Sculpting raises a ring of white-hot flame 3 blocks round you for a minute: a foe in its wall takes 27 fire damage once every 3 s.
