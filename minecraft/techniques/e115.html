Blacklight

Consume the living for Biomass and their shape, reshape it into claws, blades and hammers, and finally release a viral bloom grown from something you ate.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Consume1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A mauling blow on the foe in front of you (3 blocks): 18 damage. But under a third of its health, a foe 10 levels under you, or 5 to 9 under and already low (half its health at 5, more as the gap grows), player or creature but never a boss, is eaten whole: Biomass by its size (5, 10 or 20) and you heal as much, and you wear its shape for two minutes: the rest of its kind doesn't know you until you strike one.

15th

Viral Ascendancy5 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute you Consume from 18 blocks: tendrils of you reach out and drag the meal in.

13th

Armor2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

10 Biomass: for a minute your hide becomes a fortress. Blows land far softer and nothing knocks you back, but you're slower.

3rd

Take Form1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Reshape your arms into the form Technique Key 1 chose (press again to return to human): Claws (9 damage more) · Musclemass (11 damage, blows throw foes, you leap higher) · Blade (6th: 13 damage, longer reach) · Hammerfist (6th: 11 damage; taking it slams the ground, knocking down everything within 3 blocks for 2 CE) · Whipfist (9th: 9 damage, reach four blocks farther, and a hit drags the foe to you for 1 CE).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Viral Cataclysm10 CE · Maximum (X)

Spend an eaten elite (a great foe, or anything of 100 health or more, that you consumed or felled): spikes of biomass burst from the ground for 15 blocks around you, and every foe in it is infected for a minute, rotting every 3 s (14 damage) and spreading it when it dies (a tough foe resists).

Passives

1st

Biomass

You hold up to 200 Biomass. Below half health, it mends you a little every second.

9th

Shield

With Variant held, once every 3 s a blow meets a shield of eaten flesh (1 CE and 5 Biomass): 22 damage plus your level softer.

18th

Critical Mass

With 75 Biomass or more your blows land 9 damage harder and you move faster; and once every 3 s a killing blow leaves you at 1 health instead (3 CE and 30 Biomass).

20th

Blacklight Apex

Your Biomass holds twice as much.

Technique feats

1st

Parkour Predator

Sprint into a wall and you run up it; you leap higher, falls don't hurt you, and sneaking in the air glides.

6th

Adaptive Biomass

Once every 10 s, a creature's blow teaches you (1 CE and 5 Biomass): the next blow from its kind misses, and feeds you 5 Biomass and health.
