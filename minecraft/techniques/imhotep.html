The Step Pyramid

Architecture raised from cursed energy, limestone-white and warm: walls, courts with laws, shafts that swallow, and the first pyramid.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Foundation2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A limestone wall, 7 wide and 4 high, rises where you look (36 blocks) for a minute. It's real stone, and it drops nothing.

6th

The Shaft3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A shaft opens under the foe you look at (36 blocks): 27 damage, held in the pit for 3 s (a nimble foe takes half and keeps its footing).

11th

The Architect's Vow6 CE · 12 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A wider court, 12 blocks from its middle to its wall, with a binding Law: for a minute a foe standing in it is slowed and takes 18 damage once every 3 s, 3 times at most on each foe.

3rd

The First Court4 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A round wall with four gateways rises where you look (36 blocks) for a minute, and the Law of Stillness holds within 9 blocks of its middle: foes inside are slowed. 3 courts at most.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: The Seventh Stage12 CE · Maximum (X)

The whole step pyramid rises where you look (72 blocks), four tiers of real stone with a gate in each face, for a minute. Every foe within 18 blocks of its middle is slowed and takes 27 damage once every 3 s, 3 times at most on each foe. Once a long rest.
