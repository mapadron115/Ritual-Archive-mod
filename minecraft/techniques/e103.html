Chronological Reserve

Time is a currency. Steal seconds from your enemies, store them as Time Units, and spend them to accelerate, freeze, rewind, or restore.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

6th

Stasis Sphere3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

3 TU: the foe you look at (36 blocks) is set aside, held where it hangs, in the air too, and weakened for a 3 s (a strong one shrugs it off). It can't counter, dodge or parry, and your own blow ends the stasis once it has landed. Precise Stasis (feat): with Variant, the ally you look at is set aside instead, held but untouchable.

11th

Accelerate4 CE · Cast (Z)

5 TU, once every 3 s: every art of yours is ready again at once, and for 3 s you move and swing far faster.

13th

Age to Dust6 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

8 TU: 106 damage to the foe you look at, and its health never recovers half of it (a player's comes back after 5 minutes; a boss loses none). A golem or anything conjured takes 130 damage instead and keeps its health. Look at a block instead (2 TU): the hour is spent on it (crops ripen, copper weathers, stone cracks).

15th

Chrono Anastasis10 CE · Cast (Z)

Every TU (at least 15), once a long rest: the last 3 s is taken back within 18 blocks. You and your allies return to where you stood and the health you had; foes return to where they stood, keeping their wounds. Grand Clock (feat): with Variant, foes are left where they are.

18th

Archive: AGE / FREEZE / RETURN0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Choose what your Domain declares each pulse.

3rd

Chrono Step2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

2 TU: you attend a different moment, slipping up to 5 blocks where you look, up or down too, untouchable for an instant.

Maximum

1st

Maximum: The Future I Refuse0 CE · Maximum (X)

Automatic, once a long rest (12 CE + 12 TU): when you'd die, you're rewound 3 s instead. It refuses an ally's death within 36 blocks the same way.

Passives

1st

Time Theft

Once every 3 s, a blow of yours that lands, by hand, shot or art, steals time (1 CE): the foe is slowed and you gain 4 TU. Up to 90 TU; a long rest starts you at your level.

9th

Stored Future

Automatic, 4 CE + 4 TU, once every 3 s: a blow that would land never happened, and you're back where you stood 3 s ago.

20th

Time Emperor

Your TU cap doubles, a fight starts you at 10 TU or more, and theft steals 2 more.

Domain

18th

Archive of the Stolen Hour

Each pulse it declares what you chose: AGE (106 damage), FREEZE (held, weakened and unable to counter, dodge or parry for 1.5 s), or RETURN (dragged back to where it stood the pulse before).

Second Hand

Once every 3 s, 1 CE + 2 TU, automatic: a hold or a fright that lands on an ally within 18 blocks is undone.

Precise Stasis

Variant + Stasis Sphere on an ally sets them aside for 3 s: held, but nothing can hurt them.

9th
