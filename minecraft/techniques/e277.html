Pain Killer

You cannot heal, but you can stop injuries from getting worse: wards that soak blows, ailments shaken off, and a clinic that never closes.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Pain Killer1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For an hour, 34 damage of borrowed health takes blows before you do, and every poison, wither and burn on you stops (one other affliction lifts too). With Variant: every ally within 4.5 blocks instead, 1 CE each after the first.

Passives

6th

Pain Breaker

Pain Killer also lifts one affliction: nausea, slowness, blindness, darkness, weakness or fatigue.

11th

Panacea

When a blow would drop you or an ally within 3 blocks, 4 CE keeps them standing with a little health, numbed.

11th

Analgesic

Once every 3 s, a heavy blow on you or an ally within 3 blocks lands at half (4 CE; free in your own Domain).

Domain

18th

Medical Clinic

The clinic never closes: at every pulse you and your allies inside are numbed again, free, and Analgesic costs nothing.
