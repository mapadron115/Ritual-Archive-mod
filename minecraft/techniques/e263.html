Maximum: Sacrifice6 CE · Maximum (X)

With 3 or more fangs out: they coil around the foe you look at (24 blocks) and bury themselves, and are spent. A trigram cylinder of real bone, three high, stands round it for two rounds and one more for each fang past three (five at most); it's held and its shots and arts shut off for 3 s, and while it stays inside the ring it takes 46 damage every 3 s. If it dies in there, one spent life comes back to you (once a long rest).
