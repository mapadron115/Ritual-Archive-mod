Prime Conduit

A living battery: drink the world's current, throw it back as bolts, shockwaves and storms, and never be fully empty while the redstone still hums.

6arts

11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Alpha Bolt0 CE · 0.4 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 Charge: a bolt of lightning where you look (18 blocks): 27 damage. A bolt that strikes copper scours it back to bright.

3rd

Shockwave1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 Charge: a 6-block cone of force where you look: 27 damage, and everything in it, creatures, dropped items, carts and boats, is thrown (a great foe holds).

6th

Discharge2 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

At 6+ Charge: everything within 4 blocks takes 9 damage per 2 Charge (a tough foe halves it). You're left empty.

18th

Ionic Storm8 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

6 Charge: the sky strikes the foe you look at (30 blocks): 133 damage (a nimble foe halves it), and 44 damage to those beside it.

1st

Drain0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Near powered redstone or a lightning rod (5 blocks), or out in the rain, you refill all your Charge. Otherwise you leech the foe you look at (6 blocks): 9 damage and 2 Charge.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Empire Thunderhead10 CE · Maximum (X)

8 Charge: a storm cloud gathers over you (12 blocks) and for two seconds the sky strikes up to 6 foes in turn: 89 damage in a 2.5-block burst each (a nimble foe halves it).

Passives

1st

Charge

You hold up to 50 Charge. Every bolt, wave and storm spends it. A thunderstorm feeds you 1 every 3 s, and so does standing near a source.

1st

Thunder Drop

With Charge in you, falls don't hurt you. Sneak in the air to dive; a landing from 4 blocks or more spends 1 Charge and shocks everything within 3 blocks (18 damage).

Technique feats

1st

Conductive Arc

Aim at the metal, not the man: Alpha Bolt on a wet or heavily armored foe arcs on to every foe within 3 blocks of it, for half.

6th

Polarity Shift

Once every 3 s, a projectile about to hit you is bent back (1 CE + 1 Charge): it strikes the nearest foe within 12 blocks instead.

11th

Karmic Overload

Technique Key 1 (2 CE, at 6+ Charge): everything at once. Foes within 4 blocks take 9 damage per Charge and are paralyzed for 3 s (a tough foe halves it and keeps moving). You're left empty.
