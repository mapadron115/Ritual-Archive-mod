Antigravity System

Gravity reversed around you: foes fall upward and come down hard, a shield that pushes blows away, flight, and a minute of zero gravity.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Antigravity3 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Gravity reverses in a 6-block column around you (not you) for 3 s: foes in it fall upward, and slam back down when it ends (11 damage per 2 blocks fallen). One that strikes a ceiling on the way takes 22 damage there too. Loose items fall up with them. A lifted foe is weakened unless it resists, and can't answer blows. Hold to pour CE: a block wider and 3 s longer per CE.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Zero Gravity10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute every foe within 12 blocks weighs nothing: they drift helplessly above the ground, and when it ends they all fall at once.

Passives

3rd

Light Fall

Falling fast, you drift down gently instead (1 CE, once every 3 s), and falls never hurt you. Allies falling fast within 72 blocks are caught the same way, for the same CE.

6th

Antigravity Shield

Once every 3 s (2 CE), a blow is pushed away from you: 34 less. It answers for an ally within 3 blocks too.

Domain

18th

Inverted Heaven

Every 3 s the chosen foes simply fall upward and are dropped from the height: 41 damage, and the fall.

3rd

Levitation System

Antigravity also lifts the foe you look at (within 12 blocks) for 2 CE more.

6th

Antigravitational Flight

You fly on reversed gravity: 1 CE for every 3 s you're aloft.

6th

Inverted Shield

When your Shield catches a melee blow, it inverts for 3 CE: the attacker is pulled in and knocked down.

11th

Dense Antigravity Shield

When your Shield catches a blow and you can spare 3 CE more, the blow is halved as well.
