Chimeric Night Cry

Wear one Aspect at a time, Monkey, Tiger, Serpent, Tanuki; at the peak, the bodies overlap.

3arts
2passives
4technique feats
18thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Night Cry2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The eerie cry down a 18-block cone: 18 damage, or 27 damage if you changed Aspect within the last 3 s. Those who don't steel themselves sicken, lose their bearings and flee for 3 s.

1st

Shift Aspect1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Become the next Aspect: Monkey (20% faster, a third of blows miss you), Tiger (your blows 30% harder), Serpent (your blows poison and reach 3 blocks farther), Tanuki (30% faster, creatures lose track of you). With Variant, 1 CE more: you keep the body you left as well for 3 s.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Chimera Beneath the Black Cloud10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute, under a black cloud, you wear your current Aspect and the next one at once. The Night Cry carries 36 blocks, and the weak it frightens are struck still 1.5 s.

Passives

6th

Crossbred Transition

Variant + Shift Aspect (1 CE more): the body you left stays with you for 3 s.

11th

Unclassifiable Beast

Once every 3 s, a foe's blow of a tenth of your health or more costs 1 CE: the body changes under it. It lands a fifth softer, and for 3 s you're the Monkey as well.

Technique feats

1st

Monkey's Cunning

As the Monkey, quick fingers: you work blocks from 3 blocks farther and swing a fifth faster.

1st

Tiger's Pounce

As the Tiger, once every 3 s a blow landed at a full run bowls the foe over (a strong one may keep its feet).

3rd

Tanuki Mist

As the Tanuki, standing still makes you all but nowhere: you vanish from sight.

6th

Serpent's Patience

As the Serpent, your blows on a poisoned foe add 7 damage.
