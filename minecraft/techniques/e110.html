Style Devil

Indecision, weaponized: switch styles and weapons, vary your attacks, and ride the Style Rank from Dope to SSStylish.

5arts
3passives

11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Stinger1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You lunge 6 blocks along your look, through the air if you aim up: 18 damage to everything you pass.

3rd

Rain Storm2 CE · 1.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You spring up and spin upside down, raining bullets: 27 damage to everything within 5 blocks.

6th

Million Stab3 CE · 1.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A flurry at the foe you look at (3 blocks): five thrusts of 11 damage, and it's pinned while they land.

11th

Devil Trigger5 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute: you fly, blows land a fifth softer, you heal 6% each 3 s, and every hit is a tenth harder.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Sin Devil Trigger10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute your rank holds at SSS in Devil Trigger. When it ends, Judgement: space around you is cut to pieces, 133 damage to every foe within 6 blocks (a nimble foe halves it).

Passives

1st

Style Rank

Land a different attack from the last (a different weapon, a shot, an art) and your rank climbs, D to SSS. Repeat yourself and it may slip; stop hitting for 5 s and it falls; a hit on you knocks it down two. Each rank: +6% damage.

1st

Styles (Technique Key 1)

Swordmaster: melee blows 9 damage harder · Gunslinger: arrows and bolts 9 damage harder · Trickster: faster, and a third of blows miss as you air-trick a step aside · Royal Guard: blows land a third softer, and what you block is stored and released on your next blow.

13th

Quadruple Style

At SS and above you keep your previous Style's benefits as well.

Technique feats

1st

Enemy Step

The enemy is terrain: once every 3 s, a melee blow landed in the air springboards you up off it, and the push-off may knock it down.

3rd

Jackpot

A critical arrow sprays: up to two other foes within 3 blocks take half of it.
