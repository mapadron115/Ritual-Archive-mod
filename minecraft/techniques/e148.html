Territorial Influence

Turn the battlefield into a Go board: Stones project overlapping Influence, and foes who let themselves be surrounded lose their freedom.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Take1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (12 blocks): 11 damage, plus as much again for every Stone whose Influence reaches it. Looking at no foe, Connect your Stones instead (1 CE more): every pair within 8 blocks draws a cutting line, 22 damage to what stands across it.

1st

Place Stone1 CE · 0.3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A Stone on the ground where you look (6 blocks), its Influence reaching 3 blocks, for five minutes. 10 at once; the oldest is lifted.
