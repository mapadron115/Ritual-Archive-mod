Disaster Flames

Reliable fire waves, planted volcanoes, explosive ember insects, and a conspicuous Maximum Meteor.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Flames of Disaster1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A 3-block cone of flame where you look: 13 damage (hotter at 11th and 17th), and it sets foes burning. Hold to keep the stream going, 1 CE a gout.

3rd

Erupting Volcanoes2 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

Plant a volcano on the ground where you look (up to 24 blocks) for a minute. Hold to plant more side by side, 2 CE each, up to 5; the oldest gives way to the newest.

6th

Ember Insects5 CE · Cast (Z)

A swarm of four ember insects for 15 s: they fly at your foes on their own, and each one bursts on what it reaches, 14 damage within 2.5 blocks, setting it alight (one swarm at a time).

3rd

Erupt1 CE · 5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Every volcano erupts at once, each a 6-block line of magma toward where you look: 33 damage, and stuck in the magma for 1.5 s. The magma slows whoever stands in it until its volcano ends. Hold Variant as you call it: the nearest volcano within 6 blocks erupts under you instead and throws you high into the air (+2 CE).

Maximum

11th

Meteor10 CE · Maximum (X)

You're lifted 12 blocks while a meteor gathers over you; then you ride it down to where you look (within 24 blocks of where you rose): 113 damage to everything within 10 blocks of the impact. If you're paralyzed before it falls, it scatters.

Passives

1st

Pyrokinesis

Your own fire doesn't take: nothing sets you burning, and flames don't hurt you (lava still does).

Domain

18th

Coffin of the Iron Mountain

Black iron seals around each foe it chose as magma erupts within: 111 damage every 3 s, and it's entombed and blind for 1.5 s.

Technique feats

1st

Ashfall

Flames of Disaster leaves choking ash where it burned: foes caught in the cone are blinded for 3 s.

3rd

Pyrokinesis Refined

Training finds more in the old flame: Flames of Disaster reaches half again as far, and your fire arts hit about a tenth harder.

11th

Hellscape

Where Meteor lands, the ground within 20 blocks runs with fire for a minute: foes standing in it take 27 damage every second.
