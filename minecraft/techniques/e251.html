Disaster Storms

Storms of Disaster, Lightning, Storm Creation, Divine Storm Sovereign, and the Heavenly Throne: wind and lightning made into a calamity.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

3rd

Lightning2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A bolt at the foe you look at (36 blocks): 54 damage, and half that to another foe within 6 blocks of it. Inside one of your storms it strikes harder, and inside the Sovereign's storm it needs no sight of its foe: the one nearest where you look is struck.

6th

Storm Creation3 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A 6-block storm around you for a minute (hold to pour CE, 3 blocks more each, up to 5): foes in it wade, each 3 s they may be knocked down, and shots that fly into it lose half their speed.

1st

Storms of Disaster1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A 9-block cone of cutting wind: 45 damage (a nimble foe may halve it). Loose things in it are blown away.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Divine Storm Sovereign10 CE · Maximum (X)

A 30-block storm where you look (up to 90 blocks) for a minute: foes in it can't see, wade, may be knocked down, and are cut by the wind each 3 s.

Domain

18th

Heavenly Throne

Every 3 s, lightning falls from the throne on every foe it chose: 79 damage.
