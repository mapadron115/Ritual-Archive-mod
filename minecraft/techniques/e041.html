Bungee Gum & Texture Surprise

Aura with the properties of rubber and gum: it sticks to anything, stretches impossibly far, and snaps back exactly when you tell it to. Only you can see it.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Bungee Gum1 CE · Cast (Z)

A strand to whatever you look at (up to 20 blocks): it sticks to a foe or a surface. You can hold 6; the oldest lets go. Only you can see them. Your newest strand on a surface is a tether: in the air you swing from it, and sneaking reels you in.

1st

Detach0 CE · Cast (Z)

Let go of every strand.

3rd

Texture Surprise1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your skin becomes wallpaper: creatures lose track of you until you attack or are touched. Cast again to drop it.

1st

Contract0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Snap the newest stuck strand back (every strand at once): you fly to a surface (up to 25 blocks) or slam into a heavy foe for 25 damage; a light foe is yanked to you and takes 18 damage.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Gum Storm5 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute, gum erupts across 20 blocks around you: every 3 s the foes in it are stuck for 1.5 s and they're slowed throughout. Contract yanks the stuck foe you look at.

Passives

1st

Rubber and Gum

Strands stretch 20 blocks, then pull back like rubber.

6th

Slingshot

Contract flings you up to 25 blocks. A strand anchored above you means no fall damage.

11th

Web of Strings

6 strands, and Contract snaps every one at once. You feel your web: a foe touching one of your strands, or within 2 blocks of where one is stuck, glows for 3 s.

18th

Prosthetic Gum

Once every 3 s (3 CE): a killing blow leaves you at half a heart instead.

20th

Post-Mortem Heartbeat

Once a day: when you'd die, the gum squeezes your heart. You lie still for 3 s and stand back up at half health.

Domain

18th

Playground of Strings

Every 3 s, every foe it chose is stuck by a strand that can't be detached, and held for 1.5 s. Contract yanks the stuck foe you look at 7 blocks.

Technique feats

1st

Tripwire

Your strands are tripwires: any foe within 10 blocks of where one is stuck glows for you.

6th

Bungee Parachute

The shimmer in the air is anchor enough: falls never hurt you, and sneaking as you fall drifts you down gently.

6th

Gum Gag

A strand across the mouth: a player you yank with Contract can't speak Cursed Speech's words or chant for 3 s.

6th

False Floor

Texture Surprise in its other form, on a floor block within 6 blocks: the floor becomes gum over a pit, and gives way under the first foe to step on it. Up to 3 at once; the ground comes back after 30 s.

1st

Card Return

A strand can stick to a dropped item or a thrown trident, and Contract brings it back to your hand.
