The Builder

Animate a small construct that builds replicas of itself from nearby materials, and direct the swarm, carefully.

2arts
2passives
3technique feats
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Animate Builder3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A tiny construct for an hour, 15 damage a slam. Each 3 s a Builder on the ground takes a soft block from beside it and builds a replica (up to 12).

11th

The Foreman2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Four Builders merge into one large brute for the hour: 23 damage a slam.

Passives

3rd

Union Rates

Replicas add your mastery to their slams.

6th

Double Shift

You can keep twice as many Builders.

Technique feats

1st

Scrap Armor

While a Builder is beside you, it interposes scrap: 4 armor more.

3rd

Assembly Line

Builders replicate twice every 3 s.

6th

Demolition Order

Technique Key 1: every Builder and brute detonates, 14 fire damage to everything within 3 blocks of each (a nimble foe may halve it), and the ground there caves.
