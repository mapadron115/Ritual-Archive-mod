The Builder

Animate a small construct that builds replicas of itself from nearby materials, and direct the swarm, carefully.

3arts
3passives
4technique feats
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Animate Builder3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A tiny construct for an hour, 15 damage a slam. Each 3 s a Builder on the ground takes a soft block from beside it and builds a replica (up to 12).

18th

Self-Sustaining3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest, for an hour: your Builders forage within 36 blocks for their material, not only beside themselves.

11th

The Foreman2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Four Builders merge into one large brute for the hour: 23 damage a slam.

Passives

3rd

Union Rates

Replicas add your mastery to their slams.

6th

Double Shift

You can keep twice as many Builders.

18th

Skilled Labor

Variant + Animate Builder: each Builder makes one plain tool or weapon from what you carry, at the vanilla recipe's own cost: the stone or iron tool in your hand again, or else an iron sword, or a stone one. Never from nothing. Up to 12 a long rest, one a Builder.

Technique feats

1st

Scrap Armor

While a Builder is beside you, it interposes scrap: 4 armor more.

3rd

Assembly Line

Builders replicate twice every 3 s.

1st

Literal Minds

Variant + The Foreman (Variant + Animate Builder below 11th) gives the standing order "hold this spot": your Builders stand where you look and slam any foe within 4 blocks of it, until you give the order again.

6th

Demolition Order

Technique Key 1: every Builder and brute detonates, 14 fire damage to everything within 6 blocks of each (a nimble foe may halve it), and the ground there caves.
