Shamebound Pursuit

Make seeing your face a terrible commitment: Witnesses become quarry, distance builds Fury, and every obstacle between you becomes part of the attack.

3arts
3passives
4technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Pursuit1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The Witness you look at (18 blocks) becomes your Quarry: a red thread always leads you to it, and every 3 s you close on it builds Fury (up to 6), 20% faster a Fury. A melee hit on it spends all your Fury: 13 damage a Fury. Backing away from it for 3 s burns your Fury.

6th

Four Pixels2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your face on the block you look at (6 blocks), up to 6 at once, for 20 minutes: the first foe within 18 blocks that clearly sees it becomes a Witness.

1st

Witness0 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

You uncover your face: every foe within 18 blocks that can see you and faces you recoils (it hits a quarter softer for 10 s) and becomes a Witness until your next long rest. Looking away is the only defense.

Passives

3rd

Nothing Stops the Pursuit

While you pursue, real blocks between you and your Quarry come apart as you charge into them (not the hardest), you climb the walls that don't, and nothing knocks you back.

11th

No Known Material

Moving toward your Quarry, holds and slows on you are halved. Once every 3 s, a heavy blow on you during Pursuit (a fifth of your health or more; 1 CE) spends up to 6 Fury to land 13 damage a Fury softer.

18th

Face in Every Frame

Pursuit's speed doubles.

Domain

18th

Face in Every Frame

Compulsory Recognition: each pulse every chosen foe becomes a Witness, looking or not. With no Quarry the nearest becomes one, and your Fury rises to at least 3. A Quarry that falls inside it is followed at once by the nearest chosen Witness, your Fury kept.

Technique feats

1st

Grief-Fueled Momentum

Once every 3 s, when your Quarry hurts you, you gain 1 Fury.

6th

Unseen Photograph

Your Four Pixels carry no mark at all: nobody sees one coming.

6th

No Rest Until Silence

When your Quarry falls you keep half your Fury for 6 s: a Witness taken as Quarry before then inherits it.

11th

Inhuman Acceleration

Technique Key 1 (1 CE, once every 5 minutes): with your Quarry 18 or more blocks away, you close the gap at once.
