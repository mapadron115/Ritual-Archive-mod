Gamma Rage

Hulk Out, Thunder Clap, Hulk Leap, regeneration, Gamma Quake, Worldbreaker and the Worldbreaker Smash.

6arts

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Smash0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A fist at the foe in front of you: 9 damage (hulked out 11 damage) plus your Rage. Worldbreaker Grip: hulked, with Variant you seize that foe and swing it into the nearest other foe within 5 blocks, 22 damage plus your Rage to both.

3rd

Thunder Clap2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 5-block cone of thunder: 33 damage, and everything in it is shoved back.

15th

Gamma Quake5 CE · Cast (Z)

Everything within 10 blocks takes 77 damage and is knocked down; the ground caves in under you.

18th

Worldbreaker8 CE · Cast (Z)

At Rage 10: a minute where you grow larger still, blows barely hurt you, every hit lands 22 damage harder, your Rage can't fall, and your stride knocks down foes within 3 blocks of your path.

1st

Hulk Out1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A minute: you grow huge and green, your bare fists hit 11 damage plus your Rage, and nothing pushes you around.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Worldbreaker Smash10 CE · Maximum (X)

At Rage 10: a 20-block cone of shattered ground, 159 damage, everything hurled away and knocked down, and a trench torn through the ground. Rage falls to 5.

Passives

1st

Rage

Every blow you take raises Rage by 1 (up to 12), and an ally going down in your sight (within 16 blocks) by 2. 3 s without being hurt cools it by 1.

6th

Hulk Leap

Hulked out, you jump far higher and falls don't hurt you. Land from 10 blocks or more (2 CE) and the landing is a burst: 33 damage, denting the ground.

9th

Regeneration

At Rage 5 or more you heal every 3 s, unless you're poisoned or withered.

13th

Rage 8

At Rage 8: tougher against blows, and once every 3 s your smash adds 11 damage.

20th

Strongest One There Is

Rage up to 12. Once a long rest, a blow that would kill you puts you into Worldbreaker at half a heart instead.

Technique feats

1st

Green Scar

Technique Key 1 (1 CE and 1 Rage, at Rage 5+): a roar. Foes within 5 blocks recoil and hit far softer for 3 s.

3rd

Hulk Catch

Hulked out, once every 3 s you may catch a shot that would hit you (2 CE): about half the time, it's yours, and you hurl it back at its shooter within 20 blocks for 33 damage.

3rd

Collateral Bulwark

Variant + Technique Key 1 (1 CE, Rage 5+): you tear up the ground into cover; for 3 s shots at you land at half.
