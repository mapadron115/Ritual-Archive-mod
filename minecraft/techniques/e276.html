Moon Dregs

A lunar jellyfish shikigami carrying escalating poisons, battlefield cover, and a neurotoxin Maximum.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Lunar Jellyfish0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Moon Dregs drifts beside you for 8 hours, stinging the foes near it each 3 s: 41 damage (free the first time each long rest, then 5 CE). It can be struck down, and then can't be called for a minute. With Variant while it's out: small or large again (1 CE). Once a round your own blow carries its poison too (1 CE): 66 damage. Large and beside you, it takes 3 in 4 of the shots at you.

3rd

Poison Jar2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

With the feat: milk Moon Dregs' venom onto your weapon. Your next 3 weapon blows within a minute add 14 damage and envenom.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Neurotoxin10 CE · Maximum (X)

Moon Dregs bursts a cloud of neurotoxin: everything within 9 blocks of it takes 41 damage poison and is paralyzed 3 s (a hardy foe may halve it and stay free).
