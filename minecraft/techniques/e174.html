Death Makes Two

Severe injury grows more spectral heads unless the wound is cauterized: more heads, more bites, more venom, more body to punish.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Hydra Strike1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Every head lunges at the foe you look at (4 blocks, 9 in the Lernaean Body): 13 damage a head, and venom. Hydra Venom: a foe already poisoned takes a die more.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Ninefold Lernaean Body10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute: nine heads, 13 damage a bite with a reach of 9 blocks, and you tower. A head regrown heals two dice (one if fire reached you). When it ends, the heads past your cap vanish.

Passives

1st

Two Grow Back

You begin with two heads (up to 9). Once every 3 s, a heavy blow (a tenth of your health) that leaves you standing opens a Neck Wound (1 CE): at the next 3 s it grows a head and mends you a die plus your Constitution, unless fire reached you first. Each head bites the nearest foe within 3 blocks every 3 s and gives you 2 armor.

1st

Shed the Neck

Once every 3 s, a blow that would leave you under half health is taken by a head instead (1 CE): no harm, one head less, and its stump is a Neck Wound. From 11th, two Head Dice and 1 CE shed a second neck in the same 3 s.

6th

Two From One

A wound that regrows takes 2 CE more, if you have it, and grows two heads.

11th

Unkillable Neck

With four heads or more, a regrowth heals a die more, free.

Technique feats

1st

Swamp-Born

You swim swiftly and breathe water, and a wound opened in water can't be cauterized until it has rooted.

3rd

Venom Reservoir

Your heads' venom is a leash: what they bite is slowed (not a strong foe), and they reach 3 blocks farther for the poisoned.

6th

Heracles Problem

Each Neck Wound cauterized by fire mends you 10% of your health.

3rd

Guarding Heads

Once every 3 s, a head intercepts a blow on an ally within 2.5 blocks (1 CE, a Head Die): it lands a die plus your Constitution softer.
