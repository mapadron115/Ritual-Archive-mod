Death Makes Two

Severe injury grows more spectral heads unless the wound is cauterized: more heads, more bites, more venom, more body to punish.

Passives

1st

Two Grow Back

A heavy blow (a tenth of your health) grows another head (up to 7); fire burns one away instead. Each head bites the nearest foe within 3 blocks every 3 s and gives you 2 armor.

Technique feats

1st

Swamp-Born

You swim swiftly and breathe water, and while you're in water fire can't burn a head away.

3rd

Venom Reservoir

Your heads' venom is a leash: what they bite is slowed (not a strong foe).

6th

Heracles Problem

Each head burned away by fire mends you 8% of your health.
