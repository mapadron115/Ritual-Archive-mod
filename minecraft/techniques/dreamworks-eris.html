Discord

The goddess of chaos with a golden apple: bind foes in spite, sting them for it, and turn them on each other.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

The Apple of Discord2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Roll the apple to where you look (36 blocks): foes within 6 blocks of it are spite-bound for a minute, muddled now and then, and each gives you a Spite, up to 18 (a steady mind resists).

6th

Sow Discord4 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 Spite: the foe you look at (36 blocks) turns on whatever is nearest it for 9 s (a steady mind resists; a player or a great foe only fights weakened).

1st

Spite0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

1 Spite: every spite-bound foe you can see, and the foe you look at, takes 4 damage.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The Apple, Perfected10 CE · Maximum (X)

The true apple where you look (72 blocks): every foe within 18 blocks takes 32 damage, is spite-bound, and turns on the others. Your Spite fills.

Passives

11th

Petty Spite

Once every 3 s, 2 Spite makes a creature's blow on you go wrong: it does nothing.

15th

Goddess of Chaos

Nothing muddles you (no nausea), and you never start a fight with less than 6 Spite.
