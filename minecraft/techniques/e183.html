Stormfather Catastrophe

Build Cataclysm by changing disasters, fire, lightning, thunder, venom, gale, layering ruin instead of repeating one best attack.

6arts

18thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Fire Breath2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 6-block cone of fire: 20 damage and burning; the ground in it catches.

1st

Lightning2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A bolt from the sky onto the foe you look at (18 blocks): 27 damage, and it leaps to one more for 18 damage.

3rd

Thunderclap2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Thunder round you (4 blocks): 18 damage, and everything is thrown back.

6th

Venom2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A gout of venom where you look (12 blocks, 3-block splash): 18 damage and badly poisoned.

11th

Gale2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 12-block gale down your look: 27 damage, and it hurls creatures and loose items alike.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Siege of Heaven10 CE · Maximum (X)

Every disaster at once where you look (6 blocks across): 111 damage, burning, poisoned and thrown.

Passives

1st

Cataclysm

A different phenomenon from your last builds Cataclysm (up to 5); the same one twice bleeds a point. Each point makes every phenomenon hit harder.

Technique feats

1st

Tireless Coils

Technique Key 1 drives your serpent limbs into the ground for 3 s: you can't move or be moved, and blows land 30% softer.

6th

Polyphonic Horror

A foe struck by two different phenomena within 3 s may break (1 Cataclysm): it stands frozen in horror 3 s (a strong will may hold).

11th

Last Challenge of Olympus

When you fall, the storm spends everything left: foes within 3 blocks take 22 damage per Cataclysm.
