Stormfather Catastrophe

Build Cataclysm by changing disasters, fire, lightning, thunder, venom, gale, layering ruin instead of repeating one best attack.

7arts
4passives
4technique feats
18thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Fire Breath2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 9-block cone of fire: 45 damage and burning; the ground in it catches.

1st

Lightning2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A bolt from the sky onto the foe you look at (18 blocks): 45 damage, and it leaps to one more for 36 damage.

1st

Thunderclap2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Thunder round you (6 blocks): 45 damage, and everything is thrown back.

1st

Venom2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A gout of venom where you look (18 blocks, 3-block splash): 45 damage and badly poisoned.

3rd

Serpent Limbs2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute your legs are serpent coils: a third faster, and you climb walls (sneak to cling). While you hold half your Cataclysm or more, storm-wings: you fly. After 12 blocks of movement, a weapon blow with Variant held spends 1 Cataclysm: 18 damage more, and the coils seize the foe for 1.5 s unless it tears free.

11th

Gale2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A 18-block gale down your look: 45 damage, and it hurls creatures and loose items alike.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Siege of Heaven8 CE · Maximum (X)

Once a long rest. For three 3 s periods you are the storm: you tower, fly and can't be thrown. At once and then every 3 s, a catastrophe the last was not falls where you look (36 blocks, 9 blocks round it): the eruption burns, the thunderhead leaves no one able to react, the hurricane hurls, the venom rain poisons. Each deals 36 damage.

Passives

1st

Cataclysm

The first time every 3 s you wound a foe with a phenomenon unlike the last you used, 1 Cataclysm (up to 6). Repeating yourself gains nothing.

1st

Discordant Layering

Hold Variant as you loose a phenomenon: 1 Cataclysm wreathes it in the next disaster in turn (fire, lightning, thunder, venom) for 9 damage more.

6th

Many-Mouthed Tempest

The wreathing takes up to 3 Cataclysm, a disaster each, and each also leaves its mark: fire sets alight, lightning leaves the foe unable to react for 3 s, thunder throws it, venom poisons.

11th

Mountain-Buried Monster

Once every 3 s, a foe's blow of a tenth of your health or more costs 3 CE and lands 33 damage softer, and rubble slows the foes within 3 blocks. Below half health your Cataclysm holds 2 more and you fly on storm-wings.

Technique feats

1st

Tireless Coils

Technique Key 1 drives your serpent limbs into the ground for 3 s: you can't move or be moved, and blows land 30% softer.

3rd

Volcanic Prison

Variant + Technique Key 1 on the foe your coils seized (within 3 s), 2 Cataclysm: cooling magma holds it 3 s unless it breaks out, and burns it for 9 fire damage as it closes and again as it cracks.

6th

Polyphonic Horror

A foe struck by two different phenomena within 3 s may break (1 Cataclysm): it stands frozen in horror 3 s (a strong will may hold).

11th

Last Challenge of Olympus

When you fall, the storm spends everything left: foes within 9 blocks take 18 damage per Cataclysm.
