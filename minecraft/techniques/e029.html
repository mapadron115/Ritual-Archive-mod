Solo Forbidden Area

A ritual zone where everything goes your way: truer blows, lighter wounds, a biting edge, full output, and a hallowed pulse.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Forbidden Area2 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute, a 18-block zone where you stand: inside it you strike true more often (15%) and blows land 15% lighter on you, and your allies inside are stronger and tougher. The zone doesn't move.

Bless3 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute the ally you look at (within 36 blocks), or else you, carries the Zone's favor anywhere (instead of a zone).
