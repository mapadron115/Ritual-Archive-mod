Emerald Will

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Emerald Construct0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) and Quick (G)

1 CE and 1 Charge: a construct of will flies where you look, 30 blocks, 18 damage.

3rd

Flight0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 CE and 1 Charge: ten minutes of flight.

6th

Complex Construct0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 CE and 2 Charge, where you look (30 blocks), chosen with Technique Key 1: a giant hand (36 damage, and held for 3 s) · a cage (held for 9 s) · a wall of will (stops foes and shots for a minute).

11th

Ring Scan0 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The living things within 48 blocks, counted, and the nearest named with their health. With Variant (2 Charge), each player also shows their technique and CE.

13th

Mecha Construct4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

5 Charge, a minute: construct armor. +10 armor, flight, and your constructs hit 22 damage harder.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Emerald Colossus10 CE · Maximum (X)

10 Charge, 9 s: a towering construct fights beside you. Every second it slams the foe nearest where you look, 66 damage within 6 blocks.

Passives

1st

Power Ring

Ring charge (20) comes back 1 every 10 s, and in full on a long rest.

3rd

Shield of Will

Once every 3 s (1 CE and 1 Charge), a blow is caught on a construct shield: it lands softer by 22% of your health, and you gain 1 Will (up to 10).

9th

Willpower

With Will, each construct spends 1 for 11 damage more.

15th

No Evil Shall Escape

While holding 3+ Will, the first construct each 3 s costs 1 less Charge.

Domain

18th

Sector 2814

Every 3 s a construct acts on its own, in turn: a blast on every foe it chose (89 damage), or a giant hand that holds them until the next.
