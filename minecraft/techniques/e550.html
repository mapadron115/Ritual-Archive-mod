Seven Seals

Your bound power is a sealed scroll. Hold the seals and be warded; break them one by one and unleash judgment, irreversibly, until the dawn.

2arts
1passives
2technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Break a Seal0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Break the next seal, irreversibly until a long rest: 1 White Horse, a third of your blows land critically for a minute; 2 Red Horse, war bursts around you, 27 damage within 6 blocks, and the ground there burns; 3 Black Horse, famine: foes within 9 blocks hit 40% softer for a minute, and the crops wither; 4 Pale Horse, 53 damage to the foe you look at; 5 The Martyrs, healing for each seal broken; 6 The Great Earthquake, 71 damage within 9 blocks, foes thrown and the soft ground caved in.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The Seventh Trumpet10 CE · Maximum (X)

Once a long rest: every foe within 36 blocks takes 22 damage plus as much again per seal broken (a tough foe halves it).

Passives

1st

The Sealed Scroll

Once every 3 s, a weapon blow spends 1 CE for a sealed strike: 9 damage more, and as much again per seal broken. While every seal holds, blows land on you softer.

Technique feats

3rd

The Sealed Scroll

Once a long rest, a blow that would kill you shatters the next seal instead: you stand at 1 health and its judgment falls.

6th

Sound It Again

Technique Key 1 (once every 2 minutes) sounds your last broken seal's judgment a second time.
