Living Circuit

Run your body as a circuit of lightning: Godspeed's Whirlwind sidesteps blows, Speed of Lightning outruns them, and every flash stuns.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Lightning Step0 CE · 0.3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

With Godspeed on: flash 5 blocks along your look (9 in Speed of Lightning, cutting through foes for 18 damage at most once a second). It stops at walls.

3rd

Speed of Lightning0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

With Godspeed on: switch between Whirlwind and Speed of Lightning.

6th

Thunderbolt2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A bolt falls where you look (18 blocks): 45 damage within 2 blocks. With the feat Thunderclap, Variant makes it a flash instead: no harm, but every foe within 9 blocks is blinded for 3 s.

20th

The Storm, Perfected0 CE · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest, for a minute: no upkeep, Whirlwind and Speed of Lightning at once, and more speed still. You're exhausted after.

1st

Godspeed2 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Throw the circuit on (Whirlwind): 1 CE every 6 s keeps it running, and nothing while you stand within 5 blocks of a source of current (powered redstone, a redstone block, a lightning rod or a lit lamp). Lightning lands at half on you while it runs. Cast again to drop it.

Maximum

1st

Maximum: Storm's Verdict8 CE · Maximum (X)

Become the bolt: dash 6 blocks along your look through everything in the way: 71 damage and stunned 2 s (a strong foe keeps its feet).

Passives

1st

Whirlwind

While Godspeed runs: 60% faster at running, swinging and mining, and one attack in three misses as you sidestep it.

3rd

Speed of Lightning

130% faster (200% Perfected); your weapon blows crackle for 18 damage more (three times a second at most), and once every 3 s one stuns its foe 3 s.

11th

Counter-Program

When Whirlwind makes a blow miss, you flash to the attacker and strike back for 18 damage (once every 3 s).

18th

Arc Discharge

Your lightning arcs to a second foe within 4 blocks for half.

Domain

18th

Thunderhead

Storm's Judgment: a free Thunderbolt falls on every chosen foe, and inside it your lightning can't be shrugged off: your stuns hold even a strong foe.

Technique feats

1st

Static Sense

While Godspeed runs you read the room by its charge: you see a spark on every living creature within 16 blocks.

1st

Programmed Fall

Whirlwind's orders include the ground: while Godspeed runs, falling never hurts you.

3rd

Ground Wire

Godspeed on or off, the current is yours: lightning about to strike you or an ally within 4.5 blocks grounds through you harmlessly, and whatever sent it glows for you 5 s.

6th

Thunderclap

Thunderbolt can be called as a flash (Variant): no harm, but every foe within 9 blocks is blinded for 3 s.

11th

Dead-Man's Program

When a blow drops you below a fifth of your health in Godspeed, you flash 6 blocks away from the nearest foe (once a minute).
