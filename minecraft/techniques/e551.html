Moonphase

Attuned to the lunar cycle: every blow carries the current moon's rider, silent at New, swift at Half, hungry at Gibbous, crushing at Full.

5arts
2passives
2technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Moonrest2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

At night under the open sky, bathe in moonlight and heal.

6th

Tidecall3 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Tidal force surges 9 blocks around where you look (18 blocks): foes are dragged down and slowed (a strong foe holds firm).

11th

Phase Eclipse10 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest: for a minute the moon counts one step fuller.

3rd

Borrowed Light3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Once a long rest: for a minute your blows carry the phase Technique Key 1 picked (not Full) instead of the real one.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Totality10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute your blows carry the Full Moon at 27 damage and the New Moon's silence at once. Cast it again inside it to call down the dark of the moon: 89 damage to every foe within 36 blocks (once).

Passives

1st

Moon-Touched

Your weapon blows carry the current phase (1 CE, once a second): New, the foe loses you; Half, a burst of speed; Gibbous, 7 damage more and a little life; Full, 18 damage more.

18th

Lunar Constant

The moon is never less than Half for you.

Technique feats

1st

The Dark Side

Under a New Moon you are the side no one sees: you're unseen, and creatures lose interest.

3rd

Lunacy

Once every 3 s, a Full Moon blow (or one in Totality) makes foes within 9 blocks strange: they hit 25% softer for 3 s.
