Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Bank a Charge1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Spend CE straight into the bank: 2 Jin charges.

6th

Fist of Explosive Power3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Spend up to 6 charges as compressed air shaped by your weapon: a sword, axe or trident fires a 9-block line (22 damage a charge); anything else a 4.5-block cone (14 damage a charge) that knocks foes down and blows loose things away. With 3 or more charges, 9 blocks farther.

1st

Store and Release0 CE · Quick (G) · hold to pour

Hold to commit charges to your next melee blow (up to 6; CE pays when the bank is empty): 9 damage each if it lands. If the swing finds nothing, they're spent (you keep up to 5).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Destructive Release5 CE · Maximum (X)

The vault emptied at once: Fist of Explosive Power at triple force a charge, and everything it catches is thrown back.

Passives

1st

Store and Release

A melee swing that misses with a foe within 4 blocks banks a Jin charge (up to 26). Charges are lost on a long rest.

1st

Kinetic Absorption

A heavy blow (a tenth of your health or more) is partly banked: 1 CE takes 27 damage off it and gives you a charge (every 2 s).

3rd

Instantaneous Movement

Stand your ground for 2 s in a fight: +1 charge. Technique Key 1: spend a charge to blink 3 blocks along your look; holding Variant, spend 3 to blink 9 and your next blow lands with thunder (a critical, and harder).

Domain

18th

Hall of Divine Bagua

Inside, your bank never runs dry; every chosen foe takes on a negative charge each pulse, its blows 10% softer each time, until the Domain ends.
