Umbral Witchcraft

Dance with gunfire for punctuation, dodge into Witch Time, and pay your infernal creditors in other people's pain.

7arts
4passives
4technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

11th

Infernal Summon4 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

One demon attack at the foe you look at (18 blocks). Gomorrah's bite: 66 damage. Madama's fist: 55 damage and a launch skyward. Phantasmaraneae's web: 44 damage and bound. Technique Key 1 picks the demon.

9th

Beast Within1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your body turns beast for 10 s: the panther, twice as fast on the ground; with Variant the crow, and you fly.

18th

Queen Sheba10 CE · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest, with 3 Tempo or more (all of it is spent): the Queen's fist comes down where you look (36 blocks). Everything within 18 blocks takes 159 damage and is hurled away (a great foe holds).

13th

Demon Masquerade5 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute you wear your chosen demon: you fly, blows land a fifth softer on you, and your blows bite 11 damage harder.

15th

Umbran Climax1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Spend 3 Tempo: for 3 s every melee blow calls a Wicked Weave for free.

3rd

Witch Time0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A split-second dodge: a blow that lands in the next moment misses, and time slows in a bubble 8 blocks around you for 2 s. Foes crawl, and arrows and fireballs hang in the air. You gain Tempo (once every 3 s).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Infernal Finale10 CE · Maximum (X)

Spend 5 Tempo: all three demons strike at once, each at a different foe near where you look.

Passives

1st

Bullet Arts

Once every 3 s, after a melee blow, your guns fire a free shot at the foe (7 damage).

6th

Wicked Weave

Two melee blows within a second call a giant infernal fist out of a portal behind you (2 CE): 33 damage, and the foe is launched skyward (a great foe holds).

3rd

Tempo

Witch Time builds Tempo, up to 10.

20th

Witch Without Equal

Tempo holds 10.

Technique feats

3rd

Umbran Sidestep

Your dodge carries you: Witch Time also slips you 3 blocks along your look.

3rd

Moon of Mahaa-Kalaa

A blow that triggers Witch Time is parried into another foe within 2 blocks of the attacker (1 Tempo): it takes the full blow.

6th

Bat Within

Once every 10 s, a blow that lands bursts you into bats (1 Tempo): it lands at half, and you reform 3 blocks away.

11th

Pure Platinum

Once a minute, your Witch Time is flawless: the slowed time lasts twice as long.
