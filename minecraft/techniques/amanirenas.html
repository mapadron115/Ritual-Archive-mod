The Bronze Head

The one-eyed Kandake of Kush: every victory is a transfer, and she fights with what she has taken.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Kandake's Spear1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A spear thrust at the foe you look at (9 blocks): 9 damage, and 1 damage more for each Trophy.

6th

The Threshold5 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute a Trophy lies beneath your feet: nothing moves you, fear's weakness slides off, and blows from foes within 9 blocks land a fifth softer.

3rd

Wear the Taken2 CE · Quick (G)

For a minute you wear a Trophy's aspect: 15% faster, 6 more armor, and one weapon blow in twenty is critical. Needs a Trophy; it isn't spent.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The Head Under the Throne10 CE · Maximum (X)

Spend every Trophy (3 at least) on one blow at the foe you look at (9 blocks): 13 damage for each Trophy.

Passives

1st

Claim the Fallen

Every foe you fell leaves a Trophy (up to 6; a long rest clears them). Each weapon blow lands 1 damage harder for each Trophy.

11th

Tithe of the Dying

Any foe that falls within 36 blocks of you leaves a Trophy.

Domain

18th

Meroe: The Palace of Taken Things

Everything breaks into her hands. Each pulse every chosen foe takes 16 damage and is disarmed: its blows on you land at half until the next.
