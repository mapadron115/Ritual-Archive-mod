Temporal Archmage

Phase out of blows, delay the enemy, retrace your steps, and finally unhappen the last few seconds.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Chronostrike1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A mote of stopped time (18 blocks): 18 damage, and what it hits is slowed.

6th

Temporal Step1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 Time Counter: you're back where you stood 6 s ago.

9th

Time Raveler3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 Time Counter: the foe you look at (18 blocks) loses its readiness for 3 s: a player can't cast and their shield is knocked down; a creature is slowed hard.

15th

Stasis6 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) is held out of the moment for 3 s: unseen, held, and out of the fight with you both ways (a strong will may hold).

13th

Temporal Reversal5 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

2 Time Counters: your health returns to what it was 6 s ago.

3rd

Delay2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) is held 2.5 s out of time (a strong will shrugs it off). +1 Time Counter.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Hero's Rewind12 CE · Maximum (X)

5 Time Counters: the last few seconds unhappen. You return to where you stood with the health you had, and every foe within 18 blocks is pulled back to where it was.

Passives

1st

Phase

Once every 3 s, a blow on you may find you a moment out of step (2 CE, one time in three): it misses, and you slip 3 blocks aside. +1 Time Counter.

11th

Hero of Dominaria

You can bank two more Time Counters, and an art of 3 CE or more readies Phase again.

20th

Master of Time

Time Counters hold 10. Once a long rest, Technique Key 1 (3 CE and 6 Time Counters) opens a Temporal Window: your cooldowns clear and you're quick for 3 s.

Domain

18th

The Slow Sunset

Each pulse the evening lengthens: three markers fall. Your allies near you move quicker for 3 s, and the rest land on chosen foes, slowed hard for 3 s.
