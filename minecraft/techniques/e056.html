Arts

1st

Release0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The wound punch at what's in front of you (3.5 blocks): 13 damage a wind. From 5 winds its blast craters the soft ground.

3rd

Shockwave Release0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The punch as a 9-block line of wind along your look: 13 damage a wind to everything in it.

11th

Split Winding0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Split your winds between the two nearest foes.

20th

The Arm Never Stops0 CE · Cast (Z)

Once a long rest, for a minute: a free wind every 3 s, no maximum and no winding down. You're exhausted after.

1st

Wind0 CE · Quick (G) · hold to pour

Tap to wind the arm once, or hold to keep winding, a turn every 0.5 s (1 CE each; up to 15).

Maximum

1st

Maximum: Full Rotation8 CE · Maximum (X)

The arm winds to its maximum (15, or what you've built if that's more) and releases: 13 damage a wind, and the foe is thrown four blocks and knocked down 2 s.

Passives

1st

A threat made in public

While you hold winds you take 15% more. Winds fade after a minute without winding or releasing.

6th

Bleed-Off

Once every 3 s, 1 CE: a heavy blow vents winds, each lessening it by 7% of your health.

18th

Ground Zero

Releasing 10 or more winds also strikes everything within 4.5 blocks of the impact for half, and knocks it down.

Domain

18th

Centrifuge

The room winds up: winding is free inside it, +2 winds a pulse, every chosen foe is dragged 4.5 blocks in a spiral toward you, and loose things orbit you.
