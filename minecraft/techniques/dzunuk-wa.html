Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Swipe1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A huge hand swipes the nearest foe (9 blocks): 15 damage.

6th

She Eats the Vanguard5 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A bite on the nearest foe (9 blocks): 36 damage. If it dies it's devoured, and you regain half the damage.

6th

Into the Basket4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Whoever you're holding goes into the basket: 53 damage, and if that doesn't end it, it's held there for 3 s (a strong foe breaks free).

11th

The Basket Is Never Full7 CE · 12 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You shake the basket: every foe within 36 blocks is frightened for a minute. Its blows on you land 15% softer, 5% more for each one devoured (half at most), and a weak mind runs.

3rd

The Woods Close2 CE · Quick (G)

For a minute the ground within 18 blocks of you is deadfall: foes in it are slowed.

Maximum

18th

Maximum: Winter's Mother10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you are famine itself: nothing moves you, your regeneration doubles, and every foe within 18 blocks starves, 12 damage every 3 s, 4 times at most on each foe, and slowed. Once a long rest.

Passives

1st

The Cry

You can't be aimed: your arts always take the nearest foe.

1st

The Forest Outlasts

Every 3 s you regain 4% of your health, though not for 3 s after fire touches you. The first blow every 3 s is dulled by 10% of your health, never below a quarter of the blow.

3rd

Snatch

Once every 3 s (3 CE), a creature that strikes you within 9 blocks is grabbed and held in your grip for 3 s (never a great foe).
