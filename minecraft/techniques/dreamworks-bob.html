No Brain, No Pain

A blob with no brain to break and no organs to find: shrug off everything, swallow whoever gets close, and become the disaster movie.

4arts

11thMaximum

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Squelch1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A gooey slam on the foe you look at (6 blocks): 16 damage, and it's slowed for 3 s.

14th

Snack Time0 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Eat one item lying within 3 blocks of you, whatever it is: regain 33% of your health. 6 times a long rest.

3rd

Engulf2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Swallow the foe you look at (6 blocks): 14 damage going in, held inside you for 3 s, and 14 damage again as it's spat out (a strong foe squirms free at once; a great foe can't be swallowed).

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The Blob10 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you're huge: every foe within 9 blocks takes 14 damage every 3 s, 6 times at most on each foe, and is slowed, blows on you land a third softer, and nothing knocks you back.

Passives

1st

No Brain to Break

Nothing frightens or muddles you: weakness from fear, nausea and darkness slide off.

6th

No Vital Organs

There's nothing for a blade to find: every blow on you lands a tenth softer.

11th

Indestructible

Every blow on you is blunted by 11% of your health, though never below a quarter of the blow.
