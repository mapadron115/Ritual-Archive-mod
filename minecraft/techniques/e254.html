Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Dark Cube2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

An opaque cube closes on the foe you look at (9 blocks): real walls for 2 rounds, and the foe is held 3 s as it shuts (a nimble foe may slip out first). Hold the cast to reinforce: up to 10 CE more, each 3 3 s longer. Technique Key 1 makes the next one a Perfect Cube (5 CE more): no one slips it.

11th

Midnight Fist0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Wearing Midnight Armor: a fist of the cube on the foe you look at (4.5 blocks), 33 damage.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Midnight Armor6 CE · Maximum (X)

For a minute you wear the cube: mended for its strength, 15 armor more and 40% less from every blow, and Midnight Fist opens.

Domain

18th

Primordial Gap

Three fresh Perfect Cubes rise around three chosen foes, holding them 1.5 s and walling them in until the next pulse.

Offensive Creations

A Dark Cube that traps a foe crushes it as it closes (4 CE more: 55 damage), and once every 3 s your weapon blows wrapped in cube add 28 damage (2 CE).
