Mind Sculptor

Read surface thoughts, fill the field with illusory doubles, send summons home, and mill an enemy's mind until it breaks.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Psychic Spike1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (20 blocks): 18 damage, and its mind is read. Its blows on you land softer for 3 s (a sharp mind isn't read). +1 Thought when it is.

6th

Unsummon3 CE · 1.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A lesser foe you look at (not a great one, not a player) is sent away: it lands 25 to 40 blocks off, dazed (a strong will resists).

9th

Memory Mill3 CE · 1.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1 Thought: the foe you look at forgets how to shoot for a minute. Its arrows, fireballs and spit dissolve as they leave it (a sharp mind resists).

13th

Thoughtseize4 CE · 1.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at loses its best ideas: it hits far softer for a minute. +1 Thought. A sorcerer's arts are named to you, and for 6 s every art they cast costs 2 CE more.

15th

Perfect Illusion5 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A room that isn't there, 4.5 blocks around where you look, for 10 minutes: foes in it hesitate at every step (slowed). One at a time.

3rd

Illusory Doubles2 CE · 2 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A minute: 6 illusory doubles. Once every 3 s, a blow on you may strike a double instead (it shatters).

Maximum

1st

Maximum: Mindbreak10 CE · Maximum (X)

6 Thought: the foe you look at takes 111 damage and is stunned for 3 s (a sharp mind halves it and keeps its wits).

Passives

1st

Thought

You hold up to 12 Thought, gained whenever a foe's mind fails against you.

11th

Mind Palace

Your focus is hard to break: nausea, blindness and darkness can't take hold of you.

20th

Living Guildpact

Thought holds 12, and the first Thought you spend each 3 s is free.

Domain

18th

Infinite Library

Each pulse the library reads one chosen foe: if its mind gives way you gain a Thought and a Page on it, and its next blow before the next pulse is foreseen, landing at half on you or an ally.
