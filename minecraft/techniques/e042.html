100-Type Guanyin Bodhisattva

A prayer completed faster than violence can begin: a colossal golden Guanyin answers with a hundred hands, and at the end the Zero Hand turns everything into light.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

Prayer Palm: Focused1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A golden palm strikes the foe you look at (120 blocks): 33 damage.

Prayer Palm: Driving1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

20 damage, and the foe is hurled six blocks away.

1st

Prayer Palm: Grasping1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

20 damage, and the foe is held 3 s (a strong foe less).

1st

First Hand: Heaven's Descent2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The palm falls from straight above, twice the size: 33 damage, driven into the ground (the soft earth craters) and held 3 s.

3rd

Third Hand: Converging Mercy3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Two palms close on one foe from either side: 33 damage in all, and it's held 3 s.

11th

Ninety-Ninth Hand0 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

6 CE: 12 palms spread among the foes in reach, 26 damage each.

1st

Prayer Palm: Merciful1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A gentle palm throws the ally you look at (120 blocks), or yourself with no ally in sight, 6 blocks along your look, and they land softly.

6th

Faster Than Sound1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

One more Focused palm, in the same breath.

Maximum

1st

Maximum: Zero Hand15 CE · Maximum (X)

All your CE (at least 15): the foe you look at (120 blocks) takes 166 damage, plus 13 damage for every CE poured in, ignoring armor. Afterwards your CE is empty and you're exhausted for a minute.

Passives

1st

Palm Reach

120 blocks (24 at 6th, 60 at 11th, 120 at 20th); the palms hit harder at each step.

3rd

Prayer Before Violence

Once every 3 s, 2 CE: when a foe in reach strikes you, the statue answers with a Driving palm.

18th

Guarding Palm

Once every 3 s, 1 CE: a heavy blow is lessened by 15% of your health. And once every 3 s, 1 CE, a heavy blow on an ally in your reach is lessened the same way, and they're moved 3 blocks clear.

20th

Prayer Outside Time

Every 3 s in a fight, a free Focused palm strikes the nearest foe in reach (1 CE).

Domain

18th

Temple of Ten Thousand Gratitudes

Hundredfold Benediction: every chosen foe takes 166 damage and is held 1.5 s. In the temple a free palm strikes every 3 s, as with Prayer Outside Time.
