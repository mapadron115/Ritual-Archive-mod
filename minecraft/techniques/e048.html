Dark Moon Covenant

Shards of cold moonlight, moon-brands that unravel a foe, a familiar that devours them, and an age of stars.

7arts
9passives
2technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Lunar Bolt1 CE · Cast (Z)

A shard of cold moonlight along your look (60 blocks): 36 damage and a Lunar Sigil on what it hits. Each sigil (up to 6) makes that foe's blows on you 5% softer and your bolts on it land harder.

6th

Ashen Familiar3 CE · Cast (Z)

A spectral wolf at your side for an hour (two from 11th): it fights what you fight, 18 damage a bite.

6th

Freezing Mist3 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute, a 4-block cloud of freezing mist where you look: every foe that lingers in it is branded and chilled each 3 s, and the water under it freezes.

11th

Dark Moon7 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A great dark moon along your look (18 blocks): 36 damage and a sigil on each foe it crosses. What it touches comes undone: a held ally is freed, someone else's conjured creature is unmade, and shots in its way stop, a Maximum's too, and a Domain barrier it meets is struck.

18th

Black Moon Transit8 CE · 10 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You and up to 6 teammates within 6 blocks step through the dark moon to where you look (up to 90 blocks).

20th

Causal Severance3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Cut one outside binding on the creature you look at (18 blocks): someone else's conjured creature is unmade; failing that, a held teammate is freed; failing that, a foe loses every boon it carries.

6th

Devour Memory1 CE · Quick (G)

Your familiar savages the most-branded foe near it (2 blocks): 7 damage, and more for every sigil it eats.

Passives

1st

Snow Witch's Step

The cold is yours: you never freeze.

1st

Moon Shards

Each blow Cold Moon Guard turns aside leaves a shard, up to 6: the next art of this technique costs 1 CE less and spends it. They keep.

3rd

Moonlit Guidance

Variant + Lunar Bolt casts it from your familiar instead, for 1 CE more.

6th

False Body

Once every 3 s, a heavy blow (4 CE) strikes a frost-doll instead: 24% of your health less of it lands, and you're 9 blocks away.

6th

Lunar Rupture

A sigiled foe that fails against your Freezing Mist is frozen in place for 3 s, spending the sigil.

11th

Moon of Weakness

A foe bearing 2 sigils can't shrug off your mist.

11th

Spirit Court

You keep two familiars.

20th

Cold Cosmos

Your own cold, and any other, never hurts you.

3rd

Cold Moon Guard

Once every 3 s, a blow on you is turned aside by moonlight (2 CE): 12% of your health less of it lands.

Domain

18th

Age of Stars

Dark Moon Passage: the moon crosses every chosen foe, a crushing cold blow (137 damage), two Lunar Sigils, and its strength stripped away (3 s).
