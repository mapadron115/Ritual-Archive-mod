Extinguishment1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (12 blocks) is struck by light: 66 damage, and its powers go out for 3 s: it can't fire, a sorcerer can't cast, Infinity falls, and it fights weaker (a strong will may hold on; the undead can't). Once every 3 s.

A pillar of light where you look (40 blocks), 12 blocks wide: everything inside takes 106 damage and is extinguished, and every hostile shot in it is unmade. Blocks a technique made come apart in it, and a closed barrier it touches takes the blow.
