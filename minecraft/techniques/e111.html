One-Winged Calamity

A long blade, a single black wing, and despair as a resource, until a star is brought down on the field.

7arts

4technique feats

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Shadow Flare2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Five dark orbs gather around you and spiral in on the foe you look at (36 blocks): 27 damage in all, and a foe that can't shrug it off gains Despair.

6th

Heartless Angel5 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A shaft of pale light falls on the foe you look at (36 blocks): 66 damage. Unless it's very tough, it can't heal for 3 s, the dark closes in on it, and it despairs.

6th

Octaslash3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Eight cuts in a breath on the foe you look at (4.5 blocks): 40 damage in all.

13th

Hell's Gate3 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

In the air, 6 blocks or more up: plunge straight down, the blade first. Everything within 9 blocks of where you land takes 66 damage, and one that can't brace is knocked flat.

15th

Pale Horse6 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (54 blocks) is ridden down by a pale shadow: 89 damage and poisoned for 3 s; one it overwhelms gains Despair.

11th

One Wing4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute a black wing unfolds from your right shoulder: you fly, faster, your blade bites 9 damage harder, and blindness and the dark can't touch you.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Supernova12 CE · Maximum (X)

Mark the ground you look at (30 blocks): a star falls on it from high overhead for 3 s, then detonates for 6 blocks around: 155 damage, fire and 2 Despair (a tough foe halves it, no Despair), and it craters the ground. Heavy blows on you before it lands may scatter it.

Passives

1st

Masamune

Your reach is a block and a half longer. Once every 3 s a melee blow draws 1 CE for 9 damage more and drags the foe toward you.

1st

Despair

Foes your magic overwhelms gain Despair (up to 3, for a minute): each point weakens their blows, and you hit them 5% harder.

20th

Safer Sephiroth

Once a long rest a killing blow leaves you at 1 health with One Wing unfolding. Your blows on a foe at 3 Despair are critical one time in ten (half again as hard).

Domain

18th

Nibelheim's Burning Memory

The village burns again. Each pulse every chosen foe faces you or flees: one that faces you takes 71 damage unless it can bear it (it can't at 3 Despair); one that turns away is driven toward the edge and despairs.
