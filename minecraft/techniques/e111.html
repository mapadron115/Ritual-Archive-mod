One-Winged Calamity

A long blade, a single black wing, and despair as a resource, until a star is brought down on the field.

4arts
2passives
3technique feats
11thMaximum
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Shadow Flare2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Five dark orbs gather around you and spiral in on the foe you look at (12 blocks): 27 damage in all, and a foe that can't shrug it off gains Despair.

6th

Heartless Angel5 CE · 2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A shaft of pale light falls on the foe you look at (12 blocks): 66 damage. Unless it's very tough, it can't heal for 3 s, the dark closes in on it, and it despairs.

11th

One Wing4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute a black wing unfolds from your right shoulder: you fly, faster, your blade bites 9 damage harder, and blindness and the dark can't touch you.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Supernova12 CE · Maximum (X)

Mark the ground you look at (30 blocks): a star falls on it from high overhead for 3 s, then detonates for 6 blocks around: 155 damage, fire and 2 Despair (a tough foe halves it, no Despair), and it craters the ground. Heavy blows on you before it lands may scatter it.

Passives

1st

Masamune

Your reach is a block and a half longer. Once every 3 s a melee blow draws 1 CE for 9 damage more and drags the foe toward you.

1st

Despair

Foes your magic overwhelms gain Despair (up to 3, for a minute): each point weakens their blows, and you hit them 5% harder.

Technique feats

1st

Long Blade

Once every 3 s, a melee blow carries on in a wide arc into a second foe within 4.5 blocks of the first, for half.

3rd

Chosen by Jenova

Jenova's cells devour what would change you: poison, hunger and nausea don't take.

11th

Black Materia

The ritual, insured: no blow can scatter a falling Supernova.
