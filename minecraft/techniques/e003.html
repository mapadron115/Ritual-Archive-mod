Blood Manipulation

Your own blood as a weapon: spinning rings, walls of congealed blood, binding nets, a flooded body, a supersonic line and waiting orbs.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Slicing Exorcism1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A spinning ring of blood flies 12 blocks along your look, cutting through everything in its path: 11 damage. Costs 1% of your health.

1st

Blood Constructs1 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A wall of congealed blood rises where you look (3 wide, 3 high, 2 wider per Convergence step): real blocks that stop foes and shots, gone after five minutes. Costs 3% of your health.

1st

Crimson Binding4 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A net of blood flies at the foe you look at (6 blocks): it's bound for 1.5 s (half a second more per Convergence step, up to 3 s; a strong foe may tear free). Costs 2% of your health.

3rd

Flowing Red Scale3 CE · Cast (Z)

For a minute your blood runs at full speed: twice as fast, 6 more armor, blows a quarter faster.

11th

Piercing Blood10 CE · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A 20-block line of supersonic blood along your look, through every foe on it: 89 damage. Costs 10% of your health.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Supernova10 CE · Maximum (X)

Up to 6 orbs of compressed blood hang around you (each beyond the first costs 10 CE and 10% of your health). When a foe comes within 2 blocks of one, or after a minute, they all burst: 89 damage each.

Technique feats

1st

Blood Transfusion

Once every 3 s, an ally within 5 blocks who falls below half health is given your blood (1 CE and 5% of your health): they heal.

1st

Crimson Clot

Your blood arts clot in the wound: a foe they hit is held for a moment (once every 3 s per foe).

3rd

Blood Crystallization

Flowing Red Scale also crystallizes your blood into armor: a quarter less damage taken and 5 more armor, but every 3 s there's a 15% chance the brittle flesh gives (weakened for 30 s).

3rd

Red Current

While Flowing Red Scale runs, you run up walls and across water.

6th

Blood Edge

Your weapon blows are edged in blood: 11 damage more each (a sliver of your health per blow).

6th

Flowing Red Scale: Stack

Use Flowing Red Scale again while it runs (3 CE) and the flood deepens: three times as fast, 10 more armor and blows half again faster.
