Projection Sorcery

A declared 24-frame route converts strict movement into accelerating speed, impact force, and frame-stun vulnerability.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Projection1 CE · Cast (Z)

Start the reel (1 CE, then 1 CE every 6 s): faster, and your swings come quicker. Every second of movement is committed once you set off: turn back inside it or stop dead against something and Projection tears. Cast again to stop.

6th

24 Frame Rule3 CE · 10 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Your next melee blow deals no damage: instead the foe is stuck in one frame, frozen for 3 s, and the first blow to reach it lands half again as hard. Then the frame breaks.

6th

Air Shatter3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

After a straight run of 8 blocks or more: the air you displaced shatters along the 12 blocks behind you, 55 damage (more with your speed).

Maximum

11th

Sakuga10 CE · Maximum (X)

Twelve frames drawn at once: you cross up to 24 blocks toward where you look at blinding speed, untouchable, striking everything on the route for 133 damage. Every foe you pass is left stuck in a frame for 3 s.

Passives

3rd

Acceleration

Every full second you move on-route while projecting: +25% speed, up to 12 stacks (never past +300% in all), and your melee blows, Air Shatter and Sakuga hit harder with it (twice as hard at +300%). Once every 3 s, a melee blow after a straight run of 2 blocks or more adds 7 damage per 2 blocks of the run. Losing Projection loses both.

6th

White Space

While projecting, once every 3 s (2 CE): a blow that would land finds you between frames instead. You slip 3 blocks away and it misses.

11th

Fastest Sorcerer Alive

Projection starts with 3 stacks of Acceleration, each paid 6 s adds 2 more, and the cap doubles.

Domain

18th

Lunar Palace: Womb of Time

A jade palace under a moon too close: every foe it chose that has moved since the last pulse takes 89 damage every 3 s, as its cells rebel against the 24-frame rule.
