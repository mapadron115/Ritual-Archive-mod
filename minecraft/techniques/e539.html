Backwards Magic

Spoken backwards: telekinesis, fire, shields, illusions, vanishing, healing, teleporting, freezing, countercharms and the Backwards Word.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Speak the Verb0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Speak the verb Technique Key 1 chose, backwards: EVOM EHT TCEJBO (1 CE) throws the foe you look at 9 blocks, or lifts the block you look at and hurls it. ERIF (1 CE) is fire, 22 damage, and lights where it lands. DLEIHS (1 CE) halves the next blow within 3 s. NOISULLI (2 CE): an illusion 6 blocks around where you look, for 10 minutes; foes in it lose the fight in it. ESAPAHC (1 CE): you vanish for 3 s. LAEH (2 CE) heals. TROPELET (2 CE) teleports you 18 blocks to where you look. EERF (2 CE) freezes the foe you look at for 3 s, and the water around it. LLEPS EHT OTUAH (3 CE): every hostile shot within 18 blocks unravels. It also breaks holds on you and your allies in reach. Every verb that harms deals 9 damage more, and once a long rest, Variant + this speaks the verb and the next together, both costs + 1 CE.

1st

Escape Artist1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

With the feat: it was a trick all along. Holds, slowing, frost and confusion fall away, and webs around you part.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: The Backwards Word10 CE · Maximum (X)

One whole sentence, backwards, without a stumble: for a minute every lesser foe within 36 blocks forgets the fight, every great one fights at half its strength, and you're mended. Once in the minute, Variant + Technique Key 1 speaks the true name of the foe you look at backwards: it's banished till the minute ends, unseen and out of the fight with you (not a boss; a strong will may hold).
