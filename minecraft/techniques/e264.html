Esoteric Art

Cursed paintings that swallow creatures into painted subspaces stocked with ink shikigami, structures and traps.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Impasto Canvas1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Place the chosen effect (Technique Key 1) where you look, inside your painting: Ink Wolves (up to 5, real wolves that fight for you, 9 damage a bite) · Difficult Ground (foes wade through 4.5 blocks of it) · a Structure (a real ink wall for 10 minutes; with Variant a bridge of ink two wide, 12 blocks from your feet the way you look) · a Pit Trap (held 3 s and 14 damage when stepped on, and the soft ground under it opens into a real pit; with Variant, 3 CE more, a Rolling Stone trap: a boulder of ink rolls 18 blocks the way you looked, 44 damage to each foe in its path, knocked down) · an Ink Crow (2 CE more, up to 2, 14 damage a peck) · the Ink Wave (1 CE more, a 18-block cone, 20 damage and shoved back).

3rd

Giant Ink Shikigami3 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

With Air Brush: a painting opens beside the foe you look at (18 blocks) and a great ink beast strikes out of it, 62 damage.

11th

Panoramic Composition2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Foes within 6 blocks of where you look (up to 10, 2 CE each beyond the first) are sucked into the painting for 3 s: held, unseen, and blind (a nimble foe resists).

1st

Paint2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The ground around where you look becomes your painting: 18 blocks around it, for 10 minutes.

Domain

18th

Magnum Opus

The Domain is your painting: two Impasto effects simply happen to every chosen foe. Ink tears at it (27 damage) and a pit takes it (held 1.5 s).

Air Brush

Inside your painting, once every 3 s a blow on you has a third of a chance to land on a painting formed in its path instead (1 CE).
