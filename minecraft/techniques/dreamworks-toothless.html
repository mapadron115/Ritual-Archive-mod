Night Fury

The unholy offspring of lightning and death itself: plasma blasts from the dark, echolocation, and a storm worn like armor.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Plasma Blast1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z) · hold to pour

A concussive blast along your look (72 blocks): 13 damage and knocked back. Hold the key a second for an explosive blast (2 CE more): 27 damage to everything within 6 blocks of where it lands, and soft ground there is blown out.

14th

Stormcall4 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

While riding the lightning, or under a thunderstorm: a bolt from the sky where you look (72 blocks), 33 damage to everything within 12 blocks (a nimble foe takes half).

6th

Lightning-Rider5 CE · 3 s cooldown · Quick (G)

A minute wreathed in lightning: you fly, your blasts deal 5 damage more, and whoever strikes you in melee takes 11 damage, once a second. The first each long rest is free.

Maximum

11th

Maximum: Lightning's Heir12 CE · Maximum (X)

A minute as the storm's heir: you ride the lightning, blasts deal 11 damage more and explosive ones burst 12 blocks wide, Stormcall is free, and once, the Plasma Blast is a full thunderhead: 66 damage within 12 blocks.

Passives

3rd

Echolocation

You see through the dark, and every creature within 24 blocks is shown to you alone, through walls.

18th

He Who Drinks the Sky

Lightning heals you instead of hurting you.
