Grow a network through the battlefield, mend from any Node, and let the fallen feed the living.

Symbiotic Pulse1 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You regain 15% of your health, 10% more within 2 blocks of a Node, and as much again for every Nutrient you hold (they're spent).

Grasping Mycelium2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

1st

Plant Node1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)
