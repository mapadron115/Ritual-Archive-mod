Gorgon's Regard

Force every opponent to choose between fighting blind and risking progressive petrification.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Stone Glare2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (36 blocks) meets your eyes: 22 damage, and it calcifies a step at once.

1st

Serpent Lash0 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The hair bites the foe you look at (6 blocks): 9 damage. Shell-Crack: one at 3 or more sheds a step and is knocked flat.

3rd

Bronze Reflection1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Make the polished thing you look at (18 blocks: glass, ice, polished stone, a metal block) a mirror for the gaze, while the Regard lasts (up to 6, within 36 blocks).

1st

The Regard2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute, each 3 s every foe within 36 blocks whose eyes are on you or a mirror may calcify a step (a resist holds it): slowed, then weakened (30% softer), then held, then stone 3 s (great foes only stiffen). A foe that can't see you for a 3 s sheds a step. A blind foe is safe.
