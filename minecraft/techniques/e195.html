Headless Road

Manifest a black steed and a detachable spectral Head; closing on your quarry builds Gallop for pursuit strikes and control.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Spine Whip1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A whip of spine lashes the foe you look at (9 blocks): 27 damage, and 9 damage more per Gallop spent (all of it). Unless it endures, it's slowed and can't react for 3 s; your Road's End is hauled to you instead.

3rd

The Severed Head2 CE · Cast (Z)

Your Head floats beside you for ten minutes, glaring at the nearest foe within 8 blocks: 9 damage every 3 s. With Variant you set it down where you look (18 blocks) to watch from there; a foe that reaches it knocks it back to you, and that hurts (9 damage).

6th

Name on the Road2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Name the foe you look at (36 blocks): unless its will holds, it's your Road's End for a minute. It glows, you're told where it is, your Gallop builds on it from any direction, and Last Name Spoken finds it wherever you look.

1st

Black Steed2 CE · 6 s cooldown · Quick (G)

The headless horse rises under you for a minute and carries you, far faster than you run (it fades when you dismount). Gallop builds as you close on your quarry.

Passives

1st

Gallop

Riding, every 4 blocks you close on the foe nearest where you look gives a Gallop (up to 5). A weapon blow with Variant held spends one for 9 damage more.

Domain

18th

The Road With No Turning

The road runs only toward you: every chosen foe is dragged toward you and takes 36 damage, and you gain Gallop.
