Zero Gravity

Five fingertips, and the world lets go. Make enemies, debris and yourself weightless, then decide exactly when gravity gets to remember.

6arts
3passives
4technique feats
noneDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Zero Gravity1 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Touch the foe you look at (3 blocks; any size): it goes weightless, drifting up 5 blocks and hanging there until you release it. With no leverage, its blows on you land at three quarters.

1st

Lighten Self1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You go weightless: you drift down slowly and never take fall damage (it counts toward your limit). Cast again to land.

6th

Debris Lift1 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

Lift 5 real blocks out of the ground around you to orbit you, weightless (each counts toward your limit). Release lets them fall where they are.

11th

Meteor Shower3 CE · Cast (Z)

Drop every lifted chunk where you look (36 blocks), spread over 12 blocks around: 7 damage each, and the rubble lands as blocks.

18th

Weightless World3 CE · Cast (Z)

Every foe within 9 blocks goes weightless at once.

1st

Release0 CE · 0.2 s cooldown · Quick (G)

Fingertips together: gravity returns to everything at once, and the weightless are slammed down to fall the whole way.

Passives

1st

Target Limit

7 weightless things at once. Over the limit your stomach rebels: each 3 s you may be sick (nausea and poison).

3rd

Gunhead Martial Arts

Your blows on weightless foes deal 5 damage more and knock them twice as far.

18th

Weightless World (passive)

Any size can be touched, and the limit doubles.

Technique feats

1st

Iron Stomach

Her stomach still complains; she has stopped listening: 2 more weightless things, and the nausea grips you less often.

3rd

Tether Pull

Technique Key 1 (1 CE): drag the weightless foe you look at (18 blocks) toward you.

11th

Precision Drop

Meteor Shower is aimed by hand: each strike falls on a foe in the radius in turn, and none on anyone else.

6th

Combat Float

While you're weightless you fly, swimming through the air.
