Fateweaver

Webs restrain. The sense warns. The spider emerges. And beneath it all, threads: every creature connected to its own fate, and the weaver pulling them.

4arts
4passives
2technique feats
18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Webshot1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A web along your look (18 blocks): what it hits is bound for 3 s (a strong foe tears free sooner), and real cobwebs are left where it lands for a few seconds.

6th

Pull a Thread2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (18 blocks) is dragged 9 blocks toward you along its fate-thread (a strong foe holds).

11th

Web Dominion6 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute, a sphere of real webbing 9 blocks around where you look: every foe in it takes 55 damage each 3 s (a tough foe halves it) and is slowed.

3rd

The Spider Emerges4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute limbs burst from you: you strike far faster and your weapon blows bite 14 damage harder, but you can't slip blows, and the spider feeds on you each 3 s.

Passives

1st

Spider Physique

Strength and Dexterity +2, and both caps with them. You jump far higher and never take fall damage. Once every 3 s your weapon blow grips, slowing the foe.

3rd

Spider-Sense

A blow about to land may be slipped by spending 3 CE (50% of the time).

3rd

Wall-Crawling

You climb walls (sneak to cling).

6th

Thread Sense

Every creature within 16 blocks shows to you.

Domain

18th

Web of Destiny

Every chosen foe's fate-thread is pulled taut: it's bound in webbing for 1.5 s and drawn toward the heart of the web.
