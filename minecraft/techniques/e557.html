Fateweaver

Webs restrain. The sense warns. The spider emerges. And beneath it all, threads: every creature connected to its own fate, and the weaver pulling them.

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Technique Key 1 (C) does something for this technique beyond any art listed on it. Look for it in the text below.

Arts

1st

Webshot1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A web along your look (36 blocks): what it hits is bound for 3 s (half that if it resists), and real cobwebs are left where it lands: one on a foe, a patch three across on a surface for 10 s. With Variant it's a web-line: you're carried to the surface you look at.

6th

Pull a Thread2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

The foe you look at (36 blocks) is dragged toward you along its fate-thread, or with Variant thrown the way you look, unless it resists. Either way its next blow within 3 s, on you or an ally, lands at half.

11th

Web Dominion6 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute, a sphere of real webbing 9 blocks around where you look: every foe in it takes 55 damage each 3 s (half if it resists) and is slowed, and shows to you alone.

6th

Swarm Body3 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A feat. For a minute you're a swarm of spiders: unseen, small enough for a one-block gap, weapon blows and shots on you land at half, and foes within 1.5 blocks take 14 damage each 3 s. You can't strike or use other arts. Cast again to reform, or with Variant to reform where you look (36 blocks).

3rd

The Spider Emerges4 CE · 1 s cooldown · Quick (G)

For a minute limbs burst from you: you strike twice as fast, reach a block farther, climb faster, your weapon blows bite 14 damage harder and your mandibles hold what they grip, but you can't slip blows, and the spider feeds on you each 3 s.

Maximum

18th

Sever Thread10 CE · Maximum (X)

Only inside your own Web of Destiny, on the foe you look at: all your CE (10 at least) cuts its thread. A lesser foe dies; anything else takes 89 damage. The Domain ends, and you're spent for a minute.

Passives

1st

Spider Physique

Strength and Dexterity +2, and both caps with them. You jump three times as high and never take fall damage. Your empty hand is a stinger (+4 attack). Once every 3 s your weapon blow grips, slowing the foe; with Variant held it's the mandibles, and the foe is held 1 s unless it resists.

3rd

Spider-Sense

A blow worth a tenth of your health may be slipped (50% of the time) for 3 CE, spent only when it works. You're warned of a creature hunting you from out of sight and of a shot flying at you.

3rd

Wall-Crawling

You climb walls (sneak to cling), and hang from a ceiling and cross it while sneaking.

6th

Thread Sense

Every creature within 36 blocks shows to you, through walls.

Domain

18th

Web of Destiny

Every chosen foe sees every path of its life at once: 89 damage, bound in webbing for 1.5 s and drawn toward the heart of the web. One that resists takes half and isn't held. The Maximum key cuts a thread.

Technique feats

1st

Venom

Your webs and your stinger bite: Webshot, and once every 3 s an empty-handed blow, also deal 14 damage and poison the foe for 6 s unless it resists.

6th

Swarm Body

Unlocks the Swarm Body art.

6th

Fate Sever

Variant + Technique Key 1 (3 CE): cut the foe you look at from its fate for 3 s: it's weakened, can't answer blows, and resists nothing of yours.

6th

Fate's Glimpse

Technique Key 1 (3 CE): touch the thread of the foe you look at. For two 3 ss, every attack it makes on you fails.
