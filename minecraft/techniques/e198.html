House of Bats

Every Echo is a placed sound on the map: a node you can swap to, hear through, and strike from.

18thDomain

Numbers follow this, as the book prints them at that level with default settings. Blows vary ±15% around them.

Arts

1st

Shriek1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

A shriek where you look (18 blocks): 18 damage to every foe within 3 blocks of it, and an Echo stays there for a minute (up to 6). Each 3 s bats boil out of every Echo at up to 3 foes within 4.5 blocks: 9 damage each.

3rd

Razor Swoop2 CE · 3 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

You fly up to 18 blocks down your look and cut the first foe in the way: 25 damage, and it can't react for 3 s. Begun within 3 blocks of an Echo, 9 damage more. A foe below a quarter of its health takes the blow twice over unless it endures.

6th

Ceiling of Wings2 CE · 1 s cooldown · Cast (Z)

For a minute a swarm fills 12 blocks round where you stand: foes inside see only darkness, and a mote shows you every creature in it.

1st

Echo Swap1 CE · 0.5 s cooldown · Quick (G)

You're at the Echo nearest where you look, and your next weapon blow within 3 s comes from the sound: 9 damage more.

House Where Dawn Was Tested

The black cave answers every sound: every chosen foe is swarmed (36 damage) and blinded, and an Echo forms at one of them.

Technique feats

1st

Roost Walker

You climb walls like a roosting bat (sneak to cling still), and sneaking under a ceiling, you hang from it.

3rd

Blood Echo

While any Echo stands, the House listens for you: a mote shows over every creature within 16 blocks.

3rd

Silent Wing

A Razor Swoop that cuts nothing carries you on to an Echo within 18 blocks of where it ended; one that lands makes its prey lose you for a moment.

11th

Vanishing Wing

Once every 10 s, a blow that would land finds only an Echo (1 CE): you're at one within 9 blocks and it misses.
