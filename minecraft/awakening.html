Section I

The picks

It opens by itself at your first join, one step at a time.

  1. Ancestry. One of five. Its shared traits come with every heritage.
  2. Heritage. The specific kind of that ancestry, which sets your increases and traits.
  3. Class. One of seven. See Classes. It comes before scores because Heavenly Restriction can buy higher.
  4. Ability Scores. Spend the point buy and place your increases. See Ability Scores.
  5. Size. A choice only for Deskunte and Cursed Corpses. A Dragon picks its Breath Weapon's element here too.
  6. Background. A free feat from its category, a small feature, and a starting kit of real items.
  7. Mindset. What your one Inspiration does when you spend it.
  8. Technique. One of the techniques. Heavenly Restriction takes none.
  9. Awaken. Review every pick, then confirm. The Ritual Archive is given to you after this.

It keeps opening once a session until it's confirmed, and K opens it instead of the book until then. Each step has arrows and a Random button, and the technique step has a search box. Once confirmed, the picks are locked.

Sumerian

From a different world. Third Eye: foes within 16 blocks show through walls every few seconds.

Deskunte+2, +1Third Eye. Large if you like. Tough Body: +1 health every 2 levels. Savage Attacks: critical blows 25% harder. Warrior Training: +2 armor.

Shaama+2, +1Third Eye. Innate Cursed Technique: one more technique feat.

Cursed Spirit

Spiritual Nature: no hunger, no air, poison can't take hold; blows from creatures without Cursed Energy are halved, and they notice you only close up. Cursed Energy Affinity: no Reverse Cursed Technique (except Dragons), but from 5th, below half health, Cursed Energy knits you (more from 10th).

Cursed Womb+2, +1Hard Shell: with no armor on, +10 armor. Strong Constitution: +1 health per level.
Dragon+2, +1Keeps Reverse Cursed Technique. Race Art on the ground: Spectral Wings, a minute of flight. Race Art in the air: Breath Weapon, a cone of your element. Each once a long rest.

Cursed Vessel

A sorcerer reborn in a stolen or painted body. This Body Is Mine: what would reshape your body against your will doesn't hold.

Cursed Corpse

An animated construct. Unnatural Nature: no hunger, no air, never sleepless. A fifth faster. Small, Medium or Large.

Race Art

J is your heritage's own art, if it has one (Imaginary, Disease, Dragon), each use once a long rest. A Dragon's key depends on where you are: on the ground it raises Spectral Wings, and in the air it's the Breath Weapon.
