Ability Scores

Six scores, each a small permanent bonus or cost in the game's own systems.

❖ Contents

Section I

Point buy

Set at the Awakening.

Every score starts at 0, and you have 66 points at one point each. Spread evenly, that's 11 in all six. A score can be bought from 0 to 20, or to 25 for Heavenly Restriction. You can't confirm until every point is spent. Your heritage's increases go on top.

Section II

What a point does

10 is neutral.

Each point above 10 is a bonus, and each point below it costs the same amount. That's steep on purpose. The one limit on the penalties: Constitution never takes your max health below 10.

ScorePer point from 10
Strengthweapon blows +3% and a little more knockback
Dexteritymovement +2% and attack speed +3%
Constitution+1 max health (half a heart)
Intelligencetechnique damage +2%
WisdomCursed Energy regen +5%, harmful effects 5% shorter
Charismafoes resist your control and mind effects 3% less

Villagers also trade 1% cheaper per point of Charisma above 10.

Strength in the world

From 15 Strength your bare hands mine like a wooden pickaxe (stone), from 20 a stone one (iron), from 30 an iron one (diamond), and from 40 a diamond one (obsidian).

Section III

Ability Score Improvements

From the book's Feats tab.

You earn one at each of 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th and 19th level, on top of your feats. Each gives +2 to one score, or +1 to two.

Section IV

The cap

Nothing passes 20, with a few listed exceptions.

SourceWhat it does
Heavenly RestrictionAll six caps start at 25 at 1st level and rise by 1 per level, to 40. Heavenly Physique also gives a point every level to put on Strength, Dexterity or Constitution.
Juggernaut, at 20thStrength and Constitution cap at 24.
Techniques: Fateweaver, Kinetic Transmission, Projection Sorcery, Shrine, SiphonTheir boosts add points on top of the score and raise its cap by the same amount. Their pages say when.
