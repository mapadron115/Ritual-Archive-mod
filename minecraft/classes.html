Classes

Seven ways to be a sorcerer, or to be something else. A class sets how deep your Cursed Energy runs and what your Class Art key does.

Section I

Juggernaut

A brawler with the least CE: a tough hide, harder melee, and a Fighting Focus that shrugs off blows and won't let you fall.

CE: 9 at 1st, +2 per level, 47 at 20th. N is the Class Art, and Variant + N its second use where it has one.

1st

Thick Hide

+1 armor per Mastery (+6 now).

1st

Combat Prowess

+5% melee damage per Mastery (+30% now).

2nd

Fighting Focus

Class Art: a minute of focus. You take 20% less damage, deal 15% more in melee, and can't be knocked back. 6 uses per long rest.

2nd

Reckless Blows

Variant + Class Art toggles it: a full-strength blow has a 15% chance to land as a critical even off the ground, but you take 15% more damage.

5th

Extra Attack

+12% attack speed.

5th

Pursuer

Entering Fighting Focus lunges you at the nearest foe.

7th

Honed Instincts

The first blow you take after a quiet spell starts Fighting Focus on its own (if you have a use left).

9th

Brutal Blows

Your criticals deal +75% more.

10th

Lock In

In Fighting Focus, a killing blow leaves you at half a heart instead: always the first time, each time after 25% less surely, until a long rest.

14th

Curse Resistance

In Fighting Focus nothing can wither, poison, weaken, blind or darken you.

15th

Endless Focus

Fighting Focus doesn't run down while you're fighting; it ends once the fight is over.

20th

Unstoppable

+4 Strength and +4 Constitution, to 24 at most.

Section II

Scout

Speed and evasion: faster on foot, a dash, reading a foe's weak points, and dodging the blow that would have mattered.

CE: 12 at 1st, +3 per level, 69 at 20th. N is the Class Art, and Variant + N its second use where it has one.

1st

Quick Strikes

+10% attack speed, and a full-strength blow has a 5% chance to land as a critical even off the ground.

1st

Unparalleled Movement

+30% movement speed (it grows at 6, 10, 14 and 18). From 9th you step up full blocks without jumping, swim half again as fast, and while sprinting you run up walls and across water (sneak to stop).

2nd

Swift Movement

Class Art: dash the way you're facing, untouchable for a moment, and every foe nearby loses track of you. Every 0.75 s.

2nd

Sharp Eyes

Variant + Class Art: read the foe nearest your crosshair for 8 s. It glows, and your full-strength blows on it land as criticals 20% more often. Every 10 s.

5th

Eagle Eyes

Hostile creatures within 32 blocks of you glow.

5th

Extra Attack

+10% more attack speed.

7th

Evasion

Projectiles and explosions hit you for 35% less.

10th

Tactical Retreat

When a foe hits you up close, you get +120% speed for 1.5 s.

13th

Scanning Eyes

Sharp Eyes also tells you the foe's health, armor and attack, and your blows on it deal 10% more.

14th

Sixth Sense

Nothing can blind or darken you.

17th

Swift Dodge

A blow of a tenth of your health or more misses you entirely: 3 times per long rest.

20th

Unfathomable Speed

Swift Movement is ready twice as often, and nothing can slow you.

Section III

Warrior

The soldier: a steel body, faster blows every few levels, a second wind, and harmful effects that slide off.

CE: 12 at 1st, +3 per level, 69 at 20th. N is the Class Art, and Variant + N its second use where it has one.

1st

Steel Body

+1 armor per Mastery (+6 now).

1st

Combat Prowess

+5% melee damage per Mastery (+30% now).

2nd

Undaunted

Class Art: a second wind, healing 28% of your health. Two charges; each comes back after 2 minutes.

5th

Extra Attack

+40% attack speed (it grows at 11, 17 and 20).

7th

Battle Readiness

The first blow you land after 10 s out of a fight deals 50% more.

9th

Unstoppable

A harmful effect that lands on you is shaken off at once: every 45 s.

14th

Steel Spirit

Harmful effects on you run out a third faster.

15th

Unerring Blows

A full-strength blow has a 10% chance to land as a critical even off the ground.

Section IV

Strategist

The deepest CE and the most control: a reserve to draw on, and modifications that bend every art you cast.

CE: 18 at 1st, +5 per level, 113 at 20th. N is the Class Art, and Variant + N its second use where it has one.

1st

Quick Recovery

A latent pool of 10 CE, refilled on a long rest. Class Art draws 1 CE from it.

2nd

Technique Creativity

Pick two modifications below (a third at 10th, a fourth at 17th). Variant + Class Art switches which one is on; while on, it bends every art you cast, for its extra CE.

15th

Sorcerous Restoration

5 minutes out of a fight puts 4 CE back in Quick Recovery's pool.

20th

Signature Move

Pick one of your technique's arts below (not the Maximum): it costs no CE.

Modification (picks at 2nd, 2nd, 10th, 17th)

Section V

Shikigami User

Fights beside a bound shikigami of its own: it strikes with you, guards you, and whisks you out of danger.

CE: 12 at 1st, +3 per level, 69 at 20th. N is the Class Art, and Variant + N its second use where it has one.

1st

Shikigami Companion

Class Art: call your bound shikigami (free once per long rest, then 3 CE). It fights beside you until it falls or 10 minutes pass. Class Art again sends it at the foe nearest your crosshair; Variant + Class Art dismisses it.

1st

Martial Arts

+10% attack speed and +5% melee damage.

2nd

Shared Technique

When you cast an art (not a Maximum; a held one as a tap), your companion casts it too, from where it stands at the foe it's fighting, for its CE again: once every 3 s.

5th

Coordinated Blow

When your blow lands on a foe, your companion leaps at it too (every 1.5 s).

7th

Shikigami Improvement

Your companion flies and bites harder. Choose its form below.

10th

United Front

Once a second, when you hit a foe near your companion, it adds a bite of its own; when you're below 40% health, it throws the foe back instead.

13th

Advanced Shikigami

It bites harder again. Choose its greater form below.

15th

Close Bond

Your companion takes 12% less damage, and once per long rest it survives a killing blow.

17th

Swift Escape

A blow of 15% of your health or more is halved, and your companion whisks you to its side: 6 times per long rest.

20th

Perfected Bond

Your companion bites 25% harder and moves 25% faster.

Shikigami Form (picks at 7th)

Weapon FormIts bite grows a step stronger.

Greater Form (picks at 13th)

OptionWhat it does

Greater Weapon FormIts bite grows two steps stronger.
Section VI

Support Specialist

Barriers and healing: the Curtain, a Barrier that stops shots and keeps foes out, and the strongest Reverse Cursed Technique.

CE: 15 at 1st, +4 per level, 91 at 20th. N is the Class Art, and Variant + N its second use where it has one.

1st

Curtain

Curtain, on the Art List (hold Switch), 5 CE: a black dome 96 blocks across, for as long as you like. Inside, the sky darkens, monsters come twice as fast, and every one of them glows.

2nd

Barrier Technique

Barrier, on the Art List (up to 5 CE): a barrier dome where you stand, 2 blocks plus 3 per CE in radius. Shots can't cross into it and foes are pushed out; each blow it stops wears it down (it holds 50% of your health per CE). Lasts until it breaks; blows from outside wear it half as fast.

5th

Reverse Cursed Technique

You know RCT, and it heals twice as much per CE.

10th

Reverse Technique Understanding

RCT heals three times as much per CE, also heals the most hurt ally within 8 blocks, and comes back twice as fast. Your RCT reserve is twice as deep. A bare-handed blow on an undead creature spends 1 CE and a tenth of the reserve to add 15.7 radiant damage (once a second).

17th

Mastered Reverse Technique

RCT heals three and a half times as much per CE and comes back three times as fast. Your reserve is three times as deep, and the radiant blow lands twice as hard.

20th

Permanent Techniques

Your Curtain and Barrier hold until they break, you raise another, or you leave.

Section VII

Heavenly Restriction

No cursed energy and no technique: a body built past every limit instead. Strength, speed, toughness, and a body no Domain can find.

No Cursed Energy at all. N is the Class Art, and Variant + N its second use where it has one.

1st

Restrained Body

No cursed energy, technique, Reverse Cursed Technique or Black Flash. In exchange: +30% max health, +35% melee damage, +1 armor per Mastery, you see in the dark, hostile creatures near you glow, and poison wears off twice as fast.

1st

Unarmored Movement

+35% movement speed (it grows at 6, 10, 14 and 18); you step up full blocks without jumping, swim half again as fast, and while sprinting you run up walls and across water (sneak to stop).

1st

Martial Arts

+10% attack speed.

1st

Heavenly Strength

Every melee blow carries the body's own force on top (+13.2 damage now), and it rises with your level like a sorcerer's arts.

1st

Unseen by Cursed Energy

There's nothing in you for a curse to sense: creatures go after another player near you first.

1st

Heavenly Physique

A body past human limits: your scores can rise to 40 (25 at 1st, one more each level, to 40), and every level gives a point to put on Strength, Dexterity or Constitution (in the Class tab).

2nd

Body Improvements

One body point per level, spent below on strength, speed, durability and mind.

2nd

Fighting Style

Choose one below (improved).

3rd

Ideal

Choose Prideful or Vengeful below; each grows at 9th, 13th and 17th. Its arts are on your Class Art key.

5th

Extra Attack

+40% more attack speed (it grows at 11, 18 and 20).

7th

Improved Body

+10% melee damage and a little more speed; nothing can poison you.

7th

Resistant

Projectiles and explosions hit you for 35% less.

9th

Weapon Specialist

Your criticals deal +75% more.

10th

Celestial Body

A Domain's guarantee can't find you: hostile sure-hits pass you by, and their barriers don't hold you in.

10th

Heavenly Physique

You take 15% less damage from everything.

14th

Improved Fighting Style

Your fighting style improves.

15th

Demigod's Figure

You hold your breath twice as long, and walls can't suffocate you.

20th

God of War

+10% damage and attack speed, 15% less damage from blows up close, and once per long rest a killing blow leaves you at half a heart.
