Section I

Levels and chapters

You grow from 1 to 20 by binding chapters into your book. Nothing else raises your level.

An Archive Chapter is a crafted item, one for each level from 2 to 20. Each is a book plus materials, and the materials follow natural progression, so reaching a chapter means reaching the part of the game that gives it.

  • A chapter works only at the level just below its own. A chest find can't skip you ahead.
  • Reading the chapter for level N costs N experience levels, and it's used up.
  • Chapters can be traded, and there's no binding to one player. Farms are fine.
  • Reading one refills your Cursed Energy, and new pages appear in the Archive.

Section II

Cursed Energy and arts

Every art costs CE. CE refills on its own.

Your maximum CE comes from your class and level. Some feats add to it.

Arts

A technique's arts sit in a cycle: V moves to the next unlocked art and Z casts it. The quick art has its own key (G), as do the Maximum (X) and Technique Key 1 (C), which does what each technique says. An art that fails to go off costs nothing and starts no cooldown.

A round is 3 s. It's the beat for once-a-round effects, per-round blows and holds. Art upkeep and drains are charged every 6 s instead.

Multi-hit arts: the first blow of a cast on a foe answers to hit immunity, as any blow does, and the rest of that cast's blows get past it. Single-hit arts don't.

Long rests

A long rest is sleeping through a night, or a full in-game day passing (24000 ticks). It refills CE, Inspiration, and everything that's "once a long rest".

How much a blow does

An art's strength is fixed, not rolled. The books print the number, and a landed technique blow varies ±15% around it. Chance effects stay chance: when a line says 60%, it's 60%.

Against players, technique damage is multiplied by the pvpDamage setting (1 by default). Healing and shields are shares of max health, so they grow with it.

Up to 30, armor works as in vanilla. Past 30 it keeps helping in stages: each point to 60 counts for half, to 80 for a quarter, and beyond that for an eighth. The most armor can stop rises from 80% toward 90%, which takes about 80 armor.

Section III

Black Flash and the Zone

A critical melee blow, and sometimes something more.

Any critical melee blow from a sorcerer with a technique has a chance to become a Black Flash: 3 times as hard. The chance is 1%, plus 1% for each stage of the Zone you're in. The feat Blessed by Black Sparks makes it four times as likely.

  • A Black Flash opens the Zone for 60 s. Each further one climbs a stage, up to 10, and starts the time again.
  • It also gives back CE equal to your Mastery, at most once every 6 s.
  • Each Zone stage is +20% potential, and so is standing inside your own Domain.

Potential raises melee damage, attack speed, movement, armor, technique damage and CE recovery, and it makes arts cheaper (never free).

Section IV

Reverse Cursed Technique

Healing, once you've found it at the edge of death.

Press R to turn CE into healing: 5% of your max health per CE, up to your CE power at a time, every 3 s. It only spends what the wound needs. Pressed within 0.5 s of being hit, it's instant and skips the wait, once every 6 s.

How you learn it: survive 5 s at 10% health or less. Each brush with death has a 25% chance. A Support Specialist simply knows it from 5th level.

Cursed Spirits can't use it, except Dragons. Heavenly Restriction has no CE to reverse.

Section V

Simple Domain

A small circle that a hostile Domain's sure-hit can't find you in.

Press B to draw a 3-block circle where you stand, for 60 s and 4 CE. Press it again to lower the circle. Stepping out ends it, and it can't be drawn during Burnout.

How you learn it: defend a village from a raid (getting Hero of the Village), or take the feat. Heavenly Restriction can't use it.

Section VI

Domains

A barrier that holds your foes in with you, and a hit that can't miss.

136 of the 222 techniques have a Domain. Most unlock at 18th. The Domain Index lists them all.

Press Y to open yours. As a rule it costs 20 CE, lasts 60 s, reaches 54 blocks and lands its sure-hit every 3 s; a few differ, with radii from 36 to 105 blocks. The first sure-hit lands as it opens. A sure-hit that holds its foe holds it for 1.5 s.

  • Who's chosen: players you can harm, monsters, neutral creatures other than golems, anything hunting you, and anything you've struck in the last 5 s. Untouched animals and villagers are left alone, and so are pets unless their owner is a player you can harm.
  • Held in: once chosen, a foe that reaches the edge of a closed Domain is put back inside until it ends.
  • Who a sure-hit misses: anyone in a Simple Domain or similar barrier, a Heavenly Restriction body with Celestial Body, and anyone inside a clashing rival's Domain.
  • Inside your own Domain: +20% potential.

The barrier

A closed Domain's barrier has health: three times your max health, plus 10 per CE power, plus a tenth of your refinement. Shots from outside stop at it and hurt it, and so do melee swings from outside.

A blow on you worth 20% of your max health cracks the barrier. A second within 6 s shatters it.

Open Domains

Some have no barrier at all: Zugzwang Engine (Kinetic Transmission), I am Atomic (Shadow Eminence), Malevolent Shrine (Shrine). Nothing holds foes in and there's nothing to break, but the reach is still its radius.

Burnout

When a Domain ends for any reason, you get 60 s of Burnout: no arts, no CE regeneration, and no opening another. Your Domain also ends if you die or leave the dimension.

Refinement

Refinement measures how practised your Domain is, from 1 to 1000. You earn it with:

Incomplete Domain

The feat of that name lets a technique with no Domain, or one not unlocked yet, open an incomplete one: 10 CE, no barrier, no sure-hit, 20 blocks, a minute. Inside it you count as in your own Domain at half the boost. Its Burnout is half as long.

With this feat and 500 refinement, the same key opens your technique's real Domain at any level, for its own CE.

Section VII

Clashes

When hostile players' Domains overlap.

Where two clashing Domains share space, their sure-hits cancel. An open Domain has no barrier to stop at: it lands everywhere outside a closed rival and batters that rival's barrier at each of its pulses.

  • Outclassed: a refinement gap of 100 or more breaks the weaker Domain at once.
  • Otherwise, a tug-of-war: each side has a hold of 100. It drains 3 per second, plus 0.1 per point the rival's clash power is ahead of yours. At nothing, your Domain breaks. Two even Domains last about 33 s. A hold with no rival draining it recovers 2 per second.
  • Only the weakest falls: when rival Domains reach nothing at the same moment, only the one with the lowest hold breaks, or the later opener on an exact tie. The rest stay at 1 and fight on.
  • The winner gets 25 refinement, and their sure-hit lands the moment the rival's Domain falls. When a Domain falls, every Domain standing against it wins together, so the last two of a three-way really fight.
  • Three at most: a knot of more than 3 overlapping rivals breaks every Domain in it, into Burnout.
