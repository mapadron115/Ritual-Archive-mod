Feats

How you get them

Picks

You get one feat pick for every level and two at 6th, 10th, 14th and 18th, taken in the book's Feats tab. A feat's "From" is the level it can first be taken. Six Eyes takes 10 picks, so level 9 is the earliest without free picks. Some backgrounds and heritages add a free pick bound to one category, and Reincarnated adds a pick of any kind. Class feats are for their own class. Heavenly Restriction can't take anything that runs on Cursed Energy, which covers every Barrier, Cursed Energy, Reverse Cursed Technique and Special feat, and a few others.

Upgrades: many feats grow, and each step lists when it opens and its price. It spends experience levels and lapis lazuli and no pick, from the same tab, and it's free in creative. A class feat's last step isn't bought: it comes at 20th.

Technique feats are separate: each technique has its own (583 across all of them), listed on its page. They're free picks of their own, earned by level. Clan Sorcerer and Shaama each add one more.
