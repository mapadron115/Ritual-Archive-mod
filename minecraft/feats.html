How you get them

Picks

You get one general feat pick for every level, taken in the book's Feats tab. Some backgrounds and heritages add a free pick bound to one category, and Reincarnated adds a pick of any kind. Heavenly Restriction can't take anything that runs on Cursed Energy (Barrier, Cursed Energy, Reverse Cursed Technique and Special feats).

Technique feats are separate: each technique has its own (486 across all of them), listed on its page. They're free picks of their own, earned by level. Clan Sorcerer and Shaama each add one more.
