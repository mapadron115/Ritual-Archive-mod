Section I

Installing

NeoForge, Java 21, and one jar. Nothing else is required.

This is the short version. Download and Install has the download and every step. The mod is in testing.

NeedsVersion
Minecraft1.21.1, exactly
NeoForge21.1.265 or newer, for 1.21.1
Java21
Other modsNone. The only dependencies it declares are NeoForge and Minecraft.
  1. Install NeoForge for Minecraft 1.21.1 and create a profile with it.
  2. Put the jar (ritualarchive-<version>-neoforge-<mc>.jar) in that profile's mods folder.
  3. For multiplayer, put the same jar on the server too. Both sides need it.
Section II

Your first session

From the first join to your first art.

  1. The Awakening opens by itself when you first join. Pick your ancestry, heritage, class, scores, size, background, mindset and technique, then confirm. See The Awakening. These picks are for good.
  2. You're given the Ritual Archive, a book. Open it with K or by using it. It has tabs for Sorcerer, Class, Technique, Feats and Sorcery, and a search bar. Underlined lines are choices.
  3. Take your feat. You get one pick each level, and two at 6th, 10th, 14th and 18th. Picks that can't be undone ask you to confirm.
  4. Cast. Z casts the selected art, and V moves to the next. Some arts are held down to pour in more, and go off when you let go.
  5. Level up. Craft the chapter for level 2 and read it. See Levels and chapters.

The Archive only shows what you've reached: each level reveals pages and lines that weren't there before. They're hidden, not greyed out. This site shows them all.

Section III

Keys

The defaults. Change them under Options, Controls, in the Ritual Archive category.

KeyDoes
KOpen the Ritual Archive
ZCast art (hold to pour)
VSwitch art
GQuick art
XMaximum
YDomain Expansion
CTechnique Key 1
RReverse Cursed Technique
BSimple Domain
NClass Art (sneak for its second use)
JRace Art (sneak: spend Inspiration)
Caps LockVariant (an art's other form, without sneaking)

Variant (Caps Lock): an art's other form answers to sneak or to this key, so you don't have to crouch. Variant + N is a class's second use, and Variant + J spends your Inspiration. Many techniques use it in their own arts, and their text says so. Clinging, gliding and stepping off read sneak alone.

The Art List: tap V to step to the next art. Hold it and a plain list comes up with no screen, so the camera stays free. The scroll wheel moves it both ways, and letting go picks the row. It also lists what every sorcerer can do and feats with no key of their own. Technique Disclosure is only there.

While an art is held out in front of you (a held sphere, say), the scroll wheel sets how far out it hangs.

Section IV

Server settings

In each world's serverconfig/ritualarchive-server.toml.

SettingDefaultWhat it does
healthPerLevel2.0Max health gained per level above 1 (2 = one heart).
damageScale1.0Multiplies all technique damage.
pvpDamage1.0Multiplies technique damage dealt to players.
techniquesBreakBlockstrueWhether techniques such as Hollow Purple erase blocks. Claims and protection mods are still respected.
domainSpacestrueWhether a closed Domain takes everyone inside it into a space of its own (its shell stays in the world). Off: Domains open in the world.

Techniques respect claims and protection mods when they break or place blocks. Chapter recipes are plain JSON in data/ritualarchive/recipe/, so a modpack can change them.

Section V

Operator commands

For testing. They all start with /ritualarchive and need operator permission.

CommandDoes
level <n> [player]Sets a level, 1 to 20, and refills CE.
ce [player]Refills Cursed Energy.
forget [player]Forgets the technique.
learn rct|simple [player]Teaches Reverse Cursed Technique or Simple Domain.
class <id|none> [player]Sets a class. Ids: juggernaut, scout, warrior, strategist, shikigami, support, heavenly.
rest [player]Takes a long rest.
unlearnfeats [player]Clears feats and gives the picks back.
blackflash [count] [player]The next critical hits are sure Black Flashes.
sun <0-4|clock>Holds Sunshine's Solar Station at a rank, or returns it to the world clock.
