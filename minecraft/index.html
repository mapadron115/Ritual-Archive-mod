Foreword

What this is

Ritual Archive is a NeoForge mod for Minecraft 1.21.1. It brings the Ritual Archive, a fan-made Jujutsu Kaisen homebrew for tabletop play, into the game: you take one innate technique, spend Cursed Energy on its arts, grow from level 1 to 20 by binding chapters into a book, and open a Domain of your own if your technique has one.

It's a rebuild of the owner's Terraria mod, and the design carries over from there. It's built for multiplayer and modpacks: player against player is what the numbers are measured against, not vanilla mobs. The server decides everything, and its settings can be changed.

The techniques aren't only from Jujutsu Kaisen. The archive also covers other anime and manga, video games, Marvel, DC, SCP, Magic: The Gathering, cryptids, mythology and its own originals.

How this site is made

Technique, class and feat text comes straight from the mod's source: each book line is worked out at every level, so what you read here is what the Archive prints in the game, with the default server settings. Blows vary a little around the printed number. Where this site and the game disagree, the game is right, and it would be worth telling the owner.
