Techniques

All 269 cursed techniques, read straight from the mod. Open one to see its arts, passives, Maximum, Domain and feats.

Numbers are averages at the level you pick, the same way the in-game book shows them. Damage jumps at Wall of Flesh, Plantera, the Lunatic Cultist and the Moon Lord. Healing and shields grow with max life. Greyed lines unlock later.
