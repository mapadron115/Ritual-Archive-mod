How it plays

Rules

The archive's rules, as they work in real time. One round is 6 seconds, 5 feet is 2.5 tiles, and the in-game book shows every number as it plays at your level.

The Ritual Archive book

Every character starts with the book. Open it with K (or use the item). It holds your class, your technique, your feats and your status: level, CE, refinement, and which features you've unlocked.

  • You pick one class and one innate technique. A new technique replaces the old one.
  • Each technique's page lists its arts with their unlock level and cost, plus its passives, Maximum, Domain and feats. Anything above your level is shown greyed out.

Levels

Your level (1 to 20) comes from bosses. A milestone boss lifts you to the start of a new band. Every other boss adds one level, up to the band's cap. Each boss counts once per character. A kill made while you're at the cap is banked and applies when the next milestone lifts it.

Your level sets your mastery bonus (2 at level 1, rising to 6 at 17) and your CE power (3, then 4 from level 6, then 5 from 11). Many arts grow with both. The book already does that math for you.

Cursed Energy

  • Every art lists its CE cost. If you can't pay all of it, the art doesn't fire. CE spent on a miss isn't refunded.
  • CE refills on its own: a full bar in about 30 seconds out of combat, and 45 seconds in a fight. You're in a fight if you dealt or took damage in the last 5 seconds. There are no CE potions.
  • The CE bar sits at the bottom of the screen. The squares above it are your cooldowns.
  • A long rest comes every 8 in-game hours. Arts marked "once per long rest" come back then, along with other daily features.

Casting arts

  • Z casts the selected art and V switches to the next one. F casts the technique's quick art, and X its Maximum. Technique Keys Q Y U P do whatever that technique gives them.
  • Some techniques also put a cycle art on F or Q as a shortcut, so you can fire their main attacks without switching. The reference lists these under "Shortcut keys".
  • Arts marked hold to charge pour CE in while you hold the key and fire when you let go.
  • An ordinary art takes half a second and shares a short cooldown with other ordinary arts. Quick arts have no cast time and share a 1-second cooldown. Instant arts (often toggles) have no cooldown. A 1 s channel holds you in place for a second.
  • An art that can't do anything (no target in range, for example) costs nothing.
  • Focus: some effects need your Focus, and you can only hold one at a time. It breaks only on a single hit of a quarter of your max life or more, or when you're stunned or frozen.
  • During Burnout your technique is locked. Weapons and RCT still work.

Damage and effects

  • Arts deal cursed damage, which your generic damage bonuses also boost. Each art's base damage grows with your level, with big jumps at Wall of Flesh, Plantera, the Lunatic Cultist and the Moon Lord.
  • No luck in an art's strength. Damage, healing and shields are fixed numbers, the ones the book and this site show. Terraria's usual small spread on each hit and its critical hits still apply. Effects that are about chance, such as the slot reels, still are.
  • Every effect expires: zones, auras, damage over time and summons all have a timer, and summons have a cap.
  • Enemies never roll hidden saves. Dodgeable attacks are skillshots, pushes and holds depend on the target's knockback resistance, and mind effects work fully on ordinary enemies.
  • Bosses resist control effects, but are never flatly immune. Bosses and worm segments can't be teleported, swallowed or dragged.
  • Marks, charges and sigils only add a bonus: every art works on a plain target.

Domain Expansion

Press C to open your technique's Domain once it unlocks (usually at level 18; each technique's page says when). Each Domain has its own name and sure-hit, so details vary. The defaults are below.

  • Cost and size: 20 CE, a 1-second channel, a 45-tile radius, 60 seconds.
  • Trapped: hostile foes inside when it opens can't leave the circle.
  • Sure-hit: fires on everything trapped as it opens, then every 3 seconds. It always lands; only its extra effects are reduced on bosses.
  • Inside your own Domain your technique hits 25% harder, rising to 60% with refinement.
  • Barrier: a red block wall with 3 × your max life + 10 × your CE power + your refinement in hit points. Enemy shots that hit it wear it down. The first big hit cracks it, and a second within 6 seconds breaks it.
  • Burnout: when any Domain ends, your technique locks for 60 seconds (shorter with refinement).

Awakening early

Your Domain can awaken before its level if you survive at least half of a Solar Eclipse and are alive when it ends, or if you reach wave 10 of a Pumpkin Moon or Frost Moon.

Refinement

Refinement points grow from every sure-hit that lands, strong foes killed inside, bosses, beating rival Domains, and a little daily training from the book. Ranks at 100, 250, 500, 750 and 1000 make your Domain faster to raise, harder-hitting, longer-lasting, wider and quicker to recover from.

Simple Domain

J raises a Simple Domain for 3 CE: 10 seconds where you stand. It halves hostile sure-hits and stops their extra effects. You learn it by beating a Goblin Army, or by clearing an Old One's Army tier for the first time (or from the Simple Domain feat).

Clashes

When two players' Domains meet, refinement decides. A gap of 100 points or more collapses the weaker Domain at once and burns its owner out.

A closer clash is a tug-of-war. Each Domain starts holding 100% of its ground and loses 3% a second, more the further its rival's clash power is ahead (a tenth of a percent per point). Clash power is refinement, plus up to 60 for the life its caster has left, minus 50 if the caster steps out of their own Domain. Two even Domains last about half a minute, so the fight inside decides it. The HUD shows your hold. At 0% the Domain breaks and its owner burns out. While the clash lasts, neither sure-hit lands inside the other's Domain, and an open Domain batters a closed barrier at each pulse.

Breaking a rival's Domain (or striking its caster down) while it clashes with yours wins the clash: 100 refinement, once per rival.

Black Flash and the Zone

  • Only a critical melee or physical hit can become a Black Flash, and rarely: a 1% chance, plus 1% per Zone stage. A Black Flash triples the hit.
  • A Black Flash puts you in the Zone for 25 seconds, and each further one refreshes it and climbs a stage, up to 10.
  • Each Zone stage, and standing in your own Domain, adds a stack of +20% potential: damage, attack speed, movement, defense, knockback and CE recovery, plus 2% crit, and arts cost less.
  • A Black Flash also restores CE equal to your mastery bonus, at most once every 6 seconds.

Reverse Cursed Technique

  • You don't start with RCT (unless your class grants it, or the setting is on). It can awaken if you survive at or below 10% life for 5 seconds: a 25% chance each time, announced in chat.
  • Press G to spend CE healing yourself, up to your CE power at a time, from a reserve that refills on a long rest. The cooldown is 3 seconds. Pressing it within half a second of taking a hit skips the cooldown (once every 6 seconds).
  • RCT can't restore CE, revive you, or end Burnout.

Feats

You get one feat pick per level, taken in the book. General feats work on their own once learned; none needs a key. Some need a level, a Domain or another feat first.

Technique feats belong to your technique and are listed on its reference page. Their picks are separate from your level picks: one each for your first kill of The Destroyer, The Twins, Skeletron Prime and Duke Fishron, then one at Plantera, one at Golem, and the rest at the Lunatic Cultist. A technique feat can add an art to F or Q, never to a new key.

Blank Scrolls

Ordinary enemies drop a Blank Scroll rarely (1 in 100), and every boss drops one. A scroll lets you change your technique, or your class, in the book. You can't change class while your Domain is open, and switching resets your class picks.

Multiplayer and PvP

  • Class, level, technique, refinement, Domains, summons and enemy statuses all sync between players.
  • With PvP on (and different teams or no team), technique damage to players is halved. Some arts also apply Held, Dazed, Sapped or Lulled.
  • Domains trap hostile players too, and hit them with their sure-hit.

Settings

Settings → Mod Configuration → Ritual Archive Settings. These are server-side in multiplayer.

  • Sandbox mode: level 20, endless CE, no Burnout, RCT known, refinement 1000, and free class and technique swaps. Turning it on resets each character to a fresh start, including Ten Shadows' tamed and fallen shikigami. Turning it off sets your level from this world's defeated bosses.
  • Level override: 0 is off. A value of 1–20 forces your level, for testing.
  • Kinder shadows: fallen Ten Shadows shikigami return after a long rest instead of dying for good.
  • RCT from the start: every sorcerer knows Reverse Cursed Technique without the breakthrough.
