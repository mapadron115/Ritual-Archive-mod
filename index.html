What's in it

Techniques come from Jujutsu Kaisen, anime and manga, video games, Marvel, DC, SCP, Magic: The Gathering, cryptids, mythology, DreamWorks, historical figures and the archive's own originals. Each one has its own arts, passives and feats. Most also have a Maximum, and more than half have a Domain Expansion with its own sure-hit.

  • Levels from bosses. Milestone bosses lift you to a new band, and other bosses add a level within it. Every level gives a feat pick.
  • Cursed Energy. Every art costs CE, and CE refills on its own. There are no potions for it.
  • Domains. A barrier that traps the foes inside and lands a sure-hit every few seconds. Fight inside it to refine it, and clash with rival players' Domains.
  • Black Flash, the Zone and Reverse Cursed Technique. These follow the archive's rules, adapted for real time.
  • Classes. Juggernaut, Scout, Warrior, Shikigami User, Support Specialist, Strategist and Heavenly Restriction, each with a Class Art on its own key.
  • Multiplayer and PvP. Domains, summons and statuses sync between players. Technique damage to players is halved.

Getting started

  1. Install tModLoader from Steam (it's free and separate from Terraria).
  2. Put JJKTechniques.tmod in Documents\My Games\Terraria\tModLoader\Mods, then enable Ritual Archive under Workshop → Manage Mods.
  3. Bind the mod's keys under Settings → Controls → Mod Controls. On a Custom controls profile they start unbound.
  4. Open the Ritual Archive book with K, pick a class, then pick your technique.
  5. Spawn a Practice Effigy (the item, or the chat command /ra dummy) to try your arts. It shows your damage per second, and stopping for 3 seconds prints a breakdown by art.

Default keys

  • Z Cast the selected art (hold to charge)
  • V Switch art
  • F Quick art
  • X Maximum
  • C Domain Expansion
  • J Simple Domain
  • G Reverse Cursed Technique
  • N Class Art (Down + N for its second use)
  • K Open the Ritual Archive
  • Q Y U P Technique Keys 1–4

Want to try everything first? Turn on Sandbox mode under Settings → Mod Configuration → Ritual Archive Settings. It sets you to level 20 with endless CE and free swaps. Turning it on resets the character, and turning it off sets your level back from the bosses this world has beaten.

Where it comes from

The mod adapts the Ritual Archive, a Jujutsu Kaisen homebrew for tabletop play. Names, costs and unlock levels follow the archive. Turns, distances, saves and dice are converted for real time: one round is 6 seconds, and 5 feet is 2.5 tiles. Each technique's numbers in this reference are shown the way they play in Terraria.
